### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

#### Inputs:
1. **Sparse Photometry**: Input vector containing observed photometric measurements in specific filters.
2. **Training Spectra**: Reference spectra used to train the PCA model.
3. **Filter Throughput Curves**: Spectral response functions of the observing instrument (e.g., JWST NIRCam).
4. **Photometric Uncertainty**: Noise level (e.g., 20%) to inject into synthetic photometry during training.

#### Outputs:
1. **Reconstructed Spectrum**: High-resolution spectrum derived from the input photometry.
2. **Taxonomic Classification**: Probabilistic assignment of the object to known spectral types (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organic-type).
3. **Uncertainty Estimates**: Quantified uncertainties on the reconstructed spectral features.

---

### Step-by-Step Pseudocode:

1. **Preprocessing:**
   - **Train PCA Model**:
     - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training spectra to obtain principal component (PC) coefficients and eigenvectors.
     - Select the number of PCs (e.g., K=8–10) based on explained variance and cross-validation results.

   - **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**:
     - Project the training spectra onto the PCA latent space.
     - Fit a Gaussian KDE to the latent coefficients to create a smooth probability distribution over the latent space.

2. **Synthetic Data Generation**:
   - For each spectrum in the training set:
     - Sample synthetic latent coefficients from the KDE.
     - Forward-model the corresponding spectrum through the instrument's filter throughput curves to compute synthetic photometry.
     - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20%) into the synthetic photometry to simulate observational uncertainties.

3. **AutoML Regressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (e.g., XGBoost) to regress from the noisy synthetic photometry to the latent coefficients.
   - Ensure the regressor captures the mapping from sparse photometric inputs to the low-dimensional latent space.

4. **Forward Inference Pipeline**:
   - **Input Photometry**: Pass the observed sparse photometry through the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - **Reconstruct Spectrum**:
     - Apply the inverse PCA transform to the predicted latent coefficients to obtain the full-resolution reconstructed spectrum.
     - Optionally, sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula to generate uncertainty estimates.

5. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Project the predicted latent coefficients into a pre-trained classifier (e.g., another AutoML model) to determine the probability distribution over spectral types.

6. **Validation and Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Compare the reconstructed spectrum to the ground truth (if available) to assess fidelity.
   - Calculate credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum using the sampled posterior distribution.

---

### Final Output:
- **Reconstructed Spectrum**: Continuous spectral estimate derived from sparse photometry.
- **Classification Probabilities**: Probabilities of belonging to different taxonomic classes.
- **Uncertainty Bounds**: Error estimates on the spectral features, providing a quantitative measure of confidence.
