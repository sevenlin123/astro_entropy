Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Input**: Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO object.
2. **Precompute PCA Basis**:
   a. Train PCA on reference TNO spectra to obtain principal components.
   b. Determine optimal number of principal components (K) via validation.
3. **Construct Latent Manifold**:
   a. Project reference spectra onto PCA basis to get latent coefficients.
   b. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create smooth probability distribution in latent space.
4. **Generate Synthetic Training Data**:
   a. Sample latent coefficients from KDE to form synthetic spectral manifold.
   b. Forward-model these spectra through target instrument filters (e.g., JWST NIRCam).
   c. Inject Gaussian noise (20% relative uncertainty) into synthetic photometry.
5. **Train AutoML Regressor**:
   a. Use synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients as training data.
   b. Train ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees to map photometry to latent space.
6. **Predict Latent Coefficients**:
   a. Pass observed photometry through trained regressor to obtain predicted latent coefficients.
7. **Reconstruct Spectrum**:
   a. Apply inverse PCA transformation to predicted latent coefficients.
   b. Obtain full-resolution reconstructed spectrum.
8. **Output**: Reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties.

End of Algorithm
