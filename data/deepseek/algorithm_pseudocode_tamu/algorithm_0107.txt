Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Input: Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO object.
2. Initialize: Load pre-trained PCA basis derived from reference TNO spectra.
3. Train: Use AutoML regressor (e.g., gradient-boosted decision trees) to map sparse photometry to latent PCA coefficients.
4. Generate Synthetic Data: Apply forward modeling through the instrument's filter response (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) to simulate photometric observations from the PCA-based synthetic spectra.
5. Augment Training Data: Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth, continuous manifold in the latent space for robust regression training.
6. Inference: 
   a. Predict latent coefficients using the trained AutoML regressor.
   b. Project latent coefficients through inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   c. Sample posterior distributions using Gaussian Copula to quantify uncertainties.
7. Classify: Apply a secondary classifier on the latent manifold to determine taxonomic categories.
8. Output: Reconstructed spectrum, credible intervals, and taxonomic probabilities.
