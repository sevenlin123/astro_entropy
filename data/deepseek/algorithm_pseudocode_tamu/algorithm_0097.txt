### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Objective:**  
Reconstruct high-resolution near-IR spectra of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs) from sparse photometric observations using a Bayesian framework and Principal Component Analysis (PCA).

**Steps:**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Train PCA on a reference set of near-IR TNO spectra to define a low-dimensional latent space.
   - The latent space captures the dominant modes of spectral variability.

2. **Data Augmentation via Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):**
   - Project the reference spectra onto the PCA latent space.
   - Use Gaussian KDE to smooth the latent distribution and generate synthetic training data.

3. **Forward Modeling for Synthetic Photometry:**
   - Simulate photometric observations by convolving the reconstructed spectra with the effective filter curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty of ~20%) into the synthetic photometry to account for observational uncertainties.

4. **Training the AutoML Regressor:**
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to regress from noisy photometric observations to latent space coordinates.
   - Input features: Sparse photometric flux measurements.
   - Target labels: Smoothed latent coefficients from KDE.

5. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):**
   - Validate the model by iteratively holding out one spectrum from the training set.
   - For each held-out object, predict its latent coefficients using the trained regressor.
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by applying the inverse PCA transform to the predicted latent coefficients.

6. **Posterior Sampling and Uncertainty Quantification:**
   - Sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula framework.
   - Preserve physical dependencies in the latent space to generate valid spectral realizations.
   - Compute credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum.

7. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Train a secondary classifier on the PCA latent space to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).
   - Use the predicted latent coefficients to determine the probability distribution over spectral types.

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed spectrum for each object, including uncertainties.
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification.

**Notes:**  
- The algorithm focuses on the core computational steps, excluding validation and visualization.
- Computational tools include PCA for dimensionality reduction, AutoML for training regressors, and KDE/Gaussian Copula for handling latent distributions.
