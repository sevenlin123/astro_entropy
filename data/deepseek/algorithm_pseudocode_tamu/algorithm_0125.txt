Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Initialize:
   - Preprocess training spectral data into a PCA basis.
   - Select the number of principal components (K) based on validation (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for high fidelity).
   
2. Train the Latent Space Model:
   a. Perform PCA on the training spectral dataset to obtain principal components.
   b. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA coefficients to create a continuous latent manifold.
   
3. Generate Synthetic Training Data:
   a. For each augmented spectrum, compute synthetic photometry by convolving with JWST/NIRCam filter throughput curves.
   b. Inject Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) into the synthetic photometry.
   
4. Train the AutoML Regressor:
   a. Use the synthetic photometry and corresponding PCA coefficients to train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees.
   
5. Forward Inference for New Observations:
   a. Input sparse photometric measurements into the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   b. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   
6. Quantify Uncertainties:
   a. Sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula framework.
   b. Propagate uncertainties through the inverse PCA transform to obtain credible intervals.
   
7. Taxonomic Classification:
   a. Project the predicted latent coefficients into a trained classifier to determine spectral type probabilities.
   
8. Output:
   - Reconstructed spectrum with uncertainties.
   - Probabilistic taxonomic classification.
