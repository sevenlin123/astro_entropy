Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Observed photometric measurements (m)
    - Pre-trained Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model
    - Training data with corresponding labels (taxonomic classes)

Output:
    - Reconstructed spectrum (S)
    - Posterior probabilities over taxonomic classes

Steps:

1. **Latent Space Definition**:
    a. Project the photometric measurements (m) into the pre-trained PCA latent space to obtain latent coefficients (z).
    b. Use an AutoML regressor (trained on augmented synthetic data) to predict the latent coefficients given the photometry.

2. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
    a. Apply the inverse PCA transform to the predicted latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum (S).
    b. Generate an ensemble of reconstructions by sampling the joint posterior distribution of the latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula framework to capture uncertainties.

3. **Taxonomic Classification**:
    a. Pass the latent coefficients (z) to a pre-trained taxonomic classifier to obtain posterior probabilities over taxonomic classes (e.g., H2O, CO2, Organic, Methanol).

4. **Validation Metrics**:
    a. Compute Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) and Pearson Correlation Coefficient (PCC) between reconstructed and true spectra.
    b. Assess credible interval coverage to evaluate uncertainty calibration.
    c. Measure taxonomic classification accuracy.

5. **Output**:
    a. Return the reconstructed spectrum (S) along with its uncertainties.
    b. Provide the taxonomic classification probabilities.

End.
