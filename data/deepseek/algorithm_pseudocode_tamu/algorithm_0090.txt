### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

#### Inputs:
- `observed_photometry`: A dictionary of observed photometric fluxes in specific filters.
- `training_spectra`: A list of reference near-infrared spectra used for PCA.
- `filter_curves`: The effective throughput curves for the NIRCam filters.

#### Outputs:
- `reconstructed_spectrum`: A high-resolution spectrum reconstructed from the observed photometry.
- `taxonomy_probability`: A dictionary of probabilities for each taxonomic classification.
- `credible_intervals`: Confidence intervals for the reconstructed spectral features.

#### Steps:

1. **Precompute Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Basis**:
   - Train PCA on `training_spectra` to obtain principal components (PCs) and loadings.
   - Select the optimal number of PCs (e.g., 4–5 for taxonomy, 8–10 for fidelity).

2. **Synthetic Data Augmentation**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA coefficients derived from `training_spectra`.
   - Generate synthetic latent vectors to augment the training data, ensuring a smooth manifold.

3. **Forward Modeling**:
   - Simulate photometric fluxes for each synthetic spectrum by convolving with `filter_curves`.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to mimic real observations.

4. **Train AutoML Regressor**:
   - Use the augmented synthetic data to train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees.
   - The regressor maps observed photometry to latent PCA coefficients.

5. **Reconstruct Spectrum**:
   - Apply the trained regressor to `observed_photometry` to predict latent coefficients.
   - Project these coefficients back to the full spectrum using the inverse PCA transformation.

6. **Generate Posterior Samples**:
   - Sample from the joint posterior of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula.
   - Ensure physical plausibility by preserving intrinsic dependencies in the latent space.

7. **Calculate Taxonomy Probabilities**:
   - Use a pre-trained classifier on the latent manifold to predict taxonomic probabilities.

8. **Compute Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Determine credible intervals for spectral features using the posterior samples.
