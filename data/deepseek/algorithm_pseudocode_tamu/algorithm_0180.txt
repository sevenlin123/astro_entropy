### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Inputs:**  
- `training_spectra`: Set of reference TNO spectra  
- `test_photometry`: Observed sparse photometric measurements  
- `filters`: Instrument-specific photometric filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam)  

**Outputs:**  
- `reconstructed_spectrum`: Full spectral energy distribution  
- `taxonomy_probability`: Probability distribution over spectral taxonomic classes  

---

**Step 1: Define the Latent Space**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Project `training_spectra` onto a PCA basis to obtain latent coefficients.
   - Determine the number of principal components (`K`) that capture the dominant spectral variability (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

2. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):**
   - Construct a smooth probability distribution in the latent space using a non-parametric KDE with a Gaussian kernel.
   - Bandwidth (`h`) is optimized (e.g., `h ≈ 0.5`) to balance fidelity and smoothness.

---

**Step 2: Training the Photometric-to-Latent Mapping**

1. **Synthetic Data Augmentation:**
   - Generate synthetic latent vectors and corresponding synthetic photometry using forward modeling with the target instrument's filter response curves.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) into synthetic photometry to simulate observational uncertainties.

2. **Automated Machine Learning (AutoML):**
   - Train an ensemble regressor (e.g., Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees) to map sparse photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Use combined synthetic and real training data for robust learning.

---

**Step 3: Reconstructing Spectra from Test Photometry**

1. **Predict Latent Coefficients:**
   - Pass `test_photometry` through the trained regressor to obtain point estimates for latent coefficients.

2. **Inverse PCA Transform:**
   - Convert predicted latent coefficients back to the full spectral space using the PCA basis.

3. **Sampling the Posterior (Optional):**
   - Generate an ensemble of reconstructions by sampling from the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula framework.
   - Preserve physical dependencies between latent dimensions to ensure physically plausible spectra.

---

**Step 4: Taxonomic Classification**

1. **Latent Space Classifier:**
   - Train a secondary classifier (e.g., AutoML) on the latent coefficients of labeled training spectra.
   - Predict the taxonomic class probabilities for `test_photometry`.

---

**Step 5: Performance Evaluation (Validation Only)**

1. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):**
   - Exclude one spectrum from training, reconstruct it, and validate against the true spectrum.
   - Metrics: RMSE, correlation coefficient, Feature Similarity Index (FSI), and credible interval coverage.

2. **Survey Design Optimization:**
   - Assess information content of filter configurations using mutual information between filters and latent features.

3. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Identify objects with reconstructed spectra deviating significantly from the latent manifold.

---

**End of Algorithm**
