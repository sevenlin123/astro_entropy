**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Data Preparation**
   - Collect a training set of reference TNO spectra.
   - Exclude outliers and rare spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Perform PCA on the training spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).
   - The PCs represent the latent space capturing spectral variability.

3. **Latent Space Augmentation**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) with a Gaussian kernel to generate synthetic latent vectors.
   - Adjust KDE bandwidth (h) to balance smoothness and fidelity.

4. **Forward Modeling**
   - Simulate photometric observations by convolving spectra with NIRCam filter curves.
   - Add Gaussian noise (20% relative uncertainty) to simulate real-world photometric errors.

5. **Training the AutoML Regressor**
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (e.g., XGBoost) to regress from photometric inputs to latent coefficients.
   - Use synthetic and real training data for robust learning.

6. **Bayesian Inference**
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Predict latent coefficients using the trained regressor.
     - Sample from the joint posterior of latent variables using Gaussian Copula to account for correlations.

7. **Spectral Reconstruction**
   - Apply inverse PCA transform to the latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Generate credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**
   - Use a secondary classifier trained on latent space to predict taxonomic labels.
   - Provide probability distributions over spectral types.

9. **Performance Evaluation**
   - Measure reconstruction error (NRMSE), credible interval coverage, classification accuracy, and filter information content via LOOCV.

10. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**
    - Quantify filter information content to optimize NIRCam band selections.
    - Flag outliers based on credible intervals, classification probabilities, or distance from the latent manifold.
