**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Input:** Reference spectra of TNOs, NIRCam filter transmission curves.
   - **Step 1:** Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the reference spectra to obtain principal components (PCs) and latent coefficients.
   - **Step 2:** Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent coefficients to create a continuous probability distribution in the latent space.
   - **Step 3:** Generate synthetic spectra by sampling the latent space using the KDE model.
   - **Step 4:** Forward-model the synthetic spectra through NIRCam filters to produce synthetic photometry with added Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty of 20%).

2. **Model Training:**
   - **Step 5:** Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map synthetic photometry to latent coefficients.
   - **Step 6:** Train a secondary AutoML classifier to classify latent coefficients into taxonomic groups.

3. **Inference Phase:**
   - **Input:** Observed photometry of a new TNO.
   - **Step 7:** Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients from the observed photometry.
   - **Step 8:** Decode the latent coefficients using the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the spectrum.
   - **Step 9:** Optionally, classify the latent coefficients to determine the taxonomic group.
   - **Step 10:** Compute uncertainties and credible intervals using a Gaussian Copula framework.

**End of Algorithm**
