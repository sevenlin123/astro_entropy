### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Step-by-Step Pseudocode:**

1. **Data Preparation**
   - Input: Sparse photometric measurements for TNOs.
   - Output: Training dataset for spectral reconstruction.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Perform PCA on a reference set of near-IR spectra to define a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Determine the number of principal components (K) based on validation (e.g., LOOCV).

3. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**
   - Use the latent coefficients from PCA to fit a non-parametric KDE, creating a smooth manifold.
   - Bandwidth (h) is optimized to balance smoothness and fidelity to the data.

4. **Forward Modeling**
   - Simulate synthetic photometry by convolving spectra with the instrument's filter response curves (e.g., JWST NIRCam).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) into synthetic photometry to account for observational errors.

5. **Training AutoML Regressor**
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (e.g., XGBoost) to map sparse photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Utilize synthetic data augmented via KDE to enhance model generalization in low-sample scenarios.

6. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**
   - Iteratively hold out one spectrum, train the model on the remaining data, and validate the reconstruction.
   - Measure reconstruction fidelity (RMSE, NRMSE, Pearson correlation) and uncertainty coverage (credible intervals).

7. **Spectral Reconstruction**
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.

8. **Uncertainty Quantification**
   - Sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula to preserve physical correlations.
   - Generate confidence intervals for the reconstructed spectrum.

9. **Taxonomic Classification**
   - Train a secondary classifier on the latent manifold to assign probabilities to spectral types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).
   - Output the probability distribution over taxonomic labels for each object.

10. **Survey Optimization**
    - Evaluate the information content of filter configurations by simulating their impact on reconstruction uncertainty.
    - Identify optimal filter sets for maximizing spectral discovery.

11. **Outlier Detection**
    - Flag objects whose posterior distributions in the latent space fall outside predefined thresholds.
    - Use photometric consistency checks to identify rare spectral types or anomalies.

**End of Algorithm**
