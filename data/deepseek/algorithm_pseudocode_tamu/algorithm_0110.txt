**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data Preparation**:
   - Obtain a dataset of reference near-infrared spectra of TNOs (seed spectra).
   - Collect sparse photometric observations for TNOs, typically 2–6 bands.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the reference spectra to reduce dimensionality.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components \( K \) (e.g., 4-5 for dominant modes, up to 10 for improved fidelity).

3. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Use the PCA basis to define a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Augment the latent space with synthetic data using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth, continuous manifold.

4. **Forward Modeling**:
   - Simulate photometric observations for each synthetic spectrum by convolving with filter responses (e.g., JWST NIRCam filters).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) into the synthetic photometry.

5. **Training the AutoML Regressor**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (e.g., XGBoost) to regress from sparse photometry to PCA latent coefficients.
   - Ensure the regressor is robust to noise and overfitting.

6. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - For new photometric observations, predict latent coefficients using the trained regressor.
   - Project latent coefficients through inverse PCA to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Sample the posterior distribution of latent coefficients using Gaussian Copula to quantify uncertainties.

7. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Classify the object into spectral types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic) by evaluating the posterior probabilities in the latent space.

8. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Identify objects whose posterior mean latent coordinates fall in regions of significantly lower density than the training manifold.

9. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Test different filter combinations to maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity and taxonomic information yield.

10. **Output**:
    - Reconstructed spectrum with uncertainties.
    - Taxonomic classification probabilities.
    - Outlier detection status.

**End of Algorithm**
