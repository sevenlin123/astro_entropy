Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Input Preparation**
   - Read sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
   - Define target spectral bands for reconstruction.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Train PCA on reference spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).
   - Project reference spectra onto the PCs to form the latent space.

3. **Data Augmentation**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on latent space to generate synthetic data.
   - Apply forward modeling to simulate photometric fluxes for synthetic spectra.

4. **Training Phase**
   - Construct training dataset combining real and synthetic photometry.
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., XGBoost) to map photometry to latent coefficients.

5. **Forward Inference**
   - For new photometric observations:
     a. Pass photometry through trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
     b. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct high-resolution spectrum.

6. **Uncertainty Quantification**
   - Sample posterior distributions of latent coefficients using Gaussian Copula.
   - Calculate credible intervals for reconstructed spectra.

7. **Taxonomic Classification**
   - Train a classifier on latent space to predict spectral type probabilities.
   - Assign taxonomic classification based on posterior probabilities.

8. **Output**
   - Provide reconstructed spectrum, credible intervals, and taxonomic probabilities.
