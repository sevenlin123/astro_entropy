```pseudocode
Algorithm Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Training Spectra: Set of reference TNO spectra
    - Photometric Observations: Sparse multiband photometry measurements
    - Filter Set: NIRCam filters used for forward modeling

Output:
    - Reconstructed Spectrum: High-resolution spectral estimate
    - Taxonomic Classification: Probabilistic assignment to spectral types
    - Uncertainty Estimates: Confidence intervals on spectral features

1. **Preprocessing and PCA Training**
    a. Normalize and preprocess the training spectra.
    b. Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to the training spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).
    c. Determine the optimal number of PCs (K) based on explained variance and LOOCV results.

2. **Synthetic Data Generation**
    a. Project the training spectra onto the PCA space to get latent coefficients.
    b. Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent coefficients to generate synthetic data points.
    c. Forward model the synthetic spectra through the selected NIRCam filters to simulate photometric observations.
    d. Inject Gaussian noise (20% relative reflectance uncertainty) into the synthetic photometry.

3. **Model Training**
    a. Split the augmented synthetic data and corresponding photometry into training and validation sets.
    b. Train an AutoML regressor (ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees) to map photometry to latent coefficients.
    c. Simultaneously train a taxonomic classifier on the latent space using labeled taxonomic groups.

4. **LOOCV Validation**
    a. For each held-out spectrum in the training set:
        i. Remove the held-out spectrum from the training data.
        ii. Refit the PCA model and generate new synthetic data.
        iii. Retrain the AutoML regressor and classifier.
        iv. Forward model the held-out spectrum to simulate photometry.
        v. Use the pipeline to reconstruct the spectrum and classify its taxonomy.
        vi. Evaluate reconstruction fidelity and uncertainty coverage.

5. **Reconstruction Pipeline**
    a. For new photometric observations:
        i. Pass the photometry through the trained regressor to obtain latent coefficient estimates.
        ii. Sample from the joint posterior distribution of the latent coefficients using Gaussian Copula.
        iii. Apply the inverse PCA transform to each sampled latent vector to reconstruct the spectrum.
        iv. Classify the spectrum into taxonomic categories using the trained classifier.

6. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**
    a. For survey optimization:
        i. Generate mock objects with random taxonomic classifications.
        ii. Compute information gain for different filter combinations.
        iii. Select filters maximizing information gain.
    b. For outlier detection:
        i. Flag objects whose reconstructed latent distributions deviate significantly from the training manifold.
```
