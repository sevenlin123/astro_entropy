Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input: 
    - Observed photometric measurements (sparse_multiband)
    - Pre-trained PCA basis (principal_components)
    - Pre-trained KDE model (latent_prior)
    - Training parameters (learning_rate, epochs)

Output:
    - Reconstructed spectrum (reconstructed_spectrum)
    - Posterior distribution of latent coefficients (latent_posterior)
    - Taxonomic classification probabilities (taxonomy_probs)

Steps:

1. **Preprocess Training Data**
    a. Extract principal components from reference spectra using PCA.
    b. Train KDE model on the latent coefficients to create a continuous prior distribution.

2. **Generate Synthetic Photometry**
    a. For each synthetic spectrum, convolve with NIRCam filter curves to simulate photometric measurements.
    b. Inject Gaussian noise (σ = 0.2 relative reflectance) to account for observational uncertainties.

3. **Train AutoML Regressor**
    a. Use synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients as training data.
    b. Train ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees to map photometry to latent space.
    c. Validate using 5-fold cross-validation to prevent overfitting.

4. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**
    a. For each held-out spectrum:
        i. Remove the spectrum from training data.
        ii. Generate synthetic latent space and train regressor.
        iii. Predict latent coefficients from photometric measurements.
        iv. Project latent coefficients through inverse PCA to get reconstructed spectrum.
        v. Compute metrics (NRMSE, FPS, UCP).

5. **Reconstruct Spectrum**
    a. Pass observed photometry through trained regressor to get predicted latent coefficients.
    b. Apply inverse PCA transform to obtain full spectrum.
    c. Sample from joint posterior of latent coefficients using Gaussian Copula to quantify uncertainties.

6. **Taxonomic Classification**
    a. Project predicted latent coefficients into pre-trained classifier.
    b. Output probability distribution over taxonomic classes (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).

7. **Outlier Detection**
    a. Compare reconstructed spectrum to expected manifold.
    b. Flag if posterior falls outside predefined thresholds.

End.
