### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Objective**: Reconstruct high-resolution spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) using sparse photometric measurements by leveraging a probabilistic latent-space framework.

**Steps**:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - **Input**: Reference TNO spectra.
   - **Output**: Principal components (PCs) representing dominant spectral variations.
   - **Process**: Perform PCA to reduce the dimensionality of the spectral data, capturing the most significant modes of variability.

2. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - **Input**: PCA components.
   - **Output**: Latent space with a probability distribution (via KDE).
   - **Process**: Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to model the probability distribution of latent coefficients derived from PCA.

3. **Synthetic Data Generation**:
   - **Input**: Latent space distribution.
   - **Output**: Synthetic latent vectors and corresponding noisy photometry.
   - **Process**: Generate synthetic data by sampling the latent space and forward-modeling spectra through NIRCam filters, adding 20% Gaussian noise.

4. **AutoML Regressor Training**:
   - **Input**: Noisy synthetic photometry.
   - **Output**: Trained model mapping photometry to latent coefficients.
   - **Process**: Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees to regress from photometric bands to latent coefficients.

5. **Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - **Input**: New photometric observations.
   - **Output**: Reconstructed spectrum and taxonomic classification.
   - **Process**:
     1. Pass photometry through the trained regressor to obtain latent coefficients.
     2. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     3. Use a secondary classifier for taxonomic grouping based on latent coefficients.

6. **Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - **Input**: Predicted latent coefficients.
   - **Output**: Ensemble of reconstructed spectra with credible intervals.
   - **Process**: Sample the joint posterior distribution using a Gaussian Copula to generate multiple spectral realizations and compute confidence intervals.

**End**: The algorithm outputs reconstructed spectra, taxonomic classifications, and uncertainty estimates for each TNO based on sparse photometry.
