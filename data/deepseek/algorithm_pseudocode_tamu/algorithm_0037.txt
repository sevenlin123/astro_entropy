Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Input: Sparse photometric measurements (m) of a trans-Neptunian object.
2. Initialize: 
   a. Training set: 51 reference TNO spectra.
   b. Principal Component Analysis (PCA) basis derived from the training set.
3. Forward Model:
   a. Simulate photometric measurements for each PCA-compressed spectrum by convolving with JWST/NIRCam filter transmission curves, adding 20% Gaussian noise.
   b. Use these simulated measurements to augment the training set with synthetic data points.
4. TrainRegressor:
   a. Use AutoML (e.g., XGBoost) to build a regressor mapping sparse photometry (m) to PCA latent coefficients.
5. PredictLatent:
   a. Given new photometry (m), use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients (z).
6. ReconstructSpectrum:
   a. Apply inverse PCA transformation to z to obtain full spectrum (S).
7. Output: Reconstructed spectrum S and associated uncertainties.
