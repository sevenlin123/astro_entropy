### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Input:**  
- Training Spectra: A set of reference TNO spectra (N=51).
- Observed Photometry: Sparse multi-band photometric measurements for a target TNO.
- Filter Set: Effective throughput curves of the target instrument (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed Spectrum: High-resolution spectral energy distribution of the target TNO.
- Taxonomic Classification: Probabilistic assignment to spectral types (H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).

---

#### Step 1: Latent Space Definition (Training Phase)

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Compute PCA on the training spectra to obtain principal components (eigenvectors) and scores.
   - Select K components (4-5 for classification, 8-10 for fidelity).
   - Latent Space: Low-dimensional representation of spectra using PCA coefficients.

2. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):**
   - Train a non-parametric KDE on the PCA scores to model the latent probability distribution.
   - Bandwidth (h): Selected empirically for smooth manifold (e.g., h=0.5).

---

#### Step 2: Training the AutoML Regressor

1. **Synthetic Data Generation:**
   - Sample latent vectors from the KDE to create a synthetic training set.
   - Forward model these spectra through the filter set to generate synthetic photometry.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative reflectance uncertainty) to simulate real-world observations.

2. **Regressor Training:**
   - Use an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to regress from synthetic photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Optimize for RMSE and uncertainty calibration during training.

---

#### Step 3: Forward Inference (Prediction Phase)

1. **Input Processing:**
   - Convert observed photometry into reflectance or flux densities.

2. **Latent Space Prediction:**
   - Pass photometry through the trained regressor to obtain predicted latent coefficients (mean and variance).

3. **Spectrum Reconstruction:**
   - Apply inverse PCA transformation using the predicted latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Ensembles: Sample from the joint posterior using Gaussian Copula to generate multiple realizations.

4. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Project predicted latent coefficients into a pre-trained classifier to assign probabilistic taxonomic labels.

---

#### Step 4: Performance Evaluation

1. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):**
   - Remove the target spectrum from training data.
   - Validate reconstruction fidelity using RMSE and coverage of credible intervals.
   - Assess classification accuracy.

---

#### Step 5: Applications

1. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Analyze filter sensitivity to identify optimal configurations for spectral information content.

2. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Flag objects with low posterior probability in the latent manifold for follow-up.

---
