**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**

1. **Preprocessing**
   - Gather near-infrared spectra of TNOs from the DiSCo-TNO survey.
   - Exclude rare spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Apply PCA to the collected spectra to derive principal components (latent space).
   - Determine the number of components (K ≈ 4-10) based on explained variance and cross-validation.

3. **Latent Space Construction**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) with a bandwidth of 0.5 to create a smooth probability distribution in the latent space.
   - This step augments the training data, enabling stable regression despite sparse samples.

4. **Forward Modeling**
   - For each spectrum in the latent space, compute mock photometric measurements by integrating over JWST/NIRCam filter curves.
   - Introduce 20% Gaussian noise to simulate realistic photometric uncertainties.

5. **Training the Regressor**
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (using AutoML) to map noisy photometry to latent coefficients.
   - The regressor learns the mapping from sparse photometric inputs to PCA coefficients.

6. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**
   - For each spectrum, remove it from the training set.
   - Use the regressor to predict latent coefficients from the remaining photometric data.
   - Reconstruct the spectrum using inverse PCA and compare with the original.

7. **Reconstruction and Uncertainty Quantification**
   - For each object, apply the regressor to observed photometry to get latent coefficients.
   - Transform coefficients to full spectrum using inverse PCA.
   - Propagate photometric uncertainties to quantify reconstruction uncertainty.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**
   - Use a secondary classifier trained on latent space to predict taxonomic class (Water, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).
   - Classify based on the latent coefficients from the regressor.

9. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**
   - Analyze filter configurations to maximize information content for spectral reconstruction.
   - Flag objects with low probability latent states as outliers.
