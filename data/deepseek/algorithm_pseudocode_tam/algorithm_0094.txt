Algorithm:
1. Initialize with spectral dataset S and filter curves F.
2. Center each spectrum in S by subtracting the mean spectrum μ_S.
3. Compute the covariance matrix C of the centered data.
4. Perform PCA on C to obtain eigenvectors (principal components) and eigenvalues.
5. Retain the top K principal components, where K is chosen based on explained variance.
6. For each spectrum s in S, generate synthetic photometric measurements by convolving s with each filter curve in F. Add Gaussian noise with standard deviation σ to simulate realistic observations.
7. Create a training dataset consisting of pairs of synthetic photometric measurements (m) and corresponding latent PCA coefficients (θ) from S.
8. Split the training dataset into training and validation sets.
9. Train an AutoML regressor to predict θ from photometric measurements.
10. Validate the model using leave-one-out cross-validation by removing each spectrum s from S, predicting θ for its synthetic photometry, and comparing to actual θ.
11. For new photometric measurements m_new, use the trained regressor to predict θ_hat.
12. Reconstruct the spectrum by applying the inverse PCA transform: s_reconstructed = θ_hat * principal_components + μ_S.
13. Optionally, classify the spectral type by training a classifier on the latent θ to predict spectral type probabilities.
14. Output s_reconstructed and spectral type probabilities.
