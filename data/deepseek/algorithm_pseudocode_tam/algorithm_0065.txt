Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

Input: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs
Output: Reconstructed spectral energy distribution (SED), Taxonomic classification probabilities

Begin:
    1. Initialize training set with N = 51 observed TNO spectra.
    2. Compute principal components (PCs) of the training spectra using PCA. Determine K = 8-10 PCs for high-fidelity reconstruction.
    3. Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA coefficients with bandwidth h = 0.5 to create a smooth latent manifold prior.
    4. Generate M = 10,000 synthetic spectra from the latent manifold. Convolve each with NIRCam filter profiles to compute synthetic photometry.
    5. Add 20% Gaussian noise to synthetic photometry to simulate realistic uncertainties.
    6. Split the combined training data (original + synthetic) into training and validation sets.
    7. Train AutoML regressor to map noisy photometric inputs to PCA coefficients using mean absolute error (MAE) loss.
    8. Train secondary AutoML classifier to map latent coefficients to taxonomic labels (Water, CO2, Organics, etc.).
    9. Perform leave-one-out cross-validation:
        a. For each spectrum, remove it from training set.
        b. Generate synthetic photometry with noise.
        c. Use AutoMLRegressor to predict PCA coefficients from photometry.
        d. Inverse PCA transform to obtain reconstructed SED.
        e. Measure reconstruction fidelity and uncertainty coverage.
        f. Classify reconstructed spectrum into taxonomic categories.
    10. For new observations:
        i. Pass sparse photometry through AutoMLRegressor to get predicted latent coefficients.
        ii. Project coefficients through inverse PCA transform to get SED.
        iii. Use classifier to assign taxonomic probabilities.
    11. Output reconstructed SED and taxonomic classification.

End.
