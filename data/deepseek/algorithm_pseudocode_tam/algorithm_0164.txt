Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Initialize the latent space:
   a. Collect reference spectra of TNOs.
   b. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to extract principal components.
   
2. Define the prior distribution:
   a. Project reference spectra onto PCA components.
   b. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to smooth latent coefficients into a continuous probability distribution.
   
3. Generate synthetic photometric data:
   a. For each latent vector:
      i. Convolve with instrument filter curves to compute expected photometry.
      ii. Add Gaussian noise to simulate measurement uncertainties.
   
4. Train the regressor:
   a. Combine synthetic photometry and latent coefficients into training dataset.
   b. Use AutoML (e.g., AutoGluon) to train a model predicting latent coefficients from photometry.
   
5. Construct the decoder:
   a. Apply inverse PCA transform to predicted latent coefficients to reconstruct full spectra.
   
6. Classify taxonomic labels:
   a. Train a classifier on the latent space to predict spectral type.
   b. For new photometry, predict latent coefficients, then classify taxonomic type.

End Algorithm
