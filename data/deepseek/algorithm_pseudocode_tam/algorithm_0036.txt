algorithm spectral_reconstruction_framework:

    input: reference_spectra, instrument_filters
    
    output: reconstructed_spectrum
    
    1. read reference_spectra
    2. perform PCA(reference_spectra) to get basis_vectors and loadings
    3. for each spectrum in reference_spectra:
        a. compute latent_coeffs = PCA.encoder(spectrum)
        b. compute photometry = forward_model(spectrum, instrument_filters)
        c. add_noise(photometry, noise_level) to get noisy_photometry
        d. append(noisy_photometry, latent_coeffs) to training_data
    4. train AutoMLRegressor(training_data) to get regressor
    5. save basis_vectors and regressor
    6. for new_photometry:
        a. predicted_latent = regressor.predict(new_photometry)
        b. reconstructed_spectrum = inverse_PCA(predicted_latent, basis_vectors)
    
    return reconstructed_spectrum
