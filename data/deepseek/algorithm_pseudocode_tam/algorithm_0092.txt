**Algorithm for Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess Training Spectra**:
   - Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to the reference TNO spectra to extract principal components and latent coefficients.

2. **Generate Synthetic Spectra**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent coefficients of the preprocessed spectra to create additional synthetic training data, expanding the training set.

3. **Forward Model Photometry**:
   - Simulate photometric measurements by convolving the synthetic spectra with the instrumental response functions (e.g., JWST NIRCam filters).

4. **Augment Training Set**:
   - Combine the original reference spectra with the synthetic spectra to form an augmented training set.

5. **Train AutoML Regressor**:
   - Train an ensemble of machine learning models (e.g., AutoGluon framework) to regress photometric measurements to latent coefficients.

6. **Predict Latent Coefficients**:
   - For new photometric observations, utilize the trained regressor to predict the latent coefficients.

7. **Reconstruct Spectrum**:
   - Use inverse PCA transformation on the predicted latent coefficients to generate the full high-resolution spectrum.

8. **Classify Taxonomic Type**:
   - Apply a secondary classifier, trained on the latent coefficients, to determine the taxonomic classification of the spectrum.

This algorithm effectively reconstructs high-resolution spectra from sparse photometric data, enabling detailed analysis of TNO surface compositions.
