### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Input:**  
- Sparse photometric measurements of a trans-Neptunian object (TNO).  
- Pre-trained principal component (PC) basis derived from a sample of TNO spectra.  
- Instrument-specific filter responses (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).  

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectrum of the TNO.  
- Probabilistic constraints on the spectral features (e.g., water, CO₂, organics).  

**Steps:**  

1. **Data Preparation:**  
   - Convert the sparse photometric measurements into reflectance units or flux densities.  

2. **Dimensionality Reduction (PCA Basis):**  
   - Project the photometric data into the pre-trained principal component space.  
   - Each spectrum is represented by a set of latent coefficients corresponding to the principal components.  

3. **Bayesian Inference:**  
   - Use the pre-trained auto-machine learning (AutoML) regressor to predict the latent coefficients from the sparse photometric inputs.  
   - The regressor was trained on synthetic photometry generated from the PCA basis.  

4. **Spectral Reconstruction:**  
   - Inverse transform the predicted latent coefficients using the PCA basis to reconstruct the high-resolution spectrum.  

5. **Error Propagation:**  
   - Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the uncertainties in the photometric measurements.  

6. **Taxonomic Classification (Optional):**  
   - Classify the spectrum into taxonomic categories (e.g., water, CO₂, organics) based on the latent coefficients.  

7. **Outlier Detection (Optional):**  
   - Check if the reconstructed spectrum lies outside the typical range of known spectral types.  

**End.**
