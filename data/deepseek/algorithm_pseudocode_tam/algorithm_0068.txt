### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Initialization**:
   - Input: Reference TNO spectra.
   - Compute PCA to derive principal components.
   - Result: Latent space defined by principal components.

2. **Data Augmentation**:
   - Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to the latent space of PCA coefficients.
   - Generate synthetic latent vectors to create a continuous probability distribution.

3. **Forward Modeling**:
   - For each synthetic spectrum, compute photometric magnitudes by convolving with NIRCam filter responses.
   - Simulate observational noise by adding Gaussian noise at 20% relative reflectance uncertainty.

4. **Training the AutoML Regressor**:
   - Use AutoGluon to train a model that maps noisy photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Input: Noisy photometric measurements.
   - Output: Predicted latent coefficients.

5. **Inverse PCA Transform**:
   - Project the predicted latent coefficients back to the full spectral space.
   - Generate reconstructed spectra constrained by the PCA basis.

6. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Classify the latent coefficients to determine spectral type (e.g., H2O, CO2, Organics).
   - Output: Probability distribution over taxonomic categories.

7. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
   - For each object, remove the true spectrum and evaluate reconstruction accuracy.
   - Measure Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) and credible interval coverage.

8. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
   - Analyze information content of filter configurations.
   - Flag objects with low probability density in the latent space for further spectroscopic study.

**End Algorithm**
