### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. **Initialize Training Data**
   - Gather a set of reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 observed spectra).
   - Exclude rare spectral types and outliers to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Project the reference spectra onto a PCA basis to derive latent coefficients.
   - Determine the number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).

3. **Generate Synthetic Training Data**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to smooth the latent space distribution.
   - Set KDE bandwidth (e.g., h ≈ 0.5) to balance local fidelity and global smoothness.

4. **Simulate Photometric Observations**
   - Convolve the augmented spectra with the instrument's filter responses (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric measurements.

5. **Train AutoML Regressor**
   - Use the synthetic photometry and latent coefficients to train an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees.
   - The regressor maps sparse photometry to latent space coordinates.

6. **Define Taxonomic Labels**
   - Classify the reference spectra into taxonomic groups (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier to partition the latent space based on these labels.

7. **Bayesian Inference for Reconstruction**
   - For new photometric observations, predict latent coefficients using the trained regressor.
   - Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Propagate uncertainties through the model to assess credible intervals.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**
   - Project the predicted latent coefficients into the classifier to determine the probability distribution over taxonomic types.

9. **Outlier Detection**
   - Identify objects whose latent states deviate significantly from the manifold.
   - Analyze reconstructed spectra to understand unique features.

10. **Survey Optimization**
    - Simulate different filter configurations to evaluate information content.
    - Select optimal filters for maximizing spectral reconstruction fidelity.

**End of Algorithm**
