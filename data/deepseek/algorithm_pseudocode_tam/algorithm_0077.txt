### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Input:**  
- Sparse photometric measurements (m)  
- Pre-trained PCA basis (principal_components)  
- Known taxonomic classes (taxonomy_labels)  

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed spectrum (S)  
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification  

**Steps:**  

1. **Define the Latent Space:**  
   - Project the reference spectra into a PCA basis to form a low-dimensional latent space.  
   - Ensure the latent space captures the dominant spectral variability modes (e.g., 4-5 components for taxonomy, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).  

2. **Prepare Training Data:**  
   - Use the reference spectra to generate synthetic photometric data by convolving with instrument filter responses.  
   - Inject noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric measurements.  

3. **Train the AutoML Regressor:**  
   - Use the synthetic photometric data as inputs and the corresponding latent coefficients as outputs.  
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to regress from photometry to latent coefficients.  

4. **Train the Taxonomic Classifier:**  
   - Project the reference spectra into the latent space.  
   - Train another AutoML classifier to predict taxonomic labels from the latent coefficients.  

5. **Reconstruct Spectra and Classify Taxonomy:**  
   - For new photometric observations:  
     a. Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.  
     b. Apply the inverse PCA transform to obtain the reconstructed spectrum (S).  
     c. Use the classifier to predict the taxonomic class probabilities.  

6. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):**  
   - For validation:  
     a. Remove one reference spectrum from the training set.  
     b. Generate synthetic photometry for the removed spectrum.  
     c. Predict latent coefficients using the regressor.  
     d. Compare predicted latent coefficients with the true values to assess reconstruction accuracy.  

**End Algorithm**
