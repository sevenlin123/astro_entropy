### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

#### Inputs:
- Sparse photometric measurements of a trans-Neptunian object.
- Pre-trained PCA basis derived from a reference spectral dataset.
- Instrument-specific filter transmission curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
- Noise model (e.g., 20% relative reflectance uncertainty).

#### Outputs:
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectrum.
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification.
- Diagnostic information for outlier detection.

#### Steps:

1. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Convert photometric measurements into a standardized format.
   - Apply noise injection based on the noise model.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Project the reference spectra onto a PCA basis to obtain latent coefficients.
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to generate synthetic spectra and augment the training data.

3. **AutoML Model Training**:
   - Train two models:
     - **Regressor**: Maps sparse photometry to PCA latent coefficients.
     - **Classifier**: Classifies latent coefficients into taxonomic categories.

4. **Forward Modeling**:
   - Simulate photometric measurements for synthetic spectra by convolving with instrument filters.
   - Introduce noise to simulate real-world observational conditions.

5. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients from observed photometry.
   - Invert the PCA transform to reconstruct the high-resolution spectrum.

6. **Classification and Outlier Detection**:
   - Apply the classifier to the latent coefficients to determine taxonomic probabilities.
   - Identify outliers based on low model evidence or extreme positions in the latent space.

7. **Validation and Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Perform LOOCV to assess reconstruction fidelity.
   - Propagate uncertainties through the model to quantify confidence intervals.

8. **Filter Configuration Optimization**:
   - Test various filter sets to determine their impact on reconstruction accuracy.
   - Select configurations that minimize RMSE and maximize spectral information content.

#### Pseudocode:

```
function spectral_reconstruction(photometry, pca_basis, filters, noise_model):
    # Step 1: Data Preprocessing
    preprocess_photometry(photometry)

    # Step 2: PCA and Data Augmentation
    latent_coefficients = pca_projection(reference_spectra)
    augmented_spectra = generate_synthetic_spectra(latent_coefficients, kde_bandwidth)

    # Step 3: AutoML Model Training
    regressor = train_auto_mlRegressor(augmented_spectra_photometry)
    classifier = train_auto_mlClassifier(latent_coefficients, taxonomic_labels)

    # Step 4: Forward Modeling
    simulated_photometry = forward_model(augmented_spectra, filters)
    add_noise(simulated_photometry, noise_model)

    # Step 5: Bayesian Inference and Reconstruction
    predicted_latents = regressor.predict(simulated_photometry)
    reconstructed_spectrum = inverse_pca(predicted_latents, pca_basis)

    # Step 6: Classification and Outlier Detection
    taxonomic_probabilities = classifier.predict_proba(predicted_latents)
    detect_outliers(taxonomic_probabilities, reconstruction_uncertainty)

    # Step 7: Validation and Uncertainty Quantification
    evaluate_LOOCV(reconstructed_spectrum, reference_spectra)
    quantify_uncertainties(reconstructed_spectrum, noise_model)

    # Step 8: Filter Optimization
    optimize_filters(filters, reconstructed_spectrum, RMSE)

    return reconstructed_spectrum, taxonomic_probabilities, outlier_flags
```
