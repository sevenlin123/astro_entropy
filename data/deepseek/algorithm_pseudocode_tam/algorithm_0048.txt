Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Observed photometric measurements (sparse)
    - Pre-trained PCA basis vectors (principal components)
    - Pre-computed kernel density estimates (KDE) for latent space

Output:
    - Reconstructed high-resolution spectrum
    - Posterior probabilities of spectral types

Steps:

1. Initialize the latent space using PCA:
    a. Project the training spectra onto principal components to obtain latent coefficients.
    b. Apply KDE to smooth the latent distributions.

2. Generate synthetic photometry:
    a. For each synthetic spectrum, convolve with the instrument's filter response curves.
    b. Add 20% Gaussian noise to simulate observational uncertainty.

3. Train the AutoML regressor:
    a. Use the sparse synthetic photometry as input features.
    b. Target the latent coefficients as output labels.
    c. Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees.

4. Set up the taxonomy classifier:
    a. Use the latent space of the training spectra.
    b. Train a second AutoML classifier to predict spectral types based on latent coordinates.

5. Inference phase:
    a. For new photometric measurements:
        i. Pass the photometry through the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
        ii. Apply the inverse PCA transform to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.
        iii. Use the latent coordinates to predict the posterior probabilities of spectral types.

6. Output the reconstructed spectrum and taxonomic probabilities.
