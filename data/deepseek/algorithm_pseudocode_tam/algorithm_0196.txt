**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Data Preparation:**
   - Collect and preprocess a set of reference TNO spectra.
   - Exclude rare spectral types and outliers to ensure a "clean" latent space.
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the selected spectra to obtain principal components (latent space).

2. **Data Augmentation:**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to generate synthetic spectra in the latent space.
   - Ensure the synthetic data fills the intermediate regions between real spectra, creating a smooth manifold.

3. **Forward Modeling:**
   - Convolve each augmented spectrum with the instrument's filter throughputs (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric measurements.

4. **Model Training:**
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees) to map sparse photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Separate training focuses on mapping photometric inputs to latent space.

5. **Reconstruction Pipeline:**
   - For new photometric observations:
     a. Use the trained AutoML model to predict latent coefficients.
     b. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     c. Propagate uncertainties from photometry to the reconstructed spectrum.

6. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Train a secondary classifier on the latent space to assign taxonomic labels (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).
   - Classify objects based on their latent coefficients.

7. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Monitor convergence and acceptance rates of sampling chains.
   - Flag objects with posterior distributions inconsistent with the training manifold as outliers.

8. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Analyze Fisher information matrix to identify optimal filter combinations.
   - Recommend specific NIRCam filters (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M) for maximizing spectral reconstruction fidelity.

9. **Cross-Validation:**
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation to assess RMSE and credible interval coverage.
   - Validate without using synthetic data to ensure unbiased evaluation.
