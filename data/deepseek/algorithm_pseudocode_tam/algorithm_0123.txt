   - **Step 2.2:** Obtain principal components (latent coefficients) representing spectral variability.

**3. Data Augmentation**
   - **Step 3.1:** Apply non-parametric Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent coefficients to generate synthetic latent vectors.
   - **Step 3.2:** Choose KDE bandwidth (e.g., h ≈ 0.5) to balance smoothness and fidelity.

**4. Forward Model Synthetic Spectra**
   - **Step 4.1:** Convolve synthetic spectra with NIRCam filter throughput curves to simulate photometry.
   - **Step 4.2:** Introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to account for observational errors.

**5. Train AutoML Encoder**
   - **Step 5.1:** Use synthetic photometry and latent coefficients to train an ensemble regressor (e.g., Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees).
   - **Step 5.2:** Regress from noisy photometry to latent coefficients.

**6. Taxonomic Classification Setup**
   - **Step 6.1:** Label reference spectra into taxonomic groups (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).
   - **Step 6.2:** Train a secondary AutoML classifier on latent coefficients to predict taxonomic labels.

**7. Spectral Reconstruction Process**
   - **Step 7.1:** For new photometry, use the trained encoder to predict latent coefficients.
   - **Step 7.2:** Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.

**8. Validate via LOOCV**
   - **Step 8.1:** Iteratively exclude each reference spectrum and validate reconstruction.
   - **Step 8.2:** Compute metrics (e.g., MSE, ECI coverage) to assess reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty calibration.

**9. Applications**
