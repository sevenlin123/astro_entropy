### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Objective:**  
Reconstruct high-resolution near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) using sparse photometric measurements through a Bayesian framework.

**Steps:**

1. **Training Data Preparation:**
   - **Collect Reference Spectra:** Gather a sample of 51 TNO spectra representing dominant taxonomic classes.
   - **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):** Apply PCA to the reference spectra to derive principal components (latent space). Use the first 8-10 components for improved fidelity.
   
2. **Data Augmentation:**
   - **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):** Smooth the latent space using KDE to create synthetic spectra, ensuring a continuous manifold for training.
   - **Noise Injection:** Add 20% Gaussian noise to synthetic photometry to simulate realistic observational uncertainties.

3. **Forward Modeling:**
   - **Filter Transmission Curves:** Convolve reference spectra with JWST/NIRCam filter responses to generate synthetic photometric measurements.
   - **Likelihood Construction:** Define the likelihood function mapping photometry to reconstructed spectra.

4. **Model Training:**
   - **AutoML Regressor:** Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees to regress photometric inputs to latent PCA coefficients.
   - **Classifier Training:** Train a secondary classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic labels.

5. **Inference Pipeline:**
   - **Photometric Input:** Accept sparse photometric measurements.
   - **Regressor Prediction:** Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - **Inverse PCA Transform:** Convert latent coefficients to full spectra.
   - **Classification:** Classify the object's taxonomic type using the latent space classifier.

6. **Validation and Performance Metrics:**
   - **LOOCV:** Validate using leave-one-out cross-validation, ensuring no overlap between training and validation sets.
   - **Reconstruction Error:** Compute mean absolute error between ground truth and reconstructed spectra.
   - **Credibility Intervals:** Assess coverage of 68.3% and 95.4% credible intervals.
   - **Filter Information Content:** Rank filters based on their contribution to PCA components.
   - **Outlier Detection:** Flag objects with Mahalanobis distance exceeding 3σ.

**End:**  
Output includes reconstructed spectra, taxonomic classifications, and outlier flags for each object.
