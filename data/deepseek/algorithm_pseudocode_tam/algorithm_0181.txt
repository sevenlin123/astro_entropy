Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input: Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO
Output: Reconstructed spectrum and taxonomic classification

1. Precompute PCA basis:
   a. Collect reference TNO spectra (51 objects).
   b. Perform PCA to derive principal components (K ≈ 4-10).

2. Augment training data:
   a. Project reference spectra onto PCA latent space.
   b. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create smooth probability distribution in latent space.

3. Train autoML regressor:
   a. Generate synthetic photometry by convolving reference spectra with JWST NIRCam filter curves.
   b. Inject 20% Gaussian noise into synthetic photometry.
   c. Train regressor to map noisy photometry to latent coefficients.

4. Cross-validate:
   a. Perform LOOCV: For each object, remove its spectrum, augment remaining, retrain regressor.
   b. Measure reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty calibration.

5. Reconstruct spectrum for new object:
   a. Pass sparse photometry through trained regressor to get latent coefficients.
   b. Apply inverse PCA transform to obtain full spectrum.

6. Classify taxonomic type:
   a. Use latent coefficients as features for trained classifier.
   b. Output probability distribution over taxonomic categories (Water, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).

Return: Reconstructed spectrum and taxonomic probabilities.
