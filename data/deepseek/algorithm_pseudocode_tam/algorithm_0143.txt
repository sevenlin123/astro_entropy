Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Initialize:
   - Train a PCA model on a set of reference TNO spectra to create a low-dimensional latent space (principal components).
   - Augment the latent space using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to smooth the probability distribution, ensuring a continuous manifold.

2. Generate Synthetic Photometry:
   - For each augmented spectrum, convolve it with the effective throughput curves of the target instrument (e.g., JWST NIRCam) to compute the synthetic photometric measurements.
   - Inject Gaussian noise with a relative reflectance uncertainty (e.g., 20%) into the synthetic photometry to simulate real-world observational noise.

3. Train AutoMLRegressor:
   - Use the synthetic photometry and corresponding PCA coefficients as input-output pairs to train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) via AutoML.
   - The regressor maps sparse photometric inputs to latent coefficients, stabilizing the inverse PCA problem.

4. Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):
   - For each held-out spectrum:
     a. Remove it from the training set.
     b. Generate synthetic data using the remaining spectra.
     c. Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients from the held-out photometry.
     d. Apply inverse PCA to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.
     e. Compute the Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) between the reconstructed and true spectrum.

5. Propagate Uncertainties:
   - Sample the posterior distribution of latent coefficients from the regressor.
   - Decode multiple samples into spectra and compute variance to assess reconstruction uncertainty.

6. Detect Outliers:
   - Quantify the divergence of predicted latent states from the KDE prior.
   - Flag objects with high divergence as outliers for further spectroscopic study.

7. Taxonomic Classification:
   - Train a secondary classifier on the latent coefficients to classify spectra into predefined taxonomic groups (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).

8. Optimize Survey Filters:
   - Evaluate the information content of different NIRCam filter configurations by assessing their ability to recover latent spectral coefficients.
   - Select configurations that maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity and taxonomic classification accuracy.
