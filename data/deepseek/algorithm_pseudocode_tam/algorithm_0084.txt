**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Initialize Training Data**:
   - Select a set of reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 spectra) representing dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   
2. **Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the training spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).
   - Determine the number of PCs (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

3. **Data Augmentation with Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**:
   - Project the training spectra onto the PCA latent space.
   - Use KDE to smooth the latent distribution, generating synthetic spectra to augment the training set.
   - Set the KDE bandwidth (e.g., h ≈ 0.5) to balance local fidelity and global smoothness.

4. **Generate Synthetic Photometry**:
   - Convolve the augmented spectra with the filter response functions of the target instrument (e.g., JWST NIRCam).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) into the synthetic photometry to simulate observational noise.

5. **Train AutoML Regressor**:
   - Use the synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients as training data.
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoGluon framework) to regress from photometry to latent space coordinates.

6. **LOOCV Validation**:
   - For each held-out spectrum, remove it from the training set.
   - Retrain the AutoML regressor and predict latent coefficients.
   - Project the predicted coefficients back to full spectra using inverse PCA.
   - Compute RMS error and empirical credible-interval coverage between ground truth and reconstructed spectra.

7. **Reconstruct Spectra from New Photometry**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Predict latent coefficients using the trained AutoML regressor.
     - Apply inverse PCA to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Classify the reconstructed spectra into predefined taxonomic categories using a secondary AutoML classifier trained on the latent space.

9. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate the information content of different filter configurations (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M) to identify optimal combinations for TNO taxonomy.

10. **Outlier Detection**:
    - Identify unusual spectral types by detecting deviations from the main clusters in the latent space.
    - Reconstruct rare spectral types by sampling low-probability regions of the latent manifold.

**End Algorithm**
