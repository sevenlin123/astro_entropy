Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- Sparse photometric observations (m)
- Pre-trained Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model with latent coefficients (u)
- Pre-trained AutoML regressor for mapping photometry to latent coefficients

Output:
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectrum (S)
- Posterior probabilities of spectral types (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organic)

Steps:

1. Dimensionality Reduction:
   - Project sparse photometric observations (m) through AutoML regressor to obtain latent coefficients (u).
   
2. Spectral Reconstruction:
   - Apply inverse PCA transformation using the pre-trained PCA model.
   - Compute reconstructed spectrum (S) as a linear combination of PCA eigenvectors weighted by latent coefficients (u).
   
3. Taxonomic Classification:
   - Project latent coefficients (u) through the trained taxonomic classifier.
   - Obtain posterior probabilities of spectral types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic).
   
4. Uncertainty Quantification:
   - Propagate uncertainties from photometric measurements to latent coefficients (u).
   - Calculate uncertainties in reconstructed spectrum (S) and spectral parameters.
   
5. Outlier Detection:
   - Assess the probability of latent coefficients (u) in the trained manifold.
   - Identify objects with low posterior probabilities as outliers.

End
