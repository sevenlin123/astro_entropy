**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a sample of reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 high-quality spectra).
   - Exclude outliers and rare spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the reference spectra to extract principal components (PCs). Retain 4-5 PCs for dimensionality reduction and spectral reconstruction.

3. **Latent Space Construction**:
   - Project the reference spectra onto the PCA basis to obtain latent coefficients.
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to construct a smooth probability distribution in the latent space, augmenting the training data to fill gaps between sparse reference spectra.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Simulate photometric observations by convolving the augmented spectra with the instrument's filter throughput curves (e.g., JWST NIRCam).
   - Introduce noise (e.g., 20% relative reflectance uncertainty) to simulate real-world observational conditions.

5. **AutoMLRegressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (GBDTs) using the synthetic photometry as inputs and latent coefficients as targets. This model acts as the "encoder" mapping sparse photometry to latent space.

6. **Taxonomic Classifier Training**:
   - Train another AutoML model to classify latent coefficients into predefined taxonomic groups (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).

7. **Spectral Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Pass photometry through the AutoMLRegressor to predict latent coefficients.
     - Project the predicted coefficients through the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     - Optionally, classify the latent coefficients to determine the taxonomic group.

8. **Validation**:
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty propagation.
   - Evaluate empirical credible-interval coverage and classification accuracy.
