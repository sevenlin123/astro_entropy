**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Data Preparation:**
     - Obtain a seed set of TNO spectra (e.g., 51 high-quality near-IR spectra).
     - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on these spectra to derive principal components (PCs) that capture the major spectral variations.

   - **Latent Space Construction:**
     - Project the spectra onto the PCA basis to get latent coefficients.
     - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to construct a continuous probability distribution in the latent space, augmenting the training data with synthetic spectra to enhance model stability.

   - **Synthetic Photometry Generation:**
     - For each augmented spectrum, compute synthetic photometric measurements by convolving with instrumental filter curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
     - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic observational conditions.

   - **Model Training:**
     - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map noisy photometric inputs to latent coefficients.
     - Additionally, train a classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic categories.

2. **Inference Phase:**
   - **Input Processing:**
     - For new photometric observations, pass them through the trained AutoML regressor to predict the latent coefficients.

   - **Spectral Reconstruction:**
     - Apply the inverse PCA transform to the predicted latent coefficients to obtain the reconstructed high-resolution spectrum.

   - **Classification:**
     - Use the latent coefficients to predict the taxonomic category of the object.

3. **Cross-Validation and Performance Assessment:**
   - Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):
     - For each test spectrum, remove it from the training set, regenerate the synthetic photometry, and evaluate the model's performance in reconstructing the spectrum and classifying its type.

   - Measure reconstruction fidelity using metrics like Normalized Mean Absolute Error (NMAE) and correlation coefficients.
   - Assess classification performance using accuracy, F1-score, and confusion matrices.

4. **Outlier Detection:**
   - For each object, determine its probability density in the latent space.
   - Identify objects in low-density regions as potential outliers, indicative of unique spectral types.
