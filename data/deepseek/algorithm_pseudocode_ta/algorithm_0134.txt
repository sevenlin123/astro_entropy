**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data**:
   - Near-infrared (NIR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Broadband photometric measurements of TNOs.
   - Training sample of NIR spectra.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Train PCA on the sample of NIR spectra.
   - Extract principal components (PCs) capturing the spectral variability.
   - Determine the number of PCs (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

3. **Latent Space Representation**:
   - Represent each TNO's spectrum in the reduced PCA space.
   - The latent space captures the essential spectral features using the selected number of PCs.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA latent space.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometric data through the latent space.
   - Reconstruct the full spectral shape from the posterior distribution in the latent space.

5. **Cross-Validation**:
   - Apply leave-one-out cross-validation to assess the reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty calibration.
   - Validate the empirical credible interval coverage (e.g., ~95%).

6. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Quantify the information content of NIRCam filters.
   - Identify optimal filter combinations for future observations.

7. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Use the framework to detect rare spectral types by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models.
   - Reconstruct and identify peculiar objects like 2006~RJ$_{103}$ and 2011~SO$_{277}$.

8. **Output**:
   - Reconstructed spectra for each TNO.
   - Uncertainty estimates for the reconstructed spectra.
   - Optimized survey configurations.
   - Identified outlier spectral types.

This algorithm provides a statistically rigorous framework to bridge sparse photometry and spectroscopy, enabling detailed analysis of the compositional structure of minor planets.
