**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Step-by-Step Pseudocode:**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Input:** Collection of near-IR spectra (training set)
   - **Compute Principal Components:**
     - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training set to obtain principal component eigenvectors (basis vectors) and corresponding eigenvalues.
     - Select the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).
   - **Store PCA Results:**
     - Save the selected principal component eigenvectors and eigenvalues for subsequent use.

2. **Processing Each Object:**
   - **For each trans-Neptunian object (test case):**
     - **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements (e.g., magnitudes in specific filters like F090W, F115W, etc.)
     - **Initialize Bayesian Inference Model:**
       - Define a Bayesian model where the spectrum is represented as a linear combination of the stored PCA basis vectors.
       - The coefficients of this linear combination are the latent variables (principal components) to be inferred.
     - **Set Up Prior Distributions:**
       - Assign prior distributions to the latent variables (e.g., Gaussian with mean 0 and variance based on PCA eigenvalues).
       - Consider photometric uncertainties as part of the likelihood model.
     - **Perform Bayesian Inference:**
       - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling or variational inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the latent variables given the photometric data.
     - **Reconstruct the Spectrum:**
       - Multiply the posterior means of the latent variables by the PCA eigenvectors and sum them to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.
     - **Propagate Uncertainties:**
       - Compute the posterior variances of the latent variables and propagate these variances through the linear combination to estimate the uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum.
     - **Output:**
       - Provide the reconstructed spectrum along with its associated uncertainties.
     - **Optional:**
       - Apply Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) or other validation methods to assess the quality of the reconstructed spectrum.

**End of Algorithm**
