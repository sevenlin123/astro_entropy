Algorithm:
1. Input: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
2. Preprocess photometric measurements into flux densities.
3. Obtain training set of near-IR spectra.
4. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training set to establish a spectral manifold.
5. For each TNO:
    a. Use photometric fluxes to project into the spectral manifold.
    b. Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA-reduced space to estimate spectral parameters.
    c. Propagate measurement uncertainties to the reconstructed spectrum.
    d. Assess posterior probabilities to detect outliers.
6. For optimal filter configuration:
    a. Quantify the information content of different filter combinations.
    b. Identify filter configurations that maximize reconstruction fidelity and utility for taxonomy.
7. Output reconstructed spectra, associated uncertainties, identified outliers, and recommended optimal filter configurations.
