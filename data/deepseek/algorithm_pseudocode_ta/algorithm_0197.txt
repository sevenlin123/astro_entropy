**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Model**
   - Input: Training dataset of near-infrared spectra.
   - Output: Principal component (PC) basis vectors (eigenvectors) and mean spectrum.
   - Normalize and preprocess the spectral data.
   - Compute covariance or correlation matrix.
   - Perform PCA to obtain PC basis vectors and mean spectrum.

2. **For Each Object**
   - Input: Observed sparse photometric measurements.
   - Output: Reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties.

   a. **Project Photometric Data into PCA Space**
      - Map observed photometric measurements into the latent PCA space.
      - Use PCA basis vectors to project data.

   b. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**
      - Define priors for PC coefficients (e.g., Gaussian prior).
      - Compute posterior distribution of PC coefficients given photometric data.
      - Include measurement uncertainties in the likelihood function.

   c. **Reconstruct Full Spectrum**
      - Use posterior estimates of PC coefficients.
      - Multiply by PCA basis vectors and add the mean spectrum to reconstruct the full spectrum.

   d. **Uncertainty Propagation**
      - Propagate uncertainties from photometric measurements through the Bayesian inference step.
      - Quantify uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum.

   e. **Output**
      - Reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties.

**End Algorithm**
