**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Precompute Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Train PCA on a reference spectral library of trans-Neptunian object spectra to capture spectral variability.

2. **Initialize Variables:**
   - Load sparse photometric flux measurements for each object.
   - Obtain the reference PCA basis vectors (principal components).

3. **Bayesian Inference in PCA Space:**
   - For each object:
     - Represent the spectral reflectance as a linear combination of PCA eigenvectors.
     - Define prior distributions for the PCA coefficients (e.g., mean and covariance based on training data).
     - Construct the likelihood function relating photometric fluxes to PCA coefficients.
     - Perform Bayesian inference to compute the posterior distribution of PCA coefficients given the photometric data.

4. **Reconstruct Full Spectrum:**
   - Multiply the posterior mean of the PCA coefficients with the PCA eigenvectors.
   - Concatenate the resulting spectra across all wavelengths to form the full reconstructed spectrum.

5. **Quantify Uncertainties:**
   - Extract the posterior covariance matrix of the PCA coefficients.
   - Propagate uncertainties through the linear combination to obtain the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum.
   - Calculate credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum.

6. **Output:**
   - Save the reconstructed spectra and associated uncertainties for further analysis or visualization.
