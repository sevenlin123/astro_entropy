**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**
   - Input: Sample of near-IR spectra
   - Output: PCA model parameters (principal components)

2. **Bayesian Inference Setup**
   - For each object:
     - Input: Photometric measurements and uncertainties
     - Represent spectrum as linear combination of PCA basis vectors plus noise

3. **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling**
   - Parameters: Coefficients of PCA basis vectors, noise parameters
   - Iterate:
     - Draw samples from posterior distribution
     - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the inference

4. **Spectrum Reconstruction**
   - Use MCMC-estimated PCA coefficients
   - Reconstruct full spectrum in original space
   - Compute uncertainty intervals

5. **Output**
   - Reconstructed spectra
   - Empirical credible-interval coverage

**End Algorithm**
