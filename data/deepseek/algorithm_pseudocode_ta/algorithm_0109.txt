Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. Initialize dataset of TNO near-IR spectra.
2. Train PCA model on the dataset to capture spectral variability:
   a. Compute covariance matrix of spectral dataset.
   b. Perform eigen decomposition to obtain principal components (PCs).
   c. Select top K PCs (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for high fidelity).
3. Project photometric measurements into the PCA space:
   a. For each object, transform observed photometric features into latent space representation.
4. Perform Bayesian inference in reduced PCA space:
   a. Define prior distribution based on training data statistics.
   b. Calculate posterior probabilities using Bayes' theorem, incorporating measurement uncertainties.
5. Reconstruct full spectra from posterior distributions:
   a. Map posterior means back to original spectral space.
   b. Propagate uncertainties to reconstructed spectra.
6. Output reconstructed spectra and associated uncertainties for each object.
