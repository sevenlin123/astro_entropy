**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**

1. **Input Data**:
   - Training set of near-IR spectra.
   - Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.

2. **Training Phase**:
   - **Step 2.1**: Compute Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training set of near-IR spectra.
     - Output: Principal Components (PCs) and their corresponding eigenvalues.

   - **Step 2.2**: Determine the number of PCs (k) that effectively capture the spectral variability relevant for taxonomic classification.
     - Empirical guideline: k ≈ 4–5 for classification purposes.

3. **Model Construction**:
   - **Step 3.1**: Construct a probabilistic latent-space model using the computed PCs.
     - Model assumes that spectral information is embedded in these lower-dimensional PCs.

   - **Step 3.2**: Define a Bayesian inference framework in the reduced PCA space.
     - Likelihood function: Relates photometric measurements to reconstructed spectra via the latent PCs.
     - Prior distribution: Incorporates uncertainties and correlations in the spectral manifold.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - **Step 4.1**: For each TNO, extract its sparse photometric measurements.

   - **Step 4.2**: Perform Bayesian inference to estimate the latent PC coefficients.
     - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or similar sampling methods to propagate measurement uncertainties.

   - **Step 4.3**: Reconstruct the full near-IR spectrum using the estimated PC coefficients.
     - Algorithm applies linear combination of basis vectors scaled by the inferred PC coefficients.

   - **Step 4.4**: Propagate uncertainties from the estimated PC coefficients to the reconstructed spectrum.

5. **Output**:
   - For each TNO:
     - Reconstructed spectrum.
     - Associated uncertainties.
     - Probability distribution over spectral models.

**End of Algorithm**
