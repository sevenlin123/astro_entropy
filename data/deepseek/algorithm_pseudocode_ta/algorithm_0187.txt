**Pseudocode Algorithm for Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Input Data**: 
   - Training Spectra (`S`): Collection of known TNO spectra.
   - Training Photometry (`P`): Corresponding photometric measurements.
   - New Observations (`P_new`): Photometric data of new TNOs to reconstruct.

2. **Preprocessing**:
   - Normalize and preprocess `S` and `P` to align spectral and photometric representations.

3. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to `S` to reduce dimensionality.
   - Determine optimal number of PCs (k) through leave-one-out cross-validation.

4. **Model Construction**:
   - Train a probabilistic model in the latent PCA space using `P` as inputs and latent components as outputs.

5. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference to compute posterior distributions of latent variables given `P`.
   - Use prior distribution informed by the manifold structure.

6. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Transform posterior means of latent variables back to spectral space to obtain reconstructed spectra.

7. **Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Propagate posterior variances to compute uncertainty intervals for reconstructed spectra.

8. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Calculate statistical measures (e.g., Mahalanobis distance) to identify discrepancies between reconstructed and expected spectral manifold.

9. **Output**:
   - Reconstructed spectra for training and new data.
   - Uncertainty estimates and outlier flags.

**End of Algorithm**
