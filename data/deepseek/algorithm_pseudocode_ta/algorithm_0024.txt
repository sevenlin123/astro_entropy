Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input: Training spectra S_train (each with known composition), Test photometry P_test

Output: Reconstructed spectra R_test, Uncertainty estimates U_test

High-Level Steps:
1. Preprocess the training spectra.
2. Decompose the preprocessed training spectra into principal components.
3. Build a forward model linking photometric measurements to PCA coefficients.
4. Perform Bayesian inference to estimate PCA coefficients using MCMC.
5. Reconstruct the spectra from the estimated PCA coefficients.
6. Calculate uncertainty estimates from the MCMC samples.

Subroutines:
a. preprocessTrainingSpectra(S_train):
   Preprocess each spectrum in S_train by normalizing and removing missing values.

b. computePrincipalComponents(S_train):
   Perform PCA on S_train to extract principal components and loadings.

c. buildForwardModel():
   Construct a probabilistic model that links photometric bands to PCA coefficients.

d. bayesianInferenceMCMC(P_test):
   Use Bayesian inference with prior distributions and MCMC sampling to estimate PCA coefficients.

e. reconstructSpectraFromPCACoefficients():
   Use PCA coefficients to reconstruct the spectra.

f. calculateUncertaintyEstimates():
   Propagate uncertainties from MCMC samples to determine U_test.
