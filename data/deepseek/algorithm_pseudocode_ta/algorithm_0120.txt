Read in the photometric data of the TNOs.

Perform principal component analysis (PCA) on a set of template spectra derived from near-IR spectroscopy of TNOs.

Model the spectral manifold using a probabilistic framework in the PCA-reduced space.

For each object, perform Bayesian inference to reconstruct its full spectrum based on the photometric measurements.

Propagate the measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

Compute the posterior probabilities of different spectral components to assess the likelihood of rare spectral types.

Output the reconstructed spectra along with their uncertainties and associated probabilities.
