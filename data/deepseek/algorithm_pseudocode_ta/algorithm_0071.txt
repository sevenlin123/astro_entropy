**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Step 1: Training of the Spectral Manifold via PCA**

1. Collect a set of reference spectra.
2. Standardize the reference spectra.
3. Compute the covariance matrix of the reference spectra.
4. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the covariance matrix to obtain principal components (PCs).
5. Store the eigenvalues and eigenvectors from PCA.

**Step 2: Bayesian Inference for Each Object**

1. For each target object:
   - Obtain photometric measurements and their uncertainties.
   - Use the known response functions of the photometric bands.
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to estimate the object's PC coefficients.
   - This involves solving the inverse problem to find the posterior distribution of the PC coefficients given the photometric data.

**Step 3: Posterior Sampling for Spectral Reconstruction**

1. Draw samples from the posterior distribution of the PC coefficients.
2. Using the PC coefficients and the PCA basis, reconstruct the full spectrum for each sample.
3. Calculate the uncertainties and credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.

**End of Algorithm**
