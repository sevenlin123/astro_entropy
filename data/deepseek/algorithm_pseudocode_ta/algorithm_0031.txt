Algorithm: ProbabilisticSpectralReconstruction

Input: 
    - Training spectra S
    - Photometric measurements P

Output:
    - Reconstructed spectrum R

1. Preprocessing:
    a. Center the training spectra S by subtracting the mean spectrum.

2. Principal Component Analysis (PCA):
    a. Compute the covariance matrix of the centered spectra.
    b. Compute the eigenvalues (D) and eigenvectors (V) of the covariance matrix.
    c. Select the top K eigenvectors (V_pca) and eigenvalues (D_pca), where K is chosen based on prior validation (e.g., K=4-5).

3. Bayesian Inference:
    a. For each test object with photometric data P, perform Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the PCA coefficients θ.

4. Spectrum Reconstruction:
    a. Use the posterior mean of θ to project back into the original spectral space.
    b. Multiply each selected PCA vector by the corresponding θ to obtain spectral contributions.
    c. Sum all contributions and add back the mean spectrum to obtain the reconstructed spectrum R.
