**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Input:**
- Sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs)
- Reference sample of known near-infrared (near-IR) spectra
- Principal component representation trained on the reference sample

**Output:**
- Reconstructed full spectrum for each TNO
- Posterior distribution of uncertainties

**Steps:**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training:**
   - Train PCA on a reference sample of known near-IR spectra.
   - Determine the number of principal components (PCs) that capture the essential spectral variability (e.g., 4-5 PCs for taxonomic classification).
   
2. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space:**
   - Represent each spectrum in the reduced PCA space.
   - Perform Bayesian inference using the photometric data to infer the latent variables (principal component coefficients) that best explain the observed data.
   - Utilize prior distributions based on the PCA basis to regularize the solution.

3. **Reconstruct Full Spectra:**
   - Use the inferred latent variables (principal component coefficients) to project back to the original spectral space.
   - Apply the inverse transform of the PCA to obtain the reconstructed full spectrum.

4. **Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - Quantify uncertainties in the photometric measurements and propagate them through the Bayesian inference process.
   - Compute the posterior distribution of the latent variables and use this to estimate the uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

5. **Optional Survey Optimization:**
   - Evaluate the information content of different JWST/NIRCam filter configurations.
   - Identify optimal filter combinations that maximize the fidelity of spectral reconstruction.

**End Algorithm**
