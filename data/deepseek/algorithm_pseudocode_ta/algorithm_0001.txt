Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction (PSR)

1. Initialize Reference Library:
   a. Collect a set of TNO spectra, S_train.

2. PCA Analysis:
   a. Apply PCA to S_train, obtaining K principal components (K=8-10).
   b. Retrieve PCA eigenvectors V and eigenvalues Λ.

3. For each Object:
   a. Obtain sparse photometric data P.
   b. Set up probabilistic model M relating P to latent PCA coefficients.
   c. Conduct Bayesian inference on M using P to determine posterior distributions of PCA coefficients.
   d. Reconstruct spectrum S using the posterior means of the coefficients: S = sum(post_coeff * V)
   e. Calculate uncertainty in S by propagating the variances of post_coeff through the reconstruction.

4. Output:
   a. Reconstructed spectra S for all objects.
   b. Uncertainty estimates for each S.
