**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Data Preparation**
   - Collect a training dataset of 160 TNO near-IR spectra.
   - Extract spectral measurements across various wavelengths.
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training spectra.
   - Transform each spectrum into a vector of principal components (PCs).

2. **Probabilistic Model Construction**
   - Implement a Gaussian Process (GP) model or equivalent.
   - Define the model to map latent PC space to original spectrum space.
   - Establish probabilistic relationships between latent PCs and observed spectra.
   - Conduct Bayesian inference to estimate posterior distributions of latent variables based on photometric data.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction Process**
   - For each TNO, utilize sparse photometric measurements.
   - Infer the latent PC vector using the probabilistic model.
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the inferred PCs through the model.
   - Propagate photometric uncertainties through the model to determine uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum.

4. **Taxonomic Classification and Outlier Detection**
   - Classify the reconstructed spectra into predefined taxonomic groups.
   - Apply clustering techniques in the latent PC space.
   - Identify outliers by assessing distance from learned clusters.

5. **Survey Optimization Analysis**
   - Evaluate the information content of NIR filters.
   - Determine optimal filter combinations for maximizing spectral recovery.
   - Select filter sets that enhance information gain for taxonomic studies.

**End Algorithm**
