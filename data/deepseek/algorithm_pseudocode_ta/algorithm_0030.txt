**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess Spectral Data**
   - Normalize and center the training spectral dataset.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**
   - Compute the principal components (PCs) and corresponding eigenvectors from the preprocessed spectral data.

3. **Construct Photometric-to-Spectral Mapping Matrix**
   - Create a matrix `A` where each column corresponds to a spectral eigenvector projected into the photometric bands.

4. **Inverse Mapping for Latent Variables**
   - For each object, compute its latent variable vector `β` using photometric data:  
     $$\beta = A^{-1} p$$  
     where `p` is the observed photometric vector.

5. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**
   - Perform Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of `β` considering measurement uncertainties and prior information.

6. **Spectral Reconstruction**
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum using the learned PCs and the posterior samples of `β`:  
     $$s = \sum_{i=1}^{n_{\text{components}}} \beta_i \cdot \text{PC}_i$$  

7. **Uncertainty Propagation**
   - Calculate the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the covariance from the posterior distribution of `β`.

**End Algorithm**
