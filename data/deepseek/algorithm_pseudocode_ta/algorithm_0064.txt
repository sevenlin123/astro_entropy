**Algorithm: Bayesian Spectral Reconstruction via PCA-based Latent Space**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Input:** Reference spectral dataset of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - **Output:** Principal Component (PC) model representing the spectral manifold.
     - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the reference spectra.
     - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for improved reconstruction).
     - Store the PC loadings and scores for each reference spectrum.

2. **Reconstruction Phase for Each Object:**
   - **Input:** Photometric flux measurements of the object.
   - **Process:**
     a. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space:**
        - For the object, obtain its photometric fluxes and associated uncertainties.
        - Set up a Bayesian model where the latent variables are the principal component coefficients.
        - Define a prior distribution for the PC coefficients (e.g., Gaussian with zero mean and variance scaled by the reference dataset).
        - Likelihood function: Project the inferred PC coefficients onto the original spectral basis and compare with the observed photometric fluxes.
     b. **Estimate PC Coefficients:**
        - Perform Bayesian inference to compute the posterior distribution of the PC coefficients given the photometric data.
        - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational inference to sample from the posterior.
     c. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
        - Multiply the posterior mean of the PC coefficients by the corresponding eigenvectors.
        - Sum the contributions of all PCs to reconstruct the full spectral shape.
     d. **Uncertainty Quantification:**
        - Propagate the uncertainties from the posterior distribution of PC coefficients to the reconstructed spectrum.
        - Calculate confidence intervals or credible intervals for the spectral features.

3. **Output:**
   - For each object, provide the reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainty measures.
   - Optionally, perform additional processing such as taxonomic classification or outlier detection based on the reconstructed spectra.

**End of Algorithm**
