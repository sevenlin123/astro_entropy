**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Preprocessing and Training (Principal Component Analysis):**
   - Input: Reference Spectra Dataset `S`
   - Output: Trained Principal Component Model `PCA_model`
   - Perform PCA on `S` to derive principal components:
     - Center the data by subtracting the mean spectrum.
     - Compute covariance matrix of centered data.
     - Diagonalize covariance matrix to obtain eigenvectors and eigenvalues.
     - Sort eigenvectors by descending eigenvalue order to form `PCA_model`.

2. **Bayesian Inference: Mapping Photometric Data to Latent Space**
   - Input: Sparse Photometric Measurements `P` and Measurement Uncertainties `U`
   - Output: Posterior Distribution in PCA Latent Space `Z_posterior`
   - Initialize prior distribution over latent variables `Z`.
   - Use Bayesian inference to update the prior with `P` and `U`:
     - Evaluate likelihood of `P` given `Z` and `U`.
     - Apply Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to approximate posterior distribution `Z_posterior`.

3. **Spectrum Reconstruction from Latent Space**
   - Input: Posterior Samples `Z_posterior`
   - Output: Reconstructed Spectra Dataset `R`
   - For each sample in `Z_posterior`:
     - Project back into original spectral space using `PCA_model`.
     - Combine all projections to form `R`, representing the reconstructed spectra.

**End of Algorithm**
