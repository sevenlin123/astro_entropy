**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess Spectral Data**:
   - Initialize PCA on the training set of near-IR spectra.
   - Extract principal components (PCs) representing the spectral manifold of TNOs.

2. **Build Probabilistic Model**:
   - Construct a latent-space model linking photometric measurements to PCA components.
   - Define the probabilistic relationship between photometric data and the reduced PCA representation.

3. **Perform Bayesian Inference**:
   - For each object, use available photometric measurements.
   - Compute posterior distributions of the latent (PCA) variables through Bayesian inference.
   - Quantify uncertainties in the inferred PCA coefficients.

4. **Reconstruct Full Spectra**:
   - Utilize the posterior means of the PCA coefficients.
   - Project back to the original spectral space to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.
   - Calculate the uncertainty propagation from the PCA posteriors to the spectral reconstruction.

5. **Output Results**:
   - Provide reconstructed spectra along with their associated uncertainties.
   - Ensure credible interval coverage for validation purposes (as described in the paper).

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometric data to detailed spectral reconstructions, enabling taxonomic studies and survey optimizations for trans-Neptunian objects.
