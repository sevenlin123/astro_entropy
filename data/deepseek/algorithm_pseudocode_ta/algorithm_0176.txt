Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs
- Pre-trained PCA model on near-IR spectra
- Reference spectral dataset for latent-space modeling

Output:
- Reconstructed full spectra with propagated uncertainties

Steps:

1. Initialize the PCA model with the reference spectral dataset.
   - PCA dimensions: 4 to 5 principal components for taxonomic classification.
   - Additional dimensions (8 to 10): improve reconstruction fidelity and uncertainty calibration.

2. Transform the sparse photometric data into the latent-space representation using the PCA model.
   - Each photometric measurement corresponds to a point in the reduced dimensional space.

3. Perform Bayesian inference in the latent-space:
   - Model the spectral manifold of TNOs.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the latent-space model.
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or similar methods for posterior distribution estimation.

4. Reconstruct the full spectra from the posterior distributions of the latent-space variables.
   - Map the latent-space representation back to the original spectral space.
   - Apply any necessary transformations (e.g., inverse PCA transformation).

5. Calculate confidence intervals for the reconstructed spectra.
   - Ensure empirical credible-interval coverage across wavelengths (~95%).

6. Optionally, apply constraints to detect rare spectral types:
   - Use low-probability intermediate models if constrained photometry is available.
   - Adjust the reconstruction to account for peculiar spectral features.

7. Output the reconstructed spectra along with their uncertainties and confidence intervals.

End.
