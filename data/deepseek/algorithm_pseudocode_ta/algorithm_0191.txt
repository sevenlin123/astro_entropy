**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Input Data:**
   - Sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Pre-trained principal component (PC) representation derived from a sample of near-IR spectra.

2. **Initialization:**
   - Load the pre-trained principal component model.
   - Define the number of components (e.g., 4 to 5 for taxonomic classification; 8 to 10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

3. **Probabilistic Latent-Space Model:**
   - Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs using the pre-trained principal components.
   - Construct a probabilistic model in the reduced space using Bayesian inference.

4. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or similar methods to perform parameter estimation in the latent space.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the model.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - Reconstruct full spectra from the sparse photometric data using the inferred parameters in the latent space.
   - Compute uncertainties and credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.

6. **Validation Metrics:**
   - Calculate the empirical credible-interval coverage (e.g., ~95% across wavelengths).
   - Assess the goodness-of-fit for the reconstructed spectra compared to reference data.

7. **Output:**
   - Reconstructed spectra with associated uncertainties.
   - Diagnostics for outlier detection and identification of rare spectral types.

**Note:** Steps involving validation procedures, plotting, or visualization are omitted as per the given instructions.
