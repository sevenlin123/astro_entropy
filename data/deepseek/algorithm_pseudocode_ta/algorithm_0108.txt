**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data Preparation**
   - Obtain sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Normalize and preprocess the photometric data to ensure consistency across observations.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**
   - Collect a reference sample of near-infrared (NIR) spectra of TNOs.
   - Perform PCA on the reference sample to reduce spectral dimensions.
   - Retain 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification and 8-10 for detailed spectral reconstruction.

3. **Probabilistic Latent-Space Model Construction**
   - Define a probabilistic model in the latent space of principal components.
   - Establish the spectral manifold using the retained principal components.

4. **Bayesian Inference for Spectral Reconstruction**
   - For each TNO, use Bayesian inference to estimate posterior distributions of the latent variables (principal components) based on sparse photometric data.
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the estimated latent variables back to the original spectral space.

5. **Uncertainty Propagation**
   - Calculate the measurement uncertainties associated with the photometric data.
   - Propagate these uncertainties through the probabilistic model to determine the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra.
   - Assess the empirical credible-interval coverage of the reconstructed spectra across wavelengths.

6. **Filter Information Content Analysis**
   - Apply the framework to different NIRCam filter configurations (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M).
   - Quantify the information content of each filter configuration to optimize future observations for TNO taxonomy.

7. **Detection and Reconstruction of Rare Spectral Types**
   - Allow the Bayesian model to consider low-probability intermediate models within the spectral manifold.
   - Use this capability to detect and reconstruct rare spectral types, such as peculiar Neptune Trojans, by identifying and constraining their photometric properties.

8. **Output**
   - Generate reconstructed spectra with propagated uncertainties for each TNO.
   - Provide optimized filter configurations and identified rare spectral types.

**End of Algorithm**
