**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data**: 
   - Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
   - Pre-trained Principal Component (PC) model derived from a sample of near-IR spectra.
   - Covariance matrix of the PC model.

2. **Initialization**:
   - Load pre-trained PCs (4-5 for classification, 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).
   - Define the reduced spectral manifold based on the PCs.

3. **Bayesian Setup**:
   - Construct the posterior distribution over the latent space parameters using the sparse photometry.
   - Incorporate measurement uncertainties from the photometric data into the likelihood function.

4. **Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced latent space to estimate the posterior distribution of the spectral parameters.
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational methods to sample the posterior.

5. **Reconstruction**:
   - Map the inferred latent parameters back to the full spectrum using the pre-trained PC model.
   - Propagate the uncertainties from the latent space to the reconstructed spectrum.

6. **Output**:
   - Reconstructed spectrum for each TNO.
   - Associated uncertainty estimates across all wavelengths.

7. **Optional Survey Optimization**:
   - Apply the framework to evaluate the information content of different JWST/NIRCam filters.
   - Select optimal filter combinations for maximizing spectral reconstruction fidelity.

8. **End**
