```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Input: Sparse photometry measurements of TNOs, pre-trained PCA model

2. Initialize:
   - Pre-trained PCA model with principal components (latent space)
   - Define spectral manifold in PCA space

3. For each TNO:
   a. Obtain sparse photometric measurements
   b. Project photometric data into PCA latent space
   c. Perform Bayesian inference in latent space:
      i. Prior distribution defined over latent variables
      ii. Compute likelihood based on PCA model
      iii. Posterior = Likelihood * Prior / Evidence
   d. Reconstruct full spectrum:
      i. Map posterior mean (or mode) of latent variables to original spectral space
   e. Propagate measurement uncertainties:
      i. Calculate variance in latent space posterior
      ii. Transform variance back to spectral space

4. Output: Reconstructed spectra with uncertainties, latent space posteriors

End Algorithm
```
