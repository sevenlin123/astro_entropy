```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. Input:
   - Training set: Reference sample of near-infrared spectra
   - Photometric measurements: Sparse observations of TNOs
   - Number of principal components: k (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for high fidelity)

2. Preprocessing:
   a. Normalize and preprocess the reference spectra dataset.
   b. Extract principal components (PCs) via dimensionality reduction technique (e.g., PCA).

3. Model Training:
   a. Train a probabilistic latent-space model using the preprocessed PC representations.
   b. Establish the spectral manifold of TNOs in the reduced space.

4. Bayesian Inference:
   a. Perform Bayesian inference in the latent space using photometric measurements as inputs.
   b. Sample the posterior distribution to reconstruct the full spectrum.

5. Spectral Reconstruction:
   a. For each object, generate multiple posterior samples based on the inferred latent variables.
   b. Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

6. Output:
   a. Reconstructed spectra with associated uncertainties.
   b. Empirical credible intervals for validation.
   c. Ranked optimal filter configurations for survey optimization.
   d. Identified rare spectral types through constrained photometry analysis.

End of Algorithm
```
