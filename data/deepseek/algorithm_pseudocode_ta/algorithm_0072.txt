**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Step 1: Data Preprocessing**

1. **Convert Photometric Data to Flux Densities:**
   - Transform raw photometric magnitudes into flux densities using an appropriate flux-magnitude relation.
   
2. **Normalize Flux Densities:**
   - Divide each flux density by the median flux density of the training set to normalize the data.

3. **Create Binary Mask:**
   - Generate a binary mask where each element is 1 if the corresponding wavelength has a non-zero flux, and 0 otherwise.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Decomposition**

1. **PCA Training:**
   - Apply PCA to the normalized flux densities of the training set comprising known TNO spectra.

2. **Select Principal Components:**
   - Retain approximately 4–5 principal components based on explained variance or cross-validation criteria.

**Step 3: Bayesian Inference**

1. **Model Representation:**
   - Express the unknown spectrum as a linear combination of the first N PCA components:
     \[
     S = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i \cdot PC_i + \text{mean spectrum}
     \]
     where \(w_i\) are the weights (coefficients) and \(PC_i\) are the PCA components.

2. **Prior Distribution:**
   - Assume Gaussian priors for the coefficients with mean 0 and variance proportional to the variance of the corresponding PCA components.

3. **Likelihood Function:**
   - Formulate a likelihood that connects the reconstructed spectrum to the observed photometric fluxes through a forward model:
     \[
     p(\text{Photometry} | w) \propto \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum (\hat{F} - F_{\text{reconstructed}})^2\right)
     \]
     where \(F_{\text{reconstructed}}\) is derived from the PCA coefficients and the forward model.

4. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Utilize a sampling method (e.g., MCMC) to draw samples from the posterior distribution of the PCA coefficients given the sparse photometric data.

**Step 4: Spectrum Reconstruction**

1. **Posterior Sampling:**
   - For each posterior sample of the PCA coefficients \(w\):
     - Compute the reconstructed spectrum by combining the PCA components:
       \[
       S = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i \cdot PC_i + \text{mean spectrum}
       \]

2. **Collect Reconstructed Spectra:**
   - Gather all reconstructed spectra from the posterior samples.

3. **Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - The spread in the reconstructed spectra across all posterior samples represents the uncertainty in the final spectral reconstruction.

**Output:**
- **Reconstructed Spectrum:** Mean of the posterior-sampled spectra.
- **Uncertainty:** Spread (variance or standard deviation) of the sampled spectra.
- **Model Parameters:** Posterior distributions of the PCA coefficients used for reconstruction.

---

This algorithm effectively translates sparse photometric data into a probabilistic spectral reconstruction, enabling taxonomic analysis and outlier detection for TNOs using Bayesian methods and PCA-based dimensionality reduction.
