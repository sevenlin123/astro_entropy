**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Inputs:**
- Training spectral dataset (X)
- Number of PCA components (K)
- Hyperparameters for Bayesian inference

**Outputs:**
- Reconstructed spectra (Z)
- Uncertainty estimates

1. **Initialize Parameters:** Set K (number of PCA components), define Bayesian model hyperparameters.

2. **Perform PCA Analysis:**
   a. Center the spectral data X by subtracting the mean spectrum.
   b. Compute principal component coefficients (U) and loadings (V) via eigenvalue decomposition.

3. **Bayesian Inference Setup:**
   a. Define prior distributions for PCA coefficients (U).
   b. Specify likelihood function based on photometric measurements.
   c. Construct posterior distribution using Bayes' theorem.

4. **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling:**
   a. Use MCMC to sample from the posterior distribution of U.
   b. Generate N posterior samples representing possible spectral reconstructions.

5. **Post-Processing MCMC Samples:**
   a. Compute the mean spectrum from the posterior samples.
   b. Determine the credible intervals (e.g., 95%) for each wavelength.

6. **Output Reconstructed Spectra:**
   a. Return the mean spectrum Z as the reconstructed spectrum.
   b. Provide uncertainty bounds for each wavelength in Z.

**End Algorithm**
