**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Input**: Sparse photometric data of TNOs, including photometric measurements and associated uncertainties.
2. **Training Data**: A sample of known near-infrared spectra used to train the PCA model.
3. **Output**: Reconstructed full spectra along with their uncertainties.

**Steps**:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Train a PCA model on the sample of known near-infrared spectra.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification and 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

2. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Normalize the photometric data to account for measurement uncertainties and biases.
   - Convert photometric measurements into flux values appropriate for spectral reconstruction.

3. **Projection into Latent Space**:
   - Project the normalized photometric data into the PCA latent space using the pre-trained PCA model.
   - Each object is represented by its latent variables corresponding to the principal components.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA space to estimate the posterior distribution of the latent variables.
   - Incorporate prior knowledge about the spectral manifold of TNOs to constrain the possible values of the latent variables.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Use the posterior estimates of the latent variables to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Apply inverse PCA transformation to map the latent variables back to the original spectral space.

6. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - Propagate the uncertainties from the photometric measurements through the PCA model to the reconstructed spectra.
   - Quantify the uncertainty in each wavelength bin of the reconstructed spectrum.

7. **Validation (Optional)**:
   - Validate the reconstructed spectra using leave-one-out cross-validation or other validation techniques.
   - Adjust the model parameters if necessary to improve reconstruction accuracy.

**End Algorithm**
