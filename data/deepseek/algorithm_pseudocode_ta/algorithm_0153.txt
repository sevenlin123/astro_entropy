**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Input:** Set of near-infrared (NIR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - **Compute Principal Components (PCA):**
     - Perform PCA on the training spectra to obtain principal component (PC) coefficients and loadings.
     - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for classification; 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

2. **Model Setup:**
   - **Define Probabilistic Model:**
     - Represent each TNO's spectrum in the reduced PCA space.
     - Assume a probability distribution over the PCA coefficients (e.g., multivariate normal distribution with mean μ and covariance Σ).

3. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - For each TNO:
     - **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements.
     - **Infer Posterior Distribution:**
       - Use Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the PCA coefficients given the photometric data.
       - Apply Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational methods to sample the posterior.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - **Reconstruct Full Spectrum:**
     - Project the sampled PCA coefficients onto the original spectral basis.
     - Combine contributions from each PC to form the reconstructed spectrum.

5. **Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - Compute the variance-covariance matrix of the PCA coefficients' posterior distribution.
   - Transform this uncertainty into the spectral space to estimate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum.

6. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types:**
   - For each TNO, assess the consistency between the reconstructed spectrum and its inferred model.
   - Identify objects where the inferred model does not match the photometric data, indicating rare or unusual spectral types.

7. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Analyze the information content of NIRCam filters (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M) to determine optimal filter combinations for future observations.

This structured approach enables the reconstruction of full spectra from sparse photometry while accounting for uncertainties and identifying outliers, facilitating taxonomic classification and survey optimization.
