```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- Photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects
- Training set of near-IR spectra
- Principal component decomposition (PCD) of spectral data

Output:
- Reconstructed full spectra with propagated measurement uncertainties

Steps:

1. Perform Principal Component Decomposition (PCD) on the training set of near-IR spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).

2. Represent each object's spectrum in the reduced PC space, where each spectrum is expressed as a linear combination of the PC loadings and their corresponding scores.

3. Apply Bayesian inference in the reduced PC space:
   a. Treat the PC scores as parameters to infer.
   b. Use a Gaussian process model conditioned on the spectral manifold to model the relationship between photometric measurements and the PC scores.

4. Reconstruct the original spectra using the inferred PC scores:
   a. Multiply each inferred PC score by its corresponding PC loading vector.
   b. Sum the resulting contributions from all PCs to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.

5. Propagate measurement uncertainties through the Gaussian process model to quantify the uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

6. Output the reconstructed spectra along with their associated uncertainties.

End Algorithm
```
