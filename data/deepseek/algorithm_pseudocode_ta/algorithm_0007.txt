**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data:**
   - `SpectralData`: Matrix of observed near-infrared spectra for training.
   - `PhotometricMeasurements`: Sparse photometric data for objects to be reconstructed.
   - `NoiseModel`: Distribution describing measurement uncertainties.

2. **Preprocessing:**
   - Normalize `SpectralData` to ensure zero mean and unit variance.
   - Extract principal components (PCs) from `SpectralData` using PCA. Let `numPCs` be the number of components.

3. **Model Setup:**
   - Construct a probabilistic model in the reduced latent space of PCs.
   - Assume spectral variability is captured by the first `numPCs` components.

4. **Bayesian Inference Loop:**
   - For each object in `PhotometricMeasurements`:
     1. Extract the object's sparse photometric measurements.
     2. Perform Bayesian inference in the latent PC space to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     3. Propagate uncertainties through the model to compute credible intervals.

5. **Output:**
   - Reconstructed spectra for each object.
   - Credible intervals for each reconstructed spectrum.
   - Posterior probabilities of spectral features.

**End Algorithm**
