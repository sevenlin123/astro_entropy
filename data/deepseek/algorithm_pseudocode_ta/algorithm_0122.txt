**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Input:**  
- Photometric measurements (sparse) for a trans-Neptunian object  
- Pre-trained Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model from a sample of near-IR spectra  
- Covariance matrix of photometric uncertainties  

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed spectrum with uncertainties propagated  

**Steps:**  

1. **Principal Component Representation:**  
   - Use the pre-trained PCA model to project the sparse photometric measurements into the latent (principal component) space.  
   - Each photometric band is treated as a linear combination of the principal components.  

2. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space:**  
   - Perform Bayesian inference to estimate the principal component coefficients (latent variables) given the sparse photometric measurements and their uncertainties.  
   - Use a prior distribution for the latent variables based on the PCA model.  
   - Incorporate the covariance matrix of photometric uncertainties into the likelihood function.  

3. **Posterior Distribution of Latent Variables:**  
   - Obtain the posterior distribution of the principal component coefficients.  
   - This accounts for the propagation of photometric uncertainties into the latent space.  

4. **Spectral Reconstruction:**  
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum using the posterior mean or mode of the latent variables.  
   - Multiply each principal component by its corresponding eigenvector and sum them up to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.  

5. **Uncertainty Propagation:**  
   - Calculate the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the uncertainties in the latent variables through the linear combination of eigenvectors.  
   - This provides the empirical credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum.  

**End Algorithm**
