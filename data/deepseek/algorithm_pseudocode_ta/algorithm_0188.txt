**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Bayesian Latent-Space Inference**

1. **Input Data Preparation**
   - Load training dataset of near-IR spectra.
   - Extract photometric measurements for target TNOs.
   
2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Train PCA model on the spectral training set.
   - Determine optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).
   
3. **Probabilistic Latent-Space Model Construction**
   - Represent spectral manifold using the reduced PCA space.
   - Define probability distribution over latent factors capturing spectral variability.
   
4. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**
   - Perform Bayesian updating using photometric measurements.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the posterior distribution.
   
5. **Spectral Reconstruction**
   - Reconstruct full spectra from the posterior latent factors.
   - Compute credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.
   
6. **Survey Optimization**
   - Evaluate information content of NIRCam filters (e.g., F090W, F115W).
   - Select optimal filter combinations for maximal spectral diversity.
   
7. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Reconstruction**
   - Apply model to constrain photometry for rare types.
   - Identify and reconstruct unusual spectral models via intermediate latent-space exploration.

8. **Output**
   - Provide reconstructed spectra with uncertainty estimates.
   - Deliver optimized survey configurations.
   - Highlight outlier cases and their reconstructions.

**End Algorithm**
