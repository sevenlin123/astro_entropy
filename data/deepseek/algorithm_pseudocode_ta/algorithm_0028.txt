**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Initialize Variables**
   - Input: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
   - Output: Reconstructed spectra with uncertainties.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Decompose the spectral dataset into principal components.
     - Each component represents a mode of spectral variability.

3. **Bayesian Inference Setup**
   - Define a probabilistic model in the latent space (principal components).
   - Model parameters include coefficients for each principal component.

4. **Perform Bayesian Inference**
   - Use photometric data to estimate posterior distributions of the latent space parameters.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometry into the latent space.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**
   - Reconstruct full spectra by projecting the inferred latent space parameters back into the original spectral space.
   - Combine contributions from each principal component.

6. **Uncertainty Propagation**
   - Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra by propagating the uncertainties from the latent space estimates.
   - Ensure uncertainties reflect the reliability of the reconstruction.

**End Algorithm**
