**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **PCA Model Training**
   - Train Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on a dataset of near-IR spectra to obtain principal component (PC) basis vectors and eigenvalues.

2. **For Each Object:**
   - **Input:** Sparse photometric flux measurements and their uncertainties.
   - **Step 1:** Extract photometric data corresponding to available bands.
   - **Step 2:** Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to estimate posterior distribution of PCA coefficients, incorporating measurement uncertainties.
   - **Step 3:** Determine posterior mean (or mode) of the coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - **Step 4:** Project the inferred coefficients back into the original spectral space using the PCA basis vectors.
   - **Step 5:** Calculate uncertainties associated with the reconstructed spectrum by evaluating the variance in the posterior distribution of the coefficients.

**End Algorithm**
