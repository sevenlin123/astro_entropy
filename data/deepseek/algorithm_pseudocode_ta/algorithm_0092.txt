Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Initialize:
   - Preprocess a training set of TNO near-infrared spectra into a principal component (PC) representation.
   - Determine the number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

2. Input:
   - Photometric measurements (sparse) for a TNO object.

3. Bayesian Inference Setup:
   - Define a probabilistic model in the reduced PC space.
   - Model includes prior distributions for the PC coefficients based on the training data.
   - Define likelihood function incorporating photometric uncertainties.

4. Perform Bayesian Inference:
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational methods to sample the posterior distribution of PC coefficients given the sparse photometry.
   - Propagate uncertainties through the inference process.

5. Reconstruct Spectrum:
   - Map the sampled PC coefficients back to the original spectral space.
   - Combine reconstructed spectrum components to form the full spectral estimate.
   - Assess credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum.

6. Output:
   - Reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties.
   - Diagnostics for model selection (e.g., Akaike Information Criterion, Bayesian Information Criterion).
