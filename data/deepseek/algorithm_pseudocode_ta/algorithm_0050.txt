```plaintext
Algorithm:

1. Read training spectral data X.
2. Preprocess X:
   a. Compute mean spectrum mu.
   b. Center X: X_centered = X - mu.
3. Compute PCA:
   a. Compute covariance matrix C = (X_centered' X_centered) / (n-1).
   b. Diagonalize C to get eigenvectors V and eigenvalues D.
   c. Sort V and D in descending order of eigenvalues.
   d. Select first K eigenvectors as PCA basis.
4. For each object:
   a. Read sparse photometric data y.
   b. Bayesian inference:
      i. Model y ~ N(Az, sigma²), where A is known.
      ii. Prior: z ~ N(0, diag(variances)).
      iii. Perform MCMC to sample z.
   c. Compute posterior mean z_mean.
   d. Reconstruct spectrum s = mu + V_K * z_mean.
5. Output reconstructed spectra S.
```
