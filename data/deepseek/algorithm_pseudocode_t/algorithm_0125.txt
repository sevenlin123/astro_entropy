Algorithm Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction
    Inputs:
        Training SEDs
        Test object sparse photometric data
    Outputs:
        Reconstructed SEDs
        Posterior predictive distributions
        Model uncertainty estimates
    Steps:
        Step 1: Obtain training data consisting of SEDs from known objects.
        Step 2: Precompute basis functions as eigenvectors of the covariance matrix of the training SEDs.
        Step 3: For each test object:
            Substep 3.1: Retrieve sparse photometric data.
            Substep 3.2: For each observed band, compute flux measurements and associated noise.
            Substep 3.3: Construct observation model incorporating noise and other variables.
            Substep 3.4: Express the SED as a linear combination of pre-trained basis functions with coefficients to be inferred.
            Substep 3.5: Define Gaussian prior on coefficients and introduce hyperparameter α for Laplace sparsity prior.
            Substep 3.6: Perform Bayesian inference using conjugate gradients to optimize coefficients.
            Substep 3.7: Compute posterior predictive distribution of the SED.
            Substep 3.8: Use bootstrapping to quantify uncertainty in the reconstructed SED.
