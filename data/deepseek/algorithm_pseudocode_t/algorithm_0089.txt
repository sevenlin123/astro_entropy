**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Initialize Data Collection**: For each TNO, gather all available photometric measurements from various surveys.

2. **Data Preprocessing**: 
    - Clean the data by removing outliers and correcting for instrumental biases.
    - Standardize the photometric measurements to a common flux scale.

3. **Define Gaussian Process (GP)**:
    - Select an appropriate kernel (e.g., Matérn kernel) suitable for spectral data.
    - Set initial hyperparameters for the GP, including length scale and variance.

4. **GP Parameter Sampling via MCMC**:
    - Use MCMC (e.g., Metropolis-Hastings) to sample hyperparameters of the GP.
    - Iterate over the chain until convergence, adjusting parameters to best fit the observed data.

5. **Reconstruct Spectral Energy Distribution (SED)**:
    - Utilize the GP to predict the SED at all wavelengths, incorporating measurement noise.
    - Generate posterior distributions of the SED components using the sampled GP parameters.

6. **Outlier Detection**:
    - Compare the reconstructed SED posteriors with theoretical models or known spectral classifications.
    - Flag objects whose SEDs deviate significantly from expected patterns as outliers.

7. **Output Results**:
    - Provide reconstructed SEDs with associated uncertainties.
    - Deliver outlier flags and metrics for survey optimization.
