Algorithm: Bayesian Stellar Age Inference from Radial Velocities

1. Read radial velocity measurements and uncertainties for each star.
2. Initialize Gaussian Process with kernel (e.g., Maternat52) and hyperparameters informed by stellar evolution.
3. Define prior distributions for Gaussian Process hyperparameters and stellar age.
4. For each velocity point:
    a. Compute the likelihood contribution using the Gaussian Process.
5. Use MCMC sampler (e.g., NUTS) to explore the posterior distribution.
6. After obtaining sufficient samples, calculate posterior statistics such as mean age and credibility intervals.
7. Output the inferred stellar ages and associated uncertainties.

End Algorithm.
