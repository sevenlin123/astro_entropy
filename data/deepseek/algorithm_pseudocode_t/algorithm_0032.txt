**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

**Input:**  
- Photometric data of a TNO (sparse flux measurements at various wavelengths).  
- Known spectral templates and color trends from taxonomic studies.  

**Output:**  
- Posterior distribution of the spectral energy distribution (SED) of the TNO.  
- Confidence intervals for the reconstructed SED.  

**Steps:**  

1. **Initialize Gaussian Process Regression (GPR):**  
   - Use known spectral templates and color trends to define a set of basis functions.  
   - Train the GPR model on these templates to capture the typical variations in TNO SEDs.  

2. **Define Prior Distribution:**  
   - Construct a prior distribution over possible SEDs using the GPR.  
   - This prior reflects the expected shape and variability of TNO spectra based on known templates and color trends.  

3. **Probabilistic Forward Model:**  
   - Define a function that maps the SED (represented as a vector of fluxes at specific wavelengths) to observed photometric magnitudes.  
   - This function incorporates the physics of light measurement and includes uncertainties in the photometric data.  

4. **Observation Model:**  
   - Model the uncertainties and possible discrepancies in the photometric measurements.  
   - Include noise parameters and potential outliers in the data.  

5. **Bayesian Inference:**  
   - Compute the posterior distribution of the SED given the observed photometric data.  
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational methods to sample from the posterior distribution.  

6. **Posterior Predictive Distribution:**  
   - For each sampled SED from the posterior, predict the corresponding photometric magnitudes.  
   - Calculate the confidence intervals for the reconstructed SED by analyzing the spread of the posterior samples.  

7. **Optimization for Computational Efficiency:**  
   - Represent the SED in a sparse basis to reduce the dimensionality of the problem.  
   - Use efficient sampling techniques to handle the high-dimensional parameter space.  

**End Algorithm**
