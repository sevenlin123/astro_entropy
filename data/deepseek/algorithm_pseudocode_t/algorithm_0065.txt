**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Input Data**: Obtain photometric time series for each object, including magnitude measurements and their associated uncertainties.

2. **Initialize Gaussian Process (GP) Model**:
   - Define the GP with initial hyperparameters.
   - The GP models the SED parameters: effective temperature (T_eff), radius (R), and albedo (α).
   - Establish the covariance matrix to reflect dependencies among T_eff, R, and α.

3. **Initialize Bayesian Blocks Model**:
   - Set up the Bayesian Blocks to handle periods of constant flux in the light curve.
   - This model is designed to manage sparse and noisy data effectively.

4. **Combine Models**:
   - Merge the GP and Bayesian Blocks into a unified probabilistic model for the light curve.
   - The combined model captures both smooth variations and regions of constant flux.

5. **Define Likelihood Function**:
   - Develop the likelihood function to compute the probability of observing the photometric data given the model parameters.
   - Incorporate measurement uncertainties and data sparsity.

6. **Set Prior Distributions**:
   - Assign prior distributions to all model parameters, including GP hyperparameters and SED parameters.

7. **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling**:
   - Utilize an MCMC sampler to draw samples from the joint posterior distribution of the model parameters.
   - Update parameters based on likelihood and prior distributions.

8. **Posterior Analysis**:
   - Calculate posterior means and credible intervals for each parameter.
   - Determine point estimates and uncertainty measures.

9. **Convergence Check**:
   - Perform checks to ensure MCMC chains have sufficiently explored the parameter space.

10. **Output Results**:
    - Provide estimated SED parameters (T_eff, R, α) with uncertainties and derived quantities.

**End Algorithm**
