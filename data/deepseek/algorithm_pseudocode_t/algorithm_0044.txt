**Algorithm: Probabilistic SED Reconstruction**

1. **Input**: Sparse photometric data of TNOs, known covariance between photometric bands.
2. **Initialize Parameters**: Define piecewise power-law segments, set priors for exponents.
3. **Define Model**: Piecewise power-law SED with exponents for each segment.
4. **Set Up Likelihood**: Incorporate data and covariance structure.
5. **Perform Bayesian Inference**: Compute posterior distributions of parameters.
6. **Reconstruct Each Segment**: Determine exponents for each segment.
7. **Combine Segments**: Form a continuous SED.
8. **Calculate Uncertainty**: Quantify uncertainty in SED estimates.
9. **Determine Posterior Probabilities**: Assess confidence for each TNO.
10. **Identify Outliers**: Flag TNOs with posteriors below threshold.

**Output**: Reconstructed SEDs, uncertainty measures, outlier flags.
