Algorithm ProbabilisticSpectralReconstruction:
    Inputs:
        sparse_photometry: dictionary of measured fluxes at various wavelengths
        pre_trained_dgm: a deep generative model pre-trained on known TNO spectra
        num_samples: number of MCMC samples to draw

    Outputs:
        reconstructed_spectrum: dictionary of estimated fluxes at original wavelengths
        uncertainty_estimates: dictionary of flux uncertainties at original wavelengths

    Begin:
        1. Initialize Gaussian Process Regressor (GPR):
            - Choose kernel (e.g., RBF + White kernels) to model smoothness and variability
            - Set hyperparameters as needed
        
        2. Train GPR using sparse_photometry data:
            - Fit the model to the provided flux measurements

        3. Compute latent representation:
            - Pass sparse_photometry through pre_trained_dgm's encoder to get latent_rep

        4. Perform variational inference on latent_rep:
            - Use pre_trained_dgm to infer distribution parameters
            - Apply reparameterization or other specific techniques as needed

        5. Define MCMC sampler setup:
            - Likelihood combines GPR predictions and DGM priors
            - Specify prior distributions based on variational inference results

        6. Run MCMC sampler for num_samples iterations:
            - Explore posterior distribution of spectral parameters

        7. Post-process MCMC samples:
            - Calculate mean and variance across all MCMC samples

        8. Map results to wavelength grid:
            - Use computed means and variances to construct reconstructed_spectrum and uncertainty_estimates

    End.
