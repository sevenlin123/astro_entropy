1. For each object, extract photometric measurements and their associated uncertainties.
2. Construct the covariance matrix Σ based on the measurement uncertainties.
3. Discretize the SED into a set of basis functions B.
4. Formulate the forward model M relating the SED to observed photometry.
5. Initialize iterative algorithm with initial weights for least squares solution.
6. Begin IRLS iteration:
   6.1. Invert matrix M^T Σ^{-1} M to estimate SED coefficients.
   6.2. Compute residuals between observed photometry and predicted values.
   6.3. Update weights based on residuals to handle outliers.
7. Continue IRLS iterations until convergence.
8. After convergence, compute posterior covariance of SED coefficients.
9. Sample from posterior distribution to obtain multiple SED estimates.
10. Output reconstructed SED and its uncertainties.
