Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. Initialize parameters and data structures.
2. Preprocess:
   a. Standardize photometric measurements.
   b. Remove outliers from observations.
3. Train:
   a. Construct basis functions using PCA of training spectra.
4. For each object:
   a. Perform Bayesian inference using MCMC sampling.
      i. Estimate posterior distributions of spectral parameters, rotational period, and orientation.
5. Post-inference processing:
   a. Compute confidence intervals from posterior variances.
   b. Identify outliers with abnormally high posterior variances.
6. Survey optimization:
   a. Simulate potential new observations.
   b. Evaluate reduction in uncertainty for prioritization.
