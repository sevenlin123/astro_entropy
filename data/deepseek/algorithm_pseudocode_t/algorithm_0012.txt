**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Initialization**:
   - Define the set of observed photometric bands, `bands = {B_1, B_2, ..., B_n}`.
   - For each band `B_i`, collect the sparse photometric measurements `P_i`.
   - Initialize the prior distributions for the spectral energy distribution (SED), `priorSED`.

2. **Bayesian Inference Setup**:
   - Define the likelihood function based on the observed photometric measurements and the SED.
   - Specify the probabilistic mixture model to account for spectral variability and uncertainty.

3. **Iterative Refinement**:
   - **For each iteration m from 1 to M**:
     - **Step 1**: Propose a new SED hypothesis using the current prior distribution.
     - **Step 2**: Compute the posterior probability of the hypothesis given the data and prior distributions.
     - **Step 3**: Update the prior distribution based on the posterior probabilities to refine the SED for the next iteration.

4. **Outlier Detection**:
   - For each object, calculate the probability of being an outlier based on the deviation of its SED from the mixture components.
   - Flag objects with a probability exceeding a threshold as outliers.

5. **Survey Optimization**:
   - For each possible observation strategy, compute the expected reduction in uncertainty of the reconstructed SEDs.
   - Select the strategy that minimizes the overall uncertainty across all objects.

6. **Output**:
   - Generate the refined SEDs for each object.
   - Provide outlier flags and optimized survey parameters.

**End Algorithm**
