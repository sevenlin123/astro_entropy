**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Initialize Variables**
   - Input: Photometric data of TNOs (flux measurements in sparse bands), Basis spectra (Kirkwood gaps template spectra)
   - Output: Posterior distributions of model parameters

2. **Compute Observed Fluxes**
   - For each object, calculate flux in each photometric band

3. **Define Model Parameters**
   - Weights matrix W (dimension: N_objects x N_basis_spectra)
   - Other parameters P (e.g., scaling factors, offsets)

4. **Set Prior Distributions**
   - Define priors based on physical properties (e.g., size, composition constraints)

5. **Define Likelihood Function**
   - Model SED = sum(W * Basis Spectra)
   - Relate model predictions to flux measurements considering their uncertainties

6. **Perform Bayesian Inference**
   - Use MCMC or other sampling methods to draw posterior samples of W and P

7. **Extract Posterior Means/Medians and Uncertainties**
   - Compute representative values and confidence intervals from posterior samples

8. **Output Reconstructed SEDs and Uncertainties**

**End Algorithm**
