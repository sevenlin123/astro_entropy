**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

**Step 1: Preprocessing**
1. Input: Observed photometric data, extinction map, and prior knowledge of interstellar reddening.
2. Adjust the observed photometric magnitudes to correct for interstellar reddening using the extinction map.
3. Output: Corrected photometric data ready for modeling.

**Step 2: Probabilistic Modeling with Gaussian Processes**
1. Define the Gaussian Process (GP) with a custom covariance function tailored to capture spectral features at relevant wavelengths.
2. Set hyperparameters for the covariance function based on known spectral characteristics of TNOs.
3. Initialize the GP with the preprocessed photometric data as training points.

**Step 3: Bayesian Inference**
1. Perform Bayesian inference to compute the posterior distribution of the spectral parameters given the corrected photometric data.
2. Use the posterior distributions to estimate the reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties.
3. Output: Reconstructed spectral parameters and their uncertainties.

**End Algorithm**
