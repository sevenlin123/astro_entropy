**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Gaussian Processes**

1. **Input Data**: 
   - Photometric time series data for each trans-Neptunian object.
   - Color information (e.g., magnitudes in multiple bands).
   
2. **Preprocessing**:
   - Normalize photometric measurements to account for observation biases.
   - Align time series data across different objects to synchronize temporal dimensions.
   
3. **Model Initialization**:
   - Define a Gaussian Process (GP) kernel that captures spectral smoothness and variability.
   - Incorporate prior knowledge about typical spectral energy distributions as part of the GP definition.
   
4. **Training the Gaussian Process**:
   - Use Bayesian inference to estimate hyperparameters of the GP.
   - Maximize the marginal likelihood to determine optimal kernel parameters.
   
5. **Spectral Prediction**:
   - For each object, apply the trained GP to predict the complete spectral energy distribution.
   - Integrate light curve information to constrain the time-varying flux, enhancing spectral inference.
   
6. **Posterior Analysis**:
   - Quantify uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra using the posterior variance provided by the GP.
   - Analyze confidence intervals to assess reliability of the spectral estimates.
   
7. **Output**:
   - Reconstructed spectral energy distributions for all objects.
   - Uncertainty metrics for each spectral component.
   
8. **End**
