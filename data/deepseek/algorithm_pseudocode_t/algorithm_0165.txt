Algorithm ProbabilisticSEDReconstruction(PhotometricData):

    Initialize GaussianProcess with specifiedKernel and hyperParameters
    For each Object in PhotometricData:
        Read sparsePhotometricMeasurements
        Perform BayesianInference on GaussianProcess using measurements
        Conduct MCMC Sampling to explore posterior distribution
        Extract mostProbableSED and uncertaintyMeasurements from posterior
    End For
    Output reconstructedSEDs
