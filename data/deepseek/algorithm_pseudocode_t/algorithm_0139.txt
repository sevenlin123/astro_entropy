**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - Extract template SEDs from the training set of TNOs with known spectra.

2. **Initialization for Test Object:**
   - Set mixing coefficients to a uniform Dirichlet distribution (α = 1).

3. **Optimizing Mixing Coefficients:**
   - Apply Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) to adjust the mixing coefficients based on observed photometric data and uncertainties.
   - Regularize using a Dirichlet prior to ensure the sum of coefficients equals 1.

4. **Posterior Predictive Distribution:**
   - Compute the distribution by integrating over all possible mixing distributions to determine the confidence in the reconstructed SED.

5. **Classification Probabilities:**
   - Calculate the posterior probability of each template SED given the data and mixing distribution.
   - Rank the spectral classifications by their posterior probabilities.

6. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Identify outliers by comparing the probability of each object's SED under the mixture distribution to a threshold.

7. **Output:**
   - Provide the reconstructed SED with confidence intervals.
   - Deliver a ranked list of spectral classifications.
   - Include outlier flags for objects not meeting the threshold.
