**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. Initialize the algorithm for the current object.
2. Gather all available photometric measurements across multiple passbands.
3. Preprocess the data by correcting for observing conditions and calibrating measurements.
4. Build a Gaussian Process (GP) model with a covariance function that captures spectral smoothness and known spectral features.
5. Incorporate a mixture of Gaussians to represent spectral features within the GP model.
6. Apply a Dirichlet process prior to account for unknown spectral features.
7. Perform Bayesian inference to compute the posterior distribution over the spectral energy distribution (SED), considering measurement errors and modeling uncertainties.
8. Calculate the posterior predictive distribution to assess the model's ability to predict new observations.
9. Classify the object into spectral classes based on the posterior probabilities of predefined templates.
10. Detect outliers by comparing the observed data with the posterior predictive distribution.
