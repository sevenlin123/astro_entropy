**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Using Gaussian Processes**

1. **Initialize Gaussian Process Prior**:
   - Define the Gaussian Process (GP) kernel with parameters that reflect the expected smoothness and variability of the spectral energy distribution of trans-Neptunian objects.
   
2. **Input Observations**:
   - Obtain sparse photometric observations for each object across multiple bands. Each observation consists of flux measurements and associated uncertainties due to instrumental noise.

3. **Condition GP on Observations**:
   - Update the Gaussian Process with the given photometric data, incorporating both the measured flux values and their uncertainties.

4. **Incorporate Physical Constraints**:
   - Enforce physically meaningful constraints on the spectral shape, such as limits on albedo, phase functions, and known spectral features typical of trans-Neptunian objects.

5. **Reconstruct the Spectrum**:
   - Use the conditioned Gaussian Process to sample the posterior distribution of the spectral energy distribution (SED). This step generates multiple plausible spectra consistent with the observed data and physical constraints.

6. **Outlier Detection**:
   - For each observation, calculate the probability that it deviates significantly from the reconstructed SED. Exclude observations classified as outliers to refine the model.

7. **Propagate Uncertainties**:
   - Compute the uncertainty in the reconstructed SED by evaluating the variance of the posterior distribution at each wavelength.

8. **Generate Taxonomic Classifications**:
   - Extract key spectral features (e.g., slopes, absorption bands) from the reconstructed SEDs to assign taxonomic categories to each object.

9. **Optimize Survey Strategy**:
   - Evaluate potential band combinations to determine those that maximize information gain for spectral reconstruction, aiding in the design of efficient observational campaigns.

**End Algorithm**
