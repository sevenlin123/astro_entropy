Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects

Input: Sparse photometric data (magnitudes, uncertainties, bandpasses)
Output: Posterior distributions of SED parameters

1. Initialize Physical Model:
   a. Define basis functions for spectral interpolation.
   b. Parameterize SED with a low-dimensional vector θ = [θ₁, θ₂, ..., θₙ].

2. Construct Likelihood Function:
   a. For each observation:
      i. Compute expected flux using the physical model and basis bandpasses.
      ii. Calculate the probability of observing the magnitude given the expected flux and measurement uncertainty.
   b. Combine individual likelihoods into a joint likelihood.

3. Define Prior Distributions:
   a. Set Gaussian or uniform priors on SED parameters based on empirical knowledge of TNOs.
   b. Assign priors to photometric measurements where direct observations are unavailable.

4. Bayesian Inference via MCMC:
   a. Combine physical model, likelihood, and priors using Bayes' theorem to form posterior distribution.
   b. Use MCMC sampler (e.g., Metropolis-Hastings) to draw samples from the posterior.
   c. Generate sufficient number of samples to accurately represent the posterior distribution.

5. Analyze Posterior Samples:
   a. Extract parameter estimates and uncertainties from the posterior samples.
   b. Reconstruct the SED by evaluating the physical model at the mean parameter values.
   c. Quantify goodness-of-fit and residuals if required.

End Algorithm
