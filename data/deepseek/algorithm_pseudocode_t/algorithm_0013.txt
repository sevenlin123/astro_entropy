```plaintext
Initialize observed photometric magnitudes M_obs and uncertainties σ.

Propose a prior probability distribution P_prior for spectral parameters θ.

Set iteration counter k = 0.

While not converged:
    k += 1
    Sample θ_k ~ P_prior
    Generate synthetic spectrum S_k using θ_k
    Compute expected magnitudes M_synthetic from S_k
    Calculate likelihood ratio LR = p(M_obs | S_k) / p(M_obs | current posterior)
    Update P_posterior ∝ P_prior * LR
    Set P_prior = P_posterior for next iteration

After convergence:
Extract posterior distributions of θ from P_posterior
Compute confidence intervals for spectral parameters
Identify outliers by comparing M_obs with expected range from posterior
```
