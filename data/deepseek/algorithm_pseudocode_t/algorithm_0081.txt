Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs

Input: Collection of sparse photometric data for TNOs
Output: Reconstructed SEDs and statistical comparisons

1. Initialize parameters:
   - Shared hyperparameters for GPs (e.g., kernel parameters, noise levels)
   - Mixture components (e.g., number of GPs, mixing coefficients)

2. For each TNO object:
   a. Collect photometric data across all bands
   b. For each band, create a data point
   c. Form a dataset of spectral measurements

3. For all objects:
   a. Build a Gaussian Process with shared hyperparameters
   b. Combine GPs into a mixture model
   c. Define prior distributions over model parameters (mixture weights, GP hyperparameters)

4. Perform variational inference:
   a. Approximate posterior distribution over mixture model parameters
   b. Compute evidence lower bound (ELBO) as objective function
   c. Use coordinate ascent or other optimization methods to maximize ELBO

5. Bayesian outlier detection:
   a. For each observation, compute posterior probability of being an outlier
   b. Classify measurements as outliers if posterior probability exceeds threshold
   c. Exclude outliers from further analysis

6. Reconstruct SEDs:
   a. For each TNO, sample from the posterior predictive distribution
   b. Obtain mean and variance of the reconstructed SED

7. Statistical comparisons:
   a. Compare SEDs between different TNO populations
   b. Compute likelihood ratios or other metrics for taxonomy and survey optimization

Return: Reconstructed SEDs and comparative statistics
