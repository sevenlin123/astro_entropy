Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs

Input: Sparse photometric observations of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs) with photometric uncertainties

Output: Posterior mean spectra and credible intervals for each TNO

1. Read input data: Obtain sparse photometric observations and their associated uncertainties for each TNO.

2. Preprocess data (optional):
   a. Remove outliers using robust statistical methods if present.
   b. Normalize photometric measurements to have unit variance to facilitate comparison across filters.

3. Select basis functions:
   a. Choose orthogonal polynomials (e.g., Chebyshev) up to a specified degree or other basis functions (e.g., B-splines, wavelets) suitable for spectral modeling.

4. Define probabilistic model:
   a. Establish a mixture of Gaussian Processes (MGP) where each component is a Gaussian Process with a covariance function (e.g., squared exponential) to model smooth spectral variations.
   b. Assign mean functions that incorporate prior knowledge about typical spectral features in the outer Solar System.
   c. Determine mixing weights, which can be uniform or learned from the data.

5. Fit the model:
   a. Maximize the marginal likelihood with respect to model hyperparameters (covariance lengthscales, noise levels, etc.) using all available photometric data.

6. Generate posterior samples:
   a. For each TNO, draw samples from the posterior distribution of spectral parameters conditioned on its photometric observations.

7. Compute credible intervals:
   a. Calculate quantiles from the posterior samples to establish credible intervals for the spectral parameters.

8. Output results:
   a. Provide the posterior mean spectrum and credible intervals for each TNO.
   b. Optionally, provide the most probable MGP component and its uncertainty.

End
