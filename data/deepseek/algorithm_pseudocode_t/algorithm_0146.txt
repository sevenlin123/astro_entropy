Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. Initialize Gaussian Process (GP) model with spectral templates as basis functions.
2. For each trans-Neptunian object:
   a. Obtain sparse photometric data.
   b. Preprocess data by accounting for measurement uncertainties.
3. Construct covariance matrix using GP with spectral variability.
4. Perform MCMC sampling to estimate model parameters:
   a. Sample from posterior distribution of spectral parameters.
   b. Incorporate interdependencies between spectral features.
5. Reconstruct spectral energy distribution (SED):
   a. Use sampled parameters to build SED.
   b. Calculate confidence intervals for reconstructed SED.
6. Apply outlier detection:
   a. Compute posterior probabilities for each object.
   b. Identify outliers based on posterior probabilities.
7. Output empirical SEDs with confidence intervals and outlier flags.

End.
