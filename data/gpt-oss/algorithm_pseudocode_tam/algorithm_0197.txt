Input:
    S_ref   – Reference set of high‑resolution spectra {s_1 … s_N}  (N≈50)
    R       – Instrument response curves for the desired photometric filters
    U       – Number of principal components to retain (U = 5–10)
    h_KDE   – Bandwidth for kernel density estimation (≈0.5)
    σ_noise – Relative photometric uncertainty to inject (≈0.20)

Output for any new object:
    S_hat   – Reconstructed high‑resolution spectrum
    C_hat   – Posterior probability distribution over taxonomic classes
