Inputs
-------
S_train   : matrix of N_spec × N_wave training spectra  
F_obs     : vector of K photometric fluxes for the target TNO  
σ_obs     : vector of K measurement uncertainties  
R_k(λ)    : transmission curve for filter k (k = 1 … K)  

Parameters
----------
K_pca     : number of principal components to retain (e.g. 4–10)  
N_draw    : number of posterior draws (e.g. 2000)  

Outputs
-------
S_mean(λ): mean reconstructed spectrum  
S_CI_low(λ), S_CI_high(λ): 95 % credible‑interval envelopes  
c_post      : posterior samples of PCA coefficients  

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 – Train PCA on the spectral library
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Center the spectra:  S_bar = S_train – mean(S_train, axis=0)
1.2 Compute singular value decomposition:
        S_bar = U_S · Σ · V_T          # U_S: N_spec×N_spec, Σ: diagonal, V_T: N_spec×N_wave
1.3 Extract first K_pca right singular vectors (columns of V_T):
        PC_basis = V_T[0:K_pca , :]     # shape K_pca × N_wave
1.4 Compute eigenvalues λ_i (i = 1…K_pca) from Σ[0:K_pca]
1.5 Store mean spectrum μ = mean(S_train, axis=0)   # shape N_wave
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 2 – Define forward model for photometry
--------------------------------------------------------------------
For any coefficient vector c ∈ ℝ^K_pca:
    2.1 Reconstructed spectrum:
            S_c(λ) = μ(λ) + Σ_{i=1}^{K_pca} c_i · PC_basis[i , λ]
    2.2 Synthetic photometry in filter k:
            f_k(c) = ∫ S_c(λ) · R_k(λ) dλ / ∫ R_k(λ) dλ
   (Numerical integration performed on the discrete wavelength grid.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 3 – Set up the Bayesian inference problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Likelihood (Gaussian):
        L(c) ∝ Π_{k=1}^{K} exp{ - [F_obs(k) – f_k(c)]² / (2 σ_obs(k)²) }
3.2 Prior on coefficients:
        p(c) = N(0, diag(λ_1,…,λ_K_pca))          # zero‑mean normal with PC variances
3.3 Posterior:
        p(c | F_obs) ∝ L(c) · p(c)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 4 – Compute posterior analytically (linear–Gaussian case)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Because f_k(c) is linear in c, the posterior is also Gaussian:
    4.1 Construct design matrix A (K × K_pca):
        A(k,i) = ∂f_k / ∂c_i evaluated at c = 0
        (Compute by differentiating the integral expression or numerically.)
    4.2 Observation noise covariance:  Σ_obs = diag(σ_obs²)
    4.3 Posterior covariance:
        Σ_post = (Aᵀ Σ_obs⁻¹ A + diag(1/λ_i))⁻¹
    4.4 Posterior mean:
        μ_post = Σ_post · Aᵀ Σ_obs⁻¹ · F_obs
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 5 – Draw samples from posterior for uncertainty propagation
--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 For j = 1 … N_draw:
        Sample c^(j) ~ N( μ_post , Σ_post )
        Store c^(j)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 6 – Reconstruct spectra and compute statistics
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 For each draw j:
        S_j(λ) = μ(λ) + Σ_{i=1}^{K_pca} c^(j)_i · PC_basis[i , λ]
6.2 After all draws:
        S_mean(λ)  = mean over j of S_j(λ)
        S_CI_low(λ)= percentile 2.5% over j of S_j(λ)
        S_CI_high(λ)= percentile 97.5% over j of S_j(λ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Output
------
Return S_mean, S_CI_low, S_CI_high, and optionally the collection {c^(j)} or
any diagnostic quantities required for further analysis.
