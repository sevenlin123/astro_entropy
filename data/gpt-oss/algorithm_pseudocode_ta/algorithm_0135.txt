Input:
    • Training spectra matrix X_train [N_train × N_wavelength]
    • Filter set F = {f_j(λ)} for j = 1 … N_filter
    • Observed photometry vector y_obs [N_filter] and covariance C_obs [N_filter × N_filter]
    • Desired number of principal components k (e.g., k = 8)

Output:
    • Posterior mean latent vector μ_z [k]
    • Posterior covariance Σ_z [k × k]
    • Sampled reconstructed spectra {x_samp_m} (m = 1 … M)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 0 – Pre‑processing of training spectra
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Compute mean spectrum μ_X = mean(X_train, axis=0).
2.  Center spectra:  X_centered = X_train - μ_X.
3.  Compute covariance matrix S = (X_centeredᵀ · X_centered) / (N_train – 1).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 – Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  Perform eigendecomposition:  S = U Λ Uᵀ.
5.  Select first k eigenvectors:  PCs = U[:, 0:k]          # shape [N_wavelength × k]
6.  Compute eigenvalues:          λ_k = Λ[0:k].

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 2 – Project training spectra into latent space
--------------------------------------------------------------------
7.  z_train = X_centered · PCs      # shape [N_train × k]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 3 – Forward model of photometry in latent space
--------------------------------------------------------------------
8.  For each filter j = 1 … N_filter:
        a.  Compute filter‑integrated mean flux:
                μ_fj = ∫ μ_X(λ) * f_j(λ) dλ
        b.  Compute filter‑integrated PC contributions:
                For i = 1 … k:
                    p_ij = ∫ PCs(λ, i) * f_j(λ) dλ
        c.  Store vector p_j = [p_1j, …, p_kj]ᵀ   # shape [k]
        d.  Store scalar μ_fj

9.  Assemble design matrix A of shape [N_filter × k]:
        A[j, i] = p_ij

10.  Assemble mean flux vector b of length N_filter:
        b[j] = μ_fj

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 4 – Bayesian inference of latent vector z
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# Assume Gaussian prior on z:  z ~ N(0, I)
# Observation model:  y_obs = A · z + b + ε ,   ε ~ N(0, C_obs)

11.  Compute posterior covariance:
        Σ_z = (Aᵀ · C_obs⁻¹ · A + I)⁻¹            # shape [k × k]

12.  Compute posterior mean:
        μ_z = Σ_z · Aᵀ · C_obs⁻¹ · (y_obs – b)     # shape [k]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 5 – Sampling reconstructed spectra
--------------------------------------------------------------------
13.  For m = 1 … M:
        a.  Sample latent vector:
                z_sample ~ N(μ_z, Σ_z)
        b.  Reconstruct spectrum:
                x_sample = μ_X + PCs · z_sample        # shape [N_wavelength]
        c.  Store x_sample

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Return:
    μ_z, Σ_z, {x_sample_m}
