μ(λ) ← mean over rows of S

S_c ← S – μ

C ← S_c × V[1..k]        // N_spec × k matrix of PC coefficients

B_j ← ∫ R_j(λ) · μ(λ) dλ

G_j,i ← ∫ R_j(λ) · V_i(λ) dλ

p̂_j(a) = B_j + Σ_{i=1}^{k} a_i · G_j,i

D ← diag(σ_p^2)          // noise covariance (N_f × N_f)

Σ_post ← (Λ^{-1} + Gᵀ D^{-1} G)^{-1}

r ← p – B                  // residual vector of length N_f
   ā  ← Σ_post · (Gᵀ D^{-1} r)

for s = 1 to N_samp:
    a_s ← draw from N(ā, Σ_post)

s_s(λ) ← μ(λ) + Σ_{i=1}^{k} a_s[i] · V_i(λ)

s_hat(λ) ← mean over samples of s_s(λ)

CI_lower(λ) ← percentile(s_s(λ), 2.5)
   CI_upper(λ) ← percentile(s_s(λ), 97.5)

train PCA ← Step 1
build G, B ← Step 2
compute ā, Σ_post ← Step 3
sample a_s ← Step 4
reconstruct s_s(λ) ← Step 5
aggregate to s_hat & CI ← Step 6
return s_hat, CI
