INPUT
    Training spectra {S_j(λ_k)}   j = 1 … M        (spectra sampled at K wavelengths)
    Filter response curves {R_i(λ_k)}  i = 1 … NF
    New photometric measurements {y_i , σ_i}      (fluxes and uncertainties)

PARAMETERS
    Npc  ← number of principal components to retain  (e.g. 8–10)
    (optionally) prior covariance Λ for coefficients

OUTPUT
    Reconstructed spectrum  Ŝ(λ_k)
    Credible‑interval envelope  CI(λ_k)  (e.g. 95 %)
    Posterior mean coefficient vector  μ_c
    Posterior covariance  Σ_c

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 – Build the spectral PCA basis
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Compute mean spectrum μ(λ_k) = (1/M) Σ_j S_j(λ_k)
1.2  Center spectra: X_jk = S_jk – μ_k          for all j,k
1.3  Compute covariance C = (Xᵀ X) / (M – 1)
1.4  Eigendecompose C → eigenvalues {λ_t} and eigenvectors {v_t}
1.5  Order eigenvectors by decreasing λ_t
1.6  Retain first Npc eigenvectors → form basis matrix
         B(λ_k , t) = v_t(k)           for t = 1 … Npc
         (each column of B is a principal component)
1.7  Store explained variances λ_t   (t=1…Npc)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 – Forward model: project basis into photometry space
--------------------------------------------------------------------
For each filter i = 1 … NF
    2.1  Compute mean flux in filter:
            m_i = Σ_k μ_k * R_i(k) / Σ_k R_i(k)
    2.2  For each component t = 1 … Npc
            f_it = Σ_k B(k,t) * R_i(k) / Σ_k R_i(k)
Store:
    m  ← vector of length NF   (mean photometry)
    F  ← NF × Npc matrix with entries f_it

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 – Bayesian inference of component coefficients
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Given observed photometry y_i and errors σ_i:

3.1  Assemble observation vector y  (length NF)
3.2  Observation covariance:
        Σ_y = diag(σ_1², …, σ_NF²)

3.3  Prior on coefficients:
        p(c) ~ N(0 , Λ)          where Λ = diag(λ_1,…,λ_Npc)
        (If prior regularization desired, replace Λ with scaled version)

3.4  Compute posterior covariance:
        Σ_c = ( Fᵀ Σ_y⁻¹ F + Λ⁻¹ )⁻¹

3.5  Compute posterior mean:
        μ_c = Σ_c  Fᵀ Σ_y⁻¹ ( y – m )

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 – Spectrum reconstruction
--------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Reconstructed spectrum:
        Ŝ(λ_k) = μ_k  +  Σ_{t=1}^{Npc} μ_c(t) * B(k,t)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 – Uncertainty propagation (credible intervals)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Draw N_samples draws from N( μ_c , Σ_c )
5.2  For each draw s:
        Ŝ_s(λ_k) = μ_k + Σ_t s(t) * B(k,t)
5.3  For each wavelength λ_k:
        Compute percentiles of {Ŝ_s(λ_k)} across samples
        Set 95 % credible interval CI_lower(k), CI_upper(k)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 – (Optional) Outlier flagging
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Evaluate marginal likelihood of y under the model:
        p(y | data) = ∫ N(y | m + F c , Σ_y) N(c | 0 , Λ) dc
                 = N( y | m , Σ_y + F Λ Fᵀ )
6.2  Compare log‑likelihood to distribution of training likelihoods
        Flag object if likelihood below threshold (e.g., bottom 5 %)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
RETURN
    Ŝ(λ_k)            – mean reconstructed spectrum
    CI_lower(k), CI_upper(k) – credible‑interval envelope
    μ_c, Σ_c          – posterior coefficient statistics
    (optional) flag – outlier status
