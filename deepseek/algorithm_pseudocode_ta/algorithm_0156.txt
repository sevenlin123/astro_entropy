**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Precompute Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Train PCA on a sample of near-IR spectra to obtain principal components and loadings.

2. **For Each Object:**
   - **Input:** Photometric measurements of the object.
   - **Express Photometry in PCA Space:**
     - Project photometric data onto PCA loadings to form an observation vector in the latent space.
   
   - **Bayesian Inference:**
     - Define a likelihood function relating latent variables (principal components) to photometric data.
     - Incorporate measurement uncertainties from photometric data into the likelihood.
     - Perform MCMC or Bayesian sampling to obtain posterior distributions of latent variables.
   
   - **Reconstruct Spectrum:**
     - Use posterior means of latent variables and PCA loadings to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   
   - **Uncertainty Propagation:**
     - Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum using the variance in the posterior distributions of latent variables.
   
   - **Output:** Reconstructed spectrum with propagated uncertainties.

**End For Each Object**

**Note:** This algorithm assumes precomputed PCA and processes each object independently.
