```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input: 
- Training dataset of near-infrared spectra
- Photometric observations for objects of interest

Output:
- Reconstructed spectra and associated uncertainties

Steps:
1. Initialize:
   - Preprocess training data to train PCA model capturing spectral variability.

2. For each object:
   a. Normalize photometric measurements.
   b. Project normalized data into PCA latent space.
   c. Perform MCMC sampling of latent variables considering photometric uncertainties.
   d. Compute posterior predictive distribution.
   e. Transform posterior mean of latent variables back to spectral space.
   f. Calculate uncertainty propagation.

3. Store reconstructed spectra and uncertainties.

Return: Reconstructed spectra and their uncertainties.
```
