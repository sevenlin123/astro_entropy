**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**

1. **Input Data:**
   - Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
   - Pre-trained Principal Component (PC) basis derived from a sample of near-IR spectra.

2. **Training Phase (Not Shown in Pseudocode):**
   - Compute Principal Components (PCA) from a sample of known near-IR spectra to establish the latent space.

3. **Model Setup:**
   - Represent each spectrum in the latent space using the pre-computed PCs.
   - Define a probabilistic model where the latent variables (PC coefficients) follow a multivariate normal distribution.
   - Model the mapping from latent space to observed photometric space.

4. **Bayesian Inference:**
   a. Prior Distribution:
      - Assume a zero-mean multivariate Gaussian prior for the PC coefficients with covariance matrix Σ.
   
   b. Likelihood Function:
      - Express the observed photometric measurements as a linear combination of the latent PC coefficients.
      - Model the uncertainty propagation from the latent space to the photometric space.
   
   c. Posterior Calculation:
      - Use Bayes' theorem to compute the posterior distribution of the PC coefficients given the photometric measurements.
   
   d. Parameter Estimation:
      - Estimate the posterior mean and covariance of the latent variables.
      - Propagate these estimates back to the original spectral space.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   a. Transform the estimated latent variables into the original spectral space using the inverse PCA transformation.
   b. Adjust for uncertainties and correlations between wavelengths.

6. **Output:**
   - Reconstructed spectrum for each TNO.
   - Associated uncertainty estimates.

This algorithm performs Bayesian inference in the reduced latent space to reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric data while accounting for measurement uncertainties.
