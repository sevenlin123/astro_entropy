**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Preprocessing**
   - Gather near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. **Dimensionality Reduction**
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training set.
   - Extract principal components (PCs) that capture the significant spectral variability (approximately 4-5 PCs for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

3. **Probabilistic Model Definition**
   - Establish a Bayesian model where photometric flux measurements are linked via latent PCs.
   - Define prior distributions over the latent variables.

4. **Inference**
   - Utilize Bayesian inference methods (e.g., Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational inference) to estimate latent variables and their uncertainties.

5. **Posterior Processing**
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum using the estimated latent variables.
   - Propagate uncertainties to assess empirical credible-interval coverage (~95%).

**End Algorithm**
