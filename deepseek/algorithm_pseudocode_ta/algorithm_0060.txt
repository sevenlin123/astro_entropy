**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Preprocess Photometric Data**
   - Convert raw photometric observations into calibrated flux densities.
   
2. **Train PCA Model**
   - Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on a reference set of near-infrared spectra to derive basis vectors.
   
3. **Express Spectrum in Latent Space**
   - Represent each object's spectrum as a linear combination of the PCA basis vectors.
   
4. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**
   - Use photometric constraints to perform Bayesian inference, obtaining posterior distributions of the PCA component weights.
   
5. **Reconstruct Full Spectrum**
   - Utilize the inferred PCA weights to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   
6. **Propagate Uncertainties**
   - Assess the reliability of the reconstructed spectrum by propagating measurement uncertainties through the model.
