**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess Training Data**
   - **Input**: Collection of near-IR spectra of TNOs.
   - **Output**: Normalized spectra ready for PCA.
     - **Step 1a**: Remove outliers or corrupted spectra from the training set.
     - **Step 1b**: Normalize each spectrum such that each band has unit variance.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - **Input**: Preprocessed spectra.
   - **Output**: Set of principal components.
     - **Step 2a**: Compute the covariance matrix of the training spectra.
     - **Step 2b**: Determine the principal components up to the required number (e.g., 10 PCs).

3. **Probabilistic Model Training**
   - **Input**: Principal components derived from PCA.
   - **Output**: Trained Bayesian model.
     - **Step 3a**: Develop a Bayesian model using the principal components as latent variables to represent the spectral manifold.
     - **Step 3b**: Train the model to infer latent variables from photometric data.

4. **Bayesian Inference for Each Object**
   - **Input**: Photometric measurements for an object.
   - **Output**: Posterior distributions of latent variables.
     - **Step 4a**: For each object, input its photometric data into the Bayesian model.
     - **Step 4b**: Perform Bayesian inference (e.g., MCMC sampling) to estimate the posterior distributions of the latent variables.

5. **Spectrum Reconstruction**
   - **Input**: Posterior distributions of latent variables.
   - **Output**: Reconstructed spectrum.
     - **Step 5a**: Multiply the estimated latent variable coefficients by their corresponding PC eigenvectors.
     - **Step 5b**: Sum the contributions to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.

6. **Uncertainty Propagation**
   - **Input**: Posterior distributions of latent variables.
   - **Output**: Uncertainties for the reconstructed spectrum.
     - **Step 6a**: Propagate the uncertainties from the latent variable posteriors to the reconstructed spectrum.

**Output**: The reconstructed spectrum along with its associated uncertainties.
