**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Precompute Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Input: Training set of near-IR spectra
   - Compute PCA eigenvectors (latent space basis vectors) and corresponding eigenvalues.
   - Output: PCA basis vectors (principal components) and associated eigenvalues.

2. **Bayesian Inference in PCA Space**
   - For each object:
     a. Obtain sparse photometric measurements.
     b. Represent the object's spectrum as a linear combination of PCA basis vectors: \( S = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i PC_i \), where \( w_i \) are coefficients.
     c. Define a probabilistic model linking photometric data to PCA coefficients.
     d. Perform Bayesian inference to estimate posterior distributions of \( w_i \).

3. **Spectrum Reconstruction**
   - Using posterior means/medians of \( w_i \):
     - Reconstruct the spectrum: \( \hat{S} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathbb{E}[w_i] PC_i \).
     - Calculate uncertainties based on posterior variances.

4. **Uncertainty Propagation**
   - Employ Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to explore the parameter space of \( w_i \).
   - Quantify the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the uncertainties from the MCMC samples.

5. **Model Evaluation**
   - Apply leave-one-out cross-validation (optional, but recommended for model assessment).

This algorithm efficiently reconstructs full spectra from sparse photometric data while quantifying uncertainties through Bayesian inference and MCMC sampling.
