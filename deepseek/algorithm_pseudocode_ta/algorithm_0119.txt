The algorithm outlines the core computational steps for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction (PSR), focusing on Bayesian inference in a PCA-derived latent space to reconstruct spectra from sparse photometric data.

```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction (PSR)

// Training Phase
1. Preprocess reference spectra:
   a. Compute mean `m` of the training spectra.
   b. Center the spectra by subtracting `m`.
   c. Calculate the covariance matrix `C` from the centered data.
   d. Decompose `C` into eigenvectors `V` (loadings) and eigenvalues.

// Object Processing
for each object:
   a. Obtain sparse photometric data `F` and its uncertainties.
   b. Normalize `F` to get feature vector.
   c. Bayesian inference:
      i. Posterior mean `c_hat = (V' * F) / sigma^2`
      ii. Compute posterior covariance `Sigma_c = inverse(V * V' / sigma^2 + I / tau^2)`
   d. Reconstruct spectrum `S = m + V * c_hat`
   e. Compute uncertainty in `S`: `spectrum_uncertainty = sqrt(diag(V * Sigma_c * V'))`
   f. Output `S` and `spectrum_uncertainty`

Output: Reconstructed spectra and their uncertainties for each object.
```
