**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Input:** Set of reference spectra \( S \).
   - **Output:** Principal Component (PC) basis vectors \( V \) and explained variance \( \Sigma \).
   - **Steps:**
     1. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on \( S \) to obtain \( V \) and \( \Sigma \).

2. **Inference Phase:**
   - **For each target object:**
     1. **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements \( M \).
     2. **Projection into PCA Space:**
        - Map \( M \) into the PCA latent space using the precomputed basis \( V \).
     3. **Bayesian Inference:**
        - Use the latent space projection to estimate the principal components' coefficients.
        - Obtain posterior distributions of the latent variables by modeling their distribution based on \( M \).
     4. **Spectrum Reconstruction:**
        - Reconstruct the full spectrum using the inferred coefficients and the PCA basis \( V \).
     5. **Uncertainty Propagation:**
        - Transfer uncertainties from the posterior distributions to the reconstructed spectrum, ensuring accurate uncertainty estimation.

**End Algorithm**
