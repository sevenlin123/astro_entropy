```
Initialize:
    Load training spectra into dataset D_train
    Perform PCA on D_train to obtain PCA basis V and mean vector μ
    Set number of principal components k

For each object:
    Retrieve observed photometric magnitudes m_obs and their uncertainties σ
    Create data structure S containing m_obs and σ
    Project S into latent space using V:
        Solve V * z ≈ S to obtain latent variable estimates z
    Perform Bayesian inference on latent variables z:
        Compute posterior distribution p(z | S, σ) considering measurement uncertainties
    Reconstruct full spectrum:
        f_hat = μ + V * E[z | S, σ]
    Compute confidence intervals for f_hat:
        For each wavelength λ, calculate the standard deviation of the posterior predictive distribution

Output:
    Reconstructed spectra f_hat and their confidence intervals for all objects
```
