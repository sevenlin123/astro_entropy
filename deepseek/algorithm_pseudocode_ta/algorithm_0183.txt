**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Load Training Data Set**:
   - Read in a collection of near-infrared (NIR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. **Preprocess Data**:
   - Normalize the spectral data to ensure all spectra are on a comparable scale.
   - Remove any outliers or anomalies in the training data set.

3. **Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Train a PCA model on the preprocessed spectral data.
   - Extract principal components to capture the majority of spectral variability.

4. **Build Latent-Space Model**:
   - Develop a probabilistic framework that maps sparse photometric observations into the latent space defined by the PCA.
   - Establish a forward model linking photometric measurements to latent variables.

5. **Process Each Test Object**:
   - **a. Obtain Sparse Photometric Measurements**: Retrieve available photometric data for the object.
   - **b. Map Photometry to Latent Space**: Use the pre-trained PCA model to transform photometric data into latent space.
   - **c. Perform Bayesian Inference**: Utilize Bayesian methods to compute posterior distributions of latent variables given photometric measurements.
   - **d. Propagate Uncertainties**: Transfer uncertainties from the latent space back to the spectral space.
   - **e. Reconstruct Full Spectrum**: Generate reconstructed spectra based on posterior means of latent variables.

6. **Identify Optimal NIRCam Filters**:
   - Evaluate the information content of various JWST/NIRCam filters.
   - Determine optimally informative filter combinations for future observations.

7. **Detect Outliers and Rare Spectral Types**:
   - Apply the framework to identify objects with photometry conforming to low-probability intermediate models.
   - Reconstruct and constrain spectra of rare types by allowing exploration of less probable latent states.

**End Algorithm**
