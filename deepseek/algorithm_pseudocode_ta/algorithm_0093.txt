Algorithm SpectralReconstruction(objectCount):
    Read referenceSpectra
    ComputePCA(referenceSpectra) to get V (eigenvectors), lambda (eigenvalues), and mean_spectrum
    For each object:
        Read photometric data F and uncertainties U
        Normalize F → F_normalized
        latent_params = F_normalized × V'
        BayesianInference(latent_params, U, V, lambda, mean_spectrum) → y_opt
        Reconstructed_spectrum = mean_spectrum + V × y_opt
        ComputeUncertainties(Reconstructed_spectrum, U)
        Output Reconstructed_spectrum and uncertainties
