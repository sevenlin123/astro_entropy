**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction using Bayesian PCA**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - Train PCA on a sample of near-infrared spectra to extract principal components (PCs).

2. **For Each Object:**
   - **Input:** Observed photometric magnitudes.
   - **Transform:** Represent the object's spectrum using PCA coefficients.
   - **Inference:** Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to estimate coefficients from photometric data.
   - **Reconstruction:** Reconstruct the full spectrum using the inferred PCA coefficients.
   - **Uncertainty:** Propagate photometric measurement uncertainties to assess spectral reconstruction reliability.

**End Algorithm**
