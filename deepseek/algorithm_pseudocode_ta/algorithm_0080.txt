### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Preprocess the near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects into a dataset.
2. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the dataset to derive principal components capturing the main spectral variations.
3. For the object of interest:
   a. Use its sparse photometric measurements.
   b. Project these measurements into the PCA space.
   c. Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA space to estimate the underlying spectral components.
4. Reconstruct the full spectrum using the estimated PCA coefficients.
   a. Propagate the uncertainties from the Bayesian inference to the reconstructed spectrum.
