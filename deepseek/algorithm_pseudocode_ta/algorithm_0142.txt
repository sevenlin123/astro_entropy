**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data Preparation**
   - Obtain the sparse photometric measurements of the trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Collect a reference sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra for training.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Perform PCA on the reference sample of near-IR spectra.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

3. **Construct Spectral Manifold**
   - Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs using the principal component representation obtained from PCA.

4. **Bayesian Inference Setup**
   - Define the Bayesian model to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data.
   - Incorporate uncertainties in photometric measurements into the inference process.

5. **Mapping Photometry to Spectral Space**
   - Use a linear transformation to map the observed photometric magnitudes into the reduced spectral space defined by the principal components.

6. **Reconstruct Spectra**
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced spectral space to obtain posterior distributions of the spectral parameters.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometry to the reconstructed spectra.

7. **Output Reconstructed Spectra and Uncertainties**
   - Generate the reconstructed full spectra along with their associated uncertainties for each TNO.

8. **End**

This algorithm outlines the core computational steps for reconstructing near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects using sparse photometric data, leveraging PCA and Bayesian inference in the reduced spectral space.
