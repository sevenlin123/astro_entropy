**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Step 1: Preprocessing**
- Convert raw spectral measurements into detrended and normalized flux densities.
  - Remove any linear trends in the spectral data.
  - Normalize flux densities to a common scale (e.g., relative to a reference spectrum).

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**
- Perform PCA on a reference set of near-IR spectra.
  - Input: Reference spectra dataset.
  - Output: Principal Component (PC) coefficients, loadings, and variance explained by each PC.
  - Number of PCs selected: Approximately 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity.

**Step 3: Bayesian Inference in PCA Latent Space**
- Given sparse photometric observations, perform Bayesian inference to estimate the latent PC coefficients.
  - Input: Photometric data (sparse measurements).
  - Prior: Gaussian distribution with mean 0 and variance determined by PCA training.
  - Likelihood: Probabilistic model linking photometric measurements to the latent PC coefficients.
  - Output: Posterior distributions of the latent variables (e.g., means and variances).

**Step 4: Spectral Reconstruction**
- Project the estimated latent variables back into the original spectral space.
  - Multiply the latent PC coefficients by the corresponding eigenvectors obtained from PCA training.
  - Sum the contributions from all PCs to reconstruct the full spectrum.

**Step 5: Uncertainty Quantification**
- Compute uncertainties on the reconstructed spectrum using the posterior variances of the latent variables.
  - Propagate uncertainties from the latent space to the spectral space.
  - Output: Standard deviations or confidence intervals for each wavelength.

**End Algorithm**
