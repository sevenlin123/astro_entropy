Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- TrainingSpectra: Collection of known TNO spectra (flux units)
- PhotometricData: Sparse photometric measurements (flux and uncertainty)
- NumComponents: Number of principal components (e.g., 4)
- FilterFunctions: Transmission curves of each photometric band

Output:
- ReconstructedSpectrum: Probabilistic full spectrum
- Uncertainty: Estimated uncertainties

Steps:

1. **PCA Training**
   a. Normalize TrainingSpectra by z-score: (F - mean)/std.
   b. Compute covariance matrix C of normalized spectra.
   c. Diagonalize C to get eigenvalues λ and eigenvectors V.
   d. Sort eigenvalues in descending order; select top NumComponents eigenvectors as PCs.
   e. Project each training spectrum onto PCs to form latent representations.

2. **Precompute Filter Contributions**
   a. For each photometric band j:
      i. Compute the inner product between each PC and the band's filter function T_j(λ):
         A_jk = ∫ PC_k(λ) * T_j(λ) dλ.
   b. Construct matrix A where rows correspond to bands and columns to PCs.

3. **Set Up Bayesian Model**
   a. Define latent variables θ as coefficients of PCs.
   b. Assume θ ~ N(0, τ²I), where τ is a scaling factor for the prior variance.
   c. For each band j, standardized observation y_j = F_j / σ_j.
   d. Likelihood: y ~ N(A * θ, I), assuming noise is independent after standardization.

4. **Perform Bayesian Inference**
   a. Initialize MCMC sampler with appropriate prior distributions.
   b. For each band j:
      i. Use A_j (row j of A) and y_j to update the posterior of θ.
   c. Sample θ from the posterior distribution.

5. **Reconstruct Spectrum from Posterior**
   a. For each sample of θ, compute the spectrum:
      S(λ) = Σ (θ_k * PC_k(λ)).
   b. Compute the posterior mean θ̂ of latent variables.
   c. Reconstruct mean spectrum: S̄(λ) = Σ (θ̂_k * PC_k(λ)).
   d. Optionally smooth S̄(λ) to reduce noise.

6. **Compute Uncertainty in Reconstructed Spectrum**
   a. Compute posterior covariance matrix of θ: Cov(θ) = Var(θ).
   b. Transform Cov(θ) via matrix A^T to get the covariance in the spectrum space:
      Cov_S = A * Cov(θ) * A^T.
   c. Extract standard deviations from Cov_S to get per-wavelength uncertainties.

7. **Output Results**
   a. Combine S̄(λ) with its uncertainties to form the final ReconstructedSpectrum.
   b. Store or display the result.

End Algorithm
