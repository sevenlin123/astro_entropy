**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Inputs:**  
- Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO object  
- Pre-trained PCA model (principal components derived from a training set of near-IR spectra)  

**Outputs:**  
- Reconstructed spectrum with uncertainties  

**Steps:**  

1. **Preprocess the photometric data:**  
   - Handle missing values (e.g., interpolate or impute).  
   - Normalize/standardize the measurements to match the scale of the PCA model.  

2. **Project photometric data into PCA latent space:**  
   - Map the normalized photometric measurements onto the principal components derived from the training set.  

3. **Perform Bayesian inference in the latent space:**  
   - Define a probabilistic model connecting the latent principal components to the observed photometric data.  
   - Specify a prior distribution over the principal components.  
   - Compute the posterior distribution of the principal components given the photometric data, incorporating measurement uncertainties.  

4. **Reconstruct the full spectrum:**  
   - Use the posterior mean (or mode) of the latent variables to invert the PCA transformation and reconstruct the original spectrum.  

5. **Propagate uncertainties:**  
   - Quantify the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the uncertainties from the photometric measurements through the Bayesian inference process.  

6. **Output the reconstructed spectrum and uncertainties:**  
   - Provide the reconstructed spectrum along with its associated uncertainties.  

**Note:** Preprocessing may include tasks like interpolation or scaling. Bayesian inference might employ MCMC or variational methods.
