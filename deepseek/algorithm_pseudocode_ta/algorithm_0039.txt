**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry**

**Step 1: Preprocess Photometric Data**
- **Action:** Normalize and format sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
- **Details:** Convert raw photometric data into a standardized format suitable for PCA analysis.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**
- **Action:** Train a PCA model on a sample of near-IR spectra.
- **Details:** Use a curated sample of near-IR spectra from TNOs to construct the PCA model, capturing the essential spectral variability.

**Step 3: Dimensionality Reduction**
- **Action:** Project the spectral data onto the PCA basis.
- **Details:** Transform the original high-dimensional spectral data into a low-dimensional PCA space, retaining the dominant modes of variability.

**Step 4: Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**
- **Action:** Perform Bayesian inference using the reduced PCA representation.
- **Details:** Utilize the PCA basis to model the spectral manifold, incorporating prior knowledge and observed photometric data to infer the underlying spectral parameters.

**Step 5: Measurement Uncertainty Propagation**
- **Action:** Quantify and propagate uncertainties through the Bayesian model.
- **Details:** Incorporate measurement errors from photometric data into the Bayesian framework to estimate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

**Step 6: Credible Interval Calculation**
- **Action:** Compute credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.
- **Details:** Determine the uncertainty ranges around the reconstructed spectra, ensuring proper coverage according to Bayesian inference.

**Step 7: Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**
- **Action:** Validate the model using LOOCV.
- **Details:** Assess the model's performance by predicting each spectrum while leaving one out, evaluating reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty calibration.

**Step 8: Information Content Analysis**
- **Action:** Apply the framework to identify optimally informative filter configurations.
- **Details:** Evaluate the information content of JWST/NIRCam filters to guide future observations for accurate TNO taxonomy.

**Step 9: Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Reconstruction**
- **Action:** Implement a procedure to detect outliers and reconstruct rare spectral types.
- **Details:** Allow constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models, enabling the detection of unusual spectral types like peculiar Neptune Trojans.

**Step 10: Final Spectral Reconstruction**
- **Action:** Combine all components to produce the final reconstructed spectra.
- **Details:** Integrate the Bayesian inference, uncertainty propagation, and outlier detection steps to deliver the final spectral reconstructions, bridging sparse photometry and spectroscopy for TNO compositional mapping.
