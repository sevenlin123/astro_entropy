**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Principal Component Model:**
   - Input: Collection of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Compute principal component (PC) decomposition using a dimensionality reduction technique (e.g., PCA).
   - Determine the number of PCs (select ~4-5 for taxonomic classification; ~8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

2. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space:**
   - Input: Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO.
   - Represent the spectrum in terms of the precomputed PC basis.
   - Perform Bayesian inference to estimate the coefficients of the PCs, incorporating prior knowledge and measurement uncertainties.
   - Output: Reconstructed full spectrum and associated uncertainties.

3. **Quantify Information Content of Filters:**
   - For each photometric filter (e.g., JWST/NIRCam):
     - Project the filter response onto the PC basis.
     - Compute the explained variance or information content.
     - Rank filters based on their contribution to spectral reconstruction.

4. **Detect Rare Spectral Types:**
   - For each object, compute its position on the spectral manifold using the estimated PC coefficients.
   - Compare the probability density of the object's position against the distribution of known spectral types.
   - Identify objects with significantly lower probabilities as rare spectral types.

**End Algorithm**
