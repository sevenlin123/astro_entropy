```python
Read training spectra.

Compute mean, center, covariance.

Diagonalize to get PCA eigenvectors and eigenvalues.

Select K eigenvectors based on cumulative explained variance.

For each TNO:
    Read photometric fluxes F.
    Project F onto PCA basis.
    Perform Bayesian inference to compute posterior distribution of latent variables.
    Reconstruct spectrum using posterior latent variables.
    Compute uncertainties by propagating variance through the transformation.

Output reconstructed spectra and uncertainties.
```
