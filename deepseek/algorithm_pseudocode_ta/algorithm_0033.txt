**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input**: 
   - Training data: Set of near-IR spectra of known TNOs.
   - Photometric measurements: Sparse observations of target TNO.
   
2. **Preprocessing**:
   - **Step 2.1**: Align all spectra to a common wavelength grid.
   - **Step 2.2**: Normalize each spectrum to unit sum to eliminate multiplicative scaling factors.
   
3. **Model Development**:
   - **Step 3.1**: Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training spectra to extract principal components (PCs).
   - **Step 3.2**: Determine the optimal number of PCs (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction) using leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV).
   
4. **Spectral Manifold Representation**:
   - **Step 4.1**: Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs in the reduced PC space.
   
5. **Probabilistic Latent Space Modeling**:
   - **Step 5.1**: Establish a probabilistic model linking photometric measurements to the latent PC coefficients.
   
6. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - **Step 6.1**: Formulate a Bayesian model where photometric data is connected to latent variables (PC coefficients).
   - **Step 6.2**: Define priors based on the distribution of PC coefficients from the training data.
   - **Step 6.3**: Conduct Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to derive posterior distributions of the latent variables.
   
7. **Reconstruct Spectrum**:
   - **Step 7.1**: Utilize the posterior means of the latent variables to reconstruct the spectrum by projecting onto the PCA basis vectors.
   
8. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - **Step 8.1**: Assess uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum by evaluating the posterior variances of the latent variables and their impact on spectral variations.
   
9. **Output**:
   - **Step 9.1**: Provide the reconstructed spectrum along with uncertainties.
   - **Step 9.2**: Deliver probabilistic metrics for outlier detection and taxonomic classification.
