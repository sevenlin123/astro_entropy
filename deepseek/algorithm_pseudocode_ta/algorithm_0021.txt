**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Data Preparation:**
   - Compile a dataset of near-IR spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Normalize and preprocess the spectra to ensure consistency.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Perform PCA on the preprocessed spectral dataset.
   - Determine the number of principal components (PCs) to retain. Use 4-5 PCs for taxonomic classification and 8-10 PCs for high-fidelity reconstruction based on cross-validation results.

3. **Latent Space Construction:**
   - Represent each TNO's spectrum in the reduced PCA latent space.
   - Each point in this space corresponds to a linear combination of the selected principal components.

4. **Bayesian Inference Setup:**
   - Define the prior distribution over the latent variables based on the PCA model.
   - Establish the likelihood function that relates the latent space representation to the observed sparse photometric measurements.

5. **Sparse Photometric Data Input:**
   - Obtain sparse photometric data for the target TNO.
   - This data consists of measurements at specific wavelengths with associated uncertainties.

6. **Bayesian Inference for Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA latent space using the observed photometric data.
   - Update the latent variables to best match the photometric observations while propagating measurement uncertainties.
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational inference methods to sample the posterior distribution of the latent variables.

7. **Spectral Parameter Estimation:**
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum from the inferred latent variables.
   - Compute the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the uncertainties from the latent space.

8. **Validation and Uncertainty Calibration:**
   - Assess the empirical credible interval coverage of the reconstructed spectra across wavelengths.
   - Adjust the uncertainty estimates if necessary to ensure accurate coverage.

9. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Identification:**
   - Analyze the posterior distributions of the latent variables to identify unusual objects.
   - Apply techniques like anomaly detection or mode selection to constrain photometry and reconstruct rare spectral types.

10. **Output the Reconstructed Spectra and Uncertainties:**
    - Generate the reconstructed spectra along with their associated uncertainties for further analysis.

**End Algorithm**
