**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Photometry**

**Inputs:**
- Training Set: Collection of TNO spectra.
- Photometric Measurements: Sparse photometric data for the target object.

**Outputs:**
- Reconstructed Spectrum: Full spectrum of the target object.
- Uncertainty Estimates: Quantitative measures of uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum.

**Steps:**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   a. **Training Set Processing:** Load the training set of TNO spectra.
   b. **Centering Data:** Subtract the mean spectrum from all training spectra to center the data.
   c. **Covariance Matrix Computation:** Calculate the covariance matrix of the centered spectra.
   d. **Eigenvalue Decomposition:** Compute eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the covariance matrix. The eigenvectors represent the principal components (PCs).
   e. **Select Principal Components:** Based on variance explained, determine the number of PCs to use (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for higher fidelity).

2. **Spectral Manifold Modeling:**
   a. **Latent Space Construction:** Represent the spectra in the reduced PCA space.
   b. **Probabilistic Model Setup:** Define a probabilistic model in the PCA space, incorporating prior knowledge about spectral variability.

3. **Bayesian Inference:**
   a. **Likelihood Function:** Define the likelihood based on the relationship between photometric measurements and the latent PCA variables.
   b. **Prior Distributions:** Specify prior distributions for the PCA coefficients and other parameters based on the training data.
   c. **Inference Method:** Use Bayesian inference (e.g., Markov Chain Monte Carlo) to estimate posterior distributions of the latent variables given the photometric data.

4. **Reconstruction of Spectrum:**
   a. **Posterior Samples:** Obtain posterior samples of the latent PCA coefficients.
   b. **Spectrum Reconstruction:** Project the latent coefficients back into the original spectral space using the PCA eigenvectors.
   c. **Uncertainty Estimation:** Calculate the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the uncertainties from the posterior distributions of the latent variables.

5. **Output:**
   a. **Return Reconstructed Spectrum:** The full spectrum obtained from the most probable latent variables.
   b. **Return Uncertainty Estimates:** Quantitative measures of uncertainty at each wavelength.

**End Algorithm**
