**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Input:**
- Training Spectra: Collection of known spectral templates.
- Photometric Data: Observed fluxes for each object.
- Filter Transmission Curves: Define how each photometric band projects onto the spectrum.
- Noise Model: Uncertainty characteristics of photometric measurements.

**Output:**
- Reconstructed Spectra: Inferred spectra for each object.
- Uncertainty Quantification: Associated uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

**Steps:**

1. **Preprocess Training Spectra:**
   - Normalize spectra for consistency.

2. **Compute Principal Component Decomposition (PCA):**
   - Construct a matrix from training spectra.
   - Center the data by subtracting the mean.
   - Compute covariance matrix \( C = \frac{1}{N-1} X^T X \).
   - Find eigenvalues \( \lambda \) and eigenvectors \( V \) of \( C \).
   - Sort eigenvalues and eigenvectors in descending order of eigenvalues.
   - Select top \( K \) PCA components (e.g., \( K = 4 \) to 5).
   - Form PCA basis matrix \( PC = V[:, :K] \).

3. **For Each Object:**
   - Extract photometric data \( P \).
   - Compute the system relating photometric data to PCA coefficients:
     - \( P = FPC \times Z \), where \( FPC \) combines filter functions and PCA basis.
   - Assume a Gaussian prior for \( Z \): \( Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \tau^2 I) \).
   - Calculate posterior distribution of \( Z \) given \( P \):
     - Posterior mean \( Z_{hat} = ( \tau^{-2} I + FPC^T \Sigma^{-1} FPC )^{-1} ( FPC^T \Sigma^{-1} P ) \).
     - Posterior covariance \( Z_{cov} = ( \tau^{-2} I + FPC^T \Sigma^{-1} FPC )^{-1} \).
   - Reconstruct spectrum \( S_{hat} = PC \times Z_{hat} \).
   - Propagate uncertainty to compute uncertainty in \( S_{hat} \).

4. **Postprocessing:**
   - Compute credible intervals for reconstructed spectra.

**End Algorithm**
