**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Step 1: Preprocessing**
- Input: Near-IR spectra of a training set of TNOs.
- Output: Pre-trained principal component (PC) decomposition.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
- Decompose the training spectra into principal components.
- Result: Set of PCs capturing dominant spectral variability.

**Step 3: Obtain Photometric Data**
- Input: Observed photometric magnitudes for each test object in multiple bands.

**Step 4: Forward Model**
- For each test object:
    - **a. Spectral Representation**: Express the object's spectrum as a linear combination of pre-trained PCs.
    - **b. Flux Contribution**: Multiply each PC coefficient by the exposure time of the corresponding filter band.
    - **c. Total Flux**: Sum the flux contributions from all PCs.
    - **d. Measurement Uncertainty**: Add Gaussian noise to the computed fluxes.

**Step 5: Bayesian Inference**
- Use MCMC to estimate posterior distributions of PC coefficients.
- Input: Observed fluxes and noise model.
- Output: Posterior distributions representing reconstructed spectral components.

**Step 6: Spectrum Reconstruction**
- Project the estimated PC coefficients back into the original spectrum space.
- Output: Reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties.

**Step 7: Anomaly Detection**
- Compare observed photometric data likelihood against expected distribution.
- Detect outliers or peculiar spectral types based on consistency with inferred spectral models.

**End of Algorithm**
