**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**

**Step 1: Preprocessing**
1. Obtain sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
2. Collect a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of TNOs.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
1. Train a PCA model on the collected near-IR spectra.
2. Represent each spectrum using the principal components (PCs) obtained from the PCA.

**Step 3: Probabilistic Latent-Space Model Construction**
1. Define the probabilistic latent-space framework using the PCA-based representation.
2. Establish the spectral manifold in the reduced PCA space, capturing the variability of TNO spectra.

**Step 4: Bayesian Inference in PCA Space**
1. Perform Bayesian inference in the lower-dimensional PCA space to reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric data.
2. Propagate measurement uncertainties through the inference process.

**Step 5: Cross-Validation and Dimensionality Assessment**
1. Use leave-one-out cross-validation to determine the optimal number of principal components for spectral reconstruction.
   - Approximately 4–5 components suffice for taxonomic classification.
   - 8–10 components improve reconstruction fidelity and uncertainty calibration.

**Step 6: Spectral Reconstruction**
1. Reconstruct full spectra from the sparse photometric measurements using the Bayesian framework.
2. Ensure that the reconstructed spectra achieve empirical credible-interval coverage of ~95% across all wavelengths.

**Step 7: Survey Optimization**
1. Quantify the information content of JWST/NIRCam filters (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M).
2. Identify filter configurations that maximize the information for TNO taxonomy.

**Step 8: Outlier Detection and Reconstruction**
1. Allow constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the spectral manifold.
2. Detect and reconstruct rare spectral types, such as peculiar Neptune Trojans, using the probabilistic framework.

**Step 9: Final Output**
1. Generate statistically rigorous reconstructions of TNO spectra from sparse photometry.
2. Provide a tool to map compositional structures of minor planets in large-scale surveys.

**End Algorithm**
