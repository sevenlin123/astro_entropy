   - Incorporate photometric observations into the model as likelihood constraints.  

4. **Bayesian Inference**

   - Propagate uncertainties from photometric measurements through the model to reconstruct the full spectra.  
   - Sample the posterior predictive distribution to generate possible reconstructed spectra.
