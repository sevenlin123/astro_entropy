```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

Input: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs
Output: Reconstructed spectra with propagated uncertainties

1. Preprocessing:
   a. Normalize photometric measurements to unit variance.
   b. Train Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on a reference spectral library of TNOs to obtain Principal Components (PCs).

2. Bayesian Inference in Latent Space:
   a. Represent spectral variability using the obtained PCs.
   b. Perform Bayesian regression where photometric data informs the latent PC coefficients, incorporating photometric uncertainties as priors.
   c. Sample the posterior distribution of PC coefficients to estimate their uncertainties.

3. Spectral Reconstruction:
   a. For each TNO, project the inferred PC coefficients back onto the original spectral basis.
   b. Combine contributions from all PCs to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   c. Calculate uncertainties for each wavelength by propagating the uncertainties in PC coefficients.

4. Outlier Detection and Rare Type Identification:
   a. Compute posterior probabilities of each object within the latent space manifold.
   b. Identify objects with significantly low posterior probabilities as potential outliers or unique spectral types.

Return: Reconstructed spectra and their uncertainties for all TNOs.
```
