**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess Spectral Data**
   - Normalize and convert reference spectra into photometric bands matching the observations.
   
2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Train PCA on the normalized reference spectra to capture the dominant spectral variations.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for improved reconstruction).
   
3. **Construct Spectral Manifold in Latent Space**
   - Represent each spectrum as a linear combination of the selected principal components.
   
4. **Bayesian Inference**
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA-reduced latent space to estimate the posterior distribution of latent variables given sparse photometric measurements.
   
5. **Reconstruct Full Spectra**
   - Use the posterior means of the latent variables to reconstruct the full spectra.
   
6. **Propagate Uncertainties**
   - Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra by considering the variance in the latent space and the photometric measurement errors.
   
**End Algorithm**
