Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. Initialize PCA Model:
   a. Collect training set of known TNO spectra.
   b. Perform PCA on the training spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).

2. Define Probabilistic Model:
   a. Express photometric measurements as linear combinations of PCA basis vectors.
   b. Establish likelihood function relating photometry to latent spectral features.
   c. Assign prior distributions to PCA coefficients based on training data statistics.

3. Bayesian Inference via MCMC:
   a. For each TNO, input its photometric measurements.
   b. Use MCMC to sample posterior distributions of PCA coefficients given photometry.
   c. Generate chains until convergence criteria are met.

4. Reconstruct Spectra:
   a. Convert posterior PCA coefficients into full spectral estimates.
   b. Combine all components to form reconstructed spectra.

5. Propagate Uncertainties:
   a. Calculate uncertainties in PCA coefficients from MCMC samples.
   b. Compute uncertainty in reconstructed spectra via error propagation.

6. Cross-Validation:
   a. Perform leave-one-out CV to assess reconstruction accuracy and validate model.

7. Analyze Filter Information:
   a. Evaluate information content of NIRCam filters.
   b. Identify optimal filter combinations for maximizing spectral resolution.

8. Outlier Detection:
   a. Use constrained photometry to find low-probability spectral models.
   b. Detect unusual spectra indicative of unique taxonomic groups.

End Algorithm
