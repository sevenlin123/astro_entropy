**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Objective:**  
Reconstruct full near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects from sparse photometric measurements using a probabilistic latent-space framework.

**Input:**  
1. Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
2. Pre-trained principal component (PC) model from a sample of near-IR spectra.
3. Bayesian inference setup in the reduced principal component space.

**Output:**  
1. Reconstructed full spectrum for each object.
2. Measurement uncertainties propagated through the reconstruction.
3. Probabilistic classification of spectral types.

**Steps:**

1. **Principal Component Representation:**
   - Project sparse photometric measurements into the pre-trained principal component space.
   - Use approximately 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification.
   - Use 8-10 principal components for high-fidelity spectral reconstruction and uncertainty calibration.

2. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced principal component space.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the inference process.
   - Quantify the posterior distribution of the principal component coefficients.

3. **Spectral Manifold Modeling:**
   - Model the spectral manifold of TNOs using the learned principal components.
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the posterior distribution of the principal components back to the spectral space.

4. **Credible Interval Calculation:**
   - Compute empirical credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.
   - Ensure interval coverage of ~95% across wavelengths.

5. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Evaluate the information content of different JWST/NIRCam filter configurations.
   - Identify optimal filter combinations for future observations (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M).

6. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Reconstruction:**
   - Allow constrained photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the continuous topological manifold.
   - Detect and reconstruct rare spectral types like the peculiar Neptune Trojans (e.g., 2006~RJ103, 2011~SO277).

7. **Classification and Mapping:**
   - Classify reconstructed spectra into taxonomic categories based on the principal component model.
   - Map the compositional structure of minor planets for large-scale surveys.

**End of Algorithm**
