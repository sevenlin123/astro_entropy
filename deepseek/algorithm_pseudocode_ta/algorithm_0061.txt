**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data**
   - Sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Training sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - Perform PCA on the training sample of near-IR spectra.
   - Determine the number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

3. **Probabilistic Latent-Space Model**
   - Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs in the reduced PCA space.
   - Bayesian inference is performed in this latent space to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data.

4. **Reconstruct Full Spectra**
   - Using the latent-space model, reconstruct the full spectrum for each object.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from the photometric data through the reconstruction process.

5. **Output**
   - Reconstructed spectra for each TNO.
   - Associated uncertainties for each wavelength bin in the reconstructed spectra.

**End of Algorithm**
