**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocessing:**
   - Train a Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model on a dataset of near-infrared spectra.
     - Input: Training spectral library of TNOs.
     - Output: Principal components (PCs) with corresponding loadings.

2. **Forward Modeling:**
   - Define a probabilistic relationship between principal components and photometric magnitudes.
     - For each photometric band:
       - Photometric magnitude = Sum of (contributions from each PC) + Observation noise
       - Observation noise follows a Gaussian distribution.

3. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Set up a Bayesian model to infer posterior distributions of principal components given photometric data.
     - Priors: Typically Gaussian for PCs.
     - Likelihood: Defined by the forward model.
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to draw posterior samples of the PCs.
     - For each object:
       - Input: Observed photometric magnitudes and uncertainties.
       - Output: Posterior samples of principal components.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - Map the sampled principal components back to the original spectral space.
     - For each posterior sample of PCs:
       - Reconstruct the spectrum by applying the inverse PCA transformation.
     - Aggregate the posterior samples to form the reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties.

**End Algorithm**
