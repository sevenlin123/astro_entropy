Algorithm:
1. Precompute PCA from training spectra
   a. Read training spectra S
   b. Center S
   c. Compute covariance matrix C of S
   d. Diagonalize C to get eigenvalues λ and eigenvectors v
   e. Sort λ and v by descending order of λ
   f. Select top k eigenvectors and eigenvalues

2. For each object:
   a. Read photometric data P
   b. Perform Bayesian inference to estimate coefficients c using P and precomputed PCA
   c. Reconstruct spectrum R = mean(S) + sum(c_j * PCA_j)
   d. Output R
