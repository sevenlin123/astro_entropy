Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Training Phase:
   a. Input: Collection of near-IR spectra of TNOs.
   b. Compute Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the spectral dataset.
   c. Output: Set of principal components capturing major spectral variability.

2. Processing Each Object:
   a. For each TNO:
      i. Input: Sparse photometric measurements.
      ii. Project photometric measurements into PCA latent space.
      iii. Perform Bayesian inference in the latent space to reconstruct full spectrum.
      iv. Consider measurement uncertainties during reconstruction.

3. Uncertainty Propagation:
   a. For each reconstructed spectrum:
      i. Propagate photometric measurement uncertainties through Bayesian model.
      ii. Determine uncertainty ranges in reconstructed spectra.

Output: Reconstructed spectra with associated uncertainties for each TNO.
