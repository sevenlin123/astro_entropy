**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Step 1: Training the Model**
1. Collect a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
2. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the collected spectra to identify the dominant modes of spectral variability.
3. Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

**Step 2: Transforming Test Object's Sparse Photometry**
1. For each test object, obtain its sparse photometric measurements.
2. Project these photometric measurements into the latent space defined by the PCA components obtained in Step 1.

**Step 3: Bayesian Inference for Spectrum Reconstruction**
1. Use the latent space representation (from Step 2) as input to perform Bayesian inference.
2. In the reduced space, infer the most probable full spectrum by considering the posterior distribution of the latent variables.
3. Propagate measurement uncertainties from the sparse photometry through the Bayesian model to the reconstructed spectrum.

**Step 4: Output the Reconstructed Spectrum with Uncertainties**
1. Generate the reconstructed full spectrum for each test object.
2. Calculate and associate the propagated uncertainties for each wavelength in the reconstructed spectrum.

**Step 5: Optimize Survey Configurations**
1. Apply the trained framework to evaluate different JWST/NIRCam filter configurations.
2. Select filter combinations that maximize the information content for taxonomic classification and spectral reconstruction fidelity.
