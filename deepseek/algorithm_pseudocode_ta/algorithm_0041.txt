**Pseudocode Algorithm:**

```
Input: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs as vectors in specified filters.

1. Initialize Training Set:
   - Collect a dataset of near-IR spectra (training spectra) from known TNOs.

2. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA):
   - Compute PCA on the training spectra to derive principal components (PCs) and their loadings.

3. Determine Significant PCs:
   - Identify the number of significant PCs (k) based on analysis (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; up to 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

4. Bayesian Inference for Each Object:
   For each object:
   a. Obtain sparse photometric measurements.
   b. Project measurements into the latent PCA space.
   c. Conduct Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of spectral parameters in the latent space, considering measurement uncertainties.

5. Reconstruct Spectra:
   For each object:
   a. Use posterior distributions of latent variables (PC coefficients).
   b. Apply inverse PCA transformation to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   c. Propagate uncertainties to the reconstructed spectrum.

6. Analyze Filter Configurations:
   - Evaluate the information content of NIRCam filters (e.g., F090W, F115W, etc.).
   - Determine optimal filter combinations for taxonomic classification.

7. Outlier Detection:
   - Apply the framework to objects with constrained photometry.
   - Identify rare spectral types by detecting low-probability points in the spectral manifold.

8. Output:
   - Reconstructed spectra and uncertainties for each object.
   - Optimal filter configurations for surveys.
   - Identified outliers or rare spectral types.

End.
```
