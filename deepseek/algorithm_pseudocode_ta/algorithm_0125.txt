Initialize with the training set of spectral data.

Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training set to derive principal components.

For each TNO:

    Extract its photometric data.

    Project the photometric data onto the PCA space to obtain latent variables.

    Perform Bayesian inference using the latent variables to estimate their posterior distributions.

    Transform the posterior distributions back into the spectral domain.

    Compute the uncertainties (credible intervals) of the reconstructed spectrum.

Store the reconstructed spectra and their uncertainties.

The algorithm concludes by providing the reconstructed spectra and associated uncertainties for taxonomic classification and further analysis.
