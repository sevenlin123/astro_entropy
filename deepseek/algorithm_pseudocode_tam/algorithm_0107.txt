**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Step 1: Define the Training Spectral Library**
- **Input**: Reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 observed spectra).
- **Process**: Use Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to compute principal components.
- **Output**: Set of principal components (latent space basis vectors).

**Step 2: Construct the Latent Space**
- **Input**: Principal components from Step 1.
- **Process**: Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to estimate the probability distribution of latent coefficients.
- **Output**: Smoothed probability distribution in the latent space.

**Step 3: Generate Synthetic Photometry**
- **Input**: Augmented latent vectors from Step 2.
- **Process**: Forward model the spectra through JWST NIRCam filter curves to compute expected reflectance in each band.
- **Output**: Synthetic photometric measurements.

**Step 4: Inject Noise into Synthetic Photometry**
- **Input**: Synthetic photometric measurements.
- **Process**: Add Gaussian noise with 20% relative uncertainty.
- **Output**: Noisy photometric data for training.

**Step 5: Train AutoML Regressor**
- **Input**: Noisy photometric data and corresponding latent coefficients.
- **Process**: Use AutoGluon to train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees.
- **Output**: Trained encoder model mapping photometry to latent space.

**Step 6: Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**
- **Input**: Each of the 51 seed spectra.
- **Process**:
  1. Hold out one spectrum.
  2. Forward model its photometry.
  3. Inject noise.
  4. Pass through the trained encoder.
  5. Project latent coefficients through inverse PCA to reconstruct spectrum.
- **Output**: Reconstructed spectra and classification probabilities.

**Step 7: Evaluate Performance Metrics**
- **Metrics**:
  - Pearson correlation between reconstructed and true spectra.
  - Coverage of 1σ credible intervals across wavelengths.
  - LOOCV classification accuracy.

**Step 8: Optimize Filters for Information Content**
- **Input**: Filter contributions to latent coefficients.
- **Process**: Rank filters based on information gain.
- **Output**: Optimally selected filter configurations.

**Step 9: Detect Outliers**
- **Input**: Predicted latent states and true labels.
- **Process**: Identify deviations from the latent manifold.
- **Output**: Anomalies in spectral types.

**End Algorithm**
