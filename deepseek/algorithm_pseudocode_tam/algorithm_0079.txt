     - For each instrument filter band:
       - Compute the observed flux F by convolving S_synthetic with the filter's IRF.

6. Add Noise to Synthetic Photometry

7. Train AutoML Regressor (Encoder)

8. Train AutoML Classifier
   - Use AutoGluon to train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees classifier that maps latent coefficients to spectral types.

9. Inference for New Observations
   - Obtain a new photometric observation m_obs.

   - Apply the inverse PCA transformation to c to reconstruct the spectrum S_recon.
