Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Photometry

1. Preprocess seed spectra S:
   a. Normalize and center S by subtracting the mean spectrum.

2. Compute PCA on S:
   a. Calculate covariance matrix C of centered S.
   b. Decompose C into eigenvectors U and eigenvalues Λ.
   c. Sort U and Λ in descending order of eigenvalues.

3. Project S into PCA latent space:
   a. For each spectrum s in S, compute latent coefficients z = s' * U'.

4. Generate synthetic photometric data:
   a. For each z in latent space:
      i. Reconstruct spectrum s = z * U' + mean(S).
      ii. Convolve s with instrument filter responses to compute expected photometry m.
      iii. Add Gaussian noise to m to simulate realistic measurements.

5. Train AutoML regressor R:
   a. Use pairs (m_noisy, z) to train R to predict z from m.

6. For new photometric data M:
   a. For each m in M:
      i. Predict z_hat = R(m).
      ii. Reconstruct spectrum s_recon = z_hat * U' + mean(S).
      iii. Output s_recon.

Return reconstructed spectra S'.
