Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- Reference spectra of TNOs
- Photometric observations of target objects
- NIRCam filter definitions
- Training set of 51 observed spectra

Output:
- Reconstructed spectra
- Taxonomic classification probabilities

Training Phase:
1. Preprocess reference spectra:
   a. Normalize and align spectra to a common wavelength grid.
   
2. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA):
   a. Compute PCA on the normalized reference spectra.
   b. Determine top K principal components (e.g., K=10).
   
3. Generate latent coefficients:
   a. Project reference spectra onto PCA basis to obtain latent coefficients.
   
4. Data augmentation using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):
   a. Fit KDE to latent coefficient distributions.
   b. Sample synthetic latent coefficients from KDE.
   
5. Simulate photometric data:
   a. Convolve synthetic spectra with NIRCam filter responses.
   b. Add 20% Gaussian noise to simulate photometric uncertainties.
   
6. Prepare training data:
   a. Pair synthetic photometric data with corresponding latent coefficients.
   
7. Train AutoML regressor:
   a. Use ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees.
   b. Input: photometric data; Output: latent coefficients.
   
8. Train taxonomic classifier:
   a. Use AutoML to classify latent coefficients into taxonomic groups.
   
9. Validate models:
   a. Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV).
   b. Evaluate reconstruction accuracy and taxonomic classification.
   
Inference Phase:
1. For each target object:
   a. Extract photometric observations.
   
2. Predict latent coefficients:
   a. Pass photometric data through trained AutoML regressor.
   
3. Reconstruct spectrum:
   a. Apply inverse PCA transform to latent coefficients.
   
4. Classify taxonomic type:
   a. Use latent coefficients in taxonomic classifier.
   b. Obtain probability distribution over taxonomic classes.
   
5. Output results:
   a. Display reconstructed spectrum and taxonomic classification probabilities.
