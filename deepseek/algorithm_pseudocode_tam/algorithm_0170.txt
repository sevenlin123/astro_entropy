The algorithm implements a probabilistic framework to reconstruct TNO spectra from sparse photometry. It employs PCA to define a latent space, AutoML to map photometry to latent coefficients, and Bayesian inference to quantify uncertainties. Here is the pseudocode:

```
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Photometry

Input:
    - Training Spectra: Set of reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 seed spectra)
    - NIRCam Filters: Filter definitions for JWST/NIRCam
    - Photometric Data: Sparse multi-band observations of TNOs
    - Noise Level: Simulated observational noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty)

Output:
    - Reconstructed Spectra: High-resolution spectral estimates
    - Classification Probabilities: Taxonomic classification outputs
    - Uncertainty Quantification: Posterior covariance in spectral domain

1. Define Latent Space:
    a. Perform PCA on Training Spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).
    b. Construct latent space using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on PCA coefficients of training spectra.
    c. Generate synthetic training data by sampling the latent space.

2. Forward Model Photometry:
    a. For each synthetic spectrum, compute photometric measurements by convolving with NIRCam filter responses.
    b. Add Gaussian noise to photometric measurements to simulate observational uncertainty.

3. Train Encoder:
    a. Use AutoML (e.g., AutoGluon) to train a regressor mapping noisy photometry to latent coefficients.
    b. Optimize hyperparameters and select best performing model (e.g., GBDT).

4. Bayesian Inference:
    a. For new photometric observations, predict latent coefficients using the trained encoder.
    b. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
    c. Compute posterior covariance of latent coefficients to derive spectral uncertainties.

5. Taxonomic Classification:
    a. Train a classifier on latent coefficients to predict taxonomic labels.
    b. For each prediction, output classification probabilities.

6. Performance Validation:
    a. Perform LOOCV: For each object, remove its photometry, predict latent coefficients, reconstruct spectrum, compute RMSE and credibility intervals.
    b. Check empirical coverage of credibility intervals across wavelengths.

7. Applications:
    a. Survey Optimization: Identify optimal NIRCam filter combinations.
    b. Outlier Detection: Flag objects with latent coefficients outside training manifold.

8. Error Propagation:
    a. Project posterior covariance from latent space to spectral domain.
    b. Derive credible intervals for reconstructed spectra.

End Algorithm
```
