### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Collect reference TNO spectra (training set).
   - Extract sparse photometric measurements for the TNOs.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the reference spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).
   - Use the top K PCs (where K is determined based on explained variance) to create a reduced-dimensional latent space.

3. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Project the reference spectra onto the PCA latent space.
   - Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to smooth the latent space and generate synthetic latent vectors, expanding the training dataset.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Forward model the synthetic spectra through the NIRCam filter set to simulate photometric measurements.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) into the synthetic photometry to account for observational uncertainties.

5. **AutoML Regressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) to regress from sparse photometry to the latent space coefficients.
   - Use synthetic photometry and corresponding latent vectors as training data.

6. **Taxonomic Classifier Training**:
   - Train another AutoML classifier on the latent space to classify TNO spectra into predefined taxonomic categories (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).

7. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
   - For each reference spectrum, remove it from the training set.
   - Retrain the AutoML regressor and classifier using the remaining data.
   - Evaluate the reconstruction accuracy and taxonomic classification.

8. **Reconstruct Spectra**:
   - For new photometric observations, pass the measurements through the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - Project the latent coefficients back to the original spectral space using inverse PCA to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.

9. **Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Propagate uncertainties from photometric measurements through the AutoML regressor.
   - Estimate credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra and validate their coverage.

10. **Applications**:
    - **Survey Optimization**: Evaluate the information content of NIRCam filters to identify optimal configurations.
    - **Outlier Detection**: Identify points in the latent space that are far from the majority, flagging rare spectral types.
