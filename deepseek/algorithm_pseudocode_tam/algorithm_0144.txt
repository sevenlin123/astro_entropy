Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Initialize:
   - Train a PCA model on a set of reference TNO spectra to create a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to define a continuous probability distribution over the latent coefficients.
   - Prepare synthetic photometry by convolving spectra with JWST NIRCam filter throughput curves, adding 20% Gaussian noise.

2. AutoML Regression:
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) to regress from sparse photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Augment training data with synthetic spectra and their corresponding noisy photometry.

3. Bayesian Inference:
   - For each object, compute the posterior distribution of latent coefficients given photometric observations.
   - Project the latent coefficients through the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.

4. Taxonomic Classification:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic probabilities.
   - Classify objects based on their latent coefficients.

5. Performance Evaluation:
   - Use LOOCV to assess reconstruction accuracy (MAE, RMSE) and confidence interval coverage.
   - Evaluate taxonomic classification accuracy.

6. Survey Optimization:
   - Determine optimal JWST NIRCam filter configurations by simulating information content for spectral reconstruction.

7. Outlier Detection:
   - Identify objects whose latent coefficients fall outside the expected manifold, flagging them as outliers.

8. Output:
   - For each object, produce reconstructed spectrum and taxonomic classification probabilities.
