Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- Reference Spectra: A set of TNO spectra used to train the model.
- Photometric Data: Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO.
- Filter Set: NIRCam filters used for forward modeling.

Output:
- Reconstructed Spectrum: High-resolution spectral reconstruction.
- Taxonomic Classification: Probabilistic assignment of spectral type.

Steps:

1. Initialize Principal Component Analysis (PCA):
   a. Train PCA on the Reference Spectra to extract Principal Components (PCs).
   b. Project Reference Spectra onto the PCs to get latent coefficients.

2. Data Augmentation using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):
   a. Fit KDE to the latent coefficients distribution.
   b. Generate synthetic latent coefficients by sampling from the KDE.
   c. Forward model synthetic spectra through the PCA to reconstruct augmented spectra.

3. Prepare Synthetic Photometry:
   a. Convolve each synthetic spectrum with NIRCam filter responses to compute theoretical photometry.
   b. Add Gaussian noise to simulate observational uncertainties.

4. Train AutoMLRegressor:
   a. Use synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients as input-output pairs.
   b. Train an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees (AutoMLRegressor) to map photometry to latent space.

5. Train Taxonomic Classifier:
   a. Use the latent coefficients from Reference Spectra labeled by taxonomic type.
   b. Train another AutoMLClassifier to predict taxonomic probabilities from latent space.

6. Reconstruct Spectrum from Photometry:
   a. For new photometric data:
      i. Use AutoMLRegressor to predict latent coefficients.
      ii. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the high-resolution spectrum.

7. Classify Taxonomic Type:
   a. Use AutoMLClassifier to predict taxonomic probabilities from the latent coefficients.
   b. Output the most probable taxonomic type and confidence intervals.

8. Validate and Calibrate:
   a. Perform LOOCV to assess reconstruction accuracy and credible interval coverage.
   b. Compute metrics like RMSE and correlation coefficients.

9. Analyze Information Content:
   a. Evaluate different filter configurations for their contribution to spectral reconstruction fidelity.

End Algorithm
