**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - Train PCA on reference spectra to obtain principal components.
     - Input: Reference spectra.
     - Output: PCA basis (principal components).

2. **Model Training:**
   - Train an AutoML regressor to map sparse photometric inputs to PCA latent coefficients.
     - Input: Sparse photometric data.
     - Output: Trained AutoML regressor (mapping photometry to latent coefficients).

3. **Reconstruction Phase:**
   - For each new photometric observation:
     - Use the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients.
     - Apply inverse PCA transformation to the latent coefficients.
     - Output: Reconstructed spectrum.

**End of Algorithm**
