**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Initialize Training Data**:
   - Collect a sample of 51 reference TNO spectra representing dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   
2. **Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Compute PCA on the reference spectra to derive principal components (PCs).
   - Select 4-5 PCs to capture dominant spectral variability for basic reconstruction, and up to 10 PCs for enhanced fidelity.

3. **Generate Synthetic Training Data**:
   - Project the reference spectra onto the PCA latent space.
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth probability distribution in the latent space, augmenting the training set.
   - Ensure the bandwidth (h) is set to ~0.5 for stable manifold representation.

4. **Create Synthetic Photometry**:
   - Convolve each augmented spectrum with the throughput curves of the target instrument (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (~20% relative uncertainty) into the synthetic photometry to simulate realistic observational noise.

5. **Train AutoML Regressor**:
   - Use an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees to map sparse photometric inputs to latent PCA coefficients.
   - This regressor acts as the "encoder," converting photometry into the latent space.

6. **Define Taxonomic Classifier**:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic categories (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol) based on the PCA coefficients.

7. **Spectral Reconstruction Workflow**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Pass the photometry through the trained encoder to predict latent coefficients.
     - Apply the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     - Obtain taxonomic probabilities from the classifier.

8. **Validation and Metrics**:
   - Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction fidelity.
   - Metrics include Empirical Coverage, RMSE, Feature Consistency, and Taxonomic Accuracy.

**End Algorithm**
