**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Step 1: Data Preparation**
- **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
- **Output:** Set of reference spectra for training.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
- **Input:** Reference spectra.
- **Output:** Latent space defined by PCA coefficients.
  - Apply PCA to the reference spectra to extract principal components.
  - Determine the number of components (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).

**Step 3: Data Augmentation**
- **Input:** PCA coefficients of reference spectra.
- **Output:** Synthetic latent vectors.
  - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to generate synthetic latent vectors, ensuring a smooth manifold.
  - Bandwidth (h) is optimized (e.g., h ≈ 0.5) for stability and realistic interpolation.

**Step 4: Training the AutoML Regressor**
- **Input:** Synthetic photometry derived from augmented spectra.
- **Output:** Trained model mapping photometry to latent coefficients.
  - Generate synthetic photometry by convolving spectra with instrument filter curves (e.g., JWST NIRCam).
  - Inject noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate real-world data.
  - Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) using AutoML.

**Step 5: Bayesian Inference**
- **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements.
- **Output:** Posterior distribution of latent coefficients.
  - Use the trained AutoML model to predict latent coefficients from photometric data.
  - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced latent space.

**Step 6: Spectral Reconstruction**
- **Input:** Predicted latent coefficients.
- **Output:** Full spectrum reconstruction.
  - Apply inverse PCA transform to recover the high-dimensional spectrum.
  - Ensure smoothness and physical plausibility by constraining to learned manifold.

**Step 7: Taxonomic Classification**
- **Input:** Latent coefficients from reconstruction.
- **Output:** Probabilistic taxonomic labels.
  - Use a secondary classifier trained on latent space to assign probabilities to spectral types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).

**Step 8: Uncertainty Quantification**
- **Input:** Ensembled predictions and variance.
- **Output:** Confidence intervals and uncertainty estimates.
  - Propagate uncertainty from latent predictions through inverse PCA to provide credible intervals on reconstructed spectra.

**End Algorithm**
