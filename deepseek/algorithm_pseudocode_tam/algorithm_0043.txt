Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

2. Initialize:

3. Data Augmentation:

4. Training:
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map sparse photometry to PCA latent coefficients.
   - Simultaneously train a classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic categories.

5. Forward Modeling:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - Project these coefficients through the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.

6. Posterior Sampling:
   - Generate multiple posterior realizations of the spectrum to assess uncertainty and credible intervals.

7. Performance Metrics:
   - Compute reconstruction error, empirical coverage, calibration, and taxonomic classification accuracy via leave-one-out cross-validation.

8. Applications:
   - Optimize survey filters by evaluating information gain.
   - Detect outliers by identifying spectra outside the manifold.
   - Model faint or partially observed objects with propagated uncertainties.
