The algorithm is structured into several steps, focusing on Bayesian inference, PCA, and AutoML for spectral reconstruction. Here's the detailed breakdown:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - **Input:** Reference TNO spectra.
   - **Process:** Decompose the spectra into principal components to create a low-dimensional latent space.
   - **Output:** Latent space representation capturing spectral variability.

2. **Density Estimation with Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):**
   - **Input:** Latent coefficients from PCA.
   - **Process:** Apply KDE to smooth the distribution and generate synthetic latent vectors.
   - **Output:** Continuous probability distribution over the latent space.

3. **Synthetic Photometry Generation:**
   - **Input:** Augmented latent vectors and instrument-specific filters.
   - **Process:** Convolve each synthetic spectrum with filter responses to compute reflectance values.
   - **Output:** Synthetic photometric measurements with noise added.

4. **Training AutoML Regressor:**
   - **Input:** Synthetic photometric data paired with ground truth latent coefficients.
   - **Process:** Train an ensemble of models (e.g., gradient-boosted trees) to map photometry to latent space.
   - **Output:** Trained model capable of predicting latent coefficients from photometry.

5. **LOOCV Validation:**
   - **Input:** Each real spectrum removed iteratively.
   - **Process:** Validate the model by reconstructing spectra from synthetic photometry generated without the held-out spectrum.
   - **Output:** Accuracy metrics (e.g., RMSE, feature fidelity score).

6. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - **Input:** Predicted latent coefficients.
   - **Process:** Use inverse PCA transform to generate full-resolution spectra.
   - **Output:** Reconstructed spectra matching physical constraints.

7. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - **Input:** Latent coefficients of reconstructed spectra.
   - **Process:** Classify using a secondary model trained on taxonomic labels.
   - **Output:** Probability distribution over spectral types.

8. **Applications:**
   - **Survey Optimization:** Evaluate filter configurations for optimal spectral information retrieval.
   - **Outlier Detection:** Flag objects with latent states outside expected distribution.

**Final Output:**
- Probabilistic spectral reconstructions from sparse photometry.
- Taxonomic classification probabilities.
- Optimized survey configurations.
- Identified outlier candidates for further study.
