**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data Preparation**
   - Obtain a set of reference TNO spectra.
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the reference spectra to derive principal components.

2. **Define the Latent Space**
   - Use the PCA basis to project the reference spectra into a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Augment this latent space using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a continuous probability distribution.

3. **Generate Synthetic Photometry**
   - For each augmented spectrum, convolve it with the instrument's filter curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) to simulate observed photometric magnitudes.
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to account for observational uncertainties.

4. **Train the AutoML Regressor**
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to map the noisy photometric inputs to the latent coefficients.
   - The regressor is trained on the synthetic photometry and corresponding true latent coefficients.

5. **Reconstruct Spectra and Classify Taxonomic Type**
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Use the trained AutoML model to predict the latent coefficients.
     - Project the predicted latent coefficients back into the full spectral space using inverse PCA to reconstruct the spectrum.
     - Apply a secondary classifier to determine the taxonomic type based on the latent space representation.

6. **Validate and Assess Performance**
   - Use leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to evaluate the fidelity of the reconstructed spectra.
   - Compute metrics such as RMSE, cosine similarity, and credible interval coverage.
   - Assess taxonomic classification accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and AUC.

7. **Apply to Survey Design and Outlier Detection**
   - Evaluate different filter configurations for JWST/NIRCam to optimize information extraction.
   - Identify outliers by detecting latent coefficients outside the established manifold or by failing to meet taxonomic confidence thresholds.

8. **Output**
   - Provide reconstructed spectra, taxonomic probabilities, and identified outliers for further analysis.
