### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Input:**  
- Sparse photometric measurements (e.g., fluxes in specific filters) for a trans-Neptunian object (TNO).  
- Training dataset of reference TNO spectra and corresponding photometric measurements.  

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed full near-infrared spectrum with uncertainty quantification.  
- Probabilistic classification of the TNO into predefined spectral taxonomic classes.  

---

#### **Step 1: Latent Space Definition**  
1. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on a reference set of TNO spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).  
2. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA coefficients of the reference spectra to define a continuous latent space prior.  

---

#### **Step 2: Training Phase**  
1. Forward Model:  
   - Simulate photometric measurements for each augmented spectrum by convolving the spectra with the filter transmission curves of the target instrument (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).  
   - Inject Gaussian noise (relative reflectance uncertainty of ~20%) into the synthetic photometry to account for observational uncertainties.  
2. Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map sparse photometry to latent space coordinates.  

---

#### **Step 3: Inference Phase**  
1. **Encoder Step:**  
   - Pass the sparse photometric measurements of the target object through the trained AutoML regressor to predict the latent space coordinates.  
2. **Decoder Step:**  
   - Apply the inverse PCA transformation to the predicted latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.  
3. **Uncertainty Quantification:**  
   - Sample the joint posterior distribution of the latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula framework to generate an ensemble of reconstructed spectra.  
   - Compute 95% credible intervals for each wavelength bin.  

---

#### **Step 4: Taxonomy Classification**  
1. Project the predicted latent space coordinates into a taxonomic classifier trained on the reference spectra.  
2. Output the probability distribution over spectral taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO$_2$-type, Organics-type).  

---

#### **Output Metrics**  
- Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) between the reconstructed and true spectrum (normalized).  
- Empirical coverage of 95% credible intervals.  
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification scores.
