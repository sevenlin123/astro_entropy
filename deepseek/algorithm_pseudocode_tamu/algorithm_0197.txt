```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Training Spectra: Set of reference TNO spectra
    - Photometric Observations: Sparse multi-band photometry for target TNO
    - NIRCam Filters: List of filters used for forward modeling

Output:
    - Reconstructed Spectrum
    - Taxonomic Classification
    - Uncertainty Quantification

Steps:

1. **Preprocessing and Dimensionality Reduction**:
    a. Normalize training spectra by dividing by the solar spectrum.
    b. Remove outliers based on S/N and spectral quality.
    c. Denoise spectra using Savitzky-Golay filter.
    d. Align all spectra to a common spectral grid.
    e. Apply PCA to the preprocessed spectra to obtain principal components.
    
2. **Latent Space Definition**:
    a. Project the seed spectra onto the PCA basis to get latent coefficients.
    b. Perform data augmentation using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent coefficients to create a continuous prior distribution.
    c. Determine the optimal KDE bandwidth (h ~ 0.5) to balance smoothing and fidelity.

3. **Forward Modeling**:
    a. For each augmented latent vector, compute synthetic photometry by convolving the reconstructed spectrum with NIRCam filter transmission curves.
    b. Introduce Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) to simulate measurement errors.

4. **Training the Regression Model**:
    a. Construct a training database pairing synthetic photometric vectors with their corresponding ground truth PCA coefficients.
    b. Train an AutoML regressor (ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to predict latent coefficients from photometric inputs.

5. **Taxonomic Classification Training**:
    a. Augment taxonomic labels using the same synthetic latent sampling process.
    b. Train a secondary AutoML classifier to predict taxonomic labels from photometric inputs.

6. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
    a. For each spectrum i:
        i. Remove spectrum i from the training set.
        ii. Retrain the regression and classification models using the remaining N-1 spectra.
        iii. Validate using the held-out spectrum i:
            - Predict latent coefficients from photometric observations.
            - Project to reconstruct the spectrum.
            - Compare with the true spectrum to assess fidelity and uncertainty coverage.
            - Classify the taxonomic type.

7. **Reconstruction and Uncertainty Quantification**:
    a. From photometric observations, predict latent coefficients using the trained regressor.
    b. Apply inverse PCA transform to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.
    c. Sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula to generate an ensemble of reconstructions.
    d. Compute confidence intervals for spectral features.

8. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
    a. Evaluate information content of NIRCam filter combinations to maximize reconstruction fidelity.
    b. Identify outliers by analyzing the overlap of reconstructed spectrum probabilities with predefined classes.
    c. Detect rare spectral types like peculiar Neptune Trojans.

End
```
