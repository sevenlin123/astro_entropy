Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- Observed photometric data (flux measurements in selected filters)
- Pre-trained PCA basis derived from reference spectra
- Kernel Density Estimate (KDE) of latent space
- Training parameters for AutoML regressor

Output:
- Reconstructed spectrum
- Uncertainty estimates
- Taxonomic classification

Steps:

1. **Dimensionality Reduction**:
   a. Project photometric data into the pre-trained PCA latent space to obtain initial latent coefficients.

2. **Likelihood-Free Inference**:
   a. Generate synthetic photometry for each augmented spectrum via forward modeling using the instrumental response functions (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   b. Inject Gaussian noise into synthetic photometry to simulate real-world uncertainties.

3. **Training of AutoML Regressor**:
   a. Use the synthetic photometry and corresponding PCA latent coefficients to train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees.
   b. Optimize hyperparameters to ensure robust prediction of latent coefficients from sparse photometry.

4. **Reconstruction of Latent Space**:
   a. Apply the trained regressor to the observed photometric data to predict latent coefficients.
   b. Sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula to account for physical dependencies.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   a. Apply inverse PCA transformation to the sampled latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   b. Compute uncertainties by propagating the variance from the posterior samples through the inverse PCA.

6. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   a. Project the reconstructed latent coefficients into a pre-trained classifier to determine the probability distribution over taxonomic classes.

7. **Validation and Output**:
   a. Compare reconstructed spectra with ground truth (if available) to assess fidelity and uncertainty calibration.
   b. Provide reconstructed spectrum, uncertainty intervals, and taxonomic probabilities as output.
