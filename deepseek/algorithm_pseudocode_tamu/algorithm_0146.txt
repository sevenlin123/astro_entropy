Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. Input: Sparse photometric observations of a TNO.

2. Precompute:
   a. Train Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on reference TNO spectra to derive principal components (PCs).
   b. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to model the latent space distribution of PCA coefficients.

3. Forward Modeling:
   a. Simulate synthetic photometry for each augmented spectrum by convolving with NIRCam filter throughput curves.
   b. Inject Gaussian noise (20% relative reflectance uncertainty) into synthetic photometry to account for observational uncertainties.

4. AutoML Training:
   a. Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees regressor (Encoder) to map photometric inputs to latent PCA coefficients.

5. Inference:
   a. Pass sparse photometric observations through the trained Encoder to predict latent coefficients.
   b. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct full spectral energy distribution (SED).

6. Uncertainty Quantification:
   a. Sample joint posterior of latent coefficients using Gaussian Copula framework.
   b. Generate ensemble of spectral reconstructions to compute 95% credible intervals.

7. Output: Reconstructed spectrum, credible intervals, and taxonomic classification probabilities.
