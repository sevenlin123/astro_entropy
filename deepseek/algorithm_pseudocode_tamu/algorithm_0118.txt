### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework for TNOs from Sparse Photometry

**Input:**  
- `Training_Spectra`: Set of reference TNO spectra.  
- `Photometry_Data`: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.  
- `Filters`: List of photometric filters used (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).  

**Output:**  
- `Reconstructed_Spectra`: Full spectral reconstruction for each TNO.  
- `Taxonomic_Classification`: Probabilistic taxonomic classification for each TNO.  
- `Uncertainty_Interval`: Credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.  

---

**Step 1: Preprocessing - Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**  
1.1. Perform PCA on `Training_Spectra` to reduce dimensionality.  
1.2. Identify the top K principal components that capture dominant spectral variability.  

**Step 2: Latent Space Definition**  
2.1. Project `Training_Spectra` onto the top K PCA components.  
2.2. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to model the probability distribution of the latent coefficients.  

**Step 3: Training the AutoML Regressor**  
3.1. Generate synthetic photometry for each augmented spectrum by forward modeling with filter throughput curves.  
3.2. Inject Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) into synthetic photometry.  
3.3. Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML regressor) to map sparse photometry to latent coefficients.  

**Step 4: Forward Inference**  
4.1. For each TNO's `Photometry_Data`, predict latent coefficients using the trained regressor.  
4.2. Apply inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.  

**Step 5: Uncertainty Quantification**  
5.1. Sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula.  
5.2. Map the samples through inverse PCA to obtain credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.  

**Step 6: Taxonomic Classification**  
6.1. Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic probabilities.  
6.2. Classify each TNO based on predicted probabilities.  

**Step 7: Performance Validation**  
7.1. Conduct leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to evaluate reconstruction accuracy, RMSE, Pearson correlation, AUC, and Bhattacharyya distance.  
7.2. Validate empirical coverage of credible intervals.  
7.3. Assess classification accuracy using confusion matrix and AUC.
