**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Input:**  
- Sparse photometric measurements of a trans-Neptunian object (TNO)  
- Training dataset of reference TNO spectra  
- Pre-computed PCA basis from the training dataset  

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed full spectrum of the TNO  
- Posterior probabilities of taxonomic classification  
- Uncertainty quantification for spectral features  

**Steps:**

1. **Data Preprocessing:**
   - Normalize the sparse photometric measurements to reflectance or flux density.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Perform PCA on the training dataset of reference spectra to obtain principal components (PCs).
   - Compute the latent coefficients (scores) for the training spectra.

3. **Synthetic Data Generation:**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent scores to generate synthetic training data.
   - Forward model the synthetic spectra through the instrument response (e.g., JWST NIRCam filters) to produce synthetic photometry with added noise (~20%).

4. **Training the AutoML Regressor:**
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees regressor on synthetic photometry to predict latent coefficients from sparse photometry.

5. **Inference Phase:**
   - For new photometric data:
     a. Predict latent coefficients using the trained regressor.
     b. Apply inverse PCA to reconstruct the full spectrum.

6. **Uncertainty Quantification:**
   - Use Gaussian Copula sampling to generate an ensemble of reconstructions.
   - Calculate quantile-based confidence intervals for the spectrum.

7. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Project the predicted latent coefficients into a pre-trained classifier to determine posterior probabilities of spectral types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).

8. **Posterior Sampling:**
   - Generate multiple realizations of the reconstructed spectrum to assess uncertainty propagation.

**End Algorithm**
