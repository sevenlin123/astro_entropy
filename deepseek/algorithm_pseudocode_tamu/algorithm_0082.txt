Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Observed photometric measurements (sparse_photometry)
    - Pre-trained PCA model parameters (PCA_model)
    - Training data with corresponding spectra (training_spectra)

Output:
    - Reconstructed spectrum (reconstructed_spectrum)
    - Uncertainty estimates (credible_intervals)
    - Taxonomic classification (taxonomic_class)

Steps:

1. **Dimensionality Reduction:**
    a. Project the observed photometric measurements (sparse_photometry) into the latent space defined by the PCA_model.
    b. Obtain the latent coefficients (z) representing the position in the PCA-manifold.

2. **Forward Modeling:**
    a. Simulate photometric measurements (synthetic_photometry) from the latent coefficients (z) using the pre-trained forward model (kernel density estimate).
    b. Inject Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) into the synthetic_photometry to account for observational uncertainties.

3. **Regression Training:**
    a. Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) using the synthetic_photometry and latent_coefficients.
    b. Validate the regressor through leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy and credible interval coverage.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
    a. Use the trained regressor to predict the latent coefficients (z_hat) from the input photometric measurements (sparse_photometry).
    b. Apply the inverse PCA transformation to z_hat to reconstruct the full spectrum (reconstructed_spectrum).

5. **Uncertainty Quantification:**
    a. Generate an ensemble of reconstructions by sampling from the joint posterior distribution of the latent coefficients.
    b. Compute credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum.

6. **Taxonomic Classification:**
    a. Project the predicted latent coefficients (z_hat) into the taxonomic classifier.
    b. Obtain a probability distribution over taxonomic classes (e.g., H2O, CO2, Organics, Methanol).

7. **Output:**
    a. Return the reconstructed_spectrum, credible_intervals, and taxonomic_class.

End
