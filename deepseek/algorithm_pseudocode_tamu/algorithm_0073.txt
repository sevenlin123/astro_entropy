```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Training Spectra: Set of reference TNO spectra
    - Photometric Data: Sparse multi-band observations of TNOs
    - Filter Set: NIRCam bandpasses (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M)

Output:
    - Reconstructed Spectra: High-resolution spectral estimates for each TNO
    - Latent Representation: Low-dimensional coefficients capturing spectral variability
    - Taxonomic Classifications: Probabilistic assignments to spectral types

Steps:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
    a. Decompose the training spectra into principal components.
    b. Determine the optimal number of principal components (k) based on explained variance and cross-validation.
    c. Project training spectra into the k-dimensional latent space.

2. **Data Augmentation (Optional):**
    a. Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to the latent vectors to create synthetic data.
    b. Sample synthetic latent vectors to augment the training set.

3. **Forward Modeling:**
    a. For each latent vector:
        i. Inverse PCA transform to obtain a full-resolution spectrum.
        ii. Convolve with instrument response functions to simulate photometric magnitudes.
        iii. Add Gaussian noise to simulate observational uncertainties.

4. **Training the Encoder:**
    a. Combine original and synthetic data.
    b. Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map photometric data to latent space.

5. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):**
    a. For each seed spectrum:
        i. Exclude from training.
        ii. Predict latent coefficients using photometric data.
        iii. Inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the spectrum.
        iv. Evaluate reconstruction quality using RMSE, Pearson correlation, and credible interval coverage.

6. **Taxonomic Classifier Training:**
    a. Use taxonomic labels of seed spectra.
    b. Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent space.

7. **Reconstruction and Classification:**
    a. For new photometric data:
        i. Predict latent coefficients.
        ii. Project to full spectrum via inverse PCA.
        iii. Classify spectral type using the trained classifier.
        iv. Generate probabilistic confidence intervals.

8. **Survey Optimization:**
    a. Analyze filter combinations using Fisher information.
    b. Identify optimal filters for maximizing spectral information.

9. **Outlier Detection:**
    a. Identify objects with posterior in low-density regions of latent space.
    b. Flag as potential outliers or novel spectral types.

End
```
