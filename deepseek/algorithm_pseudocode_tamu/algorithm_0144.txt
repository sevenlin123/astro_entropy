### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Initialize the Training Dataset**:
   - Collect and preprocess near-infrared spectra of TNOs, normalizing and scaling to a common magnitude system.

2. **Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Decompose the training spectra into principal components to define a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Extract latent coefficients for each spectrum.

3. **Generate Synthetic Latent Vectors**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) with a Gaussian kernel to create a continuous probability distribution over the latent space.
   - Bandwidth (`h`) is optimized empirically (e.g., `h ≈ 0.5`).

4. **Augment Training Data**:
   - Sample 10,000 synthetic latent vectors from the KDE distribution.
   - Simulate photometric measurements by convolving each synthetic spectrum with JWST/NIRCam filter transmission curves.
   - Add Gaussian noise (20% relative reflectance uncertainty) to the simulated photometry.

5. **Train AutoML Regressor**:
   - Use an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (e.g., XGBoost) to regress from sparse photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Apply automated hyperparameter tuning (e.g., AutoGluon) for optimal model performance.

6. **Conduct Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
   - For each seed spectrum:
     - Temporarily exclude the spectrum from the training set.
     - Retrain the model using the remaining data.
     - Predict latent coefficients from the real photometry of the excluded spectrum.
     - Reconstruct the spectrum using inverse PCA.

7. **Evaluate Reconstruction Accuracy**:
   - Compute the Mean Absolute Difference (MAD) between reconstructed and original spectra across all wavelengths.
   - Assess the empirical coverage of credible intervals using Gaussian Copula sampling.

8. **Classify Taxonomic Types**:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organic-type).
   - Evaluate classification accuracy and confidence intervals.

9. **Apply to Survey Optimization**:
   - Identify optimal JWST/NIRCam filter combinations (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M) by evaluating information content for spectral reconstruction fidelity.

10. **Detect Outliers**:
    - Flag spectra with low probability in the KDE-learned latent distribution as potential outliers, such as peculiar Neptune Trojans.
    - Perform detailed analysis of outliers to confirm unique spectral types.

**End of Algorithm**
