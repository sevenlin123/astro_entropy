```pseudocode
Algorithm Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Training Spectra Set: S = {S_1, S_2, ..., S_N}
    - Photometric Filters: F = {F_1, F_2, ..., F_M}
    - Number of Principal Components K

Output:
    - Reconstructed Spectrum: S_reconstructed
    - Latent Coefficients: z
    - Taxonomic Classification: C

1. Initialize Principal Component Analysis (PCA):
    a. Compute PCA basis vectors from training spectra S.
    b. Determine the number of principal components K based on variance explained and cross-validation.

2. Construct Latent Space:
    a. Project training spectra S onto PCA basis to get latent coefficients z_real.
    b. Perform Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on z_real to create a smooth probability distribution in latent space.

3. Forward Modeling:
    a. For each synthetic latent vector z_synthetic sampled from KDE:
        i. Forward model through NIRCam filters to compute synthetic photometry m_synthetic.
        ii. Add Gaussian noise to m_synthetic to simulate observational uncertainty.

4. Train AutoML Regressor:
    a. Use pairs of (m_synthetic, z_synthetic) to train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees.
    b. Optimize regressor to predict latent coefficients z from photometric inputs m.

5. Cross-Validation:
    a. Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):
        i. For each held-out spectrum S_i:
            A. Generate synthetic training data excluding S_i.
            B. Retrain regressor and classifier on new synthetic data.
            C. Predict latent coefficients z_hat for S_i's photometry.
            D. Inverse PCA transform to get S_reconstructed.

6. Evaluate Reconstruction:
    a. Compare S_reconstructed with S_i using RMSE, Pearson correlation, and credible interval coverage.

7. Taxonomic Classification:
    a. Train classifier on latent coefficients to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., H2O, CO2, Organics, Methanol).

8. Predict for New Object:
    a. Obtain photometry m_new for the object.
    b. Use trained regressor to predict latent coefficients z_new.
    c. Inverse PCA transform to reconstruct S_reconstructed.
    d. Use classifier to get taxonomic probabilities.

9. Outlier Detection:
    a. Flag objects if z_new falls outside high-probability regions or taxonomic confidence is low.

10. Output Results:
    a. Return S_reconstructed, z_new, and taxonomic classification C.
```
