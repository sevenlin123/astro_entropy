**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data Preparation**
   - **Observations**: Obtain sparse photometric measurements for trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - **Reference Spectra**: Use a pre-existing set of near-infrared TNO spectra for training.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - **Training Set**: Apply PCA to the reference spectra to derive principal components.
   - **Latent Space Definition**: Represent the spectral variability using a low-dimensional latent space defined by the PCA eigenvectors.

3. **Data Augmentation via Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**
   - **Synthetic Data Generation**: Use KDE to generate synthetic spectra within the latent space, ensuring a smooth and continuous manifold.
   - **Noise Injection**: Add Gaussian noise to synthetic photometry to simulate real-world observational uncertainties.

4. **Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) Training**
   - **Regressor Training**: Train an AutoML regressor (ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees) to map sparse photometry to latent PCA coefficients.
   - **Classifier Training**: Concurrently train a classifier within the latent space for taxonomic classification.

5. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**
   - **Model Validation**: For each TNO spectrum, validate the model by holding out one spectrum and training the regressor and classifier on the remaining data.

6. **Spectral Reconstruction**
   - **Prediction**: For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - **Inverse PCA Transform**: Convert latent coefficients back to full spectral resolution using the inverse PCA transformation.

7. **Uncertainty Quantification**
   - **Posterior Sampling**: Use a Gaussian Copula framework to sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients.
   - **Confidence Intervals**: Generate credible intervals for reconstructed spectra to quantify uncertainties.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**
   - **Classification**: Predict taxonomic categories (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic) using the latent space classifier.
   - **Probability Estimation**: Output probabilities for each taxonomic class.

9. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**
   - **Filter Selection**: Assess the information content of different photometric filters to optimize survey designs.
   - **Outlier Identification**: Detect objects with latent coordinates in low-density regions indicative of rare spectral types.

**End of Algorithm**
