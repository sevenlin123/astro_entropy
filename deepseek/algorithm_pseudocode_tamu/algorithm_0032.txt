Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Observed photometric measurements (m) for a TNO
    - Training set of reference spectra (S_train)
    - Precomputed principal component (PC) basis (U)
    - Synthetic photometry and augmented latent space from KDE

Output:
    - Reconstructed spectrum (S_pred) with uncertainty quantification
    - Probabilistic taxonomic classification

Steps:

1. **Preprocessing**:
    a. Project S_train onto U to obtain latent coefficients (z_train).
    b. Apply KDE to z_train to create a smooth latent space prior.
    c. Generate synthetic photometry (m_synthetic) for manifold augmentation.
    d. Inject Gaussian noise (σ_rel = 20%) into m_synthetic to simulate real-world uncertainty.

2. **Training Phase**:
    a. Use AutoML regressor (e.g., XGBoost) to train encoder mapping m -> z_posterior_mean.
    b. Train taxonomic classifier on z_train and corresponding labels.

3. **Forward Inference**:
    a. Pass observed photometry (m) through trained encoder to obtain z_posterior_mean.
    b. Sample from joint posterior of z using Gaussian Copula to handle latent correlations.
    c. Apply inverse PCA transform: S_pred = U * z_posterior + mean_spectrum.

4. **Uncertainty Quantification**:
    a. Compute confidence intervals for S_pred based on posterior z_sample.
    b. Validate coverage using LOOCV results.

5. **Classification**:
    a. Apply trained taxonomic classifier to z_posterior_mean.
    b. Output probability distribution over taxonomic labels.

End.
