### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Objective**: Reconstruct high-resolution spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) from sparse photometric measurements using a probabilistic framework.

**Core Steps**:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Input: Reference TNO spectra.
   - Output: PCA eigencomponents (principal eigenvectors) and mean spectrum.
   - Purpose: Define a low-dimensional latent space capturing spectral variability.

2. **Data Augmentation with Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**:
   - Input: Latent coefficients from PCA.
   - Method: Apply Gaussian KDE to smooth and interpolate the latent space.
   - Output: Synthetic latent vectors representing plausible spectral morphologies.

3. **Forward Modeling and Likelihood Construction**:
   - Input: Synthetic spectra from augmented latent space.
   - Process: Convolve spectra with instrumental filter responses (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Add Gaussian noise to simulate observational uncertainties.
   - Output: Synthetic photometric observations paired with ground-truth spectra.

4. **Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) Regression Training**:
   - Input: Synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients.
   - Model: Train an ensemble regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees).
   - Output: Trained encoder mapping photometric inputs to latent coefficients.

5. **Inverse PCA Transformation**:
   - Input: Predicted latent coefficients from the encoder.
   - Process: Transform coefficients back to full-resolution spectra.
   - Output: Reconstructed high-resolution spectrum.

6. **Uncertainty Quantification via Gaussian Copula Sampling**:
   - Input: Predicted latent coefficients.
   - Method: Sample joint posterior distribution considering latent dependencies.
   - Output: Ensemble of reconstructed spectra with credible intervals.

7. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
   - Input: Observed photometry of held-out object.
   - Process: Remove one spectrum, retrain PCA and AutoML, predict latent coefficients.
   - Output: Reconstructed spectrum validated against true spectrum.

**Explanation**:

- **PCA Training**: Reduces the spectral data to a few principal components, capturing the most significant variations in the data.
- **KDE Augmentation**: Generates a smooth manifold of plausible spectral states, enhancing the training data for the regressor.
- **Forward Modeling**: Simulates the photometric measurements corresponding to synthetic spectra, introducing realistic noise.
- **AutoML Regression**: Learns the mapping from sparse photometry to the latent space, enabling reconstruction of unseen spectra.
- **Inverse PCA**: Converts the latent space predictions back into high-resolution spectra, leveraging the PCA basis.
- **Uncertainty Quantification**: Provides probabilistic constraints on the reconstructed spectra, quantifying the uncertainty introduced by sparse measurements.
- **LOOCV Validation**: Ensures the robustness of the reconstruction framework by evaluating its performance on held-out data.

This algorithm efficiently bridges the gap between sparse photometric data and detailed spectral reconstruction, providing a statistically rigorous framework for studying TNO surface compositions.
