### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

**Inputs:**
- `photometry`: Observed sparse photometric data (e.g., magnitudes in selected filters).
- `training_spectra`: Reference near-infrared spectra from a curated training set.
- `filters`: Definition of photometric filters used (e.g., JWST/NIRCam bands).

**Outputs:**
- `reconstructed_spectrum`: Full spectral energy distribution (SED) estimated from photometry.
- `credible_intervals`: Uncertainty bounds on the reconstructed spectrum.
- `taxonomy_prediction`: Probabilistic assignment to spectral taxonomic classes.

**Steps:**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
     1. Perform PCA on `training_spectra` to derive principal components (PCs) and their coefficients.
     2. Determine the optimal number of PCs (K) that balance reconstruction fidelity and model complexity (e.g., via LOOCV).

   - **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):**
     1. Use the PCA coefficients of `training_spectra` to construct a KDE model, generating synthetic latent vectors for robust regression training.
     2. Set the Gaussian kernel bandwidth (h) to ensure smooth manifold approximation.

   - **Forward Modeling:**
     1. Simulate synthetic photometry for each augmented spectrum by convolving with filter transmission curves.
     2. Inject Gaussian noise (relative reflectance uncertainty) to mimic real-world photometric uncertainties.

   - **AutoMLRegressor Training:**
     1. Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (e.g., XGBoost) to map photometric inputs to latent PCA coefficients.
     2. Validate using synthetic data to ensure robustness in low-sample scenarios.

   - **TaxonomyClassifier Training:**
     1. Train a secondary AutoML model to classify latent coefficients into taxonomic groups (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).

2. **Inference Phase:**
   - **Photometry to Latent Space:**
     1. Pass the observed `photometry` through the trained `AutoMLRegressor` to predict latent coefficients.
     2. Sample the joint posterior distribution of latent coefficients using a Gaussian Copula framework to account for dependencies.

   - **Spectrum Reconstruction:**
     1. Apply the inverse PCA transform to the latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     2. Generate credible intervals based on the posterior samples.

   - **Taxonomy Classification:**
     1. Use the predicted latent coefficients to determine the probability distribution over taxonomic classes.

**End of Algorithm**
