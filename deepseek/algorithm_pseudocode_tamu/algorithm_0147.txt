Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Training Spectra: Set of near-IR spectra of TNOs (N = 51)
    - Photometric Filters: Bandpasses of JWST/NIRCam (F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M)
    - Observed Photometry: Sparse multi-band photometric measurements of TNOs
    - Uncertainty Levels: Relative reflectance uncertainties (e.g., 20%)

Output:
    - Reconstructed Spectra: High-resolution spectral estimates for each object
    - Taxonomic Classifications: Probabilistic assignments to spectral types
    - Credible Intervals: Uncertainty bounds on reconstructed spectra

Steps:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
    a. Perform PCA on the training spectra to extract principal components (PCs).
    b. Determine the number of PCs (K) that capture spectral variability (4-5 for classification, 8-10 for fidelity).
    c. Project training spectra onto the PCA basis to get latent coefficients.

2. **Data Augmentation with KDE:**
    a. Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) with Gaussian kernels to the latent coefficients.
    b. Bandwidth (h) optimized for smoothness and fidelity (~0.5).
    c. Generate synthetic latent vectors to augment training data.

3. **Forward Modeling Synthetic Photometry:**
    a. Convolve each augmented spectrum with photometric filter responses.
    b. Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) into synthetic photometry.

4. **AutoML Regression Training:**
    a. Use AutoGluon's TabularPredictor to train a model mapping synthetic photometry to latent coefficients.
    b. Enable automated feature engineering and hyperparameter tuning.
    c. Validate using LOOCV, ensuring held-out data is excluded during training.

5. **Inverse PCA Transformation:**
    a. For new photometric observations, predict latent coefficients using the trained regressor.
    b. Apply inverse PCA to reconstruct the full spectrum.

6. **Uncertainty Sampling with Gaussian Copula:**
    a. Sample the joint posterior of latent coefficients.
    b. Preserve physical dependencies between PCs using a structured correlation matrix.
    c. Generate an ensemble of reconstructed spectra and compute credible intervals.

7. **Taxonomic Classification:**
    a. Train a classifier on the PCA latent space to predict spectral types.
    b. Assign probabilities to each taxonomic class for new observations.

8. **LOOCV Validation Metrics:**
    a. Compute RMSE, MAE, Pearson correlation, and credible interval coverage for each held-out object.
    b. Assess taxonomic confidence and outlier detection capabilities.

9. **Filter Information Content Analysis:**
    a. Evaluate different filter combinations using reconstruction fidelity metrics.
    b. Identify optimal configurations for future surveys.

End.
