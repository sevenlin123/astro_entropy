Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input: 
- Sparse photometric observations (m)
- Pre-trained PCA basis (principal_components)
- Pre-trained KDE model (kernel_density_estimation)
- Pre-trained AutoML regressor (encoder)

Output:
- Reconstructed spectrum (S)
- Classification probabilities (C)

Steps:
1. **Latent Space Prediction:**
   - Use the AutoML regressor (encoder) to predict the latent coefficients (z) from the sparse photometry (m).
   
2. **Generate Posterior Samples:**
   - Sample from the joint posterior distribution of the latent coefficients using the Gaussian Copula framework.
   
3. **Reconstruct Spectrum:**
   - Apply the inverse PCA transform to each sampled latent coefficient to obtain a high-resolution spectrum (S).
   
4. **Compute Classification Probabilities:**
   - Project the latent coefficients into the pre-trained taxonomy classifier to predict the probability distribution over spectral types (C).
   
5. **Return Result:**
   - Output the mean reconstructed spectrum (S) and the classification probabilities (C).

End
