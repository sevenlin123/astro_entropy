Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
- Observed photometric measurements m
- Training set of reference spectra S_train
- Principal component (PC) decomposition of S_train

Output:
- Reconstructed spectrum S_reconstructed
- Posterior distribution over latent coefficients

Steps:

1. Latent Space Definition:
   a. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on S_train to obtain principal components.
   b. Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to estimate the prior distribution of latent coefficients.

2. Training Phase:
   a. Generate synthetic photometry from the augmented latent space using forward modeling through PCA.
   b. Inject Gaussian noise with 20% relative uncertainty into synthetic photometry.
   c. Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to regress photometric measurements to latent coefficients.

3. Forward Inference:
   a. Pass observed photometry m through the trained regressor to obtain predicted latent coefficients z_hat.
   b. Apply inverse PCA transformation to z_hat to reconstruct high-dimensional spectrum S_reconstructed.
   c. Sample from the joint posterior of latent coefficients using Gaussian Copula to generate multiple realizations of S_reconstructed.

4. Classification (Optional):
   a. Use a trained classifier on latent space to predict taxonomic classification probabilities.

5. Uncertainty Quantification:
   a. Propagate uncertainties from photometry through the reconstruction pipeline to compute credible intervals.

End.
