Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Input:
    - Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO
    - Precomputed PCA basis derived from a sample of reference spectra
    - Training data consisting of synthetic photometry and corresponding PCA coefficients
    - Validation metrics including RMSE and correlation coefficient

Output:
    - Reconstructed spectrum with uncertainty quantification
    - Probabilistic taxonomic classification

Steps:

1. **Preprocessing**:
    a. Normalize the input photometric measurements.
    b. Project the normalized photometry through the precomputed PCA basis to obtain latent coefficients.

2. **Forward Modeling**:
    a. Simulate synthetic photometry for each augmented spectrum by convolving with instrument-specific filter curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
    b. Inject Gaussian noise at a relative uncertainty level (e.g., 20%) into the synthetic photometry to account for observational noise.

3. **Training Phase**:
    a. Construct a training database by augmenting the real spectra with synthetic ones generated via Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA latent space.
    b. Split the training data into training and validation subsets (80:20 ratio).
    c. Train an AutoML regressor (ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees) to predict latent coefficients from photometric measurements.

4. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
    a. For each held-out spectrum:
        i. Recalibrate the latent space using the remaining data.
        ii. Generate synthetic mock data to fill the latent space.
        iii. Evaluate the model's performance using metrics like RMSE and Pearson correlation.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
    a. Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients from new photometric measurements.
    b. Apply the inverse PCA transform to obtain the full spectrum.
    c. Sample the joint posterior distribution using a Gaussian Copula to quantify uncertainties.

6. **Taxonomy Classification**:
    a. Project the predicted latent coefficients into a taxonomic classifier trained on the labeled PCA space.
    b. Output the probability distribution over taxonomic categories.

7. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
    a. Quantify information content of filter configurations via KL divergence between conditional distributions.
    b. Detect outliers by identifying low-density regions in the latent space.

End.
