Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. Initialize probabilistic model with parameters to capture uncertainty in sparse photometric data.
2. For each trans-Neptunian object:
   a. Use Bayesian inference to estimate underlying spectral energy distribution (SED) given sparse photometric observations.
   b. Apply Gaussian Processes to handle missing and uncertain data points in photometric measurements.
3. Cluster estimated SEDs into taxonomic categories using Dirichlet Process Mixture Models (DPMM).
4. Perform posterior predictive checks to identify outliers in the reconstructed SEDs.
5. Emulate SEDs for purposes of survey optimization, considering the probabilistic constraints and uncertainties.
6. Output reconstructed SEDs, cluster assignments, and outlier flags.
