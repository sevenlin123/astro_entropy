**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction using Bayesian Inference and Gaussian Processes**

1. **Parameterize the Spectral Energy Distribution (SED)**:
   - Select a set of basis functions (e.g., orthogonal polynomials) to represent the SED.
   - Determine the number of basis parameters, ensuring a minimal representation.

2. **Define the Gaussian Process (GP) Model**:
   - Establish the GP to relate the basis parameters to observed flux measurements across photometric bands.
   - Specify the covariance function (kernel) and associated hyperparameters (signal variance, length scale, noise variance).

3. **Set Up Bayesian Inference**:
   - Assign prior distributions to all parameters, including basis parameters and GP hyperparameters.
   - Use priors such as uniform or weakly informative distributions to reflect prior knowledge.

4. **Implement Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling**:
   - Apply an MCMC method (e.g., Metropolis-Hastings, Gibbs sampler) to generate posterior samples.
   - Iterate through the parameter space to explore posterior distributions of basis parameters and GP hyperparameters.

5. **Reconstruct the SED**:
   - Utilize the posterior estimates of basis parameters to construct the SED for each object.
   - Obtain uncertainty estimates through the posterior distributions.

6. **Model Comparison (Optional)**:
   - Calculate Bayes factors to compare alternative models if necessary.
   - Consider different basis functions or GP kernels to assess model fit.

This algorithm efficiently handles sparse photometric data by leveraging a reduced parameter space and Bayesian techniques, enabling robust SED reconstruction and probabilistic analysis.
