Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry

Input: Set of photometric measurements for each TNO, each consisting of filter identifier and flux.

Output: Posterior mean and variance of the SED for each TNO.

Begin

For each TNO in dataset:

    1. Initialize Gaussian Process (GP) model with kernel reflecting physical constraints on TNO reflectivity.
        a. Select kernel type (e.g., squared exponential)
        b. Set hyperparameters based on prior knowledge of TNO reflectivity and albedo.

    2. Collect all photometric measurements for the TNO.
        a. Organize measurements into input vector X (filters) and output vector Y (fluxes).

    3. Perform Bayesian inference on the GP model using the collected measurements.
        a. Compute the covariance matrix K between training points.
        b. Compute the Cholesky decomposition of K + σ²I, where σ² is the noise variance.
        c. Solve for the posterior mean μ = (K^T * inv(L L^T)) Y, where L is the lower triangular matrix from Cholesky decomposition.
        d. Compute the posterior covariance Σ = (K^T * inv(L L^T) * K) - diag(K^T * inv(L L^T) * Y)

    4. Compute the posterior predictive distribution for new points.
        a. For each new filter, compute the predictive mean E[y*] = μ*.
        b. Compute the predictive variance Var(y*) = k(**) - k(*, *)^T (K^−1 Y + σ²I)^{-1} (K^−1 Y + σ²I) k(*, *) 
            where k(**) is the covariance between new point and training points.

    5. Extract posterior mean and variance of the SED.
        a. Store μ as the posterior mean.
        b. Store Σ as the posterior variance.

End

Output the posterior mean and variance for each TNO's SED.
