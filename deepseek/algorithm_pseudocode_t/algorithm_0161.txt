```pseudocode
Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for TNOs

Input:
    - TemplateLibrary: Collection of basis spectra
    - PhotometryData: Sparse and noisy photometric observations
    - NoiseModel: Distribution describing observational noise

Output:
    - ReconstructedSED: Most probable spectral energy distribution
    - Uncertainties: Variance estimates for each SED component
    - Outliers: List of flagged outlier observations

Steps:

1. Initialize Parameters:
    - Coefficients ← Uniform distribution over possible values

2. Bayesian Inference Setup:
    - Define Likelihood(PhotometryData | Coefficients, NoiseModel)
    - Define Prior(Coefficients | TemplateLibrary)

3. Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC):
    - Sample Posterior(Coefficients | PhotometryData, NoiseModel, TemplateLibrary)
    - Store posterior distributions

4. Outlier Detection:
    - Compute Residuals = PhotometryData - ExpectedSED(Coefficients)
    - Flag Outliers where |Residuals| exceeds thresholds

5. Outlier Exclusion:
    - FilterOutliers = PhotometryData without flagged outliers
    - Repeat Bayesian Inference with FilterOutliers

6. Reconstruct SED:
    - ReconstructedSED = LinearCombination(TemplateLibrary, posterior mode of Coefficients)

7. Uncertainty Quantification:
    - Uncertainties = Variance(Posterior distributions of Coefficients)

8. Output:
    - Return ReconstructedSED, Uncertainties, and Outliers

End Algorithm
```
