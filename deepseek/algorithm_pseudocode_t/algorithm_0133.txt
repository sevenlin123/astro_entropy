**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

**Step 1: Data Processing**
1. Collect sparse photometric data points from various surveys.
2. Record associated uncertainties for each measurement.
3. Note the observation times corresponding to each measurement.

**Step 2: Probabilistic Modeling**
1. Initialize a Gaussian Process (GP) model to represent the spectral energy distribution.
2. Define the GP kernel to capture the smoothness of the spectrum.
3. Incorporate measurement and observing process uncertainties into the GP model.

**Step 3: Bayesian Inference**
1. Define prior distributions for GP hyperparameters and spectral parameters based on known physics.
2. Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to explore the posterior distributions of hyperparameters and spectral parameters.
3. Generate posterior samples from the MCMC chain.

**Step 4: Spectral Feature Extraction**
1. Extract meaningful spectral features (e.g., slopes, colors, absorption bands) from the posterior samples.
2. Compute confidence intervals for these features based on the posterior distributions.

**Step 5: Outlier Detection**
1. Analyze the posterior distributions to identify objects whose spectra deviate significantly from the modeled distribution.
2. Classify such objects as potential outliers or peculiar cases requiring further investigation.

**End Algorithm**
