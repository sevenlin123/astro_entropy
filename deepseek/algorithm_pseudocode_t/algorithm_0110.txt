**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

**Step 1: Initialize Parameters**
- Input sparse photometric observations.
- Define prior distributions for spectral parameters based on domain knowledge.
- Set up Gaussian Process (GP) hyperparameters, including kernel type and noise level.

**Step 2: Bayesian Parameter Estimation**
- For each object:
  - Perform Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to estimate posterior distributions of spectral parameters.
  - Use the GP as a prior to regularize the spectral continuum, enforcing smoothness.
  
**Step 3: Gaussian Process Regression**
- Train the GP using the spectral parameters from Bayesian estimation.
- Predict the spectral continuum at all wavelengths, capturing uncertainty and smoothness.

**Step 4: Emission Line Detection**
- Apply Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) to identify emission lines in the spectrum.
  - Use GMM to detect peaks in the spectrum corresponding to emission lines.
  - Validate line positions against known TNO spectral features.

**Step 5: Rejection Sampling**
- Filter out implausible solutions based on posterior predictive checks.
  - Remove solutions where observed data is inconsistent with the reconstructed spectrum.

**Step 6: Posterior Predictive Checks**
- For validation purposes (not executed in this step), assess the goodness-of-fit by comparing observed data with predictions from the model.

**Output:**
- Reconstructed spectra for taxonomic classification, survey optimization, and outlier detection.

**Note:** This algorithm focuses solely on the core computational steps without validation or visualization procedures as per the provided constraints.
