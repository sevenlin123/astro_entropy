Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction

Input: Sparse photometric data of trans-Neptunian objects

Output: Reconstructed spectral energy distributions with uncertainty quantification

Steps:

1. For each object:
   - Read sparse photometric data across multiple bands
   - Initialize Gaussian Process (GP) model for the spectral data

2. Construct covariance matrix using GP kernels to capture band correlations

3. Fit GP model to observed photometric magnitudes

4. Use GP to predict posterior distributions across full spectrum

5. Quantify uncertainties in reconstructed spectra

6. Classify objects into taxonomic groups based on spectral features

7. Identify spectral bands contributing most to classification

8. Detect outliers in the dataset

9. Optimize survey strategy based on identified key bands

10. Output reconstructed spectra and associated uncertainties
