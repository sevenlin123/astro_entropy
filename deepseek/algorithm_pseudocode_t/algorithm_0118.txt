**Algorithm: Core Computational Method for Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Preprocess Photometric Data**
   - Remove outliers from the input photometric data.
   - Standardize the measurements to a consistent scale.

2. **Model Selection**
   - For each candidate spectral template:
     - Apply Bayesian methods to assess fit to the preprocessed data.
     - Determine the posterior probability of the template given the data.
   - Select the spectral template with the highest posterior probability.

3. **Parameter Estimation**
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) to estimate parameters of the selected template.
   - Obtain posterior distributions of the model parameters.

4. **Uncertainty Quantification**
   - Analyze the posterior distributions to derive confidence intervals and uncertainty estimates for the spectral parameters.

5. **Classification**
   - Classify the object into predefined taxonomic classes based on its reconstructed spectral features.

**End Algorithm**
