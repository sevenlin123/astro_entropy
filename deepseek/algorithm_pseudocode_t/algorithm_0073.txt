Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects

1. Initialize dataset containing multiple trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs), each with photometric measurements across various wavelengths.

2. For each TNO in the dataset:
    a. Collect all available photometric measurements across multiple wavelengths.
    b. Construct a Gaussian Process (GP) model for the spectral data, treating each observed flux as a noisy measurement.
    c. Apply Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) to sample the posterior distribution of the spectral parameters, capturing uncertainties in the spectral shape due to sparse observations.
    d. Compare the posterior distributions against known spectral templates for different taxonomic classes to classify the TNO.
    e. Calculate the probability of the TNO belonging to each taxonomic class based on the posterior distributions, then assign the TNO to the class with the highest probability.
    f. Identify potential outliers by assessing deviations from expected spectral shapes derived from the posterior distributions.

3. Return the taxonomic classifications for each TNO and flags indicating outliers.
