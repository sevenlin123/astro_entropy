**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Gaussian Process with MCMC**

1. **Initialize Gaussian Process Parameters**
   - Define mean function \( m(\lambda) \), typically zero unless specified.
   - Set covariance function \( k(\lambda, \lambda') \), e.g., squared exponential kernel.
   
2. **Set Up Data**
   - Input: Sparse photometric measurements \( \{ (\lambda_i, y_i) \}_{i=1}^n \).
   - Output: Reconstructed SED \( s(\lambda) \).

3. **Define Likelihood Model**
   - Assume measurement noise \( \sigma^2 \).
   - Likelihood: \( p(y | s) = \mathcal{N}(y, s(\lambda), \sigma^2) \).

4. **Estimate Hyperparameters**
   - Use Bayesian inference to estimate \( \theta \) (e.g., variance, length scale of kernel).
   - Compute posterior \( p(\theta | y) \) via marginal likelihood.

5. **MCMC Sampling**
   - Initialize MCMC chain with starting values.
   - Iterate:
     - Sample \( s^{(k)}(\lambda) \) from \( p(s | y, \theta^{(k)}) \).
     - Update \( \theta^{(k+1)} \) using posterior distribution.
   - Conduct sufficient iterations for convergence.

6. **Post-Process Samples**
   - Extract posterior median or mean SED from MCMC samples.
   - Calculate credibility intervals if needed.

7. **Output Reconstructed SED**
   - Provide \( \hat{s}(\lambda) \) and associated uncertainties.

**End Algorithm**
