**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Input Data Preparation:**
   - Load sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects.
   - Include multi-wavelength observations and handle missing data as part of the input.

2. **Probabilistic Forward Model Setup:**
   - Define a truncated basis expansion in SED space.
   - Establish an empirical prior distribution on the physical parameters (e.g., albedo, phase function, thermal emission).

3. **Hierarchical Model Construction:**
   - Link parameters across multiple observations to enable joint parameter estimation.
   - Structure the model to allow parameter sharing and hierarchical dependencies.

4. **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling:**
   - Initialize MCMC chains with appropriate starting values.
   - Perform MCMC sampling to explore the posterior distribution of parameters.
   - Characterize the uncertainty in reconstructed SEDs through posterior analysis.

5. **SED Reconstruction:**
   - Use the sampled parameters to reconstruct the spectral energy distribution (SED).
   - Apply the probabilistic forward model to transform parameters into SED predictions.

6. **Outlier Detection and Classification (Taxonomy):**
   - Identify outliers in the data or parameter space using the posterior distributions.
   - Classify objects into taxonomic categories based on their reconstructed SEDs and parameters.

7. **Survey Design Optimization:**
   - Optimize future survey strategies by evaluating the information gain from additional observations.
   - Adjust observing parameters (e.g., exposure time, wavelength selection) to maximize scientific returns.

8. **Output the Reconstructed SEDs and Parameters:**
   - Provide the reconstructed SEDs for each object.
   - Deliver the estimated physical parameters along with their uncertainties.

9. **End of Algorithm**

**Note:** This algorithm focuses solely on the core reconstruction framework and excludes validation procedures, plotting, and visualization steps.
