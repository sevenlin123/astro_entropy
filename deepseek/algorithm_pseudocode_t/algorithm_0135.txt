**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Input Data**: 
   - Photometric measurements of TNOs across multiple bands.
   - Uncertainties associated with each measurement.
   
2. **Preprocessing**:
   - Normalize the photometric data to a common flux scale.
   - Remove obvious outliers based on predefined criteria (e.g., deviations exceeding a threshold relative to uncertainties).

3. **Define Basis Components**:
   - Select a set of representative spectral templates (`B` components) that capture the diversity of TNO spectra.
   - Each template has an associated spectral shape and uncertainty.

4. **Mixture Model Setup**:
   - Represent the SED of each TNO as a weighted sum of selected basis components.
   - Weights correspond to probabilities, ensuring they sum to 1.

5. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Define the likelihood function based on the observed photometric data and the modeled SED.
   - Specify a prior distribution for the weights and model parameters.
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) to sample the posterior distribution of the weights and parameters, incorporating all uncertainties.

6. **Posterior Analysis**:
   - For each TNO, determine the most probable spectral type by identifying the basis component with the highest posterior weight.
   - Calculate confidence intervals on the reconstructed SED.

7. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Compare observed photometric measurements with best-fit SED predictions.
   - Flag as outliers objects where the discrepancy exceeds a defined threshold.

8. **Output**:
   - Reconstructed SEDs for each TNO.
   - Probabilities of spectral types.
   - Flags for outlier detection.
