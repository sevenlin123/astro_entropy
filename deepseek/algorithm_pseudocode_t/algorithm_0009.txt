**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

**Input:** Sparse photometric data of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs)  
**Output:** Reconstructed spectral energy distributions (SEDs)

1. Initialize Gaussian Process (GP) parameters.  
2. For each TNO in the dataset:  
   a. Use the GP to model the observed photometric bands.  
   b. Predict the SED at unobserved wavelengths using the GP.  
   c. Input the predicted SED into the Mixture Density Network (MDN) for refinement.  
   d. Obtain the refined SED from the MDN.  
   e. Store the refined SED for the TNO.  

**End Algorithm**
