**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

**Step 1: Data Collection**
For each Trans-Neptunian Object (TNO):
- Collect sparse photometric data from various instruments.

**Step 2: Data Preprocessing**
- Correct photometric data for known effects (e.g., extinction, background noise).

**Step 3: Gaussian Process Prior Definition**
- Initialize Gaussian Process with specified kernel and hyperparameters to model SEDs.

**Step 4: Forward Model Construction**
- Develop a model to map SEDs to observed fluxes using instrument response functions.

**Step 5: Hierarchical Model Integration**
- Introduce latent variables to account for unknown systematic errors in photometric data.

**Step 6: Bayesian Inference via Variational Approximation**
- Apply mean-field approximation for Bayesian inference to compute approximate posteriors.

**Step 7: Gaussian Process Approximation**
- Utilize inducing points to reduce computational complexity, approximating the Gaussian Process.

**Step 8: Posterior Distribution Calculation**
- Compute posterior distributions for SEDs and latent error variables.

**Step 9: Spectrum Selection**
- Choose a representative spectrum from the posterior distribution (e.g., mean or median).

**Step 10: Output Reconstructed SEDs**
- Provide reconstructed SEDs for subsequent analysis.
