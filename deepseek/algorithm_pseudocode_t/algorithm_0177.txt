**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Input Data**: Read sparse photometric observations of TNOs across multiple wavelengths.
2. **Preprocess Data**: Normalize and format observations for Gaussian Process (GP) modeling.
3. **Construct Covariance Matrices**:
   - Compute kernel matrices for the full dataset and inducing points.
4. **Define Priors**:
   - Set hierarchical prior distributions for spectral features.
   - Assign hyperparameters controlling smoothness and variance.
5. **Set Up HMC Parameters**:
   - Configure Hamiltonian Monte Carlo settings, including step size and number of iterations.
6. **Run HMC Inference**:
   - Use NUTS sampler to explore posterior distributions of SED coefficients.
7. **Infer Posterior SEDs**:
   - Extract mean and variance of inferred spectral energy distributions.
8. **Output Results**:
   - Provide reconstructed SEDs and associated uncertainties.

**End Algorithm**
