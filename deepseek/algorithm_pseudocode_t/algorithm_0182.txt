The algorithm presented is designed to reconstruct the spectral energy distributions (SEDs) of trans-Neptunian objects using sparse photometric data. It employs a Bayesian hierarchical model combined with a black-box forward model to estimate SED parameters, quantify uncertainties, detect outliers, and identify spectral features. The steps involve initializing model parameters, defining prior distributions, implementing the forward model, computing the likelihood, performing Bayesian inference via MCMC, checking convergence, analyzing posteriors, detecting outliers, discovering spectral features, and outputting results.

```plaintext
Algorithm: Probabilistic SED Reconstruction

1. **Model Initialization**:
   - Define SED parameters, measurement uncertainties, and hyperparameters.

2. **Prior Distributions**:
   - Assign prior probabilities to model parameters based on existing knowledge.

3. **Forward Model Implementation**:
   - Develop a function converting SED parameters into expected flux measurements, incorporating instrument calibrations.

4. **Likelihood Function**:
   - Calculate the probability of observed photometric data given the model parameters.

5. **Bayesian Inference via MCMC**:
   - Use MCMC methods to sample the posterior distribution, generating numerous samples to explore the distribution thoroughly.

6. **Convergence Check**:
   - Assess convergence using PSRF and Gelman-Rubin statistics across multiple independent chains.

7. **Posterior Analysis**:
   - Compute summary statistics, credible intervals, and posterior predictive distributions to understand parameter behavior.

8. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Compare posterior-predicted fluxes with observed data; flag objects with significant discrepancies.

9. **Spectral Feature Discovery**:
   - Identify features by examining credible intervals and using statistical tests to confirm their significance.

10. **Result Compilation**:
    - Output posterior means, credible intervals, and outlier flags for further analysis.

This structured approach ensures comprehensive SED reconstruction, enabling taxonomy, survey optimization, and outlier detection through robust statistical methods.
```
