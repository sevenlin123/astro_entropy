**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Initialization**
   - Read input data: sparse photometric observations of trans-Neptunian objects.

2. **Preprocessing**
   - Normalize photometric measurements to a standard scale.
   - Remove identified artifacts and corrupted data points.
   - Align measurements across different photometric bands to ensure consistency.

3. **Gaussian Process Training**
   - Construct the Gaussian Process model using an empirical prior derived from large surveys.
   - Set hyperparameters based on the empirical prior statistics.
   - Train the GP model using the preprocessed data.

4. **SED Inference**
   - For each object:
     - Use the trained GP model to predict the underlying SED based on sparse photometric data.
     - Obtain posterior distributions of the SED components.

5. **Goodness of Fit and Outlier Detection**
   - Compute the goodness of fit between inferred SED and observed data.
   - Identify outliers based on discrepancy between inferred and observed SEDs.

6. **Optimal Observation Scheduling**
   - Utilize information criteria (e.g., AIC, BIC) to determine optimal future observation frequencies and bands.
   - Suggest the most informative observations based on uncertainty reduction potential.

**End Algorithm**
