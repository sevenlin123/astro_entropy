Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction

Inputs:
    photometric_data: Sparse photometric measurements
    spectral_templates: Predefined spectral features and templates
    parameters: Model parameters including priors

Outputs:
    reconstructed_sed: Probabilistic spectral energy distribution
    uncertainties: Associated uncertainties

1. Initialize the model with parameters and priors
    Initialize the Gaussian process with hyperparameters

2. Preprocess the photometric data and spectral templates
    Normalize and align spectral features for compatibility

3. Perform Bayesian inference using MCMC
    Sample from posterior distribution of SED parameters

4. Apply Gaussian process modeling
    Compute covariance matrix between spectral features

5. Implement nested sampling
    Calculate Bayesian evidence for model selection

6. Post-process the MCMC samples
    Determine the most probable SEDs and their uncertainties

7. Output the reconstructed SED and uncertainties
