**Algorithm: Core Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - For each object, gather flux measurements and their uncertainties.
   - Ensure data alignment across photometric bands.

2. **Mixture Model Fitting**:
   - Initialize a finite mixture of Gaussians with K components.
   - Apply the EM algorithm augmented with a Dirichlet prior to estimate the mixture parameters.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - For each object, employ Gaussian Process regression using a rational quadratic kernel.
   - Integrate the propagated uncertainties from the mixture model into the GP regression to reconstruct the full spectrum.

4. **Classification and Outlier Detection**:
   - Categorize each reconstructed spectrum into predefined taxonomic classes.
   - Identify outliers based on spectral consistency with the majority.

5. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Determine the most informative wavelengths by assessing information gain.
   - Develop an optimal survey strategy based on these wavelengths.

**Output**:
- Reconstructed spectra for each object.
- Optimized survey strategy.
