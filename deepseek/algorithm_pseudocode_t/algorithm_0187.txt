**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Initialize Gaussian Process Model**: 
   - For each TNO, define a Gaussian process (GP) with specified kernel parameters that capture the spectral correlation structure.

2. **Set Informative Priors**:
   - Incorporate prior knowledge about TNO spectral features by setting mean functions and covariance parameters in the GP to reflect expected spectral behavior.

3. **Condition on Observed Data**:
   - Use the available sparse photometric measurements to update the GP. This involves conditioning the GP on the observed data points to obtain the posterior distribution of the spectral reflectance.

4. **Sample Posterior Spectra**:
   - Draw multiple samples from the posterior distribution of the spectral reflectance to generate plausible spectra consistent with the observations.

5. **Analyze Posterior Uncertainty**:
   - Quantify uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra by examining the variance of the posterior distribution and identifying regions of high confidence.

6. **Identify Spectral Features**:
   - Extract key spectral features from the posterior samples to determine taxonomic groupings and detect anomalies in the data.

7. **Perform Unsupervised Clustering**:
   - Utilize the reconstructed spectra to cluster TNOs into taxonomic groups based on similarity measures applied to the posterior distributions.

8. **Output Taxonomic Classifications**:
   - Generate classifications of TNOs into spectral taxonomic groups based on the clustering results.
