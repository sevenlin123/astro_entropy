**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Precompute Basis Templates**
   - Collect known spectral types of trans-Neptunian objects.
   - Generate a set of basis templates representing these spectral types.

2. **Karhunen-Loève Transform**
   - Decompose the basis templates into an orthogonal basis using the Karhunen-Loève transform.
   - This step reduces dimensionality and ensures orthogonality.

3. **Bayesian Inference for Template Weights**
   - For each observed object, express its spectral energy distribution (SED) as a linear combination of the orthogonal basis templates.
   - Determine the posterior distribution of the weights using Bayesian inference, constrained by observed flux measurements.
   - Model the weights with a Dirichlet distribution.

4. **Gaussian Mixture Model Training**
   - Train a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) on the posterior weight distributions obtained from Bayesian inference.
   - Each GMM component corresponds to a spectral type, capturing variability within that type.

5. **Assign Spectral Types**
   - For each object, calculate the probability of belonging to each GMM component.
   - Assign the object to the GMM component with the highest probability, determining its most probable spectral type.

6. **Identify Outliers**
   - Calculate the probability that an object belongs to any GMM component.
   - Identify outliers as objects with significantly low probability across all components.

This algorithm efficiently reconstructs spectral types, accounts for uncertainty, and identifies outliers through Bayesian inference and Gaussian Mixture Modeling.
