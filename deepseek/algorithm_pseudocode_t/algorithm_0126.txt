**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input**: Observed sparse flux measurements of trans-Neptunian objects.
2. **Initialize**: 
   - Define the partial differential equation (PDE) representing the light curve dynamics.
   - Establish a sparse sampling operator linking the true light curve to the observations.
3. **Define Gaussian Priors**: 
   - Set prior distributions for parameters controlling spectral features to enforce smooth temporal variations.
4. **Solve the Inverse Problem**:
   - Apply a numerical method, such as the Gauss-Markov-Kolmogorov (GMK) filter, to solve the PDE with the given priors.
5. **Reconstruct Light Curve**:
   - Generate the continuous light curve from the solution of the PDE.
6. **Extract Features**:
   - Identify variability patterns and spectral characteristics from the reconstructed light curve.
7. **Detect Outliers**:
   - Compare observed flux measurements against the reconstructed light curve to identify significant deviations.
8. **Output**: Reconstructed light curves and identified outliers.

**End of Algorithm**
