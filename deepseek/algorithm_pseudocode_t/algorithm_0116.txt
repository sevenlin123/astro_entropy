**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Initialization:**
   - Initialize Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) with `K` components.
   - Initialize Variational Autoencoder (VAE) with encoder and decoder networks.

2. **Training Phase:**
   - Train GMM on the sparse multi-band photometric data.
   - Train VAE on the same sparse photometric data, learning latent representations.

3. **Reconstruction:**
   - Use GMM to cluster data points into spectral components.
   - Use VAE to decode observed photometric data into complete SEDs.

4. **Fusion:**
   - Combine GMM-based and VAE-based reconstructions to obtain final SED estimates.

5. **Anomaly Detection:**
   - Apply unsupervised anomaly detection to flag outliers as candidates for follow-up.

**Output:** Reconstructed SEDs and outlier flags.
