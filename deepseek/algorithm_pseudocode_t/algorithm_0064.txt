Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs

Input: Sparse photometric data of trans-Neptunian objects
Output: Reconstructed intrinsic spectra, taxonomic classifications, and outlier detection

1. Initialize dataset containing sparse photometric observations of TNOs.
2. For each object in the dataset:
   a. Define a Gaussian Process (GP) model with a covariance function appropriate for TNO spectral features.
   b. Assign prior distributions to GP hyperparameters.
   c. Perform Bayesian inference using an optimized MCMC sampling strategy to obtain posterior distributions of the hyperparameters.
   d. Reconstruct the intrinsic spectrum by sampling from the posterior predictive distribution of the GP.
   e. Classify the taxonomic type based on the reconstructed spectral features.
3. After processing all objects, identify outliers based on the consistency of spectral reconstructions with the modeled spectral variations.
