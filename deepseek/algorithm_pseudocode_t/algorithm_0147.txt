**Algorithm: Probabilistic SED Reconstruction via Gaussian Processes and MCMC**

1. **Initialize Parameters**
   - Set prior distributions for SED parameters (e.g., amplitude, spectral index).
   - Define Gaussian process hyperparameters (amplitude, length scale).

2. **Set Up Gaussian Process Model**
   - Specify the covariance matrix using a spectral kernel (e.g., Matern kernel).
   - Apply smoothness constraints based on known spectral properties of TNOs.

3. **Forward Model Implementation**
   - Relate observed flux measurements to the SED using the probabilistic forward model.
   - Incorporate observation geometry and instrument characteristics.

4. **Define Likelihood Function**
   - Express the probability of observed fluxes given the SED parameters.
   - Include measurement uncertainties and noise characterization.

5. **MCMC Sampling**
   - Use MCMC (e.g., Metropolis-Hastings) to draw samples from the posterior distribution.
   - Update SED parameters based on the forward model and observed data.
   - Adjust hyperparameters to maintain physical plausibility.

6. **Posterior Analysis**
   - Extract converged posterior distributions of SED parameters.
   - Analyze the uncertainty quantification for downstream tasks.

**End of Algorithm**
