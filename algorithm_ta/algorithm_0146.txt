**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training:**
   - Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - The PCA captures the dominant modes of spectral variability, reducing the dimensionality of the spectra.

2. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference using the PCA-reduced spectral manifold.
   - Reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric measurements by propagating measurement uncertainties through the Bayesian framework.

3. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Evaluate the information content of different photometric filter configurations (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   - Identify optimal filter combinations that maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity and taxonomic classification accuracy.

4. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Reconstruction:**
   - Allow exploration of the latent spectral manifold to constrain photometry for rare or peculiar spectral types.
   - Detect and reconstruct rare types by identifying low-probability intermediate models within the continuous manifold.

This algorithm effectively bridges sparse photometry and spectroscopy, enabling robust reconstruction and analysis of TNO compositions.
