Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Inputs:
- Training set of near-IR spectra
- Photometric measurements for the object of interest

Outputs:
- Reconstructed spectrum
- Posterior distribution of principal component coefficients

Steps:
1. Preprocess the training set of near-IR spectra.
2. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the training spectra to extract principal components.
3. Collect the sparse photometric measurements for the object.
4. Build a probabilistic model in the PCA space, incorporating measurement uncertainties and correlations between photometric bands.
5. Use Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the PCA coefficients.
6. Transform the posterior into the original spectral space to reconstruct the full spectrum.
