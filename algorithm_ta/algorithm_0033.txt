**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **PCA Model Training**
   - **Input**: Collection of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - **Process**: Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to the spectral data to capture the primary modes of variability.
   
2. **Bayesian Inference for Latent Space Representation**
   - **For each TNO**:
     - **Input**: Photometric measurements.
     - **Process**: Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA latent space to determine the most probable representation that explains the photometric data.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction**
   - **Using the inferred latent variables**:
     - Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the latent space back to the original spectral space.

4. **Uncertainty Quantification**
   - **Calculate uncertainties** in the reconstructed spectra based on the posterior distributions obtained from Bayesian inference.

This structured approach efficiently translates the described framework into a computational algorithm, focusing solely on the core steps without additional explanations.
