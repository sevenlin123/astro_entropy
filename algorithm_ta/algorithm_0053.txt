**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Latent Space Inference**

1. **Training Phase**:
   - Train a principal component analysis (PCA) model using a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (K) based on the application, where K=4-5 captures taxonomic classification, and K=8-10 improves spectral fidelity and uncertainty.
   
2. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Obtain sparse photometric measurements of the target TNO from near-IR surveys.
   - Normalize and preprocess the photometric data to account for measurement noise and instrument characteristics.

3. **Latent Space Mapping**:
   - Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs in a reduced-dimensional space using the pre-trained PCA model.
   - Map the sparse photometric measurements into this latent space.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the latent space to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the model to quantify reconstruction uncertainties.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Project the inferred latent space parameters back to the original spectral space.
   - Obtain the reconstructed spectrum, ensuring credible-interval coverage (~95%).

6. **Outlier Detection and Rare Type Reconstruction**:
   - Apply constraints to photometric measurements to identify and reconstruct rare spectral types by sampling low-probability intermediate models.
   - Detect outliers by evaluating the probability of the inferred models against the spectral manifold.

This structured approach efficiently bridges sparse photometric data with detailed spectral reconstruction, enabling robust taxonomic classification and outlier detection for TNOs.
