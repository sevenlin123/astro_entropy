**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Input:** Collection of near-infrared (NIR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - **PCA Model Training:** 
     - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the input spectra to derive principal components (PCs). The PCs capture the major spectral variations within the dataset.
   - **Latent Space Construction:** 
     - Represent the spectral manifold in a reduced-dimensional space using the obtained principal components.

2. **Probabilistic Inference:**
   - **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements of a TNO.
   - **Bayesian Inference Setup:**
     - Utilize the pre-trained PCA model to infer the underlying spectral representation in the latent space.
     - Propagate measurement uncertainties from the photometric data into the latent space.
   - **Reconstruct Spectra:**
     - Reconstruct the full spectrum from the latent space representation, incorporating the propagated uncertainties.

3. **Component Selection:**
   - **Determine Optimal Components:**
     - Use the results from leave-one-out cross-validation to identify the optimal number of principal components. Typically, 4-5 PCs are used for taxonomic classification, and 8-10 PCs enhance spectral reconstruction accuracy.

4. **Output:**
   - **Result:** The reconstructed spectrum along with uncertainty estimates, enabling taxonomic classification and outlier detection.

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometry with spectral reconstruction, providing a robust framework for studying TNOs.
