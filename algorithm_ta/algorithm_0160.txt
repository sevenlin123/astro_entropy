**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**
   - **Input**: Training set of near-infrared spectra of TNOs.
   - **Output**: Principal components representing the spectral manifold.
   - Perform PCA on the training spectra to capture the dominant modes of spectral variability.

2. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**
   - **Input**: Sparse photometric measurements (e.g., magnitudes in specific bands).
   - **Process**: Use the trained PCA model to project photometric data into the latent space.
   - **Output**: Posterior distributions of principal component weights, enabling full spectrum reconstruction.

3. **Uncertainty Propagation with MCMC**
   - **Method**: Apply Markov Chain Monte Carlo sampling on the Bayesian model.
   - **Outcome**: Obtain uncertainties for the inferred principal components, quantifying the reliability of the reconstruction.

4. **Cross-Validation for Optimal Components**
   - **Procedure**: Conduct leave-one-out cross-validation.
   - **Determine**: Optimal number of principal components (4-5 for classification; 8-10 for higher fidelity).

5. **Spectral Reconstruction and Uncertainty Quantification**
   - **Transform**: Convert photometric data into the latent space using the PCA model.
   - **Infer**: Reconstruct full spectra and their uncertainties via Bayesian inference.

6. **Filter Configuration Optimization**
   - **Objective**: Maximize information content for TNO taxonomy.
   - **Select**: Optimal NIRCam filters (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M) based on their effectiveness in spectral reconstruction.

7. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Identification**
   - **Analyze**: Constrained photometric data within the spectral manifold.
   - **Identify**: Deviations indicating unique or rare spectral types, enhancing detection of unusual objects.

This algorithm integrates statistical modeling and dimensionality reduction to reconstruct detailed spectra from sparse photometry, supporting both scientific analysis and observational strategy optimization.
