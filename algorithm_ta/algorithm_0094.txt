**Algorithm Description: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Data Preparation**: 
   - Collect sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Convert photometric data into a format suitable for spectral analysis.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Train a PCA model using a sample of near-infrared (NIR) spectra of TNOs.
   - The PCA captures the dominant modes of spectral variability.

3. **Latent-Space Representation**:
   - Represent the spectral data in a reduced-dimensional latent space using the trained PCA model.
   - Each object is projected into this space, preserving the essential spectral features.

4. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference within the latent space to reconstruct full spectra from the sparse photometric data.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

5. **Model Selection**:
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

6. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Use the selected principal components to probabilistically reconstruct the full spectra of TNOs.
   - Quantify uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra to assess reliability.

7. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate the information content of different NIR filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters) using the framework.
   - Identify optimal filter configurations for future surveys to enhance spectral classification.

8. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Apply the framework to detect unusual spectral types, such as peculiar Neptune Trojans.
   - Use photometric constraints to identify low-probability intermediate models on the spectral manifold.

9. **Final Output**:
   - Provide reconstructed spectra with associated uncertainties for each TNO.
   - Deliver optimal filter combinations for maximizing spectral information in future observations.
