### Computational Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

**Step 1: Data Preprocessing**
- **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) and a reference dataset of near-infrared (NIR) spectra.
- **Process:** Normalize and align the spectral data to a common wavelength grid. Remove outliers and noise from the photometric data to enhance accuracy.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
- **Apply PCA:** Transform the reference NIR spectra dataset into a principal component (PC) representation.
- **Determine Components:** Retain approximately 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification, and up to 8-10 for improved spectral fidelity and uncertainty handling.

**Step 3: Probabilistic Latent-Space Modeling**
- **Construct Manifold:** Use the PCA-derived components to model the spectral manifold of TNOs.
- **Bayesian Inference:** Perform Bayesian inference within the reduced latent space to estimate the underlying spectral parameters from photometric data.

**Step 4: Spectral Reconstruction**
- **Reconstruct Spectra:** Map the inferred latent space parameters back to full-spectrum estimates.
- **Uncertainty Propagation:** Calculate uncertainties associated with each reconstructed spectrum, ensuring reliable credible intervals.

This structured approach efficiently reconstructs full spectra from sparse photometry, leveraging Bayesian methods and dimensionality reduction to handle uncertainty and variability effectively.
