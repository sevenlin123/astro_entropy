**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - **Input**: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs.
   - **Output**: Processed data ready for spectral analysis.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - **Input**: Near-infrared (near-IR) spectra from a training sample.
   - **Output**: Principal component representation capturing the major spectral variations.

3. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**:
   - **Input**: Processed photometric data and PCA model.
   - **Output**: Probabilistic reconstruction of full spectra using the reduced PCA space.

4. **Model Training**:
   - **Input**: Training set of near-IR spectra.
   - **Output**: Trained PCA model with defined number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

5. **Spectral Manifold Construction**:
   - **Input**: PCA model and processed photometric data.
   - **Output**: Representation of the spectral manifold for TNOs.

6. **Reconstruction and Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - **Input**: Photometric data and trained PCA model.
   - **Output**: Reconstructed spectra with associated uncertainties, ensuring credible interval coverage (~95%).

7. **Survey Optimization and Filter Selection**:
   - **Input**: Information content of NIRCam filters.
   - **Output**: Optimally informative filter configurations for future observations.

8. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types**:
   - **Input**: Photometric data and PCA model.
   - **Output**: Identification of unusual spectral types by constraining photometry to low-probability intermediate models.

9. **Final Output**:
   - **Output**: Probabilistic spectral reconstructions, uncertainty estimates, optimized survey configurations, and identified outlier spectral types.

This algorithm bridges sparse photometry and detailed spectral analysis, enabling robust compositional mapping of TNOs in large-scale surveys.
