**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Train a PCA model using a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of TNOs.
   - Determine the number of principal components needed for accurate spectral reconstruction (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for high fidelity).

2. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space:**
   - Use the trained PCA model to represent the spectral manifold of TNOs in a lower-dimensional latent space.
   - Perform Bayesian inference in this space to reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric data.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

3. **Model Construction:**
   - Develop a probabilistic latent-space framework that quantifies the spectral information retained in photometric measurements.

4. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Apply the framework to evaluate the information content of different filter combinations (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   - Identify optimal configurations for future observations based on their ability to enhance spectral reconstruction fidelity.

5. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types:**
   - Allow the framework to constrain photometry in a way that identifies unusual spectral types.
   - Detect and reconstruct rare types like peculiar Neptune Trojans by selecting low-probability intermediate models.

This structured approach effectively bridges sparse photometry and spectroscopy, offering a robust tool for mapping the compositional structure of minor planets.
