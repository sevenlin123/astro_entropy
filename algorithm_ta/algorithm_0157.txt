Algorithm:

1. Preprocess the training dataset of near-infrared spectra using Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to extract the principal components that capture the spectral variability of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
2. For each TNO:
   a. Extract its sparse photometric measurements.
   b. Project the photometric data into the precomputed PCA space.
   c. Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to reconstruct the full spectrum, considering the measurement uncertainties.
   d. Quantify the posterior probabilities of the spectral components to determine the most likely spectral model.
   e. Propagate the uncertainties through the inference process to obtain the uncertainty estimates for the reconstructed spectrum.
3. Output the reconstructed spectrum and associated uncertainties for each TNO.
