**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**: 
   - Train a PCA model using a reference dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra to capture the spectral manifold of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   
2. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space using sparse photometric data to reconstruct full spectra.
   
3. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties throughout the reconstruction process to assess the reliability of the reconstructed spectra.
   
4. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate the information content of different observational filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) to identify optimal configurations for future surveys, enhancing TNO taxonomy.
   
5. **Outlier Detection and Rare Type Reconstruction**:
   - Use the framework to detect and reconstruct rare spectral types by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the continuous spectral manifold.
   
6. **Output the Reconstructed Spectra and Uncertainties**:
   - Generate the reconstructed spectra along with their associated uncertainties for each object based on the probabilistic framework.
