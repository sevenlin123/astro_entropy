**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Preprocess Spectral Data**: 
   - Collect a dataset of near-infrared spectra of TNOs.
   - Normalize and align the spectra to a uniform wavelength grid.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Apply PCA to the aligned spectral dataset to extract principal components.
   - Select the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for classification, up to ~10 for detailed reconstruction) to capture significant spectral variability.

3. **Construct Latent Spectral Manifold**:
   - Represent each spectrum in the PCA-derived latent space.
   - Model the manifold to capture the possible spectral variations of TNOs.

4. **Bayesian Inference for Reconstruction**:
   - Use photometric measurements as inputs.
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the latent space to estimate spectral parameters, incorporating measurement uncertainties.

5. **Full Spectrum Reconstruction**:
   - Transform the inferred latent parameters back to the original spectral space using inverse PCA.
   - Calculate credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra based on posterior distributions.

6. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Identify objects with posterior distributions significantly deviating from the manifold.
   - Flag potential outliers or unique spectral types.

7. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate information content of filter configurations.
   - Rank filters based on their effectiveness in constraining spectral parameters.
   - Choose optimal filters to maximize information gain for future surveys.

This algorithm effectively bridges sparse photometry and spectroscopy, enabling robust spectral reconstruction and taxonomy of TNOs.
