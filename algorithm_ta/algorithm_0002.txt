**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Preprocess Photometric Data**: 
   - Clean and normalize the sparse photometric observations of TNOs.

2. **Retrieve Reference Spectra**: 
   - Collect existing near-infrared (NIR) spectra of TNOs from datasets.

3. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the reference spectra to extract principal components (PCs).
   - Select the number of dominant PCs (e.g., 4-5 for classification, up to 8-10 for higher fidelity) based on the amount of variance explained.

4. **Probabilistic Model Construction**:
   - Develop a model in the reduced PCA space using the selected PCs as latent variables.

5. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Utilize Bayesian methods to estimate the PCs from the photometric data, considering measurement uncertainties.

6. **Spectrum Reconstruction**:
   - Map the estimated PCs back to the original spectral space to reconstruct the full spectrum.

7. **Uncertainty Calculation**:
   - Determine uncertainties and confidence intervals by analyzing the posterior distributions of the PCs.

8. **Application**:
   - Use the reconstructed spectra for survey optimization, outlier detection, or taxonomic classification of TNOs.
