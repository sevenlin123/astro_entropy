**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Principal Component Extraction**:
   - Obtain a reference dataset of near-infrared spectra of TNOs.
   - Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to this dataset to derive principal components (PCs), capturing the main sources of spectral variation.

2. **Probabilistic Model Construction**:
   - Develop a probabilistic model representing the spectral manifold within the PCA space.
   - The model should account for uncertainties and correlations in the latent features.

3. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Using the probabilistic model and observed photometric data, perform Bayesian inference.
   - Sample the posterior distribution in the PCA space to reconstruct the underlying spectral components.

4. **Posterior Transformation**:
   - Convert the posterior samples from the latent (PCA) space back to the original spectral space.
   - Calculate credible intervals to assess the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra.

5. **Spectral Manifold Analysis**:
   - Analyze the reconstructed spectra to understand the structure and distribution within the spectral manifold.
   - Identify outlying objects with unusual spectral types based on their deviation from the manifold.

This algorithm enables the reconstruction of detailed spectra from sparse photometric data, supporting taxonomic classification and outlier detection for TNOs.
