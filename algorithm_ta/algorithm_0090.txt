**Core Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training:**
   - Train a PCA model using a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of TNOs.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (approximately 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for improved spectral reconstruction).

2. **Spectral Manifold Modeling:**
   - Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs using the principal components obtained from the PCA training.
   - Construct a probabilistic latent-space model capturing the low-dimensional structure of spectral variability.

3. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced principal component space to reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric data.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

4. **Model Application:**
   - Apply the framework to new photometric data to reconstruct the corresponding near-IR spectra.
   - Quantify the information content of different filter configurations (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters) for survey optimization.

5. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Reconstruction:**
   - Use the probabilistic framework to detect and reconstruct rare spectral types by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the spectral manifold.

This algorithm effectively bridges sparse photometry and spectroscopy, enabling robust spectral reconstruction and taxonomic classification of TNOs.
