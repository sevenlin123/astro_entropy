**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Preprocess Training Spectra**: Normalize and align the training near-infrared spectra to a common wavelength grid.

2. **Perform PCA**: Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to the preprocessed spectra to extract principal components, capturing the major axes of spectral variation.

3. **Select Principal Components**: Retain 8-10 principal components to ensure accurate spectral reconstruction and uncertainty estimation.

4. **Construct Spectral Manifold**: Utilize the selected principal components to build the spectral manifold, encoding the spectral diversity of TNOs.

5. **Bayesian Inference per Object**:
   - For each object, input its sparse photometric measurements.
   - Conduct Bayesian inference within the spectral manifold to determine the most probable spectral representation.
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the inferred manifold position back to the original spectral space.

6. **Uncertainty Calculation**: Compute uncertainty intervals for the reconstructed spectra by propagating uncertainties from photometric measurements.

7. **Outlier Detection**: Evaluate posterior probabilities of spectral models; flag objects with significantly low probabilities as outliers.

8. **Survey Filter Optimization**: Assess each filter configuration's information content, selecting optimal configurations to enhance taxonomic classification and spectral reconstruction.

9. **Output Results**: Provide reconstructed spectra with uncertainties and recommended filter configurations for survey planning.

This structured approach efficiently implements the core computational framework for spectral reconstruction from sparse photometry, focusing on Bayesian inference and manifold learning.
