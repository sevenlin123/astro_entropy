### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Input:** Reference spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) in near-infrared wavelengths.
   - **Step 1:** Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the reference spectra to derive a set of principal components that capture the spectral variability.
   - **Step 2:** Construct a spectral manifold in the reduced PCA space, representing the distribution of spectra in terms of their principal component scores.

2. **Spectral Reconstruction for Each Object:**
   - **Input:** Sparse photometric measurements (e.g., flux values in selected NIR bands).
   - **Step 1:** Project the sparse photometric measurements into the precomputed PCA latent space.
   - **Step 2:** Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA latent space to determine the most probable principal component scores that explain the observed photometric data.
   - **Step 3:** Map the inferred principal component scores back to the full spectral space to reconstruct the full near-infrared spectrum.
   - **Step 4:** Propagate uncertainties from the Bayesian inference process to quantify the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum.

### Output:
- For each object:
  - Reconstructed full spectrum in near-infrared wavelengths.
  - Associated uncertainties for each wavelength bin.
