**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction using PCA and Bayesian Inference**

1. **Preprocess Reference Spectra**: Normalize the set of near-infrared reference spectra.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Apply PCA to the normalized spectra to extract principal components (eigenvectors and eigenvalues).

3. **Spectral Representation**: Represent each TNO's spectrum as a linear combination of the principal components: 
   \[
   s = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \text{PC}_i
   \]
   where \(w_i\) are the coefficients.

4. **Bayesian Model Setup**: For each TNO, using its photometric measurements \(p\), establish a Bayesian model linking \(p\) to the spectrum \(s\) through a forward model. The prior on \(w_i\) is informed by their distribution in the training set.

5. **Posterior Sampling**: Utilize MCMC or another method to sample the posterior distribution of \(w_i\) given the photometric data \(p\).

6. **Reconstruct Spectrum**: Reconstruct the spectrum \(s\) using the posterior mean or median of the coefficients \(w_i\).

7. **Uncertainty Propagation**: Calculate uncertainties in \(s\) by assessing the spread of the posterior samples.

8. **Output**: Provide the reconstructed spectrum \(s\) and its associated uncertainties.

This algorithm effectively bridges sparse photometry with spectral reconstruction, enabling probabilistic analysis of TNO spectra.
