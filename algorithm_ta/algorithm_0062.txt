The computational algorithm implementing the core reconstruction framework can be structured as follows:

1. **Precompute PCA Basis Functions**
   - Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-IR spectra of TNOs to create low-dimensional basis functions.

2. **Input Sparse Photometric Observations**
   - For each target object, input the available sparse photometric measurements along with their uncertainties.

3. **Bayesian Inversion with Gaussian Process Regression (GPR)**
   - Perform Bayesian inversion in the PCA space using GPR to predict the most probable spectral parameters based on the observed photometric data.

4. **Uncertainty Propagation**
   - Calculate uncertainties on the reconstructed spectra by propagating the uncertainties from the photometric measurements.

5. **Nested-Sampling Posterior Exploration**
   - Use a nested-sampling approach within the Bayesian framework to explore the posterior distribution of possible spectral reconstructions.

6. **Outlier Detection**
   - Identify unusual spectral types or outliers by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the continuous topological manifold.

This algorithm effectively reconstructs full spectra from sparse photometry while quantifying uncertainties and detecting outliers.
