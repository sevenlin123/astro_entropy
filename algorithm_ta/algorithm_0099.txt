1. **Input Data**: Obtain sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) across multiple wavelengths.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Train a PCA model using a sample of known near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of TNOs.
   - Use the PCA to reduce the dimensionality of the spectral data, capturing the dominant modes of variability.

3. **Probabilistic Latent-Space Model**:
   - Construct a probabilistic model based on the PCA representation.
   - Map the observed sparse photometric measurements into this latent space.
   - Perform Bayesian inference within the latent space to estimate the underlying spectral properties.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from the photometric data into the spectral reconstruction.
   - Use Bayesian methods to infer the most probable spectral parameters and their associated uncertainties.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Reconstruct the full near-IR spectrum for each TNO using the inferred latent variables.
   - Calculate confidence intervals (credible intervals) for the reconstructed spectra to assess the uncertainty.

6. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
   - Apply the reconstructed spectral information to evaluate the effectiveness of different observing strategies (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filter configurations).
   - Identify rare or peculiar spectral types by analyzing the probability distribution in the latent space.

7. **Output**:
   - Provide reconstructed spectra along with uncertainty estimates.
   - Generate recommendations for optimal survey configurations.
   - Highlight outliers or unusual spectral types for further investigation.
