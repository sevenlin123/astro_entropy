The computational algorithm involves the following steps:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**: Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-infrared spectra to capture the dominant spectral variability modes.

2. **Bayesian Inference Setup**: Develop a Bayesian model within the PCA-reduced space to perform spectral reconstruction from photometric data.

3. **Latent Variable Inference**: For each object, use observed photometric measurements to infer latent variables via Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction**: Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the inferred latent variables back to the original spectral space.

5. **Uncertainty Propagation**: Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra by propagating the measurement uncertainties from the photometric data through the Bayesian model.

This algorithm efficiently reconstructs full spectra from sparse photometry, enabling detailed analysis of trans-Neptunian objects' compositions.
