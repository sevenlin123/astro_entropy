**Algorithm Description**

1. **Data Preparation**: Collect sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) across multiple bands.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for high-fidelity spectral reconstruction).

3. **Probabilistic Latent-Space Model**:
   - Construct a latent space using the selected principal components.
   - Model the spectral manifold of TNOs within this space.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space.
   - Reconstruct full spectra from photometric data.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Use the inferred posterior distributions to obtain the reconstructed spectra.
   - Ensure empirical credible-interval coverage (approximately 95%) across all wavelengths.

6. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Analyze the information content of different filter configurations (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Identify optimal filter combinations for maximizing spectral reconstruction fidelity.

7. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Detect unusual spectral types by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models.
   - Reconstruct and identify peculiar objects like Neptune Trojans.

8. **Classification and Uncertainty Calibration**:
   - Classify TNOs based on reconstructed spectra.
   - Calibrate uncertainties to ensure reliable taxonomic assignments.

9. **Application**:
   - Utilize the framework for taxonomic classification, survey optimization, and outlier detection in large-scale surveys.

This algorithm effectively bridges sparse photometry and spectroscopy, enabling robust statistical analysis of minor planets' compositions.
