**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Data Preparation**: 
   - Collect a sample of near-infrared (NIR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on this dataset to obtain principal component representations.

2. **Spectral Manifold Modeling**:
   - Use the PCA-derived principal components to model the spectral manifold of TNOs.
   - This manifold represents the low-dimensional structure of spectral variability in the dataset.

3. **Bayesian Inference Setup**:
   - Construct a probabilistic model in the reduced PCA space.
   - Define prior distributions for the parameters based on domain knowledge or data characteristics.

4. **Reconstruction Process**:
   - For each object, given sparse photometric measurements, perform Bayesian inference to estimate the underlying spectral components.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the inference process to quantify uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra.

5. **Dimensionality Consideration**:
   - Use 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification due to their ability to capture the dominant spectral variability.
   - For improved spectral fidelity and uncertainty calibration, employ 8-10 principal components.

6. **Credible Interval Validation**:
   - After reconstruction, check the empirical credible-interval coverage across the wavelengths to ensure reliable uncertainty estimation.

7. **Survey Optimization Application**:
   - Apply the framework to evaluate the information content of different NIR filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters like F090W, F115W, etc.).
   - Determine optimal filter combinations to maximize the information gain for TNO taxonomy.

8. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types**:
   - Use the framework to detect rare spectral types by constraining photometric data.
   - Identify and reconstruct objects with low-probability intermediate models on the spectral manifold.

9. **Final Output**:
   - Generate reconstructed spectra along with their associated uncertainties for each TNO.
   - Provide recommendations for optimal survey configurations based on the analysis.

This algorithm bridges the gap between sparse photometric data and detailed spectral information, enabling robust statistical reconstruction and classification of TNO spectral properties.
