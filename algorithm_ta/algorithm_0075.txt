**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**
   - Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. **Spectral Manifold Modeling**
   - Represent the spectral data in a lower-dimensional latent space using the trained PCA model, capturing the essential variability of the spectra.

3. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**
   - Perform Bayesian inference within this reduced PCA space to reconstruct full spectral curves from sparse photometric measurements.

4. **Uncertainty Propagation**
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from the photometric data through the Bayesian model to estimate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

This structured approach allows for the probabilistic reconstruction of full spectra from sparse photometric data, enabling taxonomic classification and analysis of TNOs.
