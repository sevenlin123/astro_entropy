### Algorithm Description: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects

**Step 1: Preprocess the Data**

1. **Input Data:** Collect near-infrared (NIR) photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
2. **Feature Extraction:** Convert photometric data into a set of features that can be used for spectral analysis.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**

1. **Training Set:** Use a sample of known NIR spectra to train a PCA model.
2. **Dimension Reduction:** Apply PCA to reduce the dimensionality of the spectral data, capturing the dominant modes of variability.
   - **Components Selection:** Retain 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification; use 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity.

**Step 3: Probabilistic Latent-Space Model**

1. **Model Construction:** Build a probabilistic model in the reduced PCA space to represent the spectral manifold of TNOs.
2. **Bayesian Inference:** Perform Bayesian inference within this reduced space to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data while accounting for measurement uncertainties.

**Step 4: Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Reconstruction:** Using the probabilistic model, reconstruct the full spectra of TNOs from their photometric measurements.
2. **Uncertainty Propagation:** Quantify the uncertainties associated with each reconstructed spectrum.

**Step 5: Validation and Application**

1. **Survey Optimization:** Utilize the framework to evaluate the information content of different NIR filters for JWST/NIRCam, identifying optimal configurations for TNO taxonomy.
2. **Outlier Detection:** Apply the model to detect and reconstruct rare spectral types by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models.

**Output:**

- Reconstructed spectra with quantified uncertainties.
- Optimized survey configurations for TNO studies.
- Identified rare spectral types and outliers.

This algorithm bridges the gap between sparse photometry and detailed spectroscopy, enabling robust compositional analysis of minor planets.
