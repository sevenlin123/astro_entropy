The computational algorithm for the probabilistic spectral reconstruction framework can be summarized as follows:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**: 
   - Use a training dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra to compute principal components. This captures the dominant modes of spectral variability relevant for taxonomic classification.

2. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**:
   - For each object, employ Bayesian methods to infer its position in the PCA-reduced latent space. This step uses the sparse photometric data and accounts for measurement uncertainties to determine the most probable latent variables.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Reconstruct the full near-IR spectrum from the inferred latent variables. This includes propagating the uncertainties from the photometric measurements to provide credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.

This framework effectively bridges the gap between sparse photometric data and detailed spectral information, enabling robust spectral reconstruction and classification of trans-Neptunian objects.
