Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry:

1. **Preprocessing**:
   - Load and prepare the training dataset of TNO near-infrared spectra.
   - Remove artifacts and anomalies from the spectral data.
   - Normalize the spectra to a common scale (e.g., unit variance).

2. **Dimensionality Reduction**:
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the normalized spectra.
   - Retain a subset of principal components that capture the majority of spectral variability (e.g., 4-5 components for classification, 8-10 for improved reconstruction).
   - Create a latent-space representation of the spectral manifold using the selected principal components.

3. **Probabilistic Model Construction**:
   - Develop a Bayesian model in the latent space where the principal components are treated as latent variables.
   - Model the relationship between the observed photometric data and the latent variables, incorporating measurement uncertainties.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling or variational inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the latent variables given the observed photometric data.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the model to account for uncertainties in the photometric data.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Project the estimated latent variables back into the original spectral space using the PCA basis vectors.
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by combining the latent variable estimates with the PCA basis.

6. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - Calculate the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra by propagating the uncertainties from the latent variables.
   - Determine credible intervals to assess the confidence in the reconstructed spectra across wavelengths.

7. **Post-Processing**:
   - Validate the reconstructed spectra against reference data if available.
   - Store the results for subsequent analysis or application.

8. **Application**:
   - Apply the reconstructed spectral information to classify TNOs, optimize surveys (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filter selection), and detect rare spectral types.
   - Use the framework to analyze new photometric data for spectral reconstruction and compositional mapping of minor planets.
