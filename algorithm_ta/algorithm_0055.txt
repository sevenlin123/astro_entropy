**Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction (PSR) Algorithm**

1. **Project Data into PCA Space**: Utilize the pre-trained PCA model to transform the sparse photometric measurements into the latent space.

2. **Conduct Bayesian Inference**: Compute the posterior distribution of the latent variables using the prior defined by the PCA covariance matrix and the observed photometric data.

3. **Reconstruct Spectra**: Convert the inferred latent variables back to the spectral domain using the PCA basis vectors.

4. **Propagate Uncertainties**: Transfer photometric uncertainties through the Bayesian model to determine uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

5. **Classify and Detect Anomalies**: Categorize TNOs into taxonomic groups and identify outliers based on the compatibility of latent variables with the expected distribution.

6. **Optimize Survey Filters**: Assess each NIRCam filter configuration's information content using PCA covariance to find optimal observing strategies.

7. **Output Results**: Present reconstructed spectra with uncertainties, taxonomic classifications, outlier flags, and filter performance metrics.

**End of Algorithm**
