**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Train a PCA model using a dataset of known near-infrared spectra to extract principal components (latent space representation).

2. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**:
   - For each object, use its sparse photometric measurements to perform Bayesian inference in the PCA-derived latent space.
   - Infer the most probable underlying spectrum by considering uncertainties and correlations in the photometric data.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the inferred latent space representation back to the original spectral space.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties to provide confidence intervals for the reconstructed spectra.

4. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
   - Apply the framework to determine optimal NIRCam filters for future observations.
   - Identify and reconstruct rare spectral types by exploring intermediate, low-probability components in the latent space.
