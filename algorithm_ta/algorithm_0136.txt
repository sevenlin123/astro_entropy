The algorithm can be broken down into the following steps:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Perform PCA on a sample of near-IR spectra to derive principal components, which capture the major variability in the spectral data.

2. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Use Bayesian methods to infer the posterior distribution of the principal components based on the provided photometric data, considering measurement uncertainties.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum from the inferred principal components, incorporating the propagated uncertainties to ensure reliable spectral recovery.

This structured approach effectively leverages PCA for dimensionality reduction and Bayesian techniques for robust spectral inference, enabling the reconstruction of detailed spectra from sparse photometric measurements.
