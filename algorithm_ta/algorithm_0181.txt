1. Input: Near-infrared photometric data of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. Preprocess: Convert photometric data into a numerical format suitable for dimensionality reduction.

3. Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on a training set of near-infrared spectra to extract principal components (PCs).

4. Model the spectral manifold of TNOs using the obtained principal components.

5. Implement Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data.

6. Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

7. Use the dominant principal components (e.g., 4–5) to perform taxonomic classification of the objects.

8. Apply additional principal components (e.g., 8–10) for enhanced spectral reconstruction fidelity and uncertainty calibration.

9. Optimize surveys by evaluating the information content of NIRCam filters to determine optimal observation configurations.

10. Detect and reconstruct rare spectral types by constraining photometric data to select low-probability intermediate models from the continuous spectral manifold.

11. Output reconstructed spectra with associated uncertainty estimates and information content metrics.
