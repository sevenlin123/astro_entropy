### Algorithm Description: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Extract sparse photometric measurements from this dataset.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Perform PCA on the near-IR spectral dataset to identify the dominant modes of spectral variability.
   - Retain approximately 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification and 8-10 components for improved spectral reconstruction and uncertainty calibration.

3. **Latent-Space Model Construction**:
   - Develop a probabilistic latent-space framework using the PCA model.
   - Map sparse photometric data into this latent space to represent the spectral manifold of TNOs.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Use Bayesian methods to perform inference in the reduced PCA space.
   - Reconstruct full spectra from the sparse photometric measurements while propagating measurement uncertainties.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - For each object, use the probabilistic framework to estimate the full spectrum.
   - Ensure that the reconstructed spectra achieve empirical credible-interval coverage of ~95% across wavelengths.

6. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Assess the information content of JWST/NIRCam filters.
   - Identify optimal filter configurations for future observations based on their ability to capture spectral variability and improve reconstruction fidelity.

7. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Type Reconstruction**:
   - Allow the framework to constrain photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the continuous topological manifold.
   - Detect and reconstruct rare spectral types, such as peculiar Neptune Trojans, by identifying outliers in the latent space.

8. **Output**:
   - Generate a statistically rigorous mapping of the compositional structure of minor planets using the reconstructed spectra.
   - Provide a tool to analyze the diversity of near-IR spectral shapes in large-scale surveys.
