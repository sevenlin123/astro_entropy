### Core Computational Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects

1. **Training Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Construct a principal component representation using a sample of near-IR spectra.
   - Determine the number of principal components needed (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for improved spectral fidelity and uncertainty calibration).

2. **Model the Spectral Manifold:**
   - Use the PCA-derived latent space to model the spectral manifold of TNOs.
   - Represent each object's spectrum as a point in this reduced-dimensional space.

3. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference within the PCA-reduced space to reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric measurements.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.

4. **Reconstruct Full Spectra:**
   - For each object, use the inferred parameters in the latent space to reconstruct the full near-IR spectrum.
   - Calculate credible intervals to assess the uncertainty of the reconstructed spectra.

5. **Survey Optimization and Filter Selection:**
   - Apply the framework to evaluate the information content of different NIRCam filters (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M).
   - Identify filter combinations that maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity and taxonomic information.

6. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types:**
   - Use the probabilistic framework to identify unusual spectral types by constraining photometry.
   - Detect objects with low-probability intermediate models, indicating rare or peculiar spectral classes.

This algorithm bridges the gap between sparse photometry and detailed spectral analysis, enabling robust reconstruction of TNO compositions from limited data.
