**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data Preparation**:
   - Obtain sparse photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   
2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Train a PCA model using a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra.
   - Determine the number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for improved reconstruction fidelity).

3. **Spectral Manifold Modeling**:
   - Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs using the trained PCA.
   - Model the spectral variability in the reduced PCA space.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA-reduced space to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data.
   - Incorporate measurement uncertainties into the reconstruction process.

5. **Reconstruction**:
   - Reconstruct full spectra from the photometric measurements using the probabilistic framework.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties to assess the reliability of the reconstructed spectra.

6. **Output**:
   - Generate reconstructed spectra along with their associated uncertainties.
   - Use the reconstructed spectra for taxonomic classification, survey optimization, or outlier detection as needed.

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometry and spectral reconstruction, enabling statistical mapping of compositional structures in TNOs.
