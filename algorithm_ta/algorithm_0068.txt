**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Step 1: Input**
- Obtain a training dataset consisting of near-infrared ( NIR ) spectra of known trans-Neptunian objects ( TNOs ).
- Gather photometric measurements for target TNOs where spectral information is sparse or absent.

**Step 2: Preprocessing**
a. **Principal Component Analysis ( PCA ):**
   - Apply PCA on the training NIR spectra to extract principal components ( PCs ), which capture the most significant variations in the spectral data.

b. **Determine Number of Components ( K ):**
   - Select K (approximately 4-5 for initial classification, up to 8-10 for detailed reconstruction) based on the explained variance and cross-validation results.

**Step 3: Model Construction**
a. **Spectral Manifold Representation:**
   - Represent the spectral data in a reduced-dimensional latent space using the PCA-derived principal components.

b. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Develop a probabilistic model within the latent space to infer possible spectra from photometric data, incorporating prior knowledge from the training set.

c. **Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - Integrate measurement uncertainties from photometry into the spectral reconstruction process using Bayesian posterior distributions.

**Step 4: Spectrum Reconstruction**
a. **Inference of Latent Variables:**
   - For each target TNO, utilize photometric data to estimate the principal components that best match the observed photometry.

b. **Reconstruction Process:**
   - Map the inferred principal components back to the original spectral space to generate the full reconstructed spectrum.

c. **Credible Intervals Calculation:**
   - Compute credible intervals for each wavelength based on posterior uncertainties to assess the confidence in the reconstructed spectra.

**Step 5: Output Generation**
- Output includes:
  - Full reconstructed spectra for each target TNO.
  - Credible intervals (uncertainties) at each wavelength.
  - Evaluation metrics for optimal survey configurations (e.g., filter selections for JWST/NIRCam).

**Step 6: Optional Post-Processing**
a. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Examine posterior probabilities of the latent space to identify anomalies or peculiar objects like 2006 RJ103 and 2011 SO277.
   - Flag objects with posterior probabilities significantly diverging from expected distributions for further analysis.

This structured approach efficiently bridges sparse photometry and detailed spectral reconstruction, enabling robust taxonomic classification and survey optimization for TNOs.
