**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Obtain Training Data**: Acquire a dataset of near-infrared spectra for trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. **Preprocess Data**: Normalize and standardize the spectral data to ensure consistency and comparability.

3. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Perform PCA on the spectral data to reduce dimensionality, capturing the dominant modes of variability (approximately 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification, up to 8-10 for improved reconstruction).

4. **Build Bayesian Model**: Develop a probabilistic model in the reduced PCA space to perform Bayesian inference, enabling the reconstruction of full spectra from sparse photometric measurements.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**: Use the Bayesian model to predict full spectra from photometric data, incorporating measurement uncertainties to propagate through the reconstruction process.

6. **Survey Configuration Optimization**: Assess the information content of various photometric filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters like F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M) to identify optimal configurations for future observations.

7. **Outlier Detection**: Analyze model outputs, including residuals and posterior probabilities, to identify objects with unusual spectral characteristics, such as peculiar Neptune Trojans like 2006~RJ$_{103}$ and 2011~SO$_{277}$.
