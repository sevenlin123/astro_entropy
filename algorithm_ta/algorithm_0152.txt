**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 8-10) to capture spectral variability and improve reconstruction fidelity.

2. **Projection of Photometric Data**:
   - For each object, use its sparse photometric measurements to project into the pre-trained PCA space.

3. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to estimate the most probable underlying spectrum.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometric data through the model to quantify uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum.

4. **Output Reconstructed Spectrum**:
   - Generate the reconstructed full spectrum for each object, including confidence intervals across wavelengths.

**Note**: This algorithm focuses solely on the core computational method, omitting validation and visualization steps.
