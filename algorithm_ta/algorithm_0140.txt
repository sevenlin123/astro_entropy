1. Input Data:
   - Photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs)
   - Pre-trained Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model derived from a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra

2. Core Algorithm:
   - Perform dimensionality reduction on the near-IR spectra using the pre-trained PCA model to obtain a latent-space representation
   - Apply Bayesian inference in this reduced PCA space to estimate the posterior distribution of the latent variables given the photometric measurements
   - Model the spectral manifold by considering the probabilistic relationships between the latent components and the original spectral data
   - Propagate the uncertainties from the photometric measurements through the Bayesian model to quantify the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra
   - Use the posterior probabilities of the latent components to reconstruct the full spectral curves for each TNO

3. Output:
   - Reconstructed spectral curves for each TNO, representing the best estimate of their near-IR spectra
   - Posterior probabilities over the principal components, capturing the uncertainty in the latent space representation
   - Uncertainty estimates at each wavelength, providing a measure of confidence in the reconstructed spectra
