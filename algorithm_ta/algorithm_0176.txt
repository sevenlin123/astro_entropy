**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Principal Component Analysis and Bayesian Inference**

1. **Training Data Preparation**: 
   - Collect a dataset of near-infrared (NIR) spectra and corresponding photometric measurements of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Perform PCA on the NIR spectra to derive principal components (PCs).
   - Retain 4–5 dominant PCs to capture the main spectral variability relevant for taxonomic classification.

3. **Probabilistic Model Construction**:
   - Develop a probabilistic model within the reduced PC space.
   - Incorporate measurement uncertainties from photometric data into the model.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Use the probabilistic model to perform Bayesian inference, inferring the underlying spectral features from sparse photometric measurements.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Map the inferred features from the reduced PC space back into the original spectral domain to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process to assess credible intervals.

**Output**: Reconstructed full spectra with propagated uncertainties, optimized survey configurations, and detection of rare spectral types from sparse data.
