Algorithm:

1. **Preprocess Input Data**: Convert photometric measurements into a suitable format for processing, ensuring proper normalization and handling of missing values.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Apply PCA on a training set of near-infrared spectra to identify the principal components capturing significant spectral variability.

3. **Bayesian Inference**: Conduct Bayesian analysis within the reduced PCA space to model the relationship between photometric measurements and spectral components, incorporating prior knowledge and measurement uncertainties.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction**: Reconstruct the full spectrum using the inferred components from Bayesian analysis.

5. **Uncertainty Propagation**: Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra by quantifying the propagation of photometric measurement uncertainties through the Bayesian model.

6. **Output**: Provide reconstructed spectra along with confidence intervals, enabling taxonomic classification and outlier detection of trans-Neptunian objects.
