**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry**

**1. Preprocessing:**
   - **Feature Extraction:** Extract spectral features from available near-infrared (near-IR) spectral data of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs).
   - **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):** Apply PCA to the extracted features to derive latent variables (principal components) capturing the main axes of variance in the data.

**2. Bayesian Inference:**
   - **Model Estimation:** Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA-reduced space to estimate the underlying spectral parameters.
   - **Posterior Distributions:** Obtain posterior distributions of the principal components using the observed photometric data.

**3. Probabilistic Model Implementation:**
   - **Uncertainty Quantification:** Develop a probabilistic model to quantify uncertainty in spectral reconstruction, capturing variability and correlations among spectral components.

**4. Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - **Error Analysis:** Compute uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra considering photometric measurement uncertainties.
   - **Reliability Assessment:** Assess the reliability of each spectral reconstruction based on propagated uncertainties.

**5. Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - **Reconstruction:** Combine reconstructed spectral parameters with uncertainties to produce final spectral estimates.
   - **Validation:** Use leave-one-out cross-validation to validate the model's accuracy and robustness.

**6. Outlier Detection:**
   - **Probability Distribution Analysis:** Analyze the probability distribution of the PCA-based model.
   - **Outlier Identification:** Flag objects with unusual spectral features as potential outliers based on deviation from expected distributions.

**7. Survey Optimization:**
   - **Filter Evaluation:** Evaluate the information content of different photometric bands and filter combinations.
   - **Optimal Configuration:** Recommend optimal filter configurations for future surveys to maximize spectral information extraction.

This algorithm enables the prediction of spectral properties of unseen TNOs from sparse photometry, facilitating taxonomy, survey optimization, and outlier detection.
