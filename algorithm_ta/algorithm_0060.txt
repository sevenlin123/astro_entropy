Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

Inputs:
- Sparse photometric data of TNOs
- Pre-trained principal component (PC) model from near-IR spectra
- Covariance matrix of photometric uncertainties

Outputs:
- Reconstructed spectral models for each object
- Probabilistic measures of uncertainty for each spectral feature
- Latent-space representation of TNO spectral manifolds

Steps:

1. **Initialize the Algorithm**: Load the pre-trained PC model, which captures the dominant spectral variability of TNOs based on near-IR spectra.

2. **Extract Photometric Data**: For each TNO, obtain its sparse photometric measurements and their associated uncertainties.

3. **Map to Latent Space**: Project the photometric data into the latent space defined by the principal components. This involves transforming the observed photometry using the pre-trained PC axes.

4. **Bayesian Inference**: Use the covariance matrix of photometric uncertainties to perform Bayesian inference in the latent space. This step estimates the posterior distribution of the underlying spectral features.

5. **Reconstruct Spectra**: Back-propagate the posterior distributions through the PC model to reconstruct the full spectral curves. Calculate the associated uncertainties for each wavelength point.

6. **Output Results**: Generate the reconstructed spectral models, their uncertainties, and the latent-space representations for further analysis.
