Algorithm:

1. Normalize the photometric data of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) to a common scale.

2. Collect a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of TNOs and train a principal component analysis (PCA) model on this sample.

3. Represent the spectral data in the PCA latent space, capturing the principal components that explain the majority of spectral variability.

4. Use the pre-trained PCA model to reconstruct full spectra for each TNO from its normalized photometric measurements.

5. Determine the optimal number of principal components (4–5 for taxonomic classification; 8–10 for improved reconstruction fidelity) based on reconstruction performance.

6. For each TNO, compute the reconstructed spectrum and propagate measurement uncertainties through the latent-space model.

7. Apply the framework to evaluate the information content of NIRCam filters, optimizing future survey configurations for TNO taxonomy.

8. Use the reconstructed spectra to detect and classify rare spectral types by constraining photometric measurements to low-probability intermediate models within the spectral manifold.
