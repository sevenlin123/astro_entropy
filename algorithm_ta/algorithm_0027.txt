**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction via Principal Component Analysis and Bayesian Inference**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training:**
   - Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Identify the dominant principal components that capture the majority of spectral variability (approximately 4-5 for classification, up to 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).

2. **Data Input:**
   - For each TNO, obtain sparse photometric measurements in selected filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters like F090W, F115W, etc.).

3. **Mapping to Latent Space:**
   - Project the photometric data into the PCA latent space. This involves expressing the photometric measurements as a linear combination of the principal components.

4. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference within the PCA-reduced space to estimate the full spectrum of the TNO.
   - Use the identified principal components to model the spectral manifold and infer the most probable underlying spectrum given the photometric data.

5. **Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - Quantify uncertainties associated with the reconstructed spectra by propagating the measurement uncertainties from the photometric data through the Bayesian model.

6. **Output Reconstructed Spectra:**
   - Generate the full reconstructed spectra along with their associated credible intervals, capturing both the central tendency and the uncertainty ranges.

This algorithm effectively bridges sparse photometric data with spectral reconstruction, enabling taxonomic classification and outlier detection in TNO studies.
