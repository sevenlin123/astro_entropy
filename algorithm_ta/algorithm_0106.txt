**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Principal Component Analysis Training:**
   - Train a Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model using a set of reference near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs). This step identifies the major modes of spectral variability, typically capturing 4 to 5 principal components for classification and up to 10 for improved reconstruction.

2. **Sparse Photometric Data Processing:**
   - For each TNO, obtain its sparse photometric measurements and associated uncertainties.

3. **Bayesian Inference in PCA Space:**
   - Utilize the PCA basis to perform Bayesian inference. This involves establishing a probabilistic model that links the photometric data to the latent PCA coefficients, considering both the data and prior distributions of the coefficients.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum of the TNO using the inferred PCA coefficients and the precomputed PCA basis vectors.

5. **Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - Calculate the uncertainties on the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the uncertainties from the photometric data through the Bayesian inference process.

This structured approach efficiently reconstructs full spectra from sparse photometry while quantifying uncertainties, enabling insights into the compositional structure of TNOs.
