**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

**Step 1:** Preprocess the sparse photometric data by converting it into flux values corresponding to specific wavelength bands.

**Step 2:** Project the processed photometric data into the pre-trained Principal Component Analysis (PCA) space. This may involve mapping the data to the PCA wavelengths and applying any necessary transformations to align with the training data.

**Step 3:** Perform Bayesian inference within the PCA space:
   - Define a prior distribution for the PCA coefficients.
   - Calculate the likelihood of the observed data using the PCA coefficients.
   - Utilize a sampling method like Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) to derive posterior distributions of the PCA coefficients.

**Step 4:** Reconstruct the full spectrum by multiplying the posterior-distributed PCA coefficients with the PCA basis vectors.

**Step 5:** Propagate the uncertainties from the PCA coefficient posteriors through the PCA basis vectors to determine the uncertainty estimates for the reconstructed spectrum.

**Step 6:** Output the mean spectrum and the associated uncertainty spectrum.
