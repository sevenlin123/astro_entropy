**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

**Inputs:**
- Collection of TNO near-IR spectra
- Corresponding photometric measurements

**Outputs:**
- Reconstructed full spectra
- Uncertainty estimates

**Steps:**

1. **Preprocess the spectral data:**
   - a. Align all spectra to a common wavelength range.
   - b. Normalize each spectrum to a reference (e.g., flux relative to a baseline or median value).
   - c. Remove spectra identified as outliers.

2. **Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the preprocessed spectra:**
   - a. Compute the covariance matrix of the spectral data.
   - b. Determine the number of principal components.
   - c. Extract the principal components.

3. **Train a probabilistic model:**
   - a. Assume a prior distribution for the latent variables.
   - b. Define the likelihood function relating photometric measurements to latent components.
   - c. Use Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the latent variables.

4. **Reconstruct full spectra:**
   - a. Input photometric measurements into the model for each TNO.
   - b. Compute the posterior distribution of the latent components.
   - c. Map latent components back to the original spectral space.
   - d. Propagate measurement uncertainties to determine uncertainty intervals.

**End.**
