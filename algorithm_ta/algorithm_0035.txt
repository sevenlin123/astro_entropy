**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Data Preparation**: Normalize the input photometric measurements to account for observation noise and calibration variations.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Train PCA on a reference dataset of near-IR spectra of TNOs.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for classification; 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

3. **Spectral Manifold Modeling**:
   - Represent the spectral manifold using the trained PCA to capture the low-dimensional structure of spectral variability.

4. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space**:
   - Use the PCA-derived latent space to perform Bayesian inference, incorporating prior knowledge of spectral features.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometry to spectral reconstruction by sampling the posterior distribution.

5. **Reconstruction**:
   - For each object, project photometric data into the latent space.
   - Perform MCMC sampling to infer the most probable spectral components, ensuring credible intervals around the reconstructed spectra.

6. **Uncertainty Calibration**:
   - Adjust uncertainties based on the posterior predictive distributions to assess the reliability of the reconstructed spectra.

**End Algorithm**
