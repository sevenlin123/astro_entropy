The final answer, after considering the steps involved in the core computational method described in the paper, is presented as follows:

\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&1.\ \text{Train a PCA model on a set of reference near-IR spectra of TNOs.} \\
&2.\ \text{For each TNO, obtain its sparse photometric measurements.} \\
&3.\ \text{Project the photometric measurements into the PCA space.} \\
&4.\ \text{Perform Bayesian inference in the PCA space to estimate the latent variables.} \\
&5.\ \text{Reconstruct the full spectrum using the estimated latent variables.}
\end{aligned}
