**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4–5 for taxonomic classification, 8–10 for improved spectral reconstruction).

2. **Bayesian Inference in Latent Space:**
   - Represent the spectral manifold of TNOs in the reduced PCA space.
   - Perform Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the latent variables (principal components) given sparse photometric measurements.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometry to the latent space.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - Reconstruct the full near-IR spectrum from the estimated latent variables in the PCA space.
   - Quantify uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra by sampling from the posterior distributions of the latent variables.

4. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection:**
   - Apply the framework to evaluate the information content of different filter configurations (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   - Detect rare or peculiar spectral types by identifying low-probability intermediate models in the spectral manifold.

This algorithm enables the reconstruction of full spectra from sparse photometry while accounting for uncertainties and provides a framework for optimizing astronomical surveys and detecting outliers.
