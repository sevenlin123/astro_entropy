**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess Training Data**: Gather and prepare a dataset of known TNO near-infrared spectra. This forms the basis for building the spectral manifold.

2. **Dimensionality Reduction**: Extract principal components (PCs) from the training spectra. Retain 8-10 PCs to adequately capture spectral variability for precise reconstruction.

3. **Spectral Manifold Construction**: Develop a model in the reduced PC space that represents the manifold of possible TNO spectra.

4. **Bayesian Inference**: For each TNO, using its photometric data, perform Bayesian inference within the spectral manifold to estimate the underlying full spectrum. This step incorporates uncertainties in the photometric measurements.

5. **Uncertainty Propagation**: Quantify the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectra by propagating the measurement uncertainties through the Bayesian model.

6. **Survey Optimization**: Evaluate different filter configurations to determine which provides the most information for taxonomic classification, aiding in designing efficient future surveys.

7. **Outlier Detection**: Identify objects with photometry inconsistent with the typical spectral manifold, necessitating detailed spectral reconstruction to detect unusual cases.

This algorithm effectively reconstructs spectra from sparse photometry, handles outliers, and optimizes survey strategies.
