Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction (PSR)

Input:
- Photometric measurements of a trans-Neptunian object
- Pre-trained Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model derived from near-infrared spectra
- Covariance matrix representing measurement uncertainties

Output:
- Reconstructed near-infrared spectrum
- Quantified uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum
- Probabilistic classification information for taxonomy

Steps:
1. **Project photometric data into the latent space**: Map the observed photometric measurements into the PCA-reduced latent space using the pre-trained PCA model.
2. **Perform Bayesian inference in latent space**: Utilize Bayesian statistical methods to estimate the most probable spectral coefficients in the latent space, considering measurement uncertainties.
3. **Reconstruct full spectrum**: Transform the inferred latent space coefficients back into the full spectral space to obtain the reconstructed spectrum.
4. **Quantify uncertainties**: Propagate the uncertainties from the photometric measurements through the probabilistic model to determine the uncertainty in the reconstructed spectrum.
5. **Apply framework for specific applications**: Use the reconstructed spectra and associated uncertainties for tasks such as survey optimization or outlier detection.
