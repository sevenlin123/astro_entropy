The computational algorithm to implement the core reconstruction framework involves the following steps:

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**: 
   - Train a PCA model on a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) to create a latent-space representation.

2. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Convert the sparse photometric measurements into the appropriate format, ensuring normalization and adjustment for different observation conditions.

3. **Bayesian Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data. This involves setting up prior distributions and updating them based on observed photometric data, considering measurement uncertainties.

4. **Spectral Manifold Traversal**:
   - Navigate the spectral manifold using the latent-space representation to select models that best fit the sparse data, especially for rare spectral types.

5. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate the information content of different photometric filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters) to identify optimal configurations for future observations, enhancing the framework's applicability to survey design.

6. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - Integrate uncertainty quantification throughout the process to ensure accurate reconstruction and credible interval estimation, despite the emphasis on ignoring validation procedures.
