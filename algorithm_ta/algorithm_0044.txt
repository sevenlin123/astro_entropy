**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Photometry**

1. **Preprocess Training Spectra**: 
   - Normalize and align the wavelengths of the reference spectra to a common grid.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Compute eigenvectors (principal components) and eigenvalues from the preprocessed spectra.
   - Determine the optimal number of components (K) based on explained variance or cross-validation.

3. **Forward Model Precomputation**:
   - For each PCA eigenvector, calculate its response in each photometric band by integrating the product of the eigenvector and the band's transmission curve.
   - Construct a matrix `F_responses` where rows are PCA components and columns are photometric bands.

4. **Bayesian Inference for Each Object**:
   - Obtain photometric fluxes and their measurement covariances for each object.
   - Set up a Bayesian model with a prior on PCA coefficients and a likelihood based on the forward model.
   - Use MCMC or another method to sample the posterior distribution of the PCA coefficients.

5. **Spectrum Reconstruction and Uncertainty Calculation**:
   - Reconstruct the spectrum using the posterior mean of PCA coefficients and the PCA eigenvectors.
   - Propagate uncertainties from the posterior covariance to estimate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum.

**Output**: Reconstructed spectra and their uncertainties for each object.
