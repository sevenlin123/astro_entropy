1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):** Train a PCA model using a sample of near-infrared spectra to create a principal component representation.

2. **Latent-Space Model:** Develop a probabilistic latent-space framework that maps sparse photometric measurements into the reduced PCA space.

3. **Bayesian Inference:** Perform Bayesian inference within the PCA space to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data while propagating measurement uncertainties.

4. **Model Validation:** Use leave-one-out cross-validation to determine the optimal number of principal components required for accurate spectral reconstruction (4-5 for taxonomy, 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

5. **Spectral Manifold Construction:** Construct the spectral manifold of TNOs based on the PCA representation to capture the underlying structure of spectral variability.

6. **Survey Optimization:** Apply the framework to evaluate the information content of NIRCam filters, identifying optimal configurations for future observations.

7. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types:** Utilize the framework to detect and reconstruct rare spectral types by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the spectral manifold.

8. **Credible Interval Calculation:** Ensure reconstructed spectra achieve ~95% credible-interval coverage across wavelengths, validating uncertainty calibration.

9. **Reconstruction Pipeline:** Implement the pipeline to process sparse photometric data, perform Bayesian inference, and output reconstructed spectra with associated uncertainties.
