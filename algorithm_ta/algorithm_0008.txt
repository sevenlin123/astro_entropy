Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction (PSR)

Input: Sparse photometric measurements of TNOs, Training set of near-IR spectra

Output: Reconstructed full spectra with uncertainty estimates

Steps:

1. Preprocess the training set of near-IR spectra using Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to obtain principal component representations.

2. Train a probabilistic model based on these principal components, capturing the spectral manifold of TNOs.

3. Perform Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space to reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric measurements.

4. Propagate measurement uncertainties through the probabilistic model to estimate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

5. Output the reconstructed spectra along with their uncertainty intervals.
