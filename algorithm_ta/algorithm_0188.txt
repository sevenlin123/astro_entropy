**Core Reconstruction Framework Algorithm**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**: 
   - Train a PCA model on a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs). This step reduces the spectral data into a set of principal components (PCs).

2. **Bayesian Inference in PCA Space**:
   - Use the trained PCA model to project the photometric data into the latent PCA space.
   - Perform Bayesian regression in this reduced space to infer the underlying spectrum. This involves treating the principal components as latent variables.

3. **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling**:
   - Employ MCMC to approximate the posterior distribution of the principal components, considering the photometric data and associated measurement uncertainties.
   - This step allows for uncertainty propagation from the photometric measurements into the reconstructed spectra.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction and Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum from the inferred principal components.
   - Compute the uncertainties on the reconstructed spectrum, capturing the propagated measurement uncertainties.

This structured approach enables the probabilistic reconstruction of spectra from sparse photometric data, facilitating taxonomic classification, survey optimization, and outlier detection.
