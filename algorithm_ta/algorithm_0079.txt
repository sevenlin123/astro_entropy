**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**  
   - Train a PCA model on a reference dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).  
   - Extract principal components (PCs) that represent the spectral manifold of TNOs.  

2. **Bayesian Inference in Reduced Space**  
   - For each TNO with sparse photometric measurements:  
     a. Represent the object’s spectrum in the latent space defined by the PCA basis.  
     b. Perform Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the principal component coefficients, conditioned on the observed photometric data.  

3. **Spectral Reconstruction**  
   - Map the inferred principal component coefficients back to the original spectral space using the PCA basis vectors.  
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum by combining the contributions of all principal components.  

4. **Uncertainty Propagation**  
   - Propagate the uncertainties from the posterior distributions of the principal component coefficients to the reconstructed spectrum.  
   - Calculate credible intervals for the reconstructed spectrum across all wavelengths.  

This algorithm enables the reconstruction of full near-IR spectra from sparse photometric data while quantifying uncertainties, facilitating taxonomic classification and outlier detection for TNOs.
