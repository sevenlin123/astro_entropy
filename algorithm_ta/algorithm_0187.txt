**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Training Data Preparation:**
   - Collect a sample of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   
2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Apply PCA to the training spectra to create a latent-space representation.
   - Determine the number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification, 8-10 for improved reconstruction).

3. **Model Construction:**
   - Use the PCA-derived latent space to build a probabilistic model.
   - Perform Bayesian inference in this reduced space to reconstruct full spectra from sparse photometric data.
   - Incorporate measurement uncertainties in the photometric data into the model.

4. **Reconstruction Process:**
   - For each object, use the sparse photometric measurements to infer the most probable spectral representation in the latent space.
   - Propagate the uncertainties from photometry through the model to the reconstructed spectrum.

5. **Dimensionality Assessment:**
   - Validate the dimensionality of the spectral manifold using the determined number of principal components.

6. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Analyze the information content of different photometric filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   - Identify optimal filter combinations for future observations based on the framework's requirements.

7. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types:**
   - Implement a method to constrain photometry and identify low-probability intermediate models.
   - Detect rare spectral types by analyzing the probability distribution in the latent space.

8. **Output Generation:**
   - Generate reconstructed spectra along with their uncertainties.
   - Provide quantitative assessment of the spectral manifold's dimensionality.
   - Output optimal photometric filter configurations for surveys.
   - Highlight detected rare spectral types and their reconstructions.

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometric data and detailed spectral reconstruction, enabling robust analysis of TNOs' compositional structures.
