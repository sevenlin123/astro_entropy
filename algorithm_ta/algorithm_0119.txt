**Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Algorithm**

1. **Input Data**: Obtain photometric measurements of the trans-Neptunian object, including observed magnitudes and their corresponding uncertainties.

2. **Preprocessing**: 
   - Normalize the photometric data.
   - Address missing values through imputation techniques.

3. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Projection**:
   - Utilize a pre-trained PCA model derived from a reference dataset of near-IR spectra.
   - Map the photometric data into the PCA latent space.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Employ Bayesian methods to estimate PCA coefficients that best fit the photometric data, incorporating measurement uncertainties.

5. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum using the estimated PCA coefficients.

6. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum by considering the uncertainties in the photometric data and the Bayesian posterior distributions of the PCA coefficients.

7. **Output**:
   - Provide the reconstructed spectrum along with its confidence intervals.

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometry with spectral analysis, enabling robust taxonomic classification and outlier detection for trans-Neptunian objects.
