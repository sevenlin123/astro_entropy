**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - Collect a sample of near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the collected spectra to create a reduced-dimensional representation (latent space).
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for improved spectral reconstruction fidelity).

2. **Model Construction:**
   - Use the PCA-derived latent space to construct a probabilistic model that represents the spectral manifold of TNOs.
   - Employ Bayesian inference within this reduced space to model the relationship between photometric measurements and underlying spectra.

3. **Reconstruction Process:**
   - For each object, obtain sparse photometric measurements.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties through the probabilistic model.
   - Reconstruct the full spectrum using the latent space representation, considering the uncertainties and the dominant spectral variability modes.

4. **Spectral Information Quantification:**
   - Assess the information content of different photometric filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters) to optimize future surveys for TNO taxonomy.
   - Evaluate the utility of each filter configuration in terms of its contribution to spectral reconstruction fidelity.

5. **Outlier Detection and Rare Spectral Types:**
   - Apply the framework to photometric data to identify objects with low-probability intermediate models, indicating unusual spectral types.
   - Reconstruct and analyze these outliers to understand their unique spectral characteristics.

**Output:** Reconstructed full spectra with propagated uncertainties, optimized survey configurations, and identified outlier objects.
