The algorithm outlines the steps to reconstruct the spectrum of trans-Neptunian objects from sparse photometry using a probabilistic framework. Here's the structured description:

1. **Preprocess Training Spectra**: Align and normalize the wavelengths and flux values of the training set of near-infrared spectra.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Apply PCA to the preprocessed spectra to extract orthogonal basis vectors that capture the major modes of spectral variability.

3. **Bayesian Model Setup**: For each TNO, express its spectrum as a linear combination of the PCA components plus an intercept term.

4. **Forward Model Definition**: Create a forward model that projects the PCA coefficients into the photometric bands.

5. **Likelihood Function**: Define a likelihood function to measure the discrepancy between the projected photometric fluxes and the observed data.

6. **Prior Distributions**: Assign prior distributions to the PCA coefficients based on the explained variance from PCA.

7. **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling**: Use MCMC to explore the posterior distribution of the PCA coefficients given the observed photometric data.

8. **Posterior Processing**: Compute the posterior mean and standard deviation of the PCA coefficients from the MCMC samples.

9. **Spectrum Reconstruction**: Reconstruct the full spectrum by combining the mean coefficients with the PCA basis vectors.

10. **Uncertainty Propagation**: Calculate uncertainties in the reconstructed spectrum by propagating the standard deviations of the posterior coefficients through the linear combination.

This algorithm efficiently reconstructs spectra from sparse photometry, leveraging PCA and Bayesian inference to quantify uncertainties.
