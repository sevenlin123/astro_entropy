**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess the Reference Spectral Library**:
   - Normalize and remove noise from the near-infrared (near-IR) spectral data of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the preprocessed spectral library to extract a set of principal components (PCs), capturing the major variations in the spectral data.

3. **For Each TNO**:
   a. **Obtain Photometric Data**: Collect the sparse photometric flux measurements.
   b. **Bayesian Inference in PCA Space**: Use the photometric data to estimate the PC coefficients, incorporating measurement uncertainties.
   c. **Spectral Reconstruction**: Reconstruct the full spectrum by mapping the estimated PC coefficients back to the original spectral domain.

4. **Output**:
   - Provide the reconstructed spectra along with their uncertainties.

This algorithm effectively bridges sparse photometric data with spectral reconstruction, utilizing Bayesian methods in a reduced PCA space to propagate uncertainties and infer spectral information.
