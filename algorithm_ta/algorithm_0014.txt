1. Preprocess observed photometric data of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) by normalizing and aligning measurements to a common wavelength grid.

2. Retrieve a pre-trained principal component analysis (PCA) model constructed from a reference set of TNO near-infrared (near-IR) spectra.

3. Apply dimensionality reduction to the reference spectra using the PCA model, retaining 4-5 principal components to capture the majority of spectral variability relevant for taxonomic classification.

4. Implement Bayesian inference in the reduced PCA space, modeling the likelihood of photometric measurements given a spectrum and the prior distribution of possible spectra based on the PCA components.

5. Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to explore the posterior distribution of possible spectra given the photometric data, quantifying uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.

6. Post-process the MCMC samples to derive the best-fit spectrum, confidence intervals, and outlier detection metrics.

7. For survey optimization, compute the information content of different NIR filter combinations by evaluating their impact on uncertainty propagation using the Bayesian framework.
