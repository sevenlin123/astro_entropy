Here is the step-by-step algorithm:

1. **PCA Model Training**: Train a Principal Component Analysis (PCA) model on a reference dataset of TNO near-infrared spectra to establish the spectral manifold.

2. **Probabilistic Modeling**: Construct a Bayesian model using the PCA-derived latent variables (principal components). This model links the photometric measurements to the underlying spectral components.

3. **Bayesian Inference**: Apply Bayesian methods to estimate the latent variables given the photometric data, enabling the reconstruction of the full spectrum.

4. **Uncertainty Propagation**: Calculate uncertainty estimates for the reconstructed spectra by evaluating the posterior distributions of the latent variables.

This algorithm leverages PCA for dimensionality reduction and Bayesian inference for reconstructing spectra from sparse photometry while considering uncertainties.
