**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Preprocess the Data**: 
   - Collect near-infrared spectral measurements of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs).
   - Extract photometric data from these spectral measurements.
   - Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to reduce the dimensionality, creating a latent space representation.

2. **Bayesian Inference in the Latent Space**:
   - Develop a probabilistic model mapping photometric data to the latent space.
   - Apply Bayes' theorem to estimate posterior distributions of latent variables.

3. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Use estimated latent variables to project back into the original spectral space, obtaining reconstructed spectra.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometric data through the model to reconstructed spectra.

4. **Determine Optimal Principal Components**:
   - Through leave-one-out cross-validation, find that 4-5 components suffice for taxonomic classification; 8-10 improve reconstruction fidelity.

5. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate information content of JWST/NIRCam filters.
   - Identify optimal filter combinations (e.g., F090W, F115W, F410M, F460M) for TNO taxonomy.

6. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Use the probabilistic framework to constrain photometric data.
   - Detect objects with photometry significantly deviating from the spectral manifold, indicating unique spectral types.

7. **Output**:
   - Provide reconstructed spectra with uncertainties.
   - Recommend optimal survey configurations.
   - Identify outliers and unusual spectral types in the dataset.

**Note**: This algorithm focuses solely on the core computational method, omitting validation and visualization steps.
