### Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a seed set of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of TNOs, excluding rare and outlier spectral types to form a clean training dataset.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the seed spectra to define a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a continuous probability distribution over the latent space, augmenting the training data.

3. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Forward model the augmented spectra with the target instrument's (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) filter responses to generate synthetic photometric measurements.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (~20% relative reflectance uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric observations.

4. **Training the AutoML Regressor**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees using the synthetic photometry as inputs and the latent coefficients as targets.
   - Use AutoML (e.g., AutoGluon) to automate hyperparameter tuning and model selection.

5. **Taxonomic Classifier Training**:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent coefficients to predict taxonomic classes (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).
   - This classifier operates directly within the latent space to improve classification accuracy.

6. **Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Pass the photometry through the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients.
     - Apply the inverse PCA transform to the latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     - Simultaneously use the taxonomic classifier to predict the spectral type probabilities.

7. **Validation**:
   - Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) on the original seed spectra to assess reconstruction fidelity (Pearson correlation coefficient, RMSE) and taxonomic classification accuracy.
   - Evaluate the empirical confidence intervals against the actual spectral uncertainties.

8. **Applications**:
   - **Survey Optimization**: Analyze different filter configurations to maximize information content for spectral reconstruction.
   - **Outlier Detection**: Identify objects with low posterior probabilities in the latent manifold, indicating unusual spectral types.

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometry and detailed spectral modeling, enabling accurate reconstruction and classification of TNO spectra while propagating uncertainties and detecting anomalies.
