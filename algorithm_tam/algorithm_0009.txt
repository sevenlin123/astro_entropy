### Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Algorithm

**Step-by-Step Description:**

1. **Preprocessing and Training Data Preparation:**
   - Collect and preprocess near-infrared spectra of TNOs into a standardized format.
   - Select a representative training set of 51 TNO spectra, focusing on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organic types).

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Perform PCA on the training spectra to extract principal components (latent space).
   - Determine the optimal number of components (typically 8–10) that capture significant spectral variability while maintaining reconstruction fidelity.

3. **Density Estimation (Kernel Density Estimation - KDE):**
   - Use non-parametric KDE to construct a smooth probability distribution in the latent space defined by the PCA components.
   - Adjust the bandwidth to balance fidelity and smoothness, ensuring a realistic manifold without overfitting or excessive smoothing.

4. **Synthetic Data Generation:**
   - Generate synthetic spectra by sampling the latent space according to the KDE-defined distribution.
   - Convolve these synthetic spectra with the instrumental response (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters) to produce corresponding synthetic photometric measurements.

5. **Model Training (AutoML Regressor and Classifier):**
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees) to map sparse photometric inputs to latent coefficients.
   - Simultaneously train a taxonomic classifier within the latent space to classify TNO spectra into predefined compositional groups.

6. **Forward Modeling and Inference:**
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - Apply the inverse PCA transform to these coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Use the taxonomic classifier to predict the spectral type based on the latent representation.

7. **Uncertainty Quantification:**
   - Propagate photometric uncertainties through the model to provide credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.
   - Assess the reliability of reconstructions using metrics like RMSE and MAE.

8. **Outlier Detection and Survey Optimization:**
   - Identify outliers by detecting latent coefficients that fall outside the expected distribution, indicating unique spectral types.
   - Evaluate different filter configurations to determine the most informative set for future surveys, optimizing for reconstruction fidelity and efficiency.

**Final Output:**
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectra from sparse photometry.
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification of TNOs.
- Assessment of photometric filter configurations for optimal survey design.
- Detection of outliers requiring targeted spectroscopic follow-up.
