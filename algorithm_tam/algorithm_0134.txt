### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. **Training Data Preparation**:
   - Collect and preprocess a set of reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 spectra) representing dominant taxonomic classes (Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - Exclude outliers and rare spectral types to focus on the main population.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the training spectra to reduce dimensionality.
   - Retain top 4-5 principal components for general reconstruction, and up to 10 for improved fidelity.
   - Use the PCA basis to define a low-dimensional latent space.

3. **Data Augmentation with Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**:
   - Project the training spectra onto the PCA latent space.
   - Apply KDE to smooth and generate synthetic latent vectors, creating a continuous manifold that interpolates between training spectra.
   - Set the KDE bandwidth (e.g., h ≈ 0.5) to balance local fidelity and global smoothness.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Simulate photometric observations by convolving the spectra with filter transmission curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric uncertainties.

5. **AutoML Regression Model Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML framework, e.g., AutoGluon) to map sparse photometry to latent space coordinates.
   - Use synthetic photometry and corresponding latent vectors as training data.

6. **Taxonomic Classifier Training**:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent space coordinates to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).
   - Use the true taxonomic labels of the training spectra for supervised learning.

7. **Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Pass the photometry through the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent space coordinates.
     - Project the predicted latent coordinates back to the full spectrum using inverse PCA transformation.
     - Obtain a reconstructed spectrum along with its uncertainty estimates.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Use the latent space coordinates from the reconstruction step.
   - Apply the trained taxonomic classifier to assign a probability distribution over spectral types.

9. **Validation and Performance Assessment**:
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to evaluate reconstruction fidelity.
   - Compute metrics: RMSE, Cosine Similarity, PCC, absolute deviation in key absorption bands, and classification accuracy.
   - Validate credible interval coverage to ensure uncertainty calibration.

10. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
    - Use the framework to simulate and optimize filter configurations for future surveys.
    - Identify outliers by analyzing deviations in the posterior distribution of latent space coordinates.

**Output**: For each object, the algorithm provides:
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectrum with uncertainty bounds.
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification.
- Detection of unusual spectral types or outliers.
