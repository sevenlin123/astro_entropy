**Algorithm Description: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Collect Reference Spectra**: Gather high-resolution near-IR spectra of TNOs, focusing on dominant taxonomic types (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).

2. **Perform PCA**: Apply Principal Component Analysis to the collected spectra to extract principal components, reducing the data into orthogonal latent variables.

3. **Determine Principal Components**: Retain 8-10 principal components to capture sufficient spectral variability for accurate reconstruction and classification.

4. **Project Spectra onto Latent Space**: Convert the seed spectra into their latent coefficients using the PCA basis.

5. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**: Smooth the latent space using KDE with a bandwidth of ~0.5 to create a continuous probability distribution, enhancing robustness.

6. **Generate Synthetic Photometry**: Convolve each augmented spectrum with instrument throughput curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) to simulate photometric measurements.

7. **Add Noise to Photometry**: Incorporate Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to account for observational uncertainties.

8. **Train AutoML Regressor**: Use AutoGluon to train a model mapping sparse photometry to latent coefficients, ensuring resistance to noise and overfitting.

9. **Reconstruct Spectra**: For new photometric data, predict latent coefficients and apply inverse PCA to obtain full spectra, constrained by the learned manifold.

10. **Classify Taxonomic Type**: Use a latent-space classifier to predict taxonomic types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic) based on latent coefficients.

11. **Performance Evaluation**: Conduct LOOCV to validate reconstruction accuracy, credible interval coverage, and classification success rates.

12. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**: Use the framework to identify optimal filter combinations and detect spectral outliers, enhancing scientific discovery and data reliability.

This structured approach effectively bridges sparse photometric data with detailed spectral analysis, enabling robust reconstruction and classification of TNO spectra.
