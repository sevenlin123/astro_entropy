**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Step-by-Step Description:**

1. **Training Data Preparation:**
   - **Select Seed Spectra:** Gather a curated set of 51 representative TNO spectra, excluding rare types, to form the training set.
   - **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):** Apply PCA to the selected spectra to identify dominant modes of spectral variability, reducing the dimensionality to a latent space (e.g., 4-5 principal components for classification, up to 10 for detailed reconstruction).
   
2. **Data Augmentation:**
   - **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE):** Use KDE on the PCA latent space to generate synthetic spectra, creating a smooth manifold that fills gaps between real data points.
   - **Parameter Tuning:** Adjust KDE bandwidth to ensure the manifold is realistic and smooth, avoiding overfitting or oversmoothing.

3. **Forward Modeling and Training:**
   - **Synthetic Photometry Generation:** Simulate photometric measurements by convolving the augmented spectra with JWST/NIRCam filter responses, adding noise to reflect real observational uncertainties.
   - **AutoML Regressor Training:** Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) to map photometric inputs to latent PCA coefficients, using the synthetic photometry.

4. **Reconstruction Pipeline:**
   - **Input Processing:** For new photometric observations, compute photometry through the NIRCam filter set.
   - **Latent Space Prediction:** Use the trained AutoML model to predict latent coefficients from the observed photometry.
   - **Spectrum Reconstruction:** Apply inverse PCA transformation to the predicted coefficients to reconstruct the full high-resolution spectrum.

5. **Classification and Outlier Detection:**
   - **Latent Space Classification:** Train a classifier on the PCA latent space to assign taxonomic labels (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics) to new observations.
   - **Outlier Identification:** Flag objects whose predicted latent coefficients fall outside the training manifold as outliers, indicating rare or unusual spectral types.

6. **Performance Evaluation (LOOCV):**
   - **Iterative Validation:** For each spectrum, remove it from the training set, retrain the model, and evaluate the reconstructed spectrum against the original using RMSE, Correlation Coefficient, and Coverage Probability.
   - **Aggregation:** Collect metrics across all iterations to assess overall framework performance.

7. **Survey Optimization and Applications:**
   - **Filter Configuration Testing:** Evaluate different NIRCam filter combinations to maximize information content for spectral reconstruction and classification.
   - **Target Selection:** Identify objects requiring spectroscopic follow-up based on their position in the latent space.

**End of Algorithm**
