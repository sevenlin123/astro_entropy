### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework for TNOs

1. **Training Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a sample of near-infrared TNO spectra, focusing on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - Exclude outliers and rare spectral types to maintain a representative and stable training set.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the collected spectra to derive principal components (PCs).
   - PCs represent the dominant modes of spectral variability, capturing the essential structure for taxonomic classification and spectral reconstruction.

3. **Density Estimation**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent PCA coefficients to create a smooth probability distribution.
   - This distribution defines the prior in the Bayesian framework, ensuring physically plausible spectral reconstructions.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - For each augmented spectrum, compute synthetic photometric measurements by convolving with the instrument's filter response curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric uncertainties.

5. **AutoML Regression Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) using synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients as input-output pairs.
   - This regressor maps sparse photometric observations to the latent PCA space.

6. **Inverse PCA Transform**:
   - Apply the inverse PCA transformation to the predicted latent coefficients to reconstruct high-resolution spectra.
   - The reconstruction inherits the physical smoothness and absorption features of the training data.

7. **Probabilistic Taxonomy Classification**:
   - Train a secondary classifier on the latent PCA coordinates to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - Use the predicted probabilities for confident spectral type identification.

8. **Reconstruction and Validation**:
   - For each object, use the AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients from sparse photometry.
   - Project these coefficients back to full spectra using the inverse PCA.
   - Validate reconstruction accuracy using metrics like RMSE and credible interval coverage.

9. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
   - Assess different photometric filter configurations to identify those with optimal information content.
   - Detect outliers by flagging objects with reconstructed spectra deviating significantly from the expected manifold.

10. **Final Output**:
    - Provide reconstructed spectra with uncertainties, taxonomic classifications, and identified outliers.
    - Enable efficient survey design and detailed analysis of TNO surface compositions.
