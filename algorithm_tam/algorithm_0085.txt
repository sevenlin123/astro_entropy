### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

**Step-by-Step Description:**

1. **Preprocessing of Training Spectra:**
   - Gather a seed set of reference TNO spectra, focusing on dominant taxonomic classes.
   - Exclude rare or outlier spectral types to avoid biasing the latent space.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Perform PCA on the preprocessed spectra to extract principal components (latent space).
   - The number of components (K) is determined based on variance explained (~4-5 for classification, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).

3. **Density Estimation for Latent Space:**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a continuous probability distribution in the latent space.
   - This generates synthetic spectra to augment the training data, stabilizing the regression model.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation:**
   - For each augmented spectrum, generate synthetic photometric measurements using the instrument's filter throughput curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Add Gaussian noise (20% uncertainty) to simulate realistic observational conditions.

5. **AutoML Regression Training:**
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) using the synthetic photometry as inputs and PCA coefficients as targets.
   - The model learns the mapping from photometry to latent space.

6. **Inverse PCA Transform:**
   - Implement the inverse PCA transform to convert predicted latent coefficients back into full spectral reflectance.

7. **Cross-Validation and Performance Assessment:**
   - Conduct leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to evaluate RMSE and Cosine Similarity between true and reconstructed spectra.
   - Measure uncertainty propagation through the model to determine credible intervals.

8. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Evaluate different filter combinations for their information content by assessing their impact on reconstruction fidelity and required principal components.

9. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Use the trained model to detect anomalies by comparing predicted latent coordinates against the training distribution.
   - Identify objects with posterior probabilities inconsistent with standard taxonomic classes.

10. **Probabilistic Taxonomy Classification:**
    - Apply a secondary classifier within the latent space to predict taxonomic categories based on the PCA coefficients.

11. **Uncertainty Quantification:**
    - Propagate photometric uncertainties through the model to determine uncertainties in reconstructed spectra, providing credible intervals for features and overall reliability.

**Summary of the Algorithm:**
The framework uses PCA to create a latent space, then trains an AutoML regressor to map sparse photometry to this space. Through inverse PCA, high-resolution spectra are reconstructed, with uncertainties quantified and outliers identified.
