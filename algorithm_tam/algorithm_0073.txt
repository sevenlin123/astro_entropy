**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

**Step-by-Step Description:**

1. **Data Preparation:**
   - Obtain a reference set of TNO spectra (e.g., 51 observed spectra) representative of known taxonomic classes (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).
   - Exclude rare or outlier spectral types to focus on dominant classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Apply PCA to the reference spectra to derive a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Determine the number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for improved fidelity).

3. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) Augmentation:**
   - Project the reference spectra onto the PCA latent space.
   - Use KDE to smooth and fill gaps in the latent space, creating a continuous manifold that approximates the spectral distribution of the training set.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation:**
   - Forward model the augmented spectra through the target instrument's filters (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) to generate synthetic photometric measurements.
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric observations.

5. **Training the AutoML Regressor:**
   - Use the synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients as training data.
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) to regress from sparse photometry to latent space coordinates.

6. **Inverse PCA Transform:**
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - Apply the inverse PCA transformation to reconstruct the full spectrum.

7. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Train a secondary classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic, Methanol) from latent coefficients.
   - Classify new objects by projecting their latent coefficients and predicting the most probable taxonomic group.

8. **Validation and Evaluation:**
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction fidelity (RMSE) and taxonomic accuracy.
   - Compute empirical credible interval coverage to verify probabilistic constraints on reconstructed spectra.

9. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection:**
   - Analyze filter sensitivity to optimize photometric surveys for maximal information yield.
   - Detect outliers by identifying latent coefficients in low-density regions of the manifold.

**Final Output:**
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectra from sparse photometry.
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification of TNOs.
- Identification of outliers for follow-up spectroscopy.
- Optimized filter configurations for future surveys.
