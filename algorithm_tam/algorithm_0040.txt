**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Data Preparation**  
   - Gather near-infrared (NIR) spectra of TNOs, focusing on dominant taxonomic types (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).  
   - Exclude outlier spectral types to ensure the PCA latent space represents the main population accurately.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**  
   - Perform PCA on the collected spectra to reduce dimensionality.  
   - Extract principal components (PCs) that capture the majority of spectral variability (typically 4-5 PCs for taxonomic classification; 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).  

3. **Latent Space Construction**  
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to smooth the distribution of latent coefficients derived from PCA.  
   - Augment the training data by sampling synthetic spectra from the smoothed latent manifold to improve model stability and generalization.  

4. **Forward Modeling of Photometry**  
   - Simulate photometric measurements by convolving the spectra with the instrument's filter transmission curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).  
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to account for observational uncertainties.  

5. **AutoML Regressor Training**  
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) to regress from sparse photometric data to latent PCA coefficients.  
   - Utilize automated machine learning frameworks (e.g., AutoGluon) to handle hyperparameter tuning and model selection.  

6. **Bayesian Inference and Posterior Sampling**  
   - Define a Bayesian model where the posterior is proportional to the likelihood of observing the photometry given a spectrum multiplied by the prior distribution of spectra (encoded in the latent space).  
   - Use the trained AutoML model to predict latent coefficients from new photometric observations.  

7. **Spectral Reconstruction**  
   - Project the predicted latent coefficients through the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.  
   - Compute uncertainty estimates by propagating the variance in the latent coefficients through the PCA inversion.  

8. **Taxonomic Classification**  
   - Classify the TNO into predefined spectral types (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organic, Methanol) using a secondary classifier trained on the latent manifold.  

9. **Validation and Performance Assessment**  
   - Conduct leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to evaluate reconstruction accuracy and credible-interval coverage.  
   - Analyze RMSE across different taxonomic classes and identify any biases or limitations.  

10. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**  
    - Assess different NIRCam filter combinations to determine those that maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity.  
    - Detect outliers by evaluating the probability density of objects in the latent space; flag those with unusually low density for detailed spectroscopic follow-up.
