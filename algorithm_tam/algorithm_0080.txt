### Algorithm Description

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a reference dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) classified into known taxonomic types (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - Exclude rare or outlier spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic groups.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the reference spectra to reduce the spectral data into a set of principal components (latent space).
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (approximately 4–5) that capture the majority of spectral variability relevant for taxonomic classification.

3. **Density Estimation**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA latent space to create a smooth probability distribution, enabling the generation of synthetic latent vectors that fill gaps between sparse reference spectra.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - For each synthetic latent vector, forward-model the corresponding spectrum through the NIRCam filter set to generate synthetic photometric measurements.
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (relative reflectance uncertainty of ~20%) to simulate realistic photometric observations.

5. **Training the AutoML Regressor**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to map sparse photometric inputs to the latent coefficients in the PCA space.
   - Use the synthetic photometry and corresponding latent vectors as the training dataset.

6. **Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained AutoML regressor to predict the latent coefficients.
   - Apply the inverse PCA transform to these coefficients to reconstruct the full spectral energy distribution (SED).

7. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Utilize a secondary classifier trained on the PCA latent space to predict the taxonomic type (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic, Methanol) of the object based on the reconstructed latent coefficients.

8. **Validation and Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - Evaluate the reconstructed spectra against the true spectra using metrics like empirical credible interval coverage.
   - Propagate photometric uncertainties into spectral uncertainties to assess the reliability of the reconstructed spectra.

9. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
   - Analyze the information content of NIRCam filters to identify optimal configurations for future surveys.
   - Detect rare or unusual spectral types by identifying points in the latent space that deviate significantly from the learned distribution.

### Key Steps:

- **Dimensionality Reduction**: PCA is used to reduce spectral data into a manageable number of components, ensuring stable inversion.
- **Generative Prior**: The KDE-based latent manifold provides a continuous probability distribution, enabling robust reconstruction from sparse inputs.
- **Probabilistic Inference**: Bayesian framework propagates uncertainties from photometry to spectral reconstructions.
- **AutoML Integration**: Efficiently maps sparse photometry to latent coefficients, avoiding manual tuning and ensuring scalability.

This algorithm bridges the gap between sparse photometric data and detailed spectral analysis, enabling efficient exploration of TNO populations with limited spectroscopic observations.
