**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Data Preparation**: Acquire a curated sample of 51 TNO spectra, focusing on dominant types (Water, CO₂, Organics, Methanol) while excluding rare outliers.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Perform PCA on the training spectra to extract principal components, capturing the main sources of spectral variability (approximately 4-5 components for basic classification, up to 10 for detailed reconstruction).

3. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) Augmentation**: Apply KDE to the PCA-derived latent space to create a smooth probability distribution, generating synthetic spectra that interpolate between the original data points.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**: For each augmented spectrum, compute expected photometric measurements using the JWST/NIRCam filter response curves.

5. **Noise Injection**: Add Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) to the synthetic photometry to simulate real observational uncertainties.

6. **AutoML Regressor Training**: Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees to predict latent PCA coefficients from the noisy photometric inputs.

7. **Taxonomy Classifier Training**: Develop a secondary AutoML model to classify latent coefficients into predefined taxonomic categories.

8. **Spectral Reconstruction and Classification**: For new observations, use the regressor to predict latent coefficients, invert PCA for full spectra, and classify using the trained taxonomy model.

9. **Cross-Validation Assessment**: Evaluate model performance using LOOCV, measuring reconstruction accuracy and classification success.

10. **Filter Optimization**: Analyze reconstructed spectra to determine optimal NIRCam filter combinations for future surveys.

11. **Outlier Detection**: Identify objects with unusual latent space positions, indicating rare spectral types, by exploring the entire latent manifold.
