### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Training Phase**:
   - **Collect Reference Spectra**: Gather a set of 51 near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) representing dominant taxonomic classes.
   - **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Apply PCA to the reference spectra to derive principal components. Retain 4-5 components for basic reconstruction and up to 10 for improved fidelity.
   - **Density Estimation**: Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a continuous manifold in the latent PCA space, ensuring smooth transitions between spectral types.
   - **Generate Synthetic Photometry**: Forward model the synthetic spectra through the NIRCam filter set to simulate photometric observations. Introduce 20% noise to account for observational uncertainties.
   - **Train AutoML Regressor**: Train an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) to map sparse photometry to latent PCA coefficients.

2. **Forward Inference**:
   - **Input Photometry**: Provide new sparse photometric observations.
   - **Predict Latent Coefficients**: Use the trained AutoML regressor to predict the latent coefficients in the PCA space.
   - **Reconstruct Spectrum**: Apply the inverse PCA transform to obtain the full spectrum, inheriting physical smoothness from the training data.
   - **Classify Taxonomic Type**: Apply a secondary classifier on the latent coefficients to determine the taxonomic type (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic) with associated probabilities.

3. **Validation and Application**:
   - **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**: Validate the model using LOOCV to assess reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty calibration.
   - **Survey Optimization**: Evaluate the information content of NIRCam filters to optimize future photometric surveys.
   - **Outlier Detection**: Identify rare spectral types by constraining photometry to select low-probability intermediate models from the latent manifold.

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometry and high-dimensional spectral reconstruction, providing probabilistic constraints on TNO surface compositions for large-scale surveys.
