### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework for TNOs

**Step 1: Define the Latent Space**

1. **Training Data Preparation**: Collect a sample of 51 near-infrared TNO spectra, excluding rare outliers.
2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Apply PCA to the training spectra to derive principal components (latent space). The number of components (K) is determined through validation, typically K=8–10 for high fidelity.
3. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**: Use non-parametric KDE to create a continuous probability distribution over the latent space, ensuring smooth transitions between spectral types.

**Step 2: Forward Modeling Photometry**

1. **Synthetic Data Generation**: For each augmented spectrum in the latent space, convolve the spectrum with the instrument's filter responses (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) to simulate photometric observations.
2. **Noise Injection**: Add Gaussian noise (20% relative uncertainty) to the synthetic photometry to account for observational uncertainties.

**Step 3: Training the AutoML Regressor**

1. **Data Pairing**: Combine the synthetic photometry with their corresponding latent coefficients to form the training dataset.
2. **AutoML Regression**: Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoGluon) to regress from photometric inputs to latent space coefficients.

**Step 4: Bayesian Inference for Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Input Photometry**: Provide new photometric observations as input.
2. **Predict Latent Coefficients**: Use the trained AutoML model to predict the latent coefficients for the input photometry.
3. **Reconstruct Spectrum**: Apply the inverse PCA transform to the latent coefficients to obtain the full spectral reconstruction.

**Step 5: Taxonomic Classification**

1. **Latent Features Extraction**: Use the predicted latent coefficients as features.
2. **Classifier Training**: Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic categories (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).

**Step 6: Performance Validation via LOOCV**

1. **Iterative Exclusion**: Exclude each seed spectrum one at a time, recompute the latent manifold with the remaining data, and retrain the models.
2. **Evaluate Metrics**: Measure RMSE, credible interval coverage, and classification accuracy to assess framework reliability.

**Step 7: Applications**

1. **Survey Optimization**: Test different filter combinations to determine the most informative configurations for spectral reconstruction.
2. **Outlier Detection**: Identify unusual spectra by analyzing density in the latent space, flagging points with significantly low density.

**End**: The algorithm provides reconstructed spectra and taxonomic classifications from sparse photometry, enabling efficient analysis of TNOs with minimal spectroscopic observations.
