### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework for TNOs

1. **Input Data Preparation**:
   - Obtain a set of near-infrared spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) as the training data.
   - Collect photometric observations from instruments like JWST/NIRCam.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the training spectra to extract principal components (PCs).
   - PCs represent the dominant modes of spectral variability.

3. **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**:
   - Use KDE on the latent coefficients derived from the PCA to create a smooth probability distribution in the latent space.
   - This distribution serves as the prior for Bayesian inference.

4. **Synthetic Data Generation**:
   - Generate synthetic photometric data by convolving the augmented spectra (from PCA and KDE) with the instrument's filter responses.
   - Inject noise (e.g., 20% relative reflectance uncertainty) to simulate realistic observational conditions.

5. **AutoML Regressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to map the synthetic photometry to the latent space (PCA coefficients).

6. **Inverse PCA Transform**:
   - Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients from new photometric observations.
   - Apply the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.

7. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Classify the predicted latent coefficients into predefined spectral types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics) using a separate AutoML classifier.

8. **Validation and Performance Metrics**:
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy (MAE, RMSE, Pearson r) and coverage ratio.
   - Evaluate feature recovery and model uncertainty.

9. **Filter Information Content Analysis**:
   - Determine the contribution of each photometric filter to spectral reconstruction by excluding them sequentially and measuring impact on metrics (MAE, RMSE).

10. **Outlier Detection**:
    - Identify objects with reconstructed spectra significantly deviating from the learned manifold, indicating rare or novel spectral types.

11. **Survey Optimization**:
    - Recommend optimal filter configurations based on information content, balancing information gain and redundancy.

12. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
    - Quantify uncertainties from photometric measurements and propagate them into spectral reconstructions for reliable confidence intervals.

13. **Output**:
    - Provide reconstructed spectra, taxonomic probabilities, and outlier flags for each object.

This algorithm bridges the gap between sparse photometry and detailed spectral analysis, enabling efficient study of TNO surface compositions.
