**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs**

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a sample of near-infrared (NIR) spectra of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs).
   - Exclude rare spectral types and outliers to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the collected spectra to derive principal components (PCs).
   - PCs represent the most significant variations in spectral data.

3. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Use PCA to project the spectra into a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth probability distribution in this latent space, augmenting the training data.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - For each augmented spectrum, generate synthetic photometric measurements by convolving with the filter response functions of the target instrument (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate real observational conditions.

5. **Model Training**:
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map the synthetic photometry to the latent space coordinates.
   - Simultaneously train a classifier within the latent space to predict taxonomic categories.

6. **Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict latent coordinates.
   - Apply inverse PCA to reconstruct the full spectrum from the latent coordinates.

7. **Performance Evaluation**:
   - Conduct leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy using metrics like RMSE, correlation coefficient, and coverage.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components for accurate reconstruction.

8. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate different photometric filter configurations to identify those that maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity.

9. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Identify objects with latent coordinates outside the expected distribution, indicating rare or unusual spectral types.

10. **Output**:
    - Provide reconstructed spectra and taxonomic classifications for each object.
    - Generate probabilistic constraints on surface composition, aiding in further spectroscopic follow-up.
