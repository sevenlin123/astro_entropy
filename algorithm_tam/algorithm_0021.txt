### Core Computational Algorithm for Spectral Reconstruction

1. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Obtain and preprocess a dataset of near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Select a representative training set of ~50 high-quality TNO spectra, focusing on dominant taxonomic types (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organic-type).

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the selected training spectra to derive principal components (PCs).
   - Determine the number of PCs that capture the dominant spectral variability (approximately 4-5 for taxonomic classification, up to 10 for detailed reconstruction).

3. **Density Estimation in Latent Space**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth probability distribution in the latent PCA space.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (~20% relative reflectance uncertainty) into synthetic photometry derived from the augmented spectra.

4. **Training the AutoML Regressor**:
   - Utilize an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (e.g., AutoGluon) to regress sparse photometric measurements onto the latent PCA coefficients.
   - Ensure the model captures the physical correlations between photometric inputs and spectral outputs.

5. **Inverse PCA Transformation**:
   - Apply the inverse PCA transformation to the predicted latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectra.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometry to the reconstructed spectra, ensuring probabilistic calibration.

6. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent PCA coefficients to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organic, Methanol-rich).
   - Use the posterior probabilities from the classifier for confident taxonomic assignments.

7. **Validation and Performance Assessment**:
   - Conduct leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to evaluate reconstruction accuracy and empirical credible interval coverage.
   - Measure Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) between reconstructed and actual spectra, focusing on key diagnostic features (e.g., CO₂ double-dip, organics absorption).

8. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
   - Simulate different filter configurations to optimize photometric surveys for TNO taxonomy.
   - Detect unusual spectral types by identifying objects whose reconstructed spectra fall outside the expected manifold.

9. **Output and Applications**:
   - Provide reconstructed spectra, taxonomic classifications, and credible intervals for each object.
   - Apply the framework to guide future observations, identify spectral outliers, and inform survey design for efficient compositional mapping.

### Summary of Steps:
1. Preprocess and PCA reduce TNO spectra.
2. Generate synthetic data using KDE for latent space regularization.
3. Train AutoML models for photometric-to-latent and taxonomic prediction.
4. Reconstruct full spectra using inverse PCA and propagate uncertainties.
5. Validate using LOOCV and assess performance metrics.
6. Apply framework to survey optimization and outlier detection.
