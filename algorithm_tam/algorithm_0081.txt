**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Data Preparation:**
   - Gather a training set of reference TNO spectra.
   - Exclude rare spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Apply PCA to the training spectra to obtain principal components (latent space).
   - Determine the number of components (4-5 for classification, 8-10 for high fidelity).

3. **Latent Space Construction:**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a continuous probability distribution in the latent space.
   - Bandwidth adjustment ensures smooth and realistic interpolation between spectra.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation:**
   - For each latent vector, generate synthetic spectra and compute forward-modeled photometry using NIRCam filter curves.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (20% relative uncertainty) to simulate real photometric data.

5. **Training the AutoML Regressor:**
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoGluon) to map sparse photometry to latent coefficients.
   - The model captures non-linear relationships between photometry and latent space.

6. **Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained AutoML model to predict latent coefficients.
   - Project these coefficients back to the full spectrum using inverse PCA.

7. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Classify the latent coefficients to determine the taxonomic type (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).
   - Output confidence probabilities for each class.

8. **Performance Evaluation:**
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess RMSE and credible interval coverage.
   - Validate reconstruction accuracy across the entire spectrum.

9. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Simulate various NIRCam filter configurations to determine optimal sets for minimizing RMSE and improving information content.
   - Balance resolution and coverage to maximize survey efficiency.

10. **Outlier Detection:**
    - Compute the probability distribution of latent coefficients for each object.
    - Flag objects with low probability as outliers, indicating rare or unusual spectral types.

This algorithm efficiently reconstructs full spectra and classifies TNOs from sparse photometry, enabling taxonomy, survey optimization, and outlier detection.
