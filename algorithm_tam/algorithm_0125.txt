### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. **Data Collection**: Gather a set of near-infrared spectra for trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs), focusing on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type). Exclude rare or singular spectral types to maintain a "clean" training set.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the collected spectra to extract principal components (PCs).
   - Use the top 4-5 PCs to represent the dominant spectral variability for initial reconstruction, and consider up to 8-10 PCs for improved fidelity and uncertainty calibration.

3. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Project the spectra onto the PCA basis to form latent coefficients.
   - Perform Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on these coefficients to create a continuous probability distribution representing the prior.

4. **Synthetic Data Augmentation**:
   - Generate synthetic spectra by sampling from the KDE-defined latent distribution.
   - Convolve these synthetic spectra with the effective throughput curves of the JWST NIRCam filters to compute synthetic photometry with added Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%).

5. **Model Training**:
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees) to map sparse photometric inputs to the latent coefficients.
   - Simultaneously train a taxonomic classifier on the latent space to predict spectral types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).

6. **Forward Inference**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
   - Apply the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Use the taxonomic classifier to predict the spectral type and assess uncertainty.

7. **Validation and Cross-Validation**:
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to evaluate reconstruction accuracy and credibility.
   - Compare reconstructed spectra with original data to measure RMSE and assess credibility intervals.

8. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Analyze information content of different filter configurations to identify optimal JWST/NIRCam settings for future observations.

9. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Identify unusual spectral types by detecting points far from the learned manifold.

10. **Uncertainty Propagation**:
    - Model probabilistic uncertainties during reconstruction and validate via MCMC sampling, ensuring credible intervals match empirical errors.

5. **Taxonomic Classification**: Classifies spectra using latent features.
