### Core Reconstruction Algorithm

1. **Training Data Preparation**:
   - Gather a curated sample of near-infrared spectra from TNOs, focusing on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).
   - Exclude outliers and rare spectral types to ensure a "clean" training set.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the training spectra to identify the dominant modes of spectral variability.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (K) that capture the majority of spectral diversity (e.g., K = 4–5 for taxonomic classification, K = 8–10 for high-fidelity reconstruction).

3. **Latent Space Construction**:
   - Use the PCA coefficients as a low-dimensional latent space representation.
   - Augment this space using Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth probability distribution, enabling interpolation between training examples.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Forward-model the synthetic photometric measurements by convolving the spectra with the instrument's filter throughput curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric observations.

5. **AutoMLRegressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to regress from sparse photometry to the latent PCA coefficients.
   - The model learns the mapping from photometric inputs to the latent space, incorporating the noise and uncertainty in the data.

6. **Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained AutoML model to predict the latent coefficients.
   - Project these coefficients back to the full spectral space using the inverse PCA transformation to reconstruct the high-resolution spectrum.

7. **Classification and Outlier Detection**:
   - Classify the latent coefficients into predefined taxonomic groups using a secondary classifier.
   - Identify outliers by detecting points in the latent space that lie beyond the typical manifold structure.

8. **Validation and Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Validate the framework through leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy and credible interval coverage.
   - Propagate photometric uncertainties through the model to provide probabilistic constraints on the reconstructed spectra.

9. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate the information content of different filter configurations by simulating photometric measurements and assessing their ability to constrain the latent space.

10. **Outlier Detection**:
    - Flag objects whose latent representations fall outside the dense regions of the training manifold for detailed spectroscopic follow-up.
