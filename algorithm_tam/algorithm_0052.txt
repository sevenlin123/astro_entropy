### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. **Training Data Preparation**:
   - Collect and preprocess near-infrared (near-IR) spectra of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Exclude rare spectral types and outliers to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the preprocessed spectra to derive principal components.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (4-5 for dominant variability, up to 10 for high fidelity).

3. **Data Augmentation**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to generate synthetic latent vectors, expanding the training dataset while maintaining physically plausible spectra.

4. **Model Training**:
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map sparse photometric inputs to latent PCA coefficients.
   - Simulate photometric observations by convolving spectra with instrument filter curves and adding noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty).

5. **Forward Inference Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Predict latent coefficients using the trained AutoML model.
     - Project these coefficients back to the full spectrum using inverse PCA transformation.

6. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Classify the object into predefined spectral types (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type) based on latent space analysis.

7. **Validation and Uncertainty Propagation**:
   - Apply leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy and empirical credible interval (ECI) coverage (~95%).
   - Calculate RMSE and classification accuracy to evaluate model performance.

8. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Compute χ² statistic between observed and reconstructed photometry.
   - Identify outliers with χ² exceeding a threshold (e.g., >12 for 3σ), indicating potential new spectral types or measurement issues.

9. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Evaluate information content of different photometric filter configurations.
   - Optimize filter choices to maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity for future surveys.

10. **Posterior Analysis**:
    - Generate reconstructed spectra and taxonomic probabilities for each object.
    - Provide confidence intervals and classification scores for scientific interpretation.
