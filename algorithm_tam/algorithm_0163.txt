**Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Training Phase:**
   - **Collect Seed Spectra:** Gather a curated set of 51 high-quality near-infrared TNO spectra representing dominant taxonomic types (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).
   - **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):** Apply PCA to the seed spectra to derive a low-dimensional latent space. Retain 4-5 principal components for taxonomic classification and 8-10 for high-fidelity reconstruction.
   - **Density Estimation:** Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth probability distribution in the latent space, augmenting the training data for robust regression.
   - **Synthetic Photometry Generation:** Convolve the augmented spectra with the JWST/NIRCam filter curves to simulate photometric measurements. Inject 20% Gaussian noise to account for observational uncertainties.

2. **Model Training:**
   - **AutoML Regressor:** Train an ensemble of Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees using the synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coefficients as input-output pairs.
   - **Classifier Training:** Train a separate AutoML classifier on the latent space to predict taxonomic labels (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics).

3. **Reconstruction Phase:**
   - **Input Photometry:** For new observations, pass the sparse photometric measurements through the trained AutoML regressor to predict the latent coefficients.
   - **Spectral Reconstruction:** Apply the inverse PCA transform to the predicted latent coefficients to obtain the full spectral energy distribution (SED).
   - **Taxonomic Classification:** Use the trained classifier to determine the probability distribution of taxonomic types based on the latent coefficients.

4. **Validation and Evaluation:**
   - **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):** Validate the framework by iteratively removing each seed spectrum and assessing reconstruction accuracy.
   - **Uncertainty Quantification:** Compute credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra to ensure proper calibration and coverage.
   - **Performance Metrics:** Evaluate using RMSE, Pearson correlation, and visual residual plots to assess accuracy and precision.

5. **Applications:**
   - **Survey Optimization:** Analyze information content of filter configurations to maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity.
   - **Outlier Detection:** Identify unusual objects by detecting low-probability latent states, aiding in discovery of rare spectral types.

6. **Output:**
   - For each input photometry:
     - Reconstructed high-resolution SED.
     - Probability distribution of taxonomic classification.
     - Uncertainty estimates for the reconstructed spectrum.
     - Flagged outliers or anomalies for further spectroscopic follow-up if applicable.
