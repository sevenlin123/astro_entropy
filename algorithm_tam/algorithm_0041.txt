**Algorithm Description: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Training Data Preparation**
   - **Seed Spectra Selection**: Collect a curated sample of TNO spectra (approximately 51) representing dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: Apply PCA to the seed spectra to derive a low-dimensional latent space. The number of principal components (e.g., 8–10) is determined to capture the majority of spectral variability relevant for taxonomic classification.

2. **Latent Space Augmentation**
   - **Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)**: Use KDE on the PCA latent coordinates to create a smooth probability distribution, enabling the generation of synthetic latent vectors. This step augments the training data to enhance the model's ability to handle sparse inputs without overfitting.

3. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**
   - **Forward Modeling**: For each augmented spectrum, simulate photometric measurements by convolving the spectrum with the effective throughput curves of the target instrument (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).
   - **Noise Injection**: Add Gaussian noise with a relative reflectance uncertainty of ~20% to the synthetic photometry to simulate realistic observational uncertainties.

4. **AutoML Regression Training**
   - **Model Training**: Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML framework) to map the sparse photometric inputs to the PCA latent coefficients. This step establishes the mapping from observed photometry to the latent space.

5. **Inverse PCA Transformation**
   - **Spectrum Reconstruction**: Using the trained AutoML model, predict latent coefficients for new photometric observations. Apply the inverse PCA transformation to these coefficients to reconstruct the full high-resolution spectrum.

6. **Taxonomic Classification**
   - **Latent Space Clustering**: Train a secondary classifier on the PCA latent space to partition the manifold into distinct taxonomic classes (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Methanol-rich, Methanol-poor organics). Classify new objects based on their latent coordinates.

7. **Outlier Detection**
   - **Probability Density Analysis**: Compute the probability density of the latent space for new objects. Flag objects with probabilities below a threshold (e.g., 5σ) as outliers, indicating potential rare spectral types.

8. **Uncertainty Quantification**
   - **Confidence Intervals**: Propagate photometric uncertainties into the latent space to derive uncertainty estimates for each reconstructed spectral point, providing a quantitative measure of confidence in the spectral features.

9. **Survey Optimization**
   - **Filter Information Content**: Evaluate the mutual information between NIRCam filters and latent spectral coefficients to identify optimal filter configurations for high-fidelity spectral reconstruction.

**Final Output:**
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectra with uncertainty quantification for each TNO.
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification of each object.
- Detection of outliers and rare spectral types.
- Optimized filter configurations for future photometric surveys.
