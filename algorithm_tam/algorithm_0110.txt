### Algorithm Steps:

1. **Training Data Preparation**: 
   - Collect a sample of known TNO spectra (e.g., 51 observed spectra) representing dominant taxonomic types (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - Exclude rare or unusual spectral types to focus on the main population.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the collected spectra to extract principal components (latent space).
   - Determine the optimal number of components (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).

3. **Data Augmentation**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA latent space to generate synthetic spectra, expanding the training dataset and creating a smooth manifold.

4. **Forward Modeling**:
   - Simulate photometric measurements by convolving synthetic spectra with instrument-specific filter responses (e.g., JWST/NIRCam).
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% uncertainty) to simulate real observational conditions.

5. **AutoMLRegressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to map sparse photometry to latent PCA coefficients.
   - Use the augmented synthetic data for training to improve model generalization.

6. **Inverse PCA Transform**:
   - Reconstruct full spectra from the predicted latent coefficients using the inverse PCA transform.
   - Ensure smooth and physically plausible spectra by constraining to the learned manifold.

7. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference in the latent space to compute posterior distributions of latent coefficients and reconstructed spectra.
   - Propagate measurement uncertainties from photometry to the spectral reconstruction.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Train a secondary classifier in the latent space to predict taxonomic types (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organic, Methanol).
   - Classify new objects based on their latent coefficients derived from photometry.

9. **LOOCV Validation**:
   - Conduct leave-one-out cross-validation to assess reconstruction accuracy and validate the framework's performance.
   - Ensure the model generalizes well to unseen data.

10. **Applications**:
    - **Survey Optimization**: Analyze information content of different filter combinations to prioritize optimal configurations for future observations.
    - **Outlier Detection**: Identify rare spectral types by detecting deviations in the latent space.
    - **Spectral Classification**: Efficiently classify objects using the reduced latent space representation.

11. **Uncertainty Quantification**:
    - Provide credible intervals for reconstructed spectra, enabling assessment of reliability and confidence in spectral features.

12. **Output**:
    - For each object, output reconstructed spectrum, taxonomic classification probabilities, and uncertainty estimates.
