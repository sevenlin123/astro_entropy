### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

**Step-by-Step Description:**

1. **Data Collection**: Gather sparse photometric measurements of TNOs, such as those obtained from JWST/NIRCam filters.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Train PCA on a reference set of TNO spectra to create a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Use the principal components to represent the dominant spectral variations.

3. **Density Estimation**:
   - Perform Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent coefficients derived from the reference spectra to create a continuous probability distribution in the latent space.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Convolve the reference spectra with the instrument's filter throughput curves to simulate observed photometry.
   - Inject Gaussian noise to account for photometric uncertainties.

5. **Model Training**:
   - Use an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees) to map the sparse photometric inputs to the latent space coordinates.
   - Train a secondary classifier within the latent space to predict taxonomic categories.

6. **Forward Inference**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained AutoML model to predict latent coefficients.
   - Apply the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Optionally, classify the spectrum into predefined taxonomic categories.

7. **Validation**:
   - Perform leave-one-out cross-validation to assess reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty calibration.
   - Evaluate the framework's ability to detect outliers and rare spectral types by constraining photometric inputs to low-probability intermediate models.

8. **Output**:
   - Generate reconstructed spectra and associated uncertainties.
   - Provide taxonomic classifications if applicable.

**Key Features**:
- Uses sparse photometry to reconstruct full spectra probabilistically.
- Combines dimensionality reduction (PCA) and probabilistic modeling for robust inference.
- Includes a classifier for taxonomic identification within the latent space.
- Propagates measurement uncertainties through the reconstruction process.
