### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework for Trans-Neptunian Objects

1. **Training Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a sample of near-infrared TNO spectra.
   - Exclude rare and outlier spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organics).
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on the selected spectra to define a low-dimensional latent space.

2. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Use PCA to project the spectra into a reduced-dimensional space, capturing the main modes of spectral variability.
   - Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to smooth and extend the latent space, creating a continuous probability distribution that approximates the spectral manifold.

3. **Synthetic Data Generation**:
   - Forward-model the spectra using the defined latent space to generate synthetic photometric measurements.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (relative reflectance uncertainty ~20%) into the synthetic photometry to simulate realistic observational conditions.

4. **Model Training**:
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map sparse photometric inputs to latent space coefficients.
   - Simultaneously train a probabilistic classifier within the latent space to identify taxonomic classes.

5. **Forward Inference Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Use the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients.
     - Project the coefficients back to the full spectral space using inverse PCA to reconstruct the spectrum.
     - Classify the spectrum into taxonomic categories based on the latent space probabilities.

6. **Validation and Performance Assessment**:
   - Conduct Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) to evaluate reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty calibration.
   - Measure Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) and check credible interval coverage for each reconstructed spectrum.

7. **Applications**:
   - **Survey Optimization**: Identify optimal filter configurations for future observations by evaluating information content and confidence intervals.
   - **Outlier Detection**: Flag objects with posterior probabilities significantly diverging from the latent manifold for detailed spectroscopic follow-up.

8. **Output**:
   - For each object, produce:
     - A reconstructed high-resolution spectrum.
     - Probabilistic taxonomic classification.
     - Confidence intervals for spectral features and classification probabilities.
