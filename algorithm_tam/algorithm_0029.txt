**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Initialize Reference Spectra**: Gather a curated set of 51 high-quality near-infrared spectra of TNOs, representative of dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Decompose the reference spectra into principal components (PCs) to define a low-dimensional latent space. Typically, retain 4-5 PCs for classification and 8-10 for detailed reconstruction.

3. **Data Augmentation with KDE**:
   - Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent space derived from PCA to smooth the distribution of spectral coefficients, creating a continuous manifold.

4. **Forward Modeling Synthetic Photometry**:
   - Simulate photometric measurements by convolving the augmented spectra with the instrumental response functions (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters).

5. **Noise Injection**:
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (relative uncertainty ~20%) to the synthetic photometry to account for observational uncertainties.

6. **AutoML Regression Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (using AutoGluon) to map the noisy photometry to the latent PCA coefficients.

7. **Latent Space Inference**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained AutoML model to predict the corresponding latent coefficients.

8. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Apply the inverse PCA transform to the predicted latent coefficients to obtain the full spectral reconstruction.

9. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Use a secondary classifier trained on the latent space to determine the posterior probabilities of taxonomic types (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organic) for each object.

10. **Output Results**:
    - Generate the reconstructed spectrum and the most probable taxonomic classification for each TNO.

This algorithm efficiently reconstructs spectra and classifies TNOs using sparse photometry, bridging the gap between available observations and detailed spectroscopic analysis.
