### Core Computational Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction

**1. Training the PCA Model:**
   - **Input:** Reference TNO spectra (seed set).
   - **Process:** Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to derive principal components capturing the main spectral variations.
   - **Output:** PCA basis vectors and eigenvalues.

**2. Generating Synthetic Spectra:**
   - **Input:** PCA coefficients from PCA model.
   - **Process:** Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth distribution of latent variables.
   - **Output:** Augmented synthetic latent vectors.

**3. Training the AutoML Regressor:**
   - **Input:** Synthetic photometry derived from forward modeling using JWST/NIRCam filters.
   - **Process:** Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees to map photometry to latent coefficients.
   - **Output:** Trained encoder model for photometric-to-latent conversion.

**4. Training the Taxonomy Classifier:**
   - **Input:** PCA coefficients and taxonomic labels of seed spectra.
   - **Process:** Train another AutoML model to classify taxonomic types based on latent variables.
   - **Output:** Taxonomic classifier model.

**5. Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):**
   - **Input:** Omitted spectrum from seed set.
   - **Process:** Retrain models using remaining data, assess reconstruction accuracy, measure credible interval coverage.
   - **Output:** Metrics for validation and uncertainty analysis.

**6. Spectral Reconstruction:**
   - **Input:** New photometric observations.
   - **Process:** Use trained regressor to predict latent coefficients; apply inverse PCA to obtain full spectrum.
   - **Output:** Reconstructed spectrum with propagated uncertainties.

**7. Taxonomic Classification:**
   - **Input:** Latent coefficients from regressor.
   - **Process:** Pass through taxonomic classifier to get probability distribution of spectral types.
   - **Output:** Probabilistic taxonomic assignment.

**8. Outlier Detection:**
   - **Input:** Latent coefficients from new observations.
   - **Process:** Compare with the distribution of training data; identify deviations as outliers or rare types.
   - **Output:** Flags for potential outliers or unusual spectral types.

**Final Output:**  
- Reconstructed spectra from photometry.  
- Probabilistic taxonomic classification of TNOs.  
- Outlier detection for rare spectral types.
