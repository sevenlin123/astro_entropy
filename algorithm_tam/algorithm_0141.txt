The algorithm extracts TNO spectral information from sparse photometric data through a probabilistic framework. It utilizes PCA to define a low-dimensional latent space, which captures the dominant spectral variations. An AutoML regressor is trained to map sparse photometry to these latent coefficients. The latent space is augmented using KDE to smooth the probability distribution. During inference, new photometric data is converted to latent coefficients, then transformed back to high-resolution spectra via inverse PCA. LOOCV validates reconstruction accuracy, and the framework identifies optimal NIRCam filters and detects spectral outliers.
