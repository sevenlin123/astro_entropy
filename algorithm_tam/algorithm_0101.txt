### Core Reconstruction Framework Algorithm

**Step-by-Step Algorithm:**

1. **Training Data Preparation:**
   - Collect a sample of reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 spectra).
   - Exclude rare spectral types and outliers to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):**
   - Apply PCA to the reference spectra to reduce dimensionality.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (PCs) that capture the majority of spectral variability (e.g., 4-5 for classification, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).

3. **Latent Space Construction:**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a smooth probability distribution in the PCA-derived latent space.
   - Augment the training data by sampling synthetic latent vectors from the KDE to enhance the dataset size.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation:**
   - Convolve each augmented spectrum with the instrument's filter response curves (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters) to simulate observed photometric magnitudes.
   - Introduce Gaussian noise to simulate realistic photometric uncertainties (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty).

5. **Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) Training:**
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map noisy photometric measurements to latent PCA coefficients.
   - Use the augmented latent training data and synthetic photometry for model training.

6. **Inverse PCA Transform:**
   - Implement the inverse PCA transformation to convert predicted latent coefficients into full-resolution spectral reconstructions.
   - Ensure the reconstructed spectra inherit physical smoothness and absorption features from the training data.

7. **Bayesian Inference and Uncertainty Propagation:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference to compute posterior distributions of latent coefficients given photometric observations.
   - Propagate uncertainties from photometric measurements through the AutoML model and PCA transform to estimate credible intervals for the reconstructed spectra.

8. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Train a secondary classifier in the latent space to assign taxonomic labels (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type) based on PCA coefficients.
   - Evaluate classification accuracy and probabilistic confidence using leave-one-out cross-validation.

9. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Identify objects with low posterior probabilities in the latent space as outliers.
   - Use these outliers to detect rare spectral types or guide follow-up spectroscopic observations.

10. **Survey Optimization:**
    - Analyze the information content of different filter configurations using the framework.
    - Optimize filter selections to maximize spectral reconstruction fidelity and scientific yield for future surveys.

**Final Output:**
- Reconstructed high-resolution spectra from sparse photometry.
- Probabilistic taxonomic classifications.
- Detection of rare or outlier spectral types.
- Optimized photometric survey configurations for TNO studies.
