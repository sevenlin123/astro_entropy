### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Gather near-infrared spectra of TNOs, focusing on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - Exclude rare or outlier spectra to maintain a representative training set.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the selected TNO spectra to extract principal components (latent space representation).
   - Determine the number of principal components (4-5 for classification, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction).

3. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA coefficients to create a continuous probability distribution in the latent space.
   - Adjust KDE bandwidth to balance smoothing and fidelity to the training data.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Forward-model the spectra using the NIRCam filter set to simulate photometric observations.
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (20% relative uncertainty) to account for observational realism.

5. **AutoML Regression Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to map synthetic photometry to latent coefficients.
   - Ensure robustness to sparse and noisy inputs by leveraging the generated training database.

6. **Inverse PCA Transformation**:
   - Use the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients from new photometric observations.
   - Apply inverse PCA to reconstruct high-resolution spectra from the latent coefficients.

7. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent manifold to predict taxonomic probabilities.
   - Classify objects based on their position in the latent space, enhancing decision boundary separation.

8. **Uncertainty Propagation and Outlier Detection**:
   - Quantify uncertainties by sampling from the posterior distributions of latent coefficients.
   - Identify out-of-manifold objects as potential outliers or rare spectral types.

9. **Performance Validation**:
   - Conduct LOOCV to evaluate reconstruction fidelity, empirical credible interval coverage, and classification accuracy.
   - Assess the framework's robustness and generalizability.

10. **Applications**:
    - Optimize filter configurations for future surveys based on information content.
    - Detect rare spectral types and inform targeted spectroscopic follow-ups.
