### Algorithm Description

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a set of reference TNO spectra (e.g., 51 spectra) from existing studies.
   - Exclude rare or outlier spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Perform PCA on the reference spectra to reduce them into a low-dimensional latent space.
   - Determine the number of principal components (e.g., 4-10) that capture the necessary spectral variability for accurate reconstruction and classification.

3. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA latent coordinates to create a smooth probability distribution, augmenting the training data.
   - Choose an appropriate bandwidth for KDE to balance local fidelity and global smoothness.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Forward-model the augmented spectra with the target instrument's (e.g., JWST/NIRCam) filter curves to simulate photometric measurements.
   - Inject noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to account for observational uncertainties.

5. **Model Training**:
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map the sparse photometry to the latent coefficients.
   - Use the generated synthetic photometry and corresponding latent coordinates for training.

6. **Bayesian Inference for Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained AutoML model to predict latent coefficients.
   - Project these coefficients through the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
   - Calculate posterior uncertainties to assess confidence in the reconstruction.

7. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Train a secondary classifier on the latent space to predict the spectral type (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).
   - Classify new objects based on their latent coordinates.

8. **Cross-Validation and Evaluation**:
   - Apply Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy and credible interval coverage.
   - Measure taxonomic classification accuracy to validate the framework's performance.

9. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
   - Use the framework to evaluate different filter configurations and optimize photometric surveys for TNO studies.
   - Identify outliers by examining the probability density of latent coordinates for new observations.

10. **Applications and Analysis**:
    - Apply the reconstructed spectra to study TNO taxonomy, surface composition, and physical properties.
    - Detect peculiar objects and refine the framework for future missions and large-scale surveys.
