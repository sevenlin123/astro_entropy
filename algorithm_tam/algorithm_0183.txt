### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Preprocessing and Training Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a set of reference TNO spectra, focusing on dominant taxonomic classes (e.g., Water-type, CO₂-type, Organics-type).
   - Exclude rare or outlier spectral types to maintain a clean training set.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Training**:
   - Perform PCA on the collected spectra to derive principal components.
   - Use the top 4-5 components for reconstruction, capturing dominant spectral variability.

3. **Latent Space Definition**:
   - Construct a latent space using PCA coefficients.
   - Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a continuous probability distribution in the latent space, augmenting the training data for stability.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - Forward model each augmented spectrum through the NIRCam filter set to generate synthetic photometric observations.
   - Introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) to simulate realistic photometric uncertainties.

5. **AutoML Regressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (AutoML) to regress from sparse photometry to latent PCA coefficients.
   - Use the synthetic photometry and corresponding PCA coefficients for training.

6. **Taxonomic Classifier Training**:
   - Train a secondary AutoML classifier on the latent PCA coefficients to predict taxonomic categories (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).

7. **Reconstruction Pipeline**:
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Pass photometry through the trained AutoML regressor to predict latent coefficients.
     - Decode the latent coefficients using inverse PCA to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     - Simultaneously classify the object into a taxonomic category using the latent space classifier.

8. **Bayesian Posterior Sampling**:
   - Perform posterior sampling in the latent space to quantify uncertainties in the reconstructed spectra.
   - Propagate these uncertainties to the spectral reconstruction, providing confidence intervals.

9. **Validation and Performance Assessment**:
   - Conduct Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) to evaluate reconstruction accuracy and confidence interval coverage.
   - Measure Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) and empirical coverage across the test set.

10. **Output**:
    - Reconstructed spectrum with uncertainty bounds.
    - Probabilistic taxonomic classification for the object.

This algorithm bridges sparse photometry and detailed spectroscopy, enabling statistically rigorous reconstruction and classification of TNO spectra.
