The algorithm is structured into five main steps: data preparation, latent space construction, training the photometric regressor, Bayesian spectral reconstruction, and performance evaluation. Here's a concise breakdown:

1. **Data Preparation**: Collect and preprocess known TNO spectra and associated photometric data from observations.

2. **Latent Space Construction**: Apply PCA to the known spectra to create a lower-dimensional latent space representation, capturing the main spectral variations.

3. **Training the Regressor**: Use AutoML to train a model that maps sparse photometric data into the latent space, predicting PCA coefficients and classifying spectral types.

4. **Bayesian Reconstruction**: Perform Bayesian inference in the latent space to reconstruct full spectra from photometric data, incorporating uncertainties and using the inverse PCA transform.

5. **Performance Evaluation**: Validate the model using LOOCV, assess reconstruction accuracy, and evaluate taxonomic classification performance.

Each step leverages statistical and machine learning techniques to enable accurate spectral reconstruction and taxonomic analysis from limited photometric data.
