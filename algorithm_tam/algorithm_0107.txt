The core computational algorithm can be broken down into the following steps:

1. **Data Preparation**:
   - Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to the reference spectra of TNOs to derive principal components, forming a latent space representation.

2. **Data Augmentation**:
   - Use non-parametric Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to smooth and extend the latent space distribution, generating synthetic latent vectors to enhance training data.

3. **Forward Modeling**:
   - Convolve each spectrum (both real and synthetic) with the instrument response functions (e.g., JWST/NIRCam filters) to compute the corresponding photometric measurements.

4. **Noise Injection**:
   - Introduce Gaussian noise with a relative reflectance uncertainty of 20% into the synthetic photometry to simulate realistic observational conditions.

5. **Model Training**:
   - Train an AutoML regressor (using AutoGluon) to map the noisy photometric data to the latent coefficients derived from PCA.

6. **Reconstruction**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients and then invert the PCA transformation to reconstruct the full spectrum.

This algorithm efficiently bridges sparse photometric data to detailed spectral reconstructions, enabling taxonomic classification and survey optimization for TNOs.
