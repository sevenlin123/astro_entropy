### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction from Sparse Photometry

1. **Training Data Preparation**:
   - Collect a sample of near-infrared spectra of TNOs (e.g., 51 spectra).
   - Exclude rare spectral types to focus on dominant taxonomic classes.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Apply PCA to the collected spectra to derive principal components (PCs).
   - PCs represent the major sources of spectral variability.

3. **Data Augmentation**:
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the latent PC space to generate synthetic spectra.
   - Inject Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty) into synthetic photometry for robust training.

4. **AutoMLRegressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDTs) using AutoML.
   - Input: Sparse photometric measurements.
   - Output: Latent PC coefficients.

5. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
   - For each spectrum, remove it from the training set.
   - Use the remaining data to augment the latent space and retrain the regressor.
   - Predict latent coefficients for the held-out spectrum.
   - Invert the PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.

6. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Project the predicted latent coefficients back into the high-dimensional spectral space using inverse PCA.
   - The reconstructed spectrum is constrained by the PCA basis, ensuring physical plausibility.

7. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Train a secondary classifier on the latent PC coordinates to predict taxonomic types (e.g., Water, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).
   - Classify new objects based on their latent coefficients.

8. **Optimal Filter Configuration**:
   - Evaluate mutual information between photometric filters and latent spectral coefficients.
   - Identify filter combinations that maximize information content for accurate spectral reconstruction.

9. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Compare reconstructed spectra against known templates.
   - Flag objects with significant deviations as potential outliers or new spectral types.
