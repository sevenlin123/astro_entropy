**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Prepare Training Data**: 
   - Select a representative sample of 51 TNO spectra from the DiSCo-TNO dataset, focusing on dominant taxonomic classes (Water, CO₂, Organics, Methanol).
   - Exclude outliers such as Centaurs, Dwarf Planets, and Neptune Trojans.

2. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**:
   - Decompose the training spectra into principal components.
   - Determine the optimal number of principal components (e.g., 4-5 for basic classification, 8-10 for detailed reconstruction) based on explained variance.

3. **Latent Space Augmentation**:
   - Apply Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to the principal component scores to create a smooth probability distribution in the latent space.
   - Use a bandwidth of 0.5 to balance local fidelity and global smoothness.

4. **Synthetic Photometry Generation**:
   - For each augmented spectrum, simulate photometric measurements using the NIRCam filter set.
   - Convolve the spectra with the instrument's throughput curves and introduce Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% relative uncertainty).

5. **AutoML Regressor Training**:
   - Train an ensemble of Gradient Boosted Decision Trees using the synthetic photometry and PCA coefficients as input-output pairs.
   - The model maps photometric inputs to PCA coefficients, enabling spectral reconstruction.

6. **Taxonomy Classifier Construction**:
   - Train a separate AutoML classifier in the PCA latent space to predict taxonomic categories.
   - Use labels from the training dataset to classify objects into Water, CO₂, Organics, and Methanol types based on principal components.

7. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - For new photometric observations, use the trained regressor to predict PCA coefficients.
   - Invert the PCA transformation to obtain the full spectral reconstruction.

8. **Taxonomic Classification**:
   - Apply the taxonomy classifier to the predicted PCA coefficients to determine the object's taxonomic category.

9. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)**:
   - Evaluate the model by comparing reconstructed spectra to the originals.
   - Assess taxonomic accuracy and spectral fidelity, ensuring reliable performance.

10. **Survey Optimization and Outlier Detection**:
    - Use the framework to identify optimal NIRCam filter configurations for future surveys.
    - Detect unusual spectral types by constraining photometry to unlikely intermediate models in the latent space.
