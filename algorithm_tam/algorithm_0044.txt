**Computational Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Preprocessing:**
   - Obtain a set of reference TNO spectra from the DiSCo-TNO sample, excluding outliers and rare types.
   - Perform Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on these spectra to extract principal components, defining a low-dimensional latent space.

2. **Data Augmentation:**
   - Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) to create a continuous probability distribution in the latent space, generating synthetic latent vectors for training stability.

3. **Forward Modeling:**
   - Simulate photometric observations by convolving the spectra with NIRCam filter throughput curves, introducing Gaussian noise (e.g., 20% uncertainty) to account for observational uncertainties.

4. **Training:**
   - Train an AutoML regressor (e.g., Gradient Boosted Decision Trees) to map the sparse photometric inputs to the latent coefficients.
   - Simultaneously train a probabilistic classifier within the latent space to categorize taxonomic types.

5. **Inference:**
   - For new photometric observations:
     - Use the trained regressor to predict latent coefficients.
     - Apply the inverse PCA transform to reconstruct the full spectrum.
     - Classify the object into taxonomic categories based on the latent space.

6. **Validation:**
   - Perform Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) to assess reconstruction accuracy and taxonomic classification, ensuring the model generalizes well.

7. **Applications:**
   - Optimize photometric filter configurations for maximum spectral information.
   - Detect outliers by constraining the latent space to extreme values, identifying rare spectral types.

**Output:** Reconstructed high-fidelity spectra and taxonomic classifications from sparse photometry, with uncertainty propagation.
