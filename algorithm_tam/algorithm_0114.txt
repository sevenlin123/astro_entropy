- Step 1: Initialize with a set of training spectra.  
- Step 2: Preprocess the training spectra by normalizing, aligning to a common wavelength grid, and removing outliers.  
- Step 3: Perform PCA on the preprocessed spectra to derive principal components, forming a latent space.  
- Step 4: Use Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on the PCA latent coefficients to generate synthetic latent points, creating a smooth manifold.  
- Step 5: Train an AutoML regressor to map synthetic photometry (derived from the latent space) to the latent coefficients.  
- Step 6: For each object, use its photometry to predict latent coefficients via the trained AutoML model.  
- Step 7: Apply the inverse PCA transform to the predicted latent coefficients to reconstruct the full spectrum.
