The algorithm systematically processes sparse photometric data to reconstruct and classify trans-Neptunian objects' spectra. It employs Bayesian inference with Gaussian Process Regression for probabilistic modeling, clusters the resulting spectra, identifies outliers, and optimizes survey strategies based on spectral informativeness.

1. **Probabilistic Model Definition**: Establish a model linking photometric magnitudes to the underlying spectrum using Bayesian principles.
2. **Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) Implementation**: Utilize GPR to model spectral variability, perform Bayesian inference, and derive posterior distributions of possible spectra from sparse data.
3. **Spectral Clustering**: Apply clustering techniques to group objects based on similarities in their posterior spectral distributions.
4. **Outlier Detection**: Identify outliers by assessing low posterior probabilities or high variance in spectral estimates.
5. **Survey Optimization**: Determine optimal spectral regions for observation by evaluating information content and uncertainty reduction potential.

This structured approach efficiently handles the reconstruction and classification of TNO spectra while optimizing observational strategies.
