Step 0: Initialize Gaussian Process Model  
Set up a Gaussian process to model the spectral energy distribution (SED), incorporating kernels that capture smoothness and wavelength correlations.  

Step 1: Define Priors Based on Taxonomic Knowledge  
Establish prior distributions for SED parameters using existing TNO taxonomic classifications to guide inference toward known spectral types.  

Step 2: Impute Missing Photometric Data  
Use the Gaussian process to model the complete SED and impute missing photometric measurements across all wavelengths.  

Step 3: Perform Bayesian Inference to Reconstruct SEDs  
Apply Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of SED parameters, combining photometric observations and spectral templates.  

Step 4: Detect Outliers Using Taxonomic Comparison  
Compare inferred SEDs against the distribution of known taxonomic classes to identify outliers, indicating potential new classes or unusual objects.  

Step 5: Assess Uncertainty and Confidence Intervals  
Calculate uncertainty estimates and confidence intervals for the reconstructed SEDs to quantify the reliability of the results.  

Step 6: Validate Through Cross-Validation (Optional)  
Optionally, perform leave-one-out cross-validation to validate the accuracy and robustness of the SED reconstruction framework.
