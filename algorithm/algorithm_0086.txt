**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Input Data:** 
   - Sparse multi-band photometric observations of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs).

2. **Model Initialization:**
   - Define a probabilistic model that represents the spectral energy distribution (SED) of TNOs using Bayesian inference.
   - Parameterize the SED with physical parameters such as albedo, thermal emission, and phase functions.

3. **Likelihood Function:**
   - Compute the likelihood of observed photometric measurements given the model parameters.
   - Account for noise and uncertainties in photometric data.

4. **Prior Distributions:**
   - Define prior probability distributions for the model parameters based on existing knowledge about TNOs (e.g., typical albedos, temperatures, etc.).

5. **Posterior Inference:**
   - Use Bayesian inference (e.g., Markov Chain Monte Carlo, MCMC) to compute the posterior distribution of model parameters given the observed data.
   - This step reconstructs the most probable SED for each TNO.

6. **SED Reconstruction:**
   - Extract the mean or median SED from the posterior distribution.
   - This reconstructed SED represents the best estimate of the object's spectral properties.

7. **Taxonomic Classification:**
   - Compare the reconstructed SEDs against reference spectra of known taxonomic groups (e.g., C-, O-, P-type).
   - Assign a classification based on the closest match or probability distributions.

8. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Analyze the sensitivity of the SED reconstruction to different observation bands.
   - Determine optimal observing strategies (e.g., band selection, exposure times) for maximizing information gain about TNO spectra.

9. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Identify TNOs whose reconstructed SEDs deviate significantly from the majority.
   - Use statistical methods (e.g., clustering, anomaly detection) to flag outliers for further study.

10. **Output:**
    - Reconstructed SEDs for each TNO.
    - Taxonomic classifications.
    - Optimized survey recommendations.
    - Identified outliers.

**End of Algorithm.**
