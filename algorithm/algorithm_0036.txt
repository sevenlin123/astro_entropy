**Algorithm Description**

1. **Input Data**: For each object, obtain sparse photometric observations across multiple bands.

2. **Gaussian Process Initialization**: Establish a Gaussian Process model to capture the spectral variability of TNOs.

3. **Latent Spectral Dimension**: Model each object's spectrum with a latent spectral dimension.

4. **Projection into Bands**: Use an invertible linear transformation to project the latent dimension into observed photometric bands.

5. **Probabilistic Transformation**: Convert the latent spectral representation into a probability distribution to account for uncertainties.

6. **Combine Spectra**: Aggregate probabilistic spectra from all bands to form a unified rest-frame spectrum.

7. **Output**: Deliver the reconstructed probabilistic spectra for each object.
