**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Initialization**: 
   - Set prior distributions for spectral templates and mixing coefficients. Spectral templates are initialized with non-negative values, and mixing coefficients are initialized to enforce sparsity using a Dirichlet prior.

2. **Likelihood Definition**: 
   - Model observed photometric data as a linear combination of spectral templates with additive Gaussian noise. The forward model is defined as observed data = templates × coefficients + noise.

3. **Prior Setup**: 
   - Apply Dirichlet distribution to mixing coefficients to ensure sparsity. Use non-informative or physically constrained priors for spectral templates.

4. **Bayesian Inference**: 
   - Utilize a computational method such as Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational inference to approximate the posterior distributions of the parameters.

5. **Posterior Estimation**: 
   - Extract point estimates (e.g., Maximum a Posteriori) and quantify uncertainties from the posterior samples.

6. **Residual Calculation**: 
   - Compute residuals by subtracting model-predicted photometry from observed data.

7. **Outlier Detection**: 
   - Identify outliers by thresholds on residuals or posterior probabilities.

8. **Output**: 
   - Reconstructed spectra and list of outliers for further analysis.

**End of Algorithm**
