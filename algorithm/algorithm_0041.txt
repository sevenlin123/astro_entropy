**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Input Data**: Obtain sparse photometric observations for each trans-Neptunian object (TNO).

2. **Initialize Gaussian Process (GP)**:
   - Define the GP with a suitable kernel that captures the smoothness of spectral energy distributions (SEDs).
   - Set hyperparameters, including length scale and variance.

3. **Probabilistic Mixture Model Setup**:
   - Formulate a mixture model to represent multiple spectral components within the object.
   - Each component is characterized by a probability distribution (e.g., Gaussian).

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Combine GP and mixture model into a joint probabilistic model.
   - Perform Bayesian parameter estimation using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational inference.
   - Update parameters based on observed photometric data, accounting for uncertainties.

5. **Reconstruct SEDs**:
   - Sample from the posterior distribution of the combined model to obtain the most probable SEDs.
   - Consider multiple spectral components identified by the mixture model.

6. **Uncertainty Assessment**:
   - Calculate confidence intervals or credible regions for the reconstructed SEDs.

7. **Analysis Applications**:
   - Utilize the reconstructed SEDs for taxonomic classification, survey optimization, and outlier detection.

8. **Output**: Provide the reconstructed SEDs along with associated uncertainties for each TNO.
