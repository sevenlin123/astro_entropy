**Algorithm:**

**Input:** Sparse photometric data of TNOs (flux measurements over time)

**Output:** Reconstructed light curves and associated uncertainties; clustered taxonomic groups; identified outliers

**Steps:**

1. **Preprocess Input Data:**
   - **Normalization:** Adjust flux measurements to account for exposure durations and instrumental biases.
   - **Handling Missing Data:** Note positions of missing data points but retain them as NaN.

2. **Pre-train Variational Autoencoder (VAE):**
   - **Training Dataset:** Utilize a collection of normalized TNO light curves.
   - **Objective:** VAE learns a latent representation capturing key aspects of TNO variability.

3. **Process Each TNO Light Curve:**
   - **Encode:** Convert the light curve into the VAE's latent space.
   - **Gaussian Process (GP) Interpolation in Latent Space:**
     - **Known Points:** Use existing latent values where data exists.
     - **Prediction:** Employ GP to forecast latent values across all time points, including missing ones.
     - **Outcome:** Retrieve predicted latent values and their variances.

4. **Model Observation Process:**
   - **Neural Network Training:** Develop a small feedforward network to map latent space representations to observed fluxes.
   - **Incorporation:** Capture relationships, including systematic biases and noise, between true light curves and observations.

5. **Reconstruct Light Curves:**
   - **Decode:** Use VAE's inverse mapping on latent values obtained from step 3.
   - **Obtain Estimates:** Derive point estimates and variance estimates for each time point.

6. **Probabilistic Clustering in Latent Space:**
   - **Clustering Method:** Group TNOs based on similarity in latent space and uncertainty.
   - **Taxonomic Grouping:** Assign clusters for taxonomy.

7. **Detect Outliers:**
   - **GP Posterior Variance Analysis:** Examine variance in latent space.
   - **Identify Outliers:** Highlight points with significantly higher variance.

8. **Generate Probabilistic Rejection Flags:**
   - **Likelihood Calculation:** Determine likelihood of each data point under the model.
   - **Reject Anomalies:** Flag points with likelihood below a threshold.

9. **Output Results:**
   - **Reconstructed Curves:** Include uncertainties.
   - **Cluster Assignments:** Reflect taxonomic groupings.
   - **Outlier List:** Catalogue identified outliers.

**End of Algorithm.**
