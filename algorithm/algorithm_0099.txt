**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Data Processing:**
   - Collect multi-band photometric measurements from various surveys (e.g., Pan-STARRS, NEOWISE, Gaia).
   - Preprocess data to remove known bad measurements.
   - Standardize photometric errors to ensure consistency across datasets.

2. **Spectral Library Construction:**
   - Build an empirical spectral library using known TNO SED templates.
   - Each template represents a distinct spectral type, characterized by features such as slope, albedo, and other spectral parameters.

3. **Bayesian Hierarchical Model Development:**
   - Establish a probabilistic model linking observed photometric magnitudes to spectral energy distributions (SEDs) through the spectral library.
   - Assume prior distributions for spectral parameters (e.g., albedo, phase function) to capture the diversity of TNOs.

4. **Posterior Inference:**
   - Perform Bayesian inference to sample the posterior distribution of spectral parameters given the observed photometry.
   - Utilize MCMC (Markov Chain Monte Carlo) methods to explore the parameter space and quantify uncertainties.

5. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Analyze the posterior distributions to identify observations inconsistent with the modeled SEDs, indicating potential outliers in the photometric data.

6. **Probabilistic Classification:**
   - Classify each object into spectral types based on the posterior probabilities of spectral parameters.
   - Assign confidence levels to each classification based on the posterior probabilities.

7. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Simulate survey designs to maximize information gain for spectral reconstruction.
   - Aid in the placement of future observations by optimizing survey strategies based on the inferred spectral parameters and their uncertainties.
