**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs**

1. **Bayesian Inference**: Apply Bayesian inference using MCMC to sample posterior distributions of spectral parameters for each TNO, incorporating ISM extinction effects.

2. **Clustering with DPM**: Use a Dirichlet Process Mixture Model to cluster TNOs based on their inferred spectral parameters, allowing the data to determine the number of clusters.

3. **Cluster Representative SED Calculation**: Compute the representative SED for each cluster, such as the mean or median of the posterior distributions of SEDs within the cluster.

4. **Information Propagation**: Refine individual TNO SEDs by propagating the cluster representative SED, particularly filling gaps in wavelengths where observations are sparse.

5. **Outlier Detection**: Identify outliers by comparing individual TNO SEDs to their cluster's representative SED, flagging those with significant deviations.
