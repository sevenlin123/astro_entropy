**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Input:** Sparse photometric data (magnitudes) for trans-Neptunian objects.

2. **Gaussian Process Modeling:**
   - Use a Gaussian process to model the spectral energy distribution (SED) as a prior.
   - The Gaussian process captures the expected smoothness and properties of spectral data.

3. **Bayesian Inference:**
   - Apply Bayesian inference to update the Gaussian process model using the observed sparse photometric data.
   - Obtain the posterior distribution of the SED, incorporating uncertainties from the data.

4. **Projection onto Templates:**
   - Project the posterior SED onto a set of predefined spectral templates.
   - Compute the probability of each template given the observed data and posterior distribution.

5. **Spectral Classification Probabilities:**
   - For each spectral template, calculate the probability that the object belongs to that class based on the data and model.
   - Aggregate these probabilities to determine the most likely spectral classification.

6. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Compare observed data against the reconstructed SEDs.
   - Identify outliers where data significantly deviate from the expected distribution.

7. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Evaluate which objects require additional observations based on reconstruction uncertainties.
   - Determine optimal survey strategies for improved spectral data collection.

8. **Output:** Probabilities for each spectral classification and flags for outliers, along with recommendations for further observations.
