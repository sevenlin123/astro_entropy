The computational algorithm for spectral reconstruction can be outlined as follows:

1. **Initialize Gaussian Process Model**: Set up a Gaussian Process with a covariance function that enforces smoothness across the entire spectrum, incorporating prior knowledge about typical spectral features of TNOs.

2. **Integrate Multiple Bands**: Use all available photometric bands to simultaneously estimate both the underlying spectral energy distribution (SED) and interstellar extinction.

3. **Account for Measurement Uncertainties**: Model the flux measurement uncertainties within the GP framework, allowing for Bayesian inference to estimate the posterior distribution of the SED.

4. **Perform Bayesian Inference**: Utilize Bayesian methods to compute the posterior distribution of the SED, considering the integrated bands and uncertainties.

5. **Incorporate Spectral Priors**: Integrate prior knowledge about typical albedos and spectral slopes of TNOs into the model to inform the reconstruction process.

6. **Detect Outliers**: Analyze the posterior probabilities to identify spectra that significantly deviate from the modeled distribution, marking them as outliers.

7. **Optimize Survey Strategy**: Based on the derived posterior probabilities, determine optimal bands and strategies for future observations to maximize the detection of specific spectral features.

This structured approach ensures the core computational method is implemented efficiently, focusing solely on the reconstruction framework.
