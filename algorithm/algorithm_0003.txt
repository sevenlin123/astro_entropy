1. Initialize the probabilistic model with parameters representing the spectral energy distribution (SED) of a trans-neptunian object (TNO), including emission across various wavelengths.
2. Incorporate sparse photometric measurements as observations within the model.
3. Define prior distributions based on known characteristics of TNO SEDs, such as typical shapes and compositional constraints.
4. Set up a hierarchical structure that connects the SED parameters to the observed photometric data, considering potential measurement uncertainties.
5. Implement a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling algorithm to explore the parameter space.
6. Use the MCMC sampler to generate posterior distributions of the SED parameters, conditioned on the observed data and prior knowledge.
7. Analyze the posterior distributions to quantify uncertainties in the reconstructed SEDs and derive statistical properties of the TNO population.
