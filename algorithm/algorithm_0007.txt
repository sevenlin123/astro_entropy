1. **Initialize Priors**: 
   - Set prior distributions for the SEDs and ISM parameters based on existing knowledge or assumptions.
   
2. **Model Basis Components**:
   - Represent each object's SED as a sum of basis components.
   
3. **Gaussian Process for ISM Extinction**:
   - Model ISM extinction curves using Gaussian processes to capture variability and uncertainty.
   
4. **Joint Inference**:
   - Use a Bayesian hierarchical model to simultaneously infer SEDs and ISM parameters.
   
5. **Noise Modeling**:
   - Include photon counting and readout noise in the likelihood function.
   
6. **Trans-Dimensional Sampling**:
   - Apply trans-dimensional sampling techniques to explore the posterior distributions of SEDs and ISM parameters.
   
7. **Posterior Refinement**:
   - Iteratively refine posterior estimates of SEDs and ISM parameters based on observed photometry.
   
8. **Probabilistic Classification**:
   - Use Bayesian inference on basis component contributions to perform taxonomic classification of objects.
