The algorithm systematically processes each trans-Neptunian object (TNO) by correcting its photometric data, constructing a forward model to link spectral parameters to observations, defining prior distributions, performing Bayesian inference, detecting outliers, calculating a figure-of-merit, and outputting the results.

**Algorithm:**

1. **Data Correction**: For each TNO, adjust photometric measurements to account for extinction and zero-point calibration.

2. **Forward Model Construction**: Develop a model that connects SED parameters to observed magnitudes, incorporating basis functions for SED representation, noise models (Gaussian and Poisson), and interstellar extinction curves.

3. **Prior Definition**: Assign informative priors to SED parameters using TNO population characteristics; use uninformative priors for noise and extinction parameters.

4. **Bayesian Inference**: Utilize a nested sampling algorithm to derive posterior distributions of SED parameters and compute Bayesian evidence for model evaluation.

5. **Posterior Predictive Distributions**: Generate distributions to assess the model's ability to predict observed magnitudes.

6. **Outlier Detection**: Compare each object's posterior probability against population priors to identify outliers.

7. **Figure-of-Merit (FoM) Calculation**: Compute FoM using Bayesian evidence to gauge the reliability of spectral reconstructions.

8. **Output**: For each TNO, output SED parameters, uncertainties, posterior probabilities, and FoM.

This structured approach ensures robust spectral reconstruction, enabling taxonomy, survey optimization, and outlier detection in sparse photometric data.
