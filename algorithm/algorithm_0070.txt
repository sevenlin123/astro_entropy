The algorithm consists of the following steps:

1. **Data Preprocessing**: Clean the input data by removing obvious errors and interpolating missing values where possible.

2. **Spectral Model Setup**:
   - Define a Gaussian Process regression model with a custom kernel designed to capture the smoothness and variability typical of TNO spectra.

3. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) to sample the posterior distribution of the spectral parameters, incorporating a hierarchical structure to account for population-wide characteristics while allowing individual variations.

4. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Identify spectra that are inconsistent with the model by evaluating high posterior probabilities of errors, marking them as outliers.

This structured approach focuses on the core computational method required for spectral reconstruction, excluding validation and visualization steps.
