**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Preprocess Photometric Data**  
   - Standardize and account for systematics in the input photometric data.

2. **Derive Basis Spectra**  
   - Select representative trans-neptunian objects (TNOs) from observed data.  
   - Extract basis spectra that will be used to model the SEDs of all TNOs.

3. **Model SED as Linear Combination**  
   - Represent the SED of each TNO as a weighted sum of the basis spectra.  
   - Introduce mixing probabilities (constrained to sum to one) to determine the contribution of each basis spectrum.

4. **Incorporate Measurement Uncertainties**  
   - Include uncertainty parameters associated with each basis spectrum.  
   - Model these uncertainties as part of the probabilistic framework.

5. **Bayesian Inference**  
   - Employ a hierarchical Bayesian model to infer the posterior distributions of mixing probabilities, SED parameters, and uncertainty parameters.  
   - Use MCMC methods (e.g., Metropolis-Hastings, Gibbs sampling) to sample the posterior distributions.

6. **Reconstruct SEDs for Target Objects**  
   - Apply the Bayesian model to each target TNO to obtain posterior distributions of mixing probabilities and SED parameters.

7. **Perform Outlier Detection**  
   - Compare observed photometric measurements with model predictions.  
   - Identify outliers by detecting significant deviations between observed data and model expectations.

8. **Output Posterior Distributions and Uncertainties**  
   - Provide the posterior distributions of SED parameters and mixing probabilities.  
   - Quantify uncertainties in the reconstructed SEDs and detected outliers.
