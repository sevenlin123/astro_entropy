**Algorithm Description: Core Reconstruction Framework for Trans-Neptunian Object SED Analysis**

1. **Input Data Collection**: 
   - Gather all available multi-band photometric observations for each trans-Neptunian object.

2. **Flux Modeling**:
   - For each photometric band, model the flux density using a Gaussian mixture distribution. Each Gaussian component represents potential flux values, accounting for measurement uncertainties.

3. **MCMC Sampling**:
   - Utilize Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods to estimate the posterior distribution of SED parameters, providing best estimates and confidence intervals for the spectral energy distribution.

4. **Quality Metric Calculation**:
   - Compute a quality metric by evaluating the agreement between the reconstructed SED and observed photometric data, assessing the reliability of the SED reconstruction.

5. **Bayesian Outlier Detection**:
   - Apply Bayesian model selection to determine if each object is an outlier. Compare the Bayesian evidence of two models: one assuming the object is typical, and another explicitly modeling it as an outlier.

6. **Population-level Analysis**:
   - Aggregate per-object SED reconstructions, quality metrics, and outlier statuses.
   - Compute statistical summaries for the entire population of TNOs to identify trends and anomalies.

7. **Survey Optimization**:
   - Simulate hypothetical survey datasets and apply the SED reconstruction framework.
   - Evaluate survey designs using quality metrics and outlier detection results to optimize detection efficiency and minimize false positives/negatives.

8. **Output Generation**:
   - Provide reconstructed SEDs, reliability metrics, outlier flags, population statistics, and optimized survey designs for each TNO.

This structured approach efficiently reconstructs the SED of TNOs, assesses data reliability, detects outliers, and optimizes survey strategies.
