**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Input Data Preparation**
   - Obtain sparse photometric data of TNOs.
   - Prepare data for probabilistic modeling.

2. **Gaussian Process Modeling**
   - Apply Gaussian Processes to model the spectral variability among TNOs.
   - Capture uncertainty due to sparse and noisy photometric data.

3. **Probabilistic Principal Component Analysis (PPCA)**
   - Apply PPCA to identify underlying spectral components.
   - Extract latent features representing the main sources of spectral variation.

4. **Bayesian Inference for Parameter Estimation**
   - Use Bayesian methods to estimate model parameters.
   - Quantify uncertainty in spectral reconstruction through posterior distributions.

5. **Forward Radiative Transfer Modeling**
   - Simulate observed magnitudes from reconstructed SEDs.
   - Apply radiative transfer models to account for physical processes affecting photometric observations.

6. **Probabilistic Clustering**
   - Group TNOs into spectral classes by comparing posterior distributions of spectral features.
   - Utilize probabilistic clustering techniques to identify spectral classes.

7. **Output**
   - Provide reconstructed SEDs, estimated parameters, and clustered spectral classes.

**End Algorithm**
