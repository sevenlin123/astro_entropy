1. **Initialize Parameters:**  
   - Set prior distributions for all model parameters, including those of the Gaussian process kernel.  

2. **Training Phase:**  
   - Fit the Gaussian process to the training data, adjusting hyperparameters to maximize the likelihood.  

3. **Forward Model Construction:**  
   - Define the radiometric transformation functions that convert the SED into photometric magnitudes across different bands.  

4. **Bayesian Inference:**  
   - Use a nested sampling algorithm to explore the parameter space, computing posterior probabilities for each SED configuration.  

5. **Gaussian Process Modeling:**  
   - Construct the Gaussian process model using the trained kernel, capturing the smoothness and covariance structure of the SED.  

6. **Nested Sampling Algorithm:**  
   - Perform nested sampling to calculate the Bayesian evidence and obtain posterior distributions of the SED parameters.  

7. **Posterior Analysis:**  
   - Summarize the posterior distributions to find the most probable SED, calculate credible intervals, and assess the uncertainty.  

8. **Applications:**  
   - Apply the reconstructed SEDs to classify TNOs, optimize survey strategies by identifying optimal bands, and detect outliers in datasets.  

9. **Output:**  
   - Provide outputs including the reconstructed SED, uncertainty estimates, classification results, optimized survey design, and identified outliers.
