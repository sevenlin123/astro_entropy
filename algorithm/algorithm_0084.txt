Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Algorithm for TNOs

Input:
- Sparse photometric data of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs), including measurements in various photometric bands, which are typically sparse, noisy, and incomplete.

Output:
- Reconstructed spectral parameters for each TNO, including uncertainties.
- Probabilistic assignment of spectral types.
- Flags indicating outliers in the dataset.
- Metrics for optimizing future surveys.

Steps:
1. **Data Preparation**: Collect and format the sparse photometric data of TNOs, normalizing and scaling measurements as necessary.
2. **Bayesian Hierarchical Model Setup**: Define a model that captures the distribution of spectral indices across the TNO population, considering each TNO as a mixture of spectral components.
3. **Gaussian Process Integration**: Implement Gaussian Processes to model relationships between spectral bands, enabling prediction of missing data points.
4. **MCMC Inference**: Perform Bayesian inference using MCMC sampling to compute posterior distributions of spectral parameters.
5. **Probabilistic Clustering**: Apply clustering techniques to assign spectral types based on posterior distributions.
6. **Outlier Detection**: Identify outliers by analyzing the spread of posterior distributions.
7. **Survey Optimization**: Use the probabilistic framework to predict optimal observing conditions and strategies for future surveys.
