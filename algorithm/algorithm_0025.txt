The probabilistic spectral reconstruction framework can be implemented as follows:

1. **Initialize Parameters**: Set up the probabilistic model with parameters including the spectral energy distribution (SED), photometric observations, and any prior constraints.

2. **Define the Forward Model**: Implement the relationship between the SED and observed photometric magnitudes, accounting for noise and uncertainties in the data.

3. **Set Prior Distributions**: Incorporate physical constraints on the SED, such as temperature limits, albedo ranges, and emission features typical of trans-Neptunian objects.

4. **Compute Posterior Distribution**: Use Bayesian inference to update the parameters based on the observed data, employing methods like Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) or variational inference to sample the posterior distribution.

5. **Reconstruct the SED**: Extract the most probable SED from the posterior distribution, representing the best estimate of the object's spectral properties.

6. **Probabilistic Outlier Detection**: Analyze the residuals or posterior probabilities to identify observations that are inconsistent with the reconstructed SED, marking them as potential outliers.

7. **Output Results**: Provide the reconstructed SED, confidence intervals, and flagged outliers for further analysis.

This algorithm efficiently reconstructs the SED and identifies outliers, enabling accurate taxonomy and survey optimization for trans-Neptunian objects.
