**Algorithm Description:**

1. **Data Collection**: Acquire sparse photometric observations of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) from the Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Exoplanets and the Universe (LS EU).

2. **Model Definition**: 
   - Represent the spectral energy distribution (SED) of each TNO using a Gaussian Process with a custom kernel designed to ensure smoothness while accommodating localized features such as absorption bands.
   
3. **Prior Construction**: 
   - Develop a hierarchical prior based on existing spectral classifications of TNOs to incorporate domain knowledge and empirical diversity.

4. **Likelihood Formulation**: 
   - Combine observations from all available photometric bands into a joint likelihood function, enabling robust SED reconstruction even from noisy and sparse data.

5. **Bayesian Inference**: 
   - Utilize Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (HMC) to perform Bayesian inference, leveraging the hierarchical structure of the model to enhance convergence and sampling efficiency.

6. **Posterior Analysis**: 
   - Extract the posterior distributions of the SEDs, calculate uncertainties, and identify outliers in the photometric data using the reconstructed SEDs.

7. **Output**: 
   - Provide reconstructed SEDs, associated uncertainties, and outlier detection results for further analysis and taxonomy studies.
