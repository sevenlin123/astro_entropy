**Core Computational Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Input Data Preparation:**
   - Obtain sparse photometric data of trans-neptunian objects (TNOs).

2. **Semi-Blind Deconvolution:**
   - Process raw photometric data.
   - Remove instrumental effects and sky background noise without prior knowledge of instrument response.
   - Output deconvolved spectrum.

3. **Adaptive Kernel Density Estimation (AKDE):**
   - Construct a probability density function of the spectral energy distribution (SED) using the deconvolved spectrum.
   - Automatically select optimal bandwidth for density estimation.
   - Generate smooth probability distributions for spectral features.

4. **Bayesian Inference Engine:**
   - Define prior distributions for spectral parameters.
   - Use observed photometric fluxes to compute posterior distributions via Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC).
   - Output credible intervals for each spectral parameter.

5. **Likelihood-Free Inference:**
   - Utilize statistics of the data for parameter inference.
   - Apply Approximate Bayesian Computation (ABC) within MCMC framework.
   - Efficiently explore parameter space without explicit likelihood function.

6. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Analyze parameter posterior distributions.
   - Identify and flag unusual spectra for review.

7. **Survey Optimization:**
   - Assess observing conditions and instrument configurations.
   - Optimize observation strategy to enhance data information content.
   - Improve detection of faint and variable TNOs.

8. **Output Results:**
   - Provide reconstructed spectra, credible intervals, outlier flags, and optimized survey parameters.

**End of Algorithm**
