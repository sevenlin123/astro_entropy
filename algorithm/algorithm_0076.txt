**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Preprocess the photometric data**: Clean and normalize the input data to ensure it is suitable for the subsequent analysis.

2. **Define a probabilistic model**: Combine Bayesian inference with Gaussian Processes to model the possible spectra, leveraging the smoothness property of Gaussian Processes.

3. **Perform Bayesian Inference**: Use the defined model to compute posterior distributions over the possible spectra based on the observed photometric data.

4. **Extract spectral features**: Identify the most probable spectral features from the posterior distributions to aid in taxonomic classification of the TNOs.

5. **Generate synthetic photometric data**: Create simulated datasets based on the reconstructed spectra to evaluate the method's performance.

6. **Optimize survey strategies**: Utilize the synthetic data to refine and improve the survey design for better detection and classification of TNOs.
