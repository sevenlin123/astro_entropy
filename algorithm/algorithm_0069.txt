**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Framework**

1. **Preprocess Photometric Data**: Clean and normalize the input photometric observations to remove artifacts and ensure consistent measurements.

2. **Define Gaussian Process Model**: Establish a Gaussian Process (GP) model that enforces spectral smoothness, capturing the typical characteristics of TNO SEDs.

3. **Integrate Prior Knowledge**: Incorporate existing knowledge about TNO materials and populations into the GP model to enhance reconstruction accuracy.

4. **Bayesian Estimation**: Utilize Bayesian inference to simultaneously estimate the underlying SED, observational noise parameters, and optimal survey cadence.

5. **Outlier Detection**: Identify anomalies by comparing the observed data against the posterior predictive distribution derived from the Bayesian analysis.

6. **Optimize Survey Strategies**: Adjust survey designs based on posterior distributions to enhance future observational outcomes.

**End of Algorithm**
