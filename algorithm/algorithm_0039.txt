The algorithm consists of the following steps:

1. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Input sparse multi-band photometric data of trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs).
   - Normalize and preprocess the data to handle missing or noisy measurements.

2. **Spectral Basis Construction**:
   - Construct a novel spectral basis that captures the variability in TNO colors.
   - This basis is derived to enable SED reconstruction from a limited number of photometric bands.

3. **Model Initialization**:
   - Initialize Gaussian processes to model the spectral energy distributions (SEDs).
   - Set up mixture models to capture the probabilistic nature of TNO classifications.

4. **Probabilistic Inference**:
   - Use Gaussian processes to infer the underlying SEDs from sparse photometric data.
   - Apply mixture models to determine the probability of each object belonging to a specific spectral class.

5. **Reconstruction of SEDs**:
   - Reconstruct the spectral energy distributions for each TNO using the inferred probabilities.
   - This step leverages the constructed spectral basis for accurate reconstruction even with limited data.

6. **Classification Probabilities**:
   - Compute the probability of each object being assigned to a particular spectral class using the mixture models.
   - These probabilities aid in taxonomic assignments and outlier detection.

7. **Uncertainty Estimation**:
   - Calculate uncertainty estimates for both the reconstructed SEDs and classification probabilities.
   - These uncertainties are crucial for assessing the reliability of the results.

8. **Outlier Detection and Survey Optimization**:
   - Identify outliers based on the uncertainty estimates and classification probabilities.
   - Use the results to optimize future astronomical surveys by highlighting areas needing more data or refining observational strategies.

By following these steps, the algorithm effectively reconstructs the SEDs of TNOs from sparse photometric data, providing a robust framework for taxonomy, survey optimization, and outlier detection.
