**Structured Algorithm Description**

1. **Data Collection**: Obtain photometric measurements for each trans-Neptunian object across various filters.

2. **Preprocessing**:
   - **Filter Data**: Exclude measurements below specified quality thresholds (e.g., due to high noise or saturation).
   - **Dimensionality Reduction**: Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to identify intrinsic color-color relationships.

3. **Probabilistic Inference**:
   - **Model Construction**: Develop a probabilistic model of Spectral Energy Distributions (SEDs) using the identified color-color relations and incorporating prior astrophysical knowledge (e.g., typical albedos).
   - **Bayesian Inference**: Utilize Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling to determine posterior distributions of SED parameters for each object.
   - **Uncertainty Quantification**: Assess uncertainties in the inferred SEDs.

4. **Postprocessing**:
   - **Derived Quantities**: Calculate absolute and relative magnitudes, colors, and other photometric features from the posterior SEDs.
   - **Synthetic Modeling**: Generate synthetic light curves or spectra based on the inferred SEDs under survey conditions.
   - **Consistency Check**: Compare synthetic data with original observations to validate consistency and detect outliers.
   - **Classification**: Categorize objects into taxonomic groups using SED-derived features.

**Final Output**: Taxonomic classifications of trans-Neptunian objects based on probabilistically inferred SEDs.
