**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

**Step 1: Data Collection**
- Collect photometric observations of TNOs, including magnitude measurements across various bands and their associated uncertainties.

**Step 2: Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
- Apply PCA to the observed magnitudes to reduce dimensionality, identifying principal components that capture major variability in SEDs.

**Step 3: Bayesian Model Definition**
- Parameterize SEDs using PCA-derived components.
- Assign prior distributions based on physical constraints (e.g., temperature, albedo).

**Step 4: Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Inference**
- Utilize MCMC (e.g., Metropolis-Hastings) to sample posterior distributions of SED parameters.
- Combine observed data likelihood with prior distributions.

**Step 5: Posterior Mode Identification**
- Determine the mode of the posterior distribution to find the most probable SED.

**Step 6: Outlier Detection**
- Calculate residuals between observed magnitudes and reconstructed SED fluxes.
- Flag observations where residuals exceed a multiple of their uncertainties as outliers.

**Step 7: Taxonomic Classification**
- Compare reconstructed SEDs to reference spectra of known taxonomic classes.
- Assign probability distributions over taxonomic classes based on SED similarity.

**Step 8: Output Generation**
- Deliver the most probable SED, classification probabilities, and outlier flags for each TNO.
