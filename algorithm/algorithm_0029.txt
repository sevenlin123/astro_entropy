1. **Initialization**: For each trans-Neptunian object, gather all available sparse photometric data across various bands.

2. **SED Representation**: Model the spectral energy distribution (SED) using a smooth function with minimal parameters, ensuring flexibility while avoiding overfitting.

3. **Forward Model Definition**: Create a probabilistic forward model that connects the SED to observed magnitudes, incorporating measurement errors and SED uncertainties.

4. **Bayesian Inference**: Employ a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampler to explore the posterior distribution of the SED parameters based on the observed data.

5. **Posterior Analysis**: Extract the most probable SED and associated uncertainties from the MCMC samples.

6. **Outlier Detection**: Identify photometric data points significantly deviating from the reconstructed SED using statistical methods.

7. **Taxonomy Formation**: Categorize multiple objects into spectral types based on their reconstructed SEDs, establishing a taxonomy.

8. **Survey Optimization**: Utilize the reconstructed SEDs and uncertainties to determine optimal survey bands and observation frequencies for detecting and studying trans-Neptunian objects.
