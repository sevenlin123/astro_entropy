**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Input Data Preparation**  
   - Collect photometric measurements for each TNO across multiple passbands (e.g., u, g, r, i, z).  
   - Remove artifacts and calibrate the data to ensure accuracy.

2. **Model Initialization**  
   - Define the set of wavelengths corresponding to the passbands used in the measurements.  
   - For each wavelength, initialize a Gaussian Process (GP) with a specified kernel (e.g., squared exponential) to model the SED.  

3. **Likelihood Construction**  
   - For each photometric measurement, compute the predicted flux and its uncertainty using the GP model.  
   - Assume the true SED varies smoothly across wavelengths, informed by the GP kernel choice.  

4. **Parameter Inference**  
   - Employ Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) to sample the posterior distributions of GP hyperparameters (e.g., length scale, variance) and the latent SED values.  
   - Use the collected measurements to condition the GP, updating the model to reflect observed data.  

5. **Posterior Prediction**  
   - After training, generate the posterior predictive distribution of the SED at all wavelengths to estimate the complete SED and associated uncertainties.  

6. **Outlier Detection**  
   - Compare the reconstructed SED against a library of reference spectra to identify outliers.  
   - Determine if the object's SED significantly deviates from typical spectral shapes.  

7. **Survey Optimization**  
   - Simulate observations of TNOs across different passbands and conditions to predict optimal survey strategies.  
   - Adjust survey parameters to maximize detection rates and minimize observational noise.  

8. **Probabilistic SED Matching**  
   - Compute likelihood ratios between the reconstructed SED and a set of template spectra.  
   - Assign probabilities to each template based on compatibility with the observed SED.  
   - Classify the object using the most probable template or mark it as unclassifiable if no template meets the threshold.  

9. **Output Generation**  
   - Deliver the reconstructed SED with uncertainties.  
   - Provide outlier status (outlier or not).  
   - Identify the best-matching template spectrum along with its probability.  
   - Offer optimized survey parameters to guide future observations.
