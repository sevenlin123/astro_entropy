**Computational Algorithm for Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction**

1. **Data Preprocessing**: Adjust raw photometric data for instrumental biases, such as zero-point errors.

2. **Feature Extraction**: Compute features from light curves, including color indices and variability metrics.

3. **Probabilistic Model Construction**: Develop a Bayesian hierarchical model to integrate all available SED information across photometric bands.

4. **Gaussian Process Modeling**: Apply a Gaussian process to model the SED, capitalizing on its capacity to handle sparse data effectively.

5. **Bayesian Parameter Estimation**: Use Bayesian techniques to determine posterior distributions of key parameters like effective temperature and surface gravity.

6. **Posterior Sampling**: Generate synthetic SEDs by sampling from the posterior distributions obtained in step 5.

7. **Physical Parameter Inference**: Extract physical parameters from the synthetic SEDs.

8. **SED Comparison and Classification**: Match inferred SEDs against templates to classify the object into appropriate taxonomic categories.

This structured approach efficiently reconstructs the SED and infers physical properties using sparse photometric data.
