**Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction Algorithm for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

**Input:**  
- Observed photometric data points (sparse and noisy)  
- Associated measurement errors  
- Observation times  

**Steps:**  

1. **Data Preparation**:  
   - Read and parse the input photometric data.  
   - Normalize the data to account for differences in exposure times or instrument calibration.  
   - Apply noise reduction techniques to minimize observational uncertainties.  

2. **Forward Modeling**:  
   - For each set of hypothesized physical parameters (e.g., diameter, albedo, rotation period, shape), simulate the expected light curve considering:  
     a. Phase variations due to rotation.  
     b. Occultations and other brightness variations.  
     c. Viewing geometry and orientation.  
   - Compare the simulated light curve with the observed data to assess goodness of fit.  

3. **Bayesian Parameter Estimation using MCMC**:  
   - Define the prior distributions for each physical parameter based on prior knowledge or assumptions.  
   - Perform Markov Chain Monte Carlo sampling to explore the posterior distribution of parameters given the observed data.  
   - Convergence diagnostics are applied to ensure the MCMC chains have adequately explored the parameter space.  

4. **Posterior Analysis**:  
   - From the MCMC samples, determine the most probable values of the physical parameters.  
   - Calculate uncertainties by quantifying the spread in the sampled posterior distribution.  

5. **SED Reconstruction**:  
   - Using the most probable physical parameters, compute the spectral energy distribution.  
   - Apply corrections for geometric and albedo variations to obtain the final SED.  
   - Include confidence intervals based on the parameter uncertainties.  

6. **Reliability Measure Calculation**:  
   - Evaluate how well the reconstructed SED can reproduce the observed light curve.  
   - Compute a figure of merit indicating the quality of the reconstruction.  

**Output:**  
- Reconstructed spectral energy distribution (SED) with confidence intervals.  
- Reliability measure assessing the consistency between the SED and the observed light curve.
