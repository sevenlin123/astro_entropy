**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Data Preprocessing**: Normalize and align the TNO light curves based on their magnitudes to adjust for differing exposure times and instrument calibrations.

2. **Spectral Basis Construction**: 
   - Utilize Gaussian processes to construct a low-dimensional basis that captures the smooth variations in spectral reflectance across multiple photometric bands.

3. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Establish a probabilistic model linking the observed photometric measurements to the underlying spectral basis functions.
   - Perform parameter estimation and quantify uncertainties using Bayesian methods.

4. **Spectral Reconstruction**:
   - Project the inferred spectral basis functions onto the observed photometric bands to reconstruct the TNO spectra.

5. **Output**:
   - Obtain reconstructed spectral features with associated uncertainties.
   - Detect outliers and anomalies based on deviations from expected spectral patterns.

6. **Survey Optimization**: Use the reconstructed spectra to optimize future surveys for efficient detection of rare spectral types.
