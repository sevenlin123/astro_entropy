**Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction (PSR) Algorithm**

**Step 1: Data Preprocessing**
- Convert observed flux measurements to magnitudes.
- Handle missing data points by normalizing magnitudes relative to a reference (e.g., V=0).

**Step 2: Gaussian Process (GP) Prior Definition**
- Parameterize the GP using a kernel function to encode SED properties, such as smoothness.

**Step 3: Adaptive Probabilistic Forward Modeling**
- Select optimal wavelengths λ* for SED estimation based on observed data, maximizing information gain about the underlying SED.

**Step 4: Bayesian Inference**
- Compute the posterior distribution over SED parameters by maximizing the log-evidence.
- Integrate out GP hyperparameters to determine the posterior.

**Step 5: Posterior Sampling**
- Sample the posterior distribution to obtain multiple SED realizations consistent with the observed data.

**Step 6: Ensemble Analysis**
- Derive best estimate and uncertainty quantification from the ensemble of sampled SEDs.

This structured approach enables robust SED reconstruction from sparse photometric data.
