**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction for Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Input Data**: Read sparse photometric data including observed color measurements and associated uncertainties.

2. **Prior Distribution Setup**: 
   - Define a prior distribution over possible spectral energy distributions (SEDs) using a Bayesian hierarchical model. This prior incorporates domain knowledge about typical SED shapes and spectral features of trans-Neptunian objects.

3. **Gaussian Process Regression**:
   - Train a Gaussian process regressor to model the relationship between the observed photometric colors and the underlying spectral features. This step captures the correlation structure in the spectral data.

4. **Bayesian Inference**:
   - Perform Bayesian inference to compute the posterior distributions of the SED parameters given the observed data. This involves updating the prior distributions with the information from the sparse photometric measurements.

5. **Posterior Analysis**:
   - Extract the most probable SEDs from the posterior distributions along with associated uncertainties. This step provides a best estimate of the SEDs and quantifies the uncertainty due to the sparsity of the data.

6. **Inference of Photometric Redshifts and Taxonomic Classifications**:
   - Use the reconstructed SEDs to derive photometric redshifts and taxonomic classifications. This involves matching the SEDs to templates or using spectral indices to determine the classification.

7. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Apply statistical methods to identify outliers in the dataset based on the posterior distributions of the SEDs. This helps in detecting unusual objects that may require further investigation.

8. **Output**: Provide the reconstructed SEDs, derived photometric redshifts, taxonomic classifications, and identified outliers.

This algorithm efficiently processes sparse photometric data to reconstruct SEDs, enabling accurate spectral analysis of trans-Neptunian objects.
