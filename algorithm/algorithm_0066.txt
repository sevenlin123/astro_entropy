**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs**

1. **Initialization**: Define parameters describing the SED of TNOs based on prior knowledge and physical constraints.

2. **Forward Modeling**: Develop a model that predicts photometric measurements from the defined SED parameters.

3. **Prior Distribution**: Establish a prior distribution over the SED parameters using known properties of TNOs.

4. **Bayesian Inversion**: Use Bayesian inference to update the parameters based on observed photometric data, incorporating the forward model and prior distribution.

5. **Outlier Detection**: Identify and handle outliers in the photometric data during the inversion process to ensure robust parameter estimation.

6. **Parameter Refinement**: Adjust parameters iteratively to maximize consistency with observed data and physical models.

7. **Final Reconstruction**: Output the most probable SED parameters after Bayesian inversion and outlier adjustment.
