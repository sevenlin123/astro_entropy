**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry**

1. **Input Data**: Sparse photometric observations of trans-neptunian objects (TNOs).
2. **Parameter Space**: Define parameters including albedo, thermal inertia, and spectral reflectance properties.
3. **Forward Model**: Simulate light absorption and emission using the defined parameters to generate expected photometric measurements.
4. **Gaussian Process (GP)**: Employ a GP to model the relationship between the parameters and observed photometric data, capturing uncertainty and correlations.
5. **Nested Sampling**: Use a nested sampling algorithm to explore the parameter space, computing posterior probabilities for each parameter configuration.
6. **Posterior Distribution**: Calculate posterior probabilities for parameters and SEDs based on Bayesian inference.
7. **Outlier Detection**: Identify objects with significantly low posterior probabilities as outliers.
8. **Survey Optimization**: Predict detection probabilities for new observations to optimize future surveys.

This algorithm reconstructs the SEDs of TNOs, quantifies uncertainties, identifies outliers, and optimizes surveys efficiently using probabilistic methods and Bayesian inference.
