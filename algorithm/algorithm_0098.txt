**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Initialize Probabilistic Model Parameters**
   - Define prior distributions for spectral parameters, incorporating spectral smoothness constraints.
   - Set up Gaussian Process Latent Space Model (GPLSM) to enforce spectral similarity among objects of the same taxonomic group.

2. **Process Sparse Photometric Data**
   - For each object, construct a probability distribution over possible fluxes at specified wavelengths based on the sparse photometric measurements.

3. **Infer Underlying Spectral Parameters**
   - Use Bayesian inference to compute the posterior distribution of spectral parameters by integrating the likelihood (relating observed fluxes to spectral parameters) and the prior distributions (including spectral smoothness and taxonomic grouping).

4. **Hierarchical Clustering of Spectra**
   - Apply a hierarchical clustering model to group objects into distinct taxonomic classes based on their inferred spectral parameters.

5. **Reconstruct and Validate Spectral Energy Distributions**
   - Reconstruct the SEDs from the inferred spectral parameters and compare them with the observed photometric data using leave-one-out cross-validation to assess model validity.

6. **Output Taxonomic Groups and Validated SEDs**
   - Deliver identified taxonomic groups and their corresponding validated SEDs for further analysis.
