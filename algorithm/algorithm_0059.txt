1. **Preprocess Photometric Data**: Correct for instrumental effects and standardize the measurements to ensure consistency across different observations.

2. **Initialize Gaussian Process Model**: For each TNO, set up a Gaussian process to represent its spectral energy distribution (SED), incorporating known constraints and properties typical of TNO spectra.

3. **Forward Model Mapping**: Apply the forward model to transform the Gaussian process representation of the SED into expected photometric magnitudes across the entire wavelength spectrum, considering the instrument's response and noise characteristics.

4. **Anomaly Detection**: Identify discrepancies between the reconstructed SED and the observed photometric data to detect potential anomalies or outliers in the dataset.

5. **Outlier Likelihood Assessment**: Calculate the probability of each object being an outlier based on the identified discrepancies, providing a measure of how unusual the object's SED is compared to typical TNO spectra.

6. **SED Reconstruction**: Using the Gaussian process, reconstruct the complete SED for each object. Adjustments are made based on the anomaly detection results if significant discrepancies are found.

7. **Output Results**: Provide the probabilistic SEDs for each TNO along with their outlier probabilities, enabling taxonomic classification, survey optimization, and targeted follow-up observations of peculiar objects.
