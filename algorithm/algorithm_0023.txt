**Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of Trans-Neptunian Objects**

1. **Initialization**:
   - Define the spectral energy distribution (SED) parameters based on prior knowledge of TNOs, incorporating physical constraints (e.g., possible emission features).

2. **Bayesian Model Setup**:
   - Construct a probabilistic model using Bayesian inference where the SED parameters are treated as random variables.
   - The model includes the likelihood function based on observed photometric fluxes and the prior distributions informed by physical constraints.

3. **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling**:
   - Utilize an MCMC sampler to explore the parameter space, generating samples from the posterior distribution of SED parameters given the observed data.
   - The MCMC process ensures efficient traversal of the parameter space, accounting for correlations between parameters.

4. **Outlier Detection**:
   - Implement mechanisms to identify unusual observations that may bias the SED reconstruction.
   - Adjust the model or exclude identified outliers to improve the accuracy of the reconstruction.

5. **Probabilistic Photometric Redshift Estimation**:
   - Estimate the photometric redshift for each object by considering the probabilistic nature of flux measurements.
   - Use the estimated redshift to account for distance-related brightness variations in the SED reconstruction.

6. **Output**:
   - Reconstruct the SED using the sampled posterior distribution.
   - Provide uncertainties on the SED parameters and identify outliers in the dataset.

**End of Algorithm**
