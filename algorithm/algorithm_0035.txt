### Algorithm: Probabilistic Spectral Reconstruction of TNOs from Sparse Photometry

1. **Data Preprocessing**  
   - Extract light curves and flux measurements from sparse photometric data of Trans-Neptunian Objects (TNOs).

2. **Spectral Parameterization**  
   - Represent each object’s spectral energy distribution as a linear combination of basis spectra derived from a training set.

3. **Bayesian Inference**  
   - Use Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods to estimate posterior distributions of weights and parameters, incorporating measurement noise and intrinsic variability.

4. **Posterior Analysis**  
   - Generate synthetic light curves by sampling from the posterior distributions to assess consistency with observed data.

5. **MCMC Diagnostics**  
   - Evaluate convergence and reliability of MCMC chains through statistical checks (e.g., effective sample size, R-hat).

6. **Result Aggregation**  
   - Compute best estimates of spectral parameters and associated uncertainties by combining posterior samples.

---

This algorithm outlines the core computational steps for reconstructing the spectral properties of TNOs from sparse photometric data, focusing on Bayesian modeling and MCMC analysis.
