**Algorithm Description:**

1. **Define the Forward Model:**
   - Establish a parametric probabilistic model linking photometric observations to spectral parameters.
   - Incorporate photon counting noise and background fluctuations.
   - Mathematically formulate the relationship between observed fluxes and spectral parameters.

2. **Set Up the Bayesian Inversion Scheme:**
   - Formulate the problem using Bayesian statistics, where the goal is to infer posterior distributions of spectral parameters given observed data.
   - Define a prior distribution over spectral parameters using a mixture of Gaussians to capture uncertainty and flexibility in spectral shapes.

3. **Compute the Likelihood Function:**
   - Use an adaptive kernel density estimation technique based on observed fluxes to calculate the likelihood function.
   - This step transforms observed photometric data into a probability distribution representation.

4. **Perform Bayesian Inference:**
   - Combine the prior distribution and likelihood function to compute the posterior distribution of spectral parameters.
   - Utilize Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods or other sampling techniques to estimate the posterior distributions.

5. **Hierarchical Clustering with Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM):**
   - Organize spectral parameters into clusters, each represented by a GMM.
   - Classify objects into predefined spectral classes using their inferred spectral parameters.
   - This step enables robust classification even when dealing with incomplete or noisy data.

6. **Outlier Detection:**
   - Analyze residuals or posterior probabilities to identify outliers in the dataset.
   - Flag objects that significantly deviate from expected spectral patterns.

7. **Output Posterior Distributions and Classifications:**
   - Provide the posterior distributions of spectral parameters for further analysis.
   - Deliver classified spectral types based on the GMM clustering.

This structured algorithm effectively captures the core computational method described in the paper, focusing on Bayesian inversion, probabilistic modeling, and hierarchical classification.
